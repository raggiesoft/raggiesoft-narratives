# The Gastric Emptying Crisis

## I. The Geographic Blacklist & Clinical Isolation
Suffering from severe, unexplained nausea and an inability to process his meticulously predictable meals, Matt Miller is scheduled for an outpatient Gastric Emptying Study. 
*   **The Intelligence Failure:** The family operates with a zero-risk geographic routing policy. Had Chloe or Sarah known that Heather Bouchard was employed as a technologist at this specific hospital, the facility would have been instantly blacklisted. They would have driven Matt completely out of Charlottesville to execute the procedure. Because hospital staffing schedules are private, the family walked blindly into the trap.
*   **The Division of Forces:** When Matt is called back from the main outpatient waiting room by a general radiology assistant, the family executes their standard "One-Person" protocol. 
*   **The Anchor:** Sarah Miller, functioning as the overarching "Charge Nurse," remains in the main hospital waiting area. 
*   **Player Two:** Shiloh Brooks pushes Matt's manual wheelchair through the double doors and into the isolated, sterile Nuclear Medicine camera suite. The assistant informs them the technologist will be right in, and closes the heavy door.

## II. The Return of the Safe Person
When the heavy, lead-lined door swings open a few minutes later, Heather Bouchard walks in wearing hospital scrubs and a dosimeter badge. 
*   **The Tragedy of the Good Variable:** Unlike past antagonists, Heather was never a bad person. During their time at the community college, her unbothered acceptance made her the very first non-family member Matt ever classified as an autistic "Safe Person". Her departure was driven entirely by silent, unarticulated burnout, not malice. 
*   **The `Err 6` Crash:** The exact millisecond Matt registers her face—his first time seeing her since she vanished at the Downtown Transit Station—his system completely crashes. His jaw locks, his dyspraxic spasticity seizes his limbs, and he begins to weep silently in his manual wheelchair, triggering a violent `Err 6 - Overflow` meltdown.

## III. The Tactical Silence
Shiloh’s reaction is immediate, clinical, and fiercely controlled. 
*   **The Triage:** Shiloh drops to the clinical floor, buries her head against Matt, and applies massive, grounding deep-pressure therapy to his locked frame. 
*   **The Shift in Power:** Heather is forced to retreat behind her professional mask. She remembers Matt and Shiloh clearly, and she recognizes Shiloh's absolute LPN authority over Matt's baseline. Heather stands silently by the camera console, completely sidelined.
*   **The Impossible Choice:** Shiloh has her Omni-Comm two-way pager in her pocket. She knows that if she transmits a `911` code, Sarah will violently bulldoze through the secure hospital doors to protect them. But Shiloh also knows Matt desperately needs this diagnostic test. She chooses to endure the emotional torture alone, keeping her cousin anchored while shooting Heather a look of absolute, glacial fury. 

## IV. The Torture of the Ghost Hugs & The Logic Collision
Once Matt is stabilized enough to consume the radiolabeled meal, the true psychological horror of the four-hour procedure begins. 
*   **The Positioning:** Because of his severe dyspraxia, Matt cannot stand upright or align himself between the two massive, flat gamma detectors. Four separate times over the course of the morning, Heather must step out from behind the lead glass and manually square his posture against the imaging plate. 
*   **The Apprehension:** Matt dreads her approach. As Heather steps into his personal space, he visibly tenses. It is not out of fear, but out of agonizing cognitive dissonance. His logical brain understands the clinical necessity of her touch, but he desperately wishes it was literally anyone else performing the scan. He cannot compartmentalize her presence; he cannot see her as just a healthcare professional. 
*   **The Reverted Memory:** The exact second Heather places her hands firmly on his shoulders and ribs, his internal processing reverts. The sterile hospital environment vanishes, and she is instantly his best friend from the CCC cafeteria again. 
*   **The Restraint:** To Matt, whose primary language is tactile, her hands on his shoulders feel exactly like the preamble to an embrace. Every single time she touches him, his muscle memory instinctively begs him to lean forward and hug her. He is forced to actively, physically fight his own dyspraxic body to remain rigid, knowing the boundary is permanently closed and he cannot embrace her.
*   **The Rejection of Physics:** The exact second his posture is squared, Heather's hands vanish. She retreats behind the console to run the camera, leaving Matt completely exposed, aching for a connection that she clinically refuses to acknowledge, while Shiloh tightly holds his hand to keep him tethered to reality.

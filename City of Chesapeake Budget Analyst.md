## Job Description

**Do you want to work for one of the best-run cities in Virginia? If you are detail-oriented, passionate about data analysis or public policy, and recognized for your outstanding analytical and customer service skills, we invite you to apply for an opening in our Budget Department!**

The City of Chesapeake is seeking applications for the position of Budget Analyst. While experience in local government budgeting is preferred, this position is also open to early-career professionals. The ideal candidate demonstrates intellectual curiosity, strong analytical abilities, and the initiative to work independently to develop solutions to complex challenges.

The Budget Analyst will join a talented and innovative Budget team in a city recognized for its stability and effective management. The Budget Department partners with more than 40 City departments to develop an annual operating budget and Capital Improvement Program (CIP) totaling more than $2 billion. This position provides the opportunity to build strong relationships with assigned departments and make data-driven recommendations to support City leadership.

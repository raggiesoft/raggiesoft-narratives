# Courtney Evans: Future Narrative & Lore

## The Premise
Following the events of *Crimson Node* (specifically Chapter 5's interlude where Courtney begins her path to redemption and rebuilds her bond with her younger brothers, Jason and Ryan), a massive tragedy strikes the Evans family. Their parents pass away unexpectedly. 

As young adults, the three siblings are forced to stick together to survive financially. They are forced to move out of their childhood home and into a cramped, tiny apartment because it's all they can afford.

## The Apartment
The siblings live in a very small 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom apartment.
- **Bedroom:** 11'0" x 13'0"
- **Living Room:** 10'0" x 16'4"
- **Kitchen:** 16'0" x 7'0"
- **Bathroom:** 5'0" x 7'0"

*(Note: This floor plan is modeled directly after the author's real-life apartment).*

## Living Arrangements & Sibling Dynamics
Because of the extreme lack of space, privacy and personal bubbles are completely thrown out the window. 
- **The Bed:** The 11x13 bedroom only comfortably fits a Queen-sized bed. Instead of drawing straws or putting the boys on the floor, all three siblings pile into the same bed every night. 
- **Sleeping Positions:** Courtney sleeps in the exact middle of the bed. This allows both Jason and Ryan to cuddle up against her sides. 
- **The Emotional Core:** This heavily cramped, zero-boundary living arrangement mirrors the "indoor camping tents" they used to build as children, and perfectly reflects the conclusion of the *Crimson Node* interlude where they huddled together during the thunderstorm. It physically represents how tightly they must cling to one another to survive the trauma of losing their parents.

## Themes
- **Fierce Protection:** Courtney's complete transition from a superficial, image-obsessed high school cheerleader into the hardened, fiercely protective matriarch of her family.
- **Codependency vs. Survival:** The extremely close physical and emotional proximity of the three siblings as they navigate adulthood, grief, and poverty together.

## Past Lore: The High School Years & The Incident
Before the tragedy of losing their parents, the narrative actually begins during Courtney's childhood and tracks her evolution.
- **The Golden Child:** Courtney grew up deeply loved, and her family (especially Jason, Ryan, and her mother) thought highly of her.
- **Crossing Paths with Matt:** As they grow up, Courtney crosses paths with Matt Miller. Interestingly, Courtney's brothers and her mother *really like Matt*. 
- **The Betrayal:** Because her family likes Matt so much, they are absolutely *furious* with Courtney when she screws everything up with him.
- **The Catalyst Event:** Courtney attempts to talk Matt into a sexual encounter in the closet on the side of the stage. 
- **Geography of the Stage:** The confrontation takes place in the band room hallway (the backstage side). If you use the backstage door, the closet is to the *right* of the stage. (Conversely, if entering from the main auditorium entrance, it's on the left).

## Family Dynamics & The Tragedy
- **The Missing Father:** During the events of *Crimson Node*, there is no mention of "Mr. Evans" in the household. This is because a bitter divorce is about to be finalized. 
- **Custody & Maiden Name:** Joanne Evans (the mother) has secured 100% full custody of Courtney, Jason, and Ryan. The father has been stripped of all visitation and parental rights. Upon finalization of the divorce, Joanne plans to revert to her maiden name.
- **The Cause of Death:** This extremely tense, one-sided divorce settlement directly factors into *why* the parents pass away. The father is furious and bitter over losing everything, which inevitably leads to the tragedy that leaves the three siblings orphaned.

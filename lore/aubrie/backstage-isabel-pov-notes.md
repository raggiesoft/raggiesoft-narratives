## 1. Move-In Friday: *"The Awkward Outlet"* from Isabel Dawson's POV (`August 22, 2014 — 13:00 EST`)
- **Corresponding Main-Novel Scene:** [`raggiesoft-narratives/books/aubrie/e001/c003/s003.md`](../../books/aubrie/e001/c003/s003.md) (*"The Awkward Outlet"*, locked strictly to Aubrey's POV).
- **Why This Cannot Be Shown in Aubrey's POV:**
  - In `e001/c003/s003.md`, eighteen-year-old Aubrey Fuller **does not know that he has bilateral progressive sensorineural hearing loss** (because his anti-doctor mother, Aubrie Sinclair, never had his hearing or vision tested).
  - Locked inside Aubrey's POV with the solid-core oak fire door of `Room 214` shut, Aubrey hears nothing in the hallway at all—the first thing he registers is the sudden change in air pressure and the draft across his neck when the motorized door swings inward, causing him to turn around in his wheelchair and see Isabel waving in the doorway.
- **What Actually Happened in the Corridor (Isabel's POV — CPI Residence Life Two-Knock Welfare-Check Protocol):**
  1. **The Friday Early-Arrival Check-In Clipboard:** At `1:00 PM` on Friday, August 22, 2014, 2nd-Floor Female RA **Isabel Claire Dawson** (`Room 215`) is walking her early-arrival check-in rounds on the second floor of Bennett Hall wearing her official two-tone Chicago Maroon, white, and Burnt Orange CPI Residence Life Student Staff polo shirt, pinned nametag, and RA duty lanyard. Her clipboard lists a single Friday early arrival for `Room 214, Bed A`: **`Aubrey Fuller`** (whom the front desk logged as having checked in earlier that day).
  2. **First Knock & RA Announcement:** Stopping outside `Room 214` (staggered across the hall from her own door at `Room 215`), Isabel raps politely on the heavy oak fire door and calls out clearly through the wood:
     > *"Residence Life! Isabel, second-floor RA—doing early check-in rounds!"*
  3. **Second Knock & Safety Concern:** Five seconds pass with complete silence from inside `Room 214`. Knowing from the front-desk log that the new resident in `214-A` is an incoming freshman wheelchair user whose mother already left campus—and concerned for his safety in case he had a fall or transfer issue inside—Isabel follows standard **CPI Housing & Residence Life "Two-Knock & Announce" Room Entry Protocol**, knocking a second time more firmly and calling out:
     > *"Residence Life! Aubrey, I'm going to tap my Poly Pass and key in to check on you!"*
  4. **RA Master Poly Pass Entry & Instant Clinical Recognition:** Hearing no response after a second pause, Isabel taps her **RA Master Poly Pass** against the black exterior RFID reader. The motorized handicap opener whirrs, swinging the heavy oak door inward into `Room 214`.
  5. **What Isabel Sees & Clocks Immediately:**
     - As the door swings open and shifts the air in the room, the boy in the manual wheelchair at the desk startles and turns around, holding an unplugged black laptop power cord in his hand in front of a dark, dead laptop screen.
     - Because Isabel grew up in Charlottesville caring for her 17-year-old sister **Elana Grace Dawson** and cousin **Stephen Dawson** alongside her uncle **Andy Dawson, BSN, RN**, she puts the puzzle pieces together in less than three seconds:
       - *He didn't ignore my knocking—he genuinely didn't hear me through the door.*
       - When he instinctively cups a hand behind his ear as she greets him, she immediately raises her speaking volume slightly and faces him directly so he can see her lips, never calling attention to his hearing or embarrassing him for not answering the knock.
       - She spots the wall outlet recessed awkwardly behind the desk legs where a wheelchair user cannot reach it, remembers the roll of double-sided mounting tape in her khaki pocket, and offers to mount his power strip right on top of the desk.

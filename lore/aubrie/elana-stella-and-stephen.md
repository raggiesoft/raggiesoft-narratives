## 2. Core Trio Profiles: Elana, Stephen, & Stella

### A. Elana Grace Dawson (`Agent(Elana_Dawson)`)
* **Age & Academic Timeline:**
  * **Age `17` in December 2014** (born `Spring 1997`; high school senior in `2014–2015`).
  * **Incoming CPI Freshman (`Fall 2015`, Class of 2019):** Accepted Early Action to **Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute** to study **Computational Linguistics & Assistive Technology Design** (Computer Science / Human-Centered Computing track), where she, **Stephen**, and **Stella** become among the very first incoming freshmen admitted into CPI's brand-new **Fall 2015 Opt-In Gender-Inclusive Housing (`GIH`) Pilot Program** (the program directly inspired by **Jordan Fuller** and **Bennett Hall Suite 214/216**!).
* **Physical & Neurological Profile:**
  * Born with congenital high-level neuromuscular quadriplegia / severe complex motor impairment affecting all four limbs and trunk stability, requiring a specialized high-back, tilt-in-space **power wheelchair** for all mobility and full physical assistance with transfers and Activities of Daily Living (`ADLs`).
  * **Vocalization & Speech:** Unlike Jordan Fuller, Bob Scott, and Emily Jenkins, **Elana *can* vocalize and speak aloud**. Her natural speaking voice is clear, expressive, and warm, though because diaphragmatic breath support can fatigue during long study sessions or when she is physically tired, she paces her spoken sentences thoughtfully and seamlessly supplements long-form writing, coding, and rapid dialogue with her **`mPad`**.
  * **Cognitive Profile:** Exceptionally intelligent, observant, witty, and fiercely independent-minded; possesses near-photographic retention for syntax, systems logic, and linguistic structure.
  * **Separation Sensitivity:** Because of early childhood hospitalizations where she was separated from family in sterile pediatric ICU wards, Elana struggles with acute physiological and emotional anxiety when separated from her cousin and Safe Person, **Stephen Dawson**.
* **Assistive Technology (`Sip-and-Puff Power Chair + mPad Bridge`):**
  * **Mobility Control:** Operates her custom high-back tilt-in-space power wheelchair via a **pneumatic Sip-and-Puff (`SnP`) straw interface** mounted on a gooseneck armature beside her right cheek:
    * **Hard Puff:** Drive forward / accelerate
    * **Hard Sip:** Reverse / emergency brake
    * **Soft Puff / Soft Sip:** Proportional left/right steering and power tilt-in-space pressure-relief recline
  * **`mPad` AAC & Computing Integration:** Mounted on a carbon-fiber swing-arm on the left rail of her chair is her **`mPad`** (in-universe iPad), loaded with both full desktop/coding accessibility software and AAC voice-synthesis software for high-noise environments or vocal rest. Using a custom ultra-low-latency Bluetooth pneumatic switch bridge engineered by her cousin **Stephen**, Elana can toggle seamlessly between driving her power chair and controlling her `mPad` cursor/keyboard with zero lag.

* **Age & Kinship:**
  * **Age `17` in December 2014** (born `Spring 1997`, six weeks apart from Elana; son of Greg Dawson's brother **Andrew "Andy" Dawson, BSN, RN**).
  * Raised under the same roof in Charlottesville as Elana and Isabel after his mother passed away when he was a toddler.
* **The Symbiotic Bond & Safe Person Role:**
  * **Inseparable Since Infancy:** Stephen and Elana grew up side by side in the same crib room, were homeschooled together at the same oak table by their grandmother **Margaret Thornton**, and operate as two halves of a single synchronized unit.
  * **Life Goal:** Stephen has consciously and joyfully chosen his life's dual vocation:
    1. Being **Elana's lifelong Safe Person, care partner, and anchor** so she never has to navigate the world or a hospital room alone.
    2. Becoming a **Systems Architect** (`Computer Engineering & Systems Architecture` major at **CPI**, `Class of 2019`) who designs fault-tolerant hardware, embedded assistive firmware, and zero-downtime enterprise architectures.
  * **Trained Care Fluency:** Trained from childhood by his father **Uncle Andy (`BSN, RN`)**, Stephen knows every detail of Elana's daily physical care—lateral and Hoyer transfers, tilt-in-space pressure relief, sip-and-puff calibration, feeding/hydration support, and reading subtle changes in her breathing or vocal cadence before anyone else in the room notices.

### C. Stella (`Elana's Service Miniature Horse`)
* **Name & Breed:** **`Stella`** — Registered **American Miniature Horse (`AMHA Class A` / Falabella cross)**.
* **Age & Bond Timeline (as of `December 2014`):**
  * **Age `6 years old`** (foaled in `Spring 2008`).
  * After completing 2.5 years of specialized indoor service-equine foundation and task training, **Stella was paired with Elana and Stephen in `Spring 2011` (when both cousins were `14` and entering 9th grade)**.
  * By `December 2014`, Stella has **`3.5 years` of seasoned, real-world working experience** with Elana and Stephen—and because miniature horses live **30 to 35+ years**, at age 6 she is in her prime and will remain Elana's working service partner through all four years at CPI (`2015–2019`) and well into Elana's thirties and forties.
* **Physical Dimensions & Working Gear:**
  * **Height & Weight:** **`28 inches` (`71 cm`) at the withers** and **`85 lbs` (`38.5 kg`)**—squarely within the federal ADA service miniature horse guidelines (`24–34 inches` at the shoulders and `70–100 lbs` under `28 CFR § 35.136(i)` / `§ 36.302(c)(9)`).
  * **Coat & Coloring:** Soft **silver-dapple coat** with a snow-white flaxen mane and tail, expressive dark brown eyes, and a calm, statuesque posture.
  * **Indoor Footwear & Harness:** Wears a custom-fitted burgundy biothane-and-leather service vest equipped with padded tug straps and a rigid stability handle, plus a set of **four custom rubber-soled indoor equine grip boots ("horse sneakers")** whenever working indoors. The rubber boots give her 100% non-slip traction on polished tile, linoleum, and hardwood floors while dampening her hoofsteps to a soft, rhythmic *tock-tock*.
* **How Having a Service Miniature Horse (`Stella`) Compares to a Service Dog:**
  1. **Lifespan (`30–35+ Years` vs. `8–12 Years` for a Service Dog):**
     * A complex-disability service animal takes 2–3 years to train. A service dog retires at age 8–10 and passes away around 10–12, forcing a handler who already struggles with separation anxiety to endure a devastating loss every decade. Because miniature horses live **30 to 35+ years**, Stella is a multi-decade partner who will never leave Elana during her school or young-adult years.
  2. **Prey-Animal Focus vs. Predator Distractions:**
     * Dogs are predators whose instincts can be triggered by squirrels on a campus quad, cats, or food scraps dropped on a dining hall floor. As an equine herd/prey animal desensitized from foalhood, Stella has zero prey drive: she ignores squirrels, birds, and dropped food completely, staying calmly locked onto her two "herd mates" (`Elana` and `Stephen`).
  3. **Precision Retrieval, Switch Activation, & Door-Pulling Power:**
     * With `85 lbs` of low-center-of-gravity four-hoof traction and remarkably prehensile equine lips, Stella can pick up a dropped `mPad` stylus, straw, or cable off the floor with velvet gentleness without tooth-marking it; pull open heavy commercial doors via handicap tug straps; nudge Elana's feet or hands back onto her footplates/armrests; nose wall-mounted ADA push-plates; and physically block crowded pedestrian traffic from jostling Elana's power chair.
  4. **Housebreaking, Hypoallergenic Coat, & Home/Campus Routine:**
     * Stella is **100% housebroken** (trained to hold her bladder and bowels indoors for 6–8+ hours and relieve herself only on command in designated outdoor grass/mulch relief areas).
     * Her equine coat sheds far less dander than canine fur and is **hypoallergenic**, making her ideal for university classrooms, residence halls, and hospitals.
     * At home in Charlottesville, Stella has a clean, cedar-and-mat bedded alcove right inside Elana's first-floor bedroom suite (and a fenced grassy paddock in the backyard for off-duty roll-and-graze time).

## 3. The Rest of the Multi-Generational Dawson Household (Charlottesville, VA)

1. **Isabel Claire Dawson (`Age 20 — Older Sister, CPI Class of 2016`):**
   * **Role:** 2nd-Floor Female Resident Advisor at Bennett Hall (`Room 215`); Human Development / Pre-Occupational Therapy & Nursing major.
   * **Why She Became an RA:** The RA compensation package (100% free single room + full meal plan + stipend) allows Isabel to put herself through CPI debt-free so her parents' resources can stay focused on Elana's assistive technology and future needs.
   * **Platonic Boundary with Jordan:** Isabel can and does give Jordan warm, **two-armed platonic hugs** (as a beloved big-sister figure and guardian angel), while both Isabel and Jordan strictly honor the CPI Residence Life professional boundary (`no romantic dating, no sleeping over, and no acting as an on-floor personal care attendant while she is his active RA`). Growing up with Elana, Stephen, and Uncle Andy is the exact reason Isabel immediately recognized Jordan's needs on Move-In Friday (`e001/c003/s003.md`).

2. **Andrew "Andy" Dawson, BSN, RN (`Age 45 — Paternal Uncle, Full-Time Caregiver, & PSU Med Student`):**
   * **Kinship:** Younger brother of Elana and Isabel's father (`Greg Dawson`), and father of **Stephen Dawson** (widower).
   * **Clinical Background & Role:** A veteran **Registered Nurse (`BSN, RN`)** with 15 years of pediatric ICU and complex neuromuscular rehabilitation experience. Andy serves as Elana's primary full-time caregiver at home and systematically taught Greg, Becca, Isabel, Stephen, and Gran Thornton the fundamentals of safe transfers, respiratory/airway hygiene, skin integrity, and emergency triage.
   * **Strict Medical Ethics Boundary:** While Andy is an `RN` and Elana's hands-on caregiver at home, he **never** crosses the professional/ethical boundary of acting as her prescribing or diagnosing medical provider. Elana has her own independent **Primary Care Physician (`PCP`)** and specialist team at **Piedmont State University Medical Center (`PSU Medical Center`)** who write all clinical orders.
   * **Current Medical School Path (`RN` $\rightarrow$ `MD`):** Seeing firsthand how badly standard adult healthcare drops the ball when pediatric complex-care patients turn 18 inspired Andy to enroll at the **Piedmont State University School of Medicine (`PSU School of Medicine`)** in Charlottesville to earn his **`MD`**, specializing in **Complex Medical & Adult Disability Medicine (`Physiatry / Complex Care`)**.

3. **Gregory "Greg" Dawson (`Age 48 — Father`) & Rebecca "Becca" Dawson (`née Thornton, Age 46 — Mother`):**
   * **Greg Dawson:** Senior civil and facilities engineer in Charlottesville (works closely with contractors at **Piedmont State University**). Alongside his brother Andy, Greg engineered the Dawson home into a zero-barrier sanctuary—flush concrete-and-flagstone entry ramps, 42-inch doorways, hardwood/tile floors for Elana's power chair and Stella's rubber boots, a roll-in wet-room bath with an overhead ceiling track lift, and an attached backyard paddock for Stella.
   * **Rebecca "Becca" Dawson:** Regional nonprofit and public library program director in Charlottesville; warm, organized, and deeply proud of all three kids (`Isabel`, `Elana`, and nephew `Stephen`).

4. **Margaret "Gran" Thornton (`Age 71 — Maternal Grandmother & Homeschool Educator`):**
   * **Background:** Rebecca's mother; a retired Virginia public school teacher with **40 years of classroom experience** (certified in secondary mathematics, sciences, and special-education curriculum design) who lives full-time in the Dawson home.
   * **The Dawson Home Academy:** Because Elana struggles with being separated from Stephen and traditional public schools repeatedly tried to separate them into different tracks, Gran Thornton took over their education from elementary school through 12th grade—running a rigorous, college-preparatory homeschool academy right in their sunlit conservatory so Elana and Stephen could learn side by side and graduate ready for **CPI (`Fall 2015`)**.

### C. Stella (`Service_Animal(Stella)` — Dual-Bonded Service Miniature Horse for Both Cousins)
* **Name & Breed:** **`Stella`** — Registered **American Miniature Horse (`AMHA Class A` / Falabella cross)**.
* **Age & Dual-Bond Timeline (as of `December 2014`):**

  * **Dual-Bonded ADA Service Animal for *Both* Cousins:** Because Elana and Stephen are both non-weight-bearing power wheelchair users who are always side by side, **while Stella is technically registered on paper as Elana's primary service animal, she has bonded equally with both cousins and performs trained ADA tasks for both Elana and Stephen!**
  * Whether Elana drops her stylus/straw or Stephen drops a soldering tool or server patch cable from his basic power chair, Stella retrieves it immediately for whichever cousin needs her, noses wall-mounted ADA door paddles for their two-chair convoy, pulls open interior doors with her tug strap, and alerts if Elana's muscles begin to lock up.
* **Physical Dimensions & Working Gear:**
  * **Height & Weight:** **`28 inches` (`71 cm`) at the withers** and **`85 lbs` (`38.5 kg`)**—squarely within the federal ADA service miniature horse guidelines (`24–34 inches` at the shoulders and `70–100 lbs` under `28 CFR § 35.136(i)` / `§ 36.302(c)(9)`).
  * **Coat & Coloring:** Soft **silver-dapple coat** with a snow-white flaxen mane and tail, expressive dark brown eyes, and a calm, statuesque posture.
  * **Indoor Footwear & Harness:** Wears a custom-fitted burgundy biothane-and-leather service vest equipped with padded tug straps and a rigid stability handle, plus a set of **four custom rubber-soled indoor equine grip boots ("horse sneakers")** whenever working indoors. The rubber boots give her 100% non-slip traction on polished tile, linoleum, and hardwood floors while silencing her hoofsteps.
* **How Having a Service Miniature Horse (`Stella`) Compares to a Service Dog:**
  1. **Lifespan (`30–35+ Years` vs. `8–12 Years` for a Service Dog):**
     * A complex-disability service animal takes 2–3 years to train. A service dog retires at age 8–10 and passes away around 10–12, forcing handlers who already struggle with separation anxiety to endure a devastating loss every decade. Because miniature horses live **30 to 35+ years**, at age 6 Stella has `3.5 years` of seasoned experience and will remain with Elana and Stephen through all four years at CPI (`2015–2019`) and well into their thirties and forties.
  2. **Prey-Animal Focus vs. Predator Distractions:**
     * Dogs are predators whose instincts can be triggered by squirrels on a campus quad, cats, or food scraps dropped on a dining hall floor. As an equine herd/prey animal desensitized from foalhood, Stella has zero prey drive: she ignores squirrels, birds, and dropped food completely, staying calmly locked onto her two "herd mates" (`Elana` and `Stephen`).
  3. **Precision Retrieval, Switch Activation, & Door-Pulling Power for Both Wheelchairs:**
     * With `85 lbs` of low-center-of-gravity four-hoof traction and remarkably prehensile equine lips, Stella picks up dropped items for either Elana or Stephen without tooth-marking them, pulls heavy doors open via tug straps, noses ADA push-plates, and walks smoothly alongside their two power wheelchairs.
  4. **Housebreaking, Hypoallergenic Coat, & Home/Campus Routine:**
     * Stella is **100% housebroken**, sheds far less dander than canine fur (**hypoallergenic** for hospitals and classrooms), and sleeps in a clean bedded alcove right inside Elana and Stephen's ground-floor bedroom suite so she can also alert to nighttime muscle lock-ups.

## 4. Fall 2015 CPI Campus Architecture: `New River Hall (Room 323)`, `Forge Market`, `Forge Dollars`, Isabel's Senior RA Apartment, & Stella's Campus Accommodations

### A. Elana, Stephen, & Stella's Freshman Residence Hall — `New River Hall, Room 323` (`Fall 2015–Spring 2016`)
* **Building Name & Hillside Geography (`New River Hall`):**
  * One of the newer, modern Ironstone-clad residence halls on the **Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (`CPI`)** campus, selected as the flagship host building for CPI's **Fall 2015 Opt-In Gender-Inclusive Housing (`GIH`) Pilot Program** (the program directly inspired by **Jordan Fuller** and **Bennett Hall Suite 214/216**).
  * **Dual Ground-Floor Hillside Architecture (`Floors 1, 2, & 3`):**
    * Because **New River Hall** is built into the steep Blacksburg campus hillside, **both the 1st Floor (lower campus quad side) and the 3rd Floor (upper street/walkway side) have zero-step, ground-level exterior exits**.
    * **Floor 2 (Middle Level)** houses **`Forge Market`**—CPI's in-house quick-service dining and campus corner-market concept—along with **CPI Dining Services administrative offices**.
    * **In-House Self-Operated Dining Canon:** **Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (`CPI`) is one of only TWO universities in the entire Commonwealth of Virginia that directly self-operates 100% of its own campus dining services in-house** (rather than outsourcing to third-party corporate food-service contractors; the only other university in Virginia to do so is in Newport News). Because **CPI Dining Services** is 100% university-owned and self-operated—with its administrative offices located right on the 2nd floor of New River Hall alongside **`Forge Market`**—it can customize menus, procurement, and medical dietary accommodations directly without corporate red tape.
    * All student residential corridors (`Floors 1, 3, 4, & 5`) are physically separated and air-gapped behind **Poly Pass** card-reader security doors so public shoppers and diners on Floor 2 can never wander into the residential halls.
* **`Room 323` (`3rd-Floor Ground-Exit Accessible Suite`):**
  * Because **`Room 323`** is on the **3rd Floor**, Elana, Stephen, and Stella can roll straight out the 3rd-floor doors onto the upper campus walkway at grade without needing an elevator—or ride the oversized building elevator down one floor to **`Forge Market`** (`Floor 2`) or down two floors to the lower campus quad (`Floor 1`).
  * **Private Accessible En-Suite Bathroom:** Unlike older suite-style dorms, `Room 323` features its own **private, fully ADA-accessible en-suite bathroom** complete with a wheelchair-transfer toilet with fold-down grab bars (giving Elana and Stephen complete privacy and sterile space for her **MACE** routine and **urinary drainage bag** care), a roll-under sink, and a **full zero-threshold roll-in shower** with a padded fold-down bench and dual handheld shower wands.
  * **The Clamped `Split-King` Bed Setup (`2 × Twin XL = 76" × 80" Eastern King`):**
    * While standard dorm rooms provide two separate `Twin XL` (`38" × 80"`) beds, the two cousins prefer sharing a bed so Stephen is right there beside Elana at night to gently massage her muscles whenever they lock up without either of them having to fully wake up.
    * Under an official **OEA (`Ms. Higgins`) + Central Housing (`Dr. Alan Mercer`)** accommodation supported by Elana's PCP at **Piedmont State University Medical Center**, `Room 323`'s two `19"` wheelchair-height `Twin XL` bed frames are **mechanically clamped side-by-side** with a high-density memory-foam **Split-King Bed Bridge** and waterproof King mattress pad over the seam—creating a seamless **`76" × 80"` King-sized bed** with full wheelchair transfer clearance on both outer sides (`Elana transfers from the right; Stephen transfers from the left`).
  * **`Forge Dollars` & Dual Liquid-Nutrition Provision (`Forge Market` + In-Room Mini-Fridge):**
    * **What `Forge Dollars` Are:** **`Forge Dollars`** are CPI's **tax-free declining-balance campus dining account** loaded directly onto a student's **Poly Pass** as part of their university meal plan (the CPI equivalent of *Dining Dollars* / *Flex Points*, exempt from Virginia state sales tax when spent at any on-campus dining hall, café, or campus market).
    * **2nd-Floor `Forge Market` Accommodation:** Because **CPI Dining Services** self-operates **`Forge Market`** on the 2nd floor of New River Hall—combining a quick-service dining counter with open reach-in refrigerated coolers and convenience grocery shelves—CPI Dining and OEA (`Ms. Higgins`) directly add the exact clinical liquid-nutrition shakes and smooth puréed broths Elana requires to `Forge Market`'s weekly distributor order.

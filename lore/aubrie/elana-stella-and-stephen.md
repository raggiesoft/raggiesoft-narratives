## 2. Core Trio Profiles: Elana, Stephen, & Stella

### A. Elana Grace Dawson (`Agent(Elana_Dawson)`)
* **Age & Academic Timeline:**
  * **Age `17` in December 2014** (born `Spring 1997`; high school senior in `2014–2015`).
  * **Incoming CPI Freshman (`Fall 2015`, Class of 2019 — Systems Architecture):** Accepted Early Action alongside **Stephen** to **Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute** so the two of them can study to be **Systems Architects together** (`Computer Science / Systems Architecture & Full-Stack Server Engineering`), where she, **Stephen**, and **Stella** become among the very first incoming freshmen admitted into CPI's brand-new **Fall 2015 Opt-In Gender-Inclusive Housing (`GIH`) Pilot Program** (the program directly inspired by **Jordan Fuller** and **Bennett Hall Suite 214/216**!).
* **Physical, Medical, & Nutritional Profile:**
  * **Neuromuscular Profile & Spasticity (Muscle Lock-Ups):** Born with congenital high-level neuromuscular quadriplegia / severe complex motor impairment affecting all four limbs and trunk stability, accompanied by painful **nocturnal muscle lock-ups (severe hypertonic spasticity / contracture spasms)** at night. Requires full physical assistance with transfers and Activities of Daily Living (`ADLs`).
  * **Strict Liquid Diet:** Due to neurogenic swallowing/motility needs, Elana is on a **100% liquid diet** (custom-blended high-calorie/high-protein clinical nutrition shakes, velvety puréed soups/broths, and electrolyte hydration formulas taken cleanly through a mounted silicone drinking straw on her wheelchair armature).
  * **MACE (`Malone Antegrade Continence Enema`) & Urinary Bag:**
    * Uses a **MACE (`Malone Antegrade Continence Enema`)** continent appendicostomy channel for predictable, dignified daily neurogenic bowel management (taught to the family by Uncle Andy under her PSU Medical Center specialist's care plan).
    * Uses a continuous **indwelling urinary catheter & drainage bag** housed discreetly inside a tailored matte-black neoprene privacy pouch along the lower frame of her power wheelchair.
  * **Vocalization & Speech:** Unlike Jordan Fuller, Bob Scott, and Emily Jenkins, **Elana *can* vocalize and speak aloud**. Her natural speaking voice is clear, expressive, and warm, though because diaphragmatic breath support can fatigue during long study sessions or when she is physically tired, she paces her spoken sentences thoughtfully and seamlessly supplements long-form writing, coding, and rapid dialogue with her **`mPad`**.
  * **Separation Sensitivity & Affectionate Bond with Stephen:** Because of early childhood hospitalizations where she was separated from family in sterile pediatric ICU wards, Elana struggles with acute physiological and emotional anxiety when separated from her cousin and Safe Person, **Stephen Dawson**. They are **always at each other's side** and are **deeply, openly affectionate with each other**—holding hands between their wheelchairs, resting their heads together, and sleeping in the same bed every night so Stephen can soothe her nocturnal muscle lock-ups without either of them having to fully wake up.
* **Heavy-Duty Power Wheelchair & Assistive Technology (`Sip-and-Puff + mPad Bridge`):**
  * **Elana's Heavy-Duty Power Wheelchair (Which Inspires Jordan!):**
    * Elana drives a **heavy-duty, high-torque, active-suspension tilt-in-space power wheelchair** with rugged all-weather casters, matte-charcoal framing, power recline/tilt, and an integrated accessory rail.
    * **Jordan's Reaction (`e002/c006`):** When Jordan visits Charlottesville and sees both Stephen's basic power chair and Elana's heavy-duty power chair, **Jordan is immediately captivated by both chairs and *especially* loves the heavy-duty power wheelchair that Elana uses**—planting the direct seed for the heavy-duty all-terrain power chair Daniel Brooks later gets for Jordan!
  * **Sip-and-Puff (`SnP`) Mobility Control:** Operates her heavy-duty power wheelchair via a **pneumatic Sip-and-Puff (`SnP`) straw interface** mounted on a gooseneck armature beside her right cheek:
    * **Hard Puff:** Drive forward / accelerate
    * **Hard Sip:** Reverse / emergency brake
    * **Soft Puff / Soft Sip:** Proportional left/right steering and power tilt-in-space pressure-relief recline
  * **`mPad` AAC, `Aurora` Terminal, & Full-Stack Dev Integration:** Mounted on a carbon-fiber swing-arm on the left rail of her chair is her **`mPad`** (in-universe iPad), loaded with AAC voice-synthesis software, an SSH terminal connected to her and Stephen's **`Aurora` (in-universe Linux) servers**, and her full-stack IDE. Using a custom ultra-low-latency (`< 8ms`) Bluetooth pneumatic switch bridge engineered by **Stephen**, Elana toggles instantaneously between driving her heavy-duty power chair, speaking via AAC, and writing front-end, back-end, and **`Aurora` server** code.

### B. Stephen Dawson (`Agent(Stephen_Dawson)` — Elana's Cousin, Co-Architect, & Primary Safe Person)
* **Age & Kinship:**
  * **Age `17` in December 2014** (born `Spring 1997`, three weeks older than Elana; son of Greg Dawson's brother **Andrew "Andy" Dawson, BSN, RN**).
  * Raised under the same roof in Charlottesville as Elana and Isabel after his mother passed away when he was a toddler.
* **Physical & Mobility Profile (`Basic Power Wheelchair` & `100% Non-Weight-Bearing`):**
  * **Stephen also uses a wheelchair:** Due to congenital/early-childhood lower-spinal paraplegia, **Stephen cannot put any weight on his legs (`100% non-weight-bearing`)** and uses a **basic, compact joystick-controlled power wheelchair** for all daily mobility.
  * **Strong Upper-Body & Fine-Motor Dexterity:** While his legs are non-weight-bearing, Stephen has full, strong upper-body, arm, and hand dexterity—allowing him to solder microcontrollers, rack server hardware from wheelchair height, manage Elana's liquid-diet nutrition lines and urinary bag drainage, perform upper-body sliding transfers, and massage Elana's muscles when they lock up at night.
  * **Jordan Seeing Both Wheelchairs:** Seeing **Stephen** in his basic joystick power wheelchair and **Elana** in her heavy-duty sip-and-puff power wheelchair rolling side by side through a home built completely around wheelchair life is an unforgettable revelation for Jordan (who especially admires Elana's heavy-duty power chair).
* **The Symbiotic Bond, Affection, & Overnight Muscle-Lock Care:**
  * **Always at Each Other's Side & Very Affectionate:** Stephen and Elana are **never apart**. Their two power wheelchairs move through the house and around town like a synchronized pair, and they are **very affectionate towards each other**—constantly holding hands across the gap between their armrests, leaning in to rest cheek-to-cheek, and offering warm, grounding touch.
  * **Sleeping Together in Bed (Nocturnal Muscle Lock-Up Care):** Every night, **Stephen sleeps in bed right beside Elana**. Whenever Elana's neuromuscular spasticity causes her muscles to painfully **lock up** in the middle of the night, Stephen is already right there against her—so attuned to the slightest tension in her limbs that **he reaches over and gently massages her locked muscles until they release without either of them even having to fully wake up**.
* **Shared Life Goal — Systems Architects & Full-Stack `Aurora` Server Engineers:**
  * Stephen has decided that **taking care of Elana and being a Systems Architect is his life's goal**—and **Elana and Stephen are studying to be Systems Architects *together*** at **CPI (`Class of 2019`)**.
  * **`Aurora` (In-Universe Linux) Server Mastery & Full-Stack Web Development:**
    * Together, **both Elana and Stephen know how to build, harden, and manage `Aurora` (in-universe Linux) servers** from the command line up (`kernel tuning`, `systemd/init daemons`, `nginx/Apache web stacks`, `SSH key infrastructure`, and automated backups).
    * **Full-Stack Website & Server Development:** They run their own two-person web and systems architecture studio from their dual-wheelchair workstation in Charlottesville—doing **100% of the front-end development, back-end development, and bare-metal `Aurora` server development** together. (This creates an instant, electric bond with **Zoë Wren Brooks** and **Jordan Fuller**, who run **`Aura Glade`** [desktop Linux Mint built on the `Aurora` kernel] on Jordan's laptop!).

### C. Stella (`Service_Animal(Stella)` — Dual-Bonded Service Miniature Horse for Both Cousins)
* **Name & Breed:** **`Stella`** — Registered **American Miniature Horse (`AMHA Class A` / Falabella cross)**.
* **Age & Dual-Bond Timeline (as of `December 2014`):**
  * **Age `6 years old`** (foaled in `Spring 2008`).
  * After completing 2.5 years of specialized indoor service-equine foundation and task training, **Stella was paired with Elana and Stephen in `Spring 2011` (when both cousins were `14` and entering 9th grade)**.
  * **Dual-Bonded ADA Service Animal for *Both* Cousins:** Because Elana and Stephen are both non-weight-bearing power wheelchair users who are always side by side, **while Stella is technically registered on paper as Elana's primary service animal, she has bonded equally with both cousins and performs trained ADA tasks for both Elana and Stephen!**
  * Whether Elana drops her stylus/straw or Stephen drops a soldering tool or server patch cable from his basic power chair, Stella retrieves it immediately for whichever cousin needs her, noses wall-mounted ADA door paddles for their two-chair convoy, pulls open interior doors with her tug strap, and alerts if Elana's muscles begin to lock up.
* **Physical Dimensions & Working Gear:**
  * **Height & Weight:** **`28 inches` (`71 cm`) at the withers** and **`85 lbs` (`38.5 kg`)**—squarely within the federal ADA service miniature horse guidelines (`24–34 inches` at the shoulders and `70–100 lbs` under `28 CFR § 35.136(i)` / `§ 36.302(c)(9)`).
  * **Coat & Coloring:** Soft **silver-dapple coat** with a snow-white flaxen mane and tail, expressive dark brown eyes, and a calm, statuesque posture.
  * **Indoor Footwear & Harness:** Wears a custom-fitted burgundy biothane-and-leather service vest equipped with padded tug straps and a rigid stability handle, plus a set of **four custom rubber-soled indoor equine grip boots ("horse sneakers")** whenever working indoors. The rubber boots give her 100% non-slip traction on polished tile, linoleum, and hardwood floors while silencing her hoofsteps.
* **How Having a Service Miniature Horse (`Stella`) Compares to a Service Dog:**
  1. **Lifespan (`30–35+ Years` vs. `8–12 Years` for a Service Dog):**
     * A complex-disability service animal takes 2–3 years to train. A service dog retires at age 8–10 and passes away around 10–12, forcing handlers who already struggle with separation anxiety to endure a devastating loss every decade. Because miniature horses live **30 to 35+ years**, at age 6 Stella has `3.5 years` of seasoned experience and will remain with Elana and Stephen through all four years at CPI (`2015–2019`) and well into their thirties and forties.
  2. **Prey-Animal Focus vs. Predator Distractions:**
     * Dogs are predators whose instincts can be triggered by squirrels on a campus quad, cats, or food scraps dropped on a dining hall floor. As an equine herd/prey animal desensitized from foalhood, Stella has zero prey drive: she ignores squirrels, birds, and dropped food completely, staying calmly locked onto her two "herd mates" (`Elana` and `Stephen`).
  3. **Precision Retrieval, Switch Activation, & Door-Pulling Power for Both Wheelchairs:**
     * With `85 lbs` of low-center-of-gravity four-hoof traction and remarkably prehensile equine lips, Stella picks up dropped items for either Elana or Stephen without tooth-marking them, pulls heavy doors open via tug straps, noses ADA push-plates, and walks smoothly alongside their two power wheelchairs.
  4. **Housebreaking, Hypoallergenic Coat, & Home/Campus Routine:**
     * Stella is **100% housebroken**, sheds far less dander than canine fur (**hypoallergenic** for hospitals and classrooms), and sleeps in a clean bedded alcove right inside Elana and Stephen's ground-floor bedroom suite so she can also alert to nighttime muscle lock-ups.

## 3. The Rest of the Multi-Generational Dawson Household (Charlottesville, VA)

1. **Isabel Claire Dawson (`Age 20 — Older Sister & Cousin, CPI Class of 2016`):**
   * **Role:** 2nd-Floor Female Resident Advisor at Bennett Hall (`Room 215`); Human Development / Pre-Occupational Therapy & Nursing major.
   * **Why She Became an RA:** The RA compensation package (100% free single room + full meal plan + stipend) allows Isabel to put herself through CPI debt-free so her family's resources stay focused on Elana and Stephen's assistive technology and care.
   * **Platonic Boundary with Jordan:** Isabel gives Jordan warm, **two-armed platonic hugs** (as a beloved big-sister figure and guardian angel), while both Isabel and Jordan strictly honor the CPI Residence Life professional boundary (`no romantic dating, no sleeping over, and no acting as an on-floor personal care attendant while she is his active RA`). Growing up with **two power wheelchair users under her own roof (`Elana` and `Stephen`)** plus **Uncle Andy (`BSN, RN`)** is the exact reason Isabel immediately recognized Jordan's needs within ten seconds on Move-In Friday (`e001/c003/s003.md`).

2. **Andrew "Andy" Dawson, BSN, RN (`Age 45 — Paternal Uncle, Full-Time Caregiver, & PSU Med Student`):**
   * **Kinship:** Younger brother of Elana and Isabel's father (`Greg Dawson`), and father of **Stephen Dawson** (widower).
   * **Clinical Background & Role:** A veteran **Registered Nurse (`BSN, RN`)** with 15 years of pediatric ICU and complex neuromuscular rehabilitation experience. Andy serves as Elana's primary full-time caregiver at home (and raised Stephen alongside her), systematically teaching Greg, Becca, Isabel, Stephen, and Gran Thornton the fundamentals of overhead ceiling-lift transfers, liquid-diet nutrition, **MACE** and **urinary bag** hygiene, skin integrity, and muscle-spasm release.
   * **Strict Medical Ethics Boundary:** While Andy is an `RN` and Elana's hands-on caregiver at home, he **never** crosses the professional/ethical boundary of acting as her prescribing or diagnosing medical provider. Elana (and Stephen) have their own independent **Primary Care Physician (`PCP`)** and specialist teams at **Piedmont State University Medical Center (`PSU Medical Center`)** who write all clinical orders.
   * **Current Medical School Path (`RN` $\rightarrow$ `MD`):** Seeing firsthand how badly standard adult healthcare drops the ball when pediatric complex-care patients turn 18 inspired Andy to enroll at the **Piedmont State University School of Medicine (`PSU School of Medicine`)** in Charlottesville to earn his **`MD`**, specializing in **Complex Medical & Adult Disability Medicine (`Physiatry / Complex Care`)**.

3. **Gregory "Greg" Dawson (`Age 48 — Father`) & Rebecca "Becca" Dawson (`née Thornton, Age 46 — Mother`):**
   * **Greg Dawson:** Senior civil and facilities engineer in Charlottesville (works closely with contractors at **Piedmont State University**). Alongside his brother Andy, Greg engineered the Dawson home into a zero-barrier two-power-chair sanctuary—extra-wide 48-inch corridors so Elana and Stephen can roll side by side holding hands, flush concrete-and-flagstone entry ramps, a roll-in wet-room bath with an overhead Hoyer ceiling track lift for both cousins, a custom dual-wheelchair `Aurora` server & workstation bay, and a fenced backyard paddock for Stella.
   * **Rebecca "Becca" Dawson:** Regional nonprofit and public library program director in Charlottesville; warm, organized, and deeply proud of all three kids (`Isabel`, `Elana`, and nephew `Stephen`).

4. **Margaret "Gran" Thornton (`Age 71 — Maternal Grandmother & Homeschool Educator`):**
   * **Background:** Rebecca's mother; a retired Virginia public school teacher with **40 years of classroom experience** (certified in secondary mathematics, sciences, and special-education curriculum design) who lives full-time in the Dawson home.
   * **The Dawson Home Academy:** Because Elana struggles with being separated from Stephen and traditional public schools repeatedly tried to separate them into different classrooms, Gran Thornton took over their education from elementary school through 12th grade—running a rigorous, college-preparatory homeschool academy right at their custom wheelchair-height oak table so Elana and Stephen could learn side by side and graduate ready to become **Systems Architects at CPI (`Fall 2015`)**.

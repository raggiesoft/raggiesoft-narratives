# Rowan & Iris Hawthorne — The Architects of the Crossroads

**Canonical Domain:** `https://crossroads.raggiesoft.com` (**RaggieSoft Story Crossroads**)  
**In-Universe Role:** Brother-and-Sister Signpost Architects, Trail Cartographers, and Medallion Keepers of RaggieSoft Story Crossroads (Sibling Counterparts to the Ocean View Archives' OVA Siblings: Isabel, Eleanor, Sophia, and Oliver)

---

## 1. Who They Are & Why They Built the Lantern Signpost Conservatory

Just as the four **OVA Siblings** (**Isabel, Eleanor, Sophia, and Oliver**) steward the interconnected linear shelves of **Ocean View Archives** in Norfolk, Virginia, brother and sister **Rowan Hawthorne** (age 23) and **Iris Hawthorne** (age 21) are the in-universe **Architects of the Crossroads**—tending **The Lantern Signpost Conservatory**, where every standalone branching novel in **RaggieSoft Story Crossroads** begins.

Growing up, Rowan and Iris loved stories where readers could choose where the characters went next, yet they hated one cruel habit of old-fashioned branching books: punishing curious readers with sudden trick endings on the very next page just for opening the wrong door or trusting their heart. Together, Rowan and Iris drafted **The Three Laws of the Lantern Signpost**:

1. **Every Trail Must Lead to a True Horizon (Coequal Routes):** Neither brother nor sister ever builds a fake trail or a dead-end trap. Whether a reader chooses Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3, every route leaving a Story Crossroads signpost is a complete, lived-in literary story written with full care.
2. **Keep Standalone Worlds Untangled from Linear Shelves:** Rowan and Iris work hand in hand with **Isabel** and **Sophia** over at Ocean View Archives. Multi-decade interconnected series keep their timelines unbroken at Ocean View Archives (with standalone *"What If?"* explorations hosted on RaggieSoft Backstage), while purpose-built branching worlds—starting with ***Cedar Run***—live right here at the Lantern Signpost so reader choices have room to reshape everything.
3. **Leave the Lantern Lit Behind Every Reader:** Nobody should ever feel locked out of the trails they didn't pick first. Iris binds every Story Crossroads edition with persistent route memory *and* an open return path back to every signpost.

## 2. Sibling Profiles & Visual Reference Canon

### Rowan Hawthorne (Age 23 — *The Trail Cartographer & Signpost Builder*)
- **Role at the Crossroads:** Surveys every branching horizon, carves the Warm Brass directional boards on each timber signpost, and walks visitors through how Crossroads choices shape shelters, friendships, and seasons—most notably in our live interactive Signpost Sampler and on `/how-it-works`.
- **Personality:** Thoughtful, steady, patient, and quietly enthusiastic about woodwork, typography, and trail maps; always carries a brass pocket compass and a cedar-handled fountain pen.
- **Visual Baseline (`ai-staging/raw-images/characters/crossroads/`):**
  - Warm dark-brown wavy hair brushed naturally back, expressive amber-hazel eyes, warm welcoming smile.
  - Deep Compass Teal (`#0F4C5C`) waffle-knit Henley with sleeves rolled to mid-forearm under a saddle-tan canvas utility vest, dark olive chinos, and worn brown leather boots.

### Iris Hawthorne (Age 21 — *The Medallion Engraver & Continuity Keeper*)
- **Role at the Crossroads:** Strikes and stamps the circular **2-Way** and **3-Way Story Crossroads Medallions** in Deep Compass Teal and Warm Brass onto every edition's cover, maintains each route's continuity ledger so separate trails never bleed into one another, and curates the Story Crossroads Bookshelf (`/seals` and `/catalog`).
- **Personality:** Warm, observant, articulate, and fiercely protective of character dignity; trades weekly book crates and tea notes with Isabel and Eleanor at Ocean View Archives.
- **Visual Baseline (`ai-staging/raw-images/characters/crossroads/`):**
  - Wavy chestnut-auburn hair pinned half-back, bright hazel eyes, light natural freckles across the bridge of her nose.
  - Cream linen collared blouse under a Deep Compass Teal (`#0F4C5C`) tailored corduroy jacket, warm tan pleated skirt or field trousers, and brown leather ankle boots.

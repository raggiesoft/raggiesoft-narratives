## 1. Core Biomechanical, Character & Visual Continuity Rules (Mandatory for All AI Video Prompts)

1. **1996 Mid-20s Photorealistic Character Styling (Never Middle-Aged):**
   - All visual references and video keyframes for *Escape Velocity: Ad Astra* MUST use the **1996 mid-20s** photorealistic character designs established in `/raggiesoft-assets/stardust-engine/images/story/ad-astra/ad-astra-hero.jpg`, `band-ready.jpg`, and `/raggiesoft-assets/raggiesoft-books/images/scenes/ad-astra/*.jpg` (never the middle-aged 2016 *Live at the Crucible* look).

   - **Cassidy O'Connell (Age 22–25, Lead Vocals & Synthesizers):** Long wavy brown hair, glasses, expressive eyes, retains an unguarded, child-like wonder due to her autism. Has **mild spastic diplegic cerebral palsy** and walks with a **noticeable, asymmetric limp**.
   - **Holly O'Connell (Age 26–27, Mission Operations, Legal/Telemetry & Sound):** Shoulder-length brown hair tied back in a practical ponytail, glasses, carries a ruggedized **MagnaPad** (screen always angled toward Holly's own eyes—never facing the camera).
   - **Tyler & Evan Wright (Age 21–24, Identical Twin Cousins — Drums, Bass, Pilot & Copilot):** Sandy-brown hair, athletic builds. In the cockpit, they **rotate between the Port Seat (Pilot) and Starboard Seat (Copilot)** on every watch shift so both twins log equal stick time.

2. **Ryan's Manual Wheelchair Push-Handles & Cassidy's Cerebral Palsy Walker Dynamic (CRITICAL FOR VIDEO MOTION):**
   - **Ryan is 100% independent in propelling his manual wheelchair:** In every moving shot on solid ground or shipboard decks, Ryan's own hands grip the anodized aluminum wheel push-rims to propel, steer, and brake his chair.
   - **Why Ryan's Wheelchair Has Rear Push-Handles:** Ryan's ultralight titanium manual wheelchair is explicitly equipped with **black rubberized ergonomic rear push-handles** rising from the backrest uprights **specifically for Cassidy**.
   - **Motion Prompt for AI Video Generators:** Whenever Ryan and Cassidy move together across a room, ramp, or shoreline, **Cassidy walks with a noticeable cerebral palsy limp while holding onto Ryan's wheelchair push-handles like a rolling walker to steady her balance**, while **Ryan's hands actively propel the wheel push-rims forward**. Nobody ever pushes Ryan as a passive passenger; his self-propelled wheelchair serves as Cassidy's moving balance anchor.

3. **Shipboard Wheelchair Securement (`4-Point Floor Tie-Downs` + `6-Point Aerospace Harness`):**
   - Whenever the *U.S.S. Aethelgard* is under thrust, maneuvering, or in `0.88G`/`1.00G` studio configuration, Ryan's manual wheelchair is anchored to the diamond-plate titanium deck by **four heavy-duty ratchet tie-down straps** (two front straps angling forward to recessed L-track floor fittings, two rear straps angling aft).
   - Ryan wears a **full 6-point quick-release aerospace flight harness** (twin padded shoulder straps, left/right pelvic lap belts, and dual anti-submarine straps clicking into a **central chest rotary quick-release puck**).
   - During **`0.00G` (Zero-Gravity Sanctuary Mode)** in Movement III, the empty wheelchair remains anchored to the floor by its four-point ratchet tie-downs while Ryan turns the chest puck counter-clockwise, unbuckles the 6-point harness (leaving the unbuckled straps floating upward), and floats weightlessly out of his chair to hug Cassidy, Holly, Tyler, and Evan eye-to-eye in mid-air.

4. **Wardrobe Variety & Family Body/ADL Comfort (Zero Need for Privacy):**
   - Do **not** lock the characters into wearing only their maroon `CPI` shirts in every scene. Rotate naturally between:
     - **Launch & Shipboard Studio (`e001`):** Maroon-and-orange `CPI` ringer t-shirts, blue jeans/khakis, canvas sneakers, and open olive/charcoal flight jackets.
     - **Planetary Exploration (`e002`):** Sleek futuristic deep-space survey outfits—charcoal, slate-blue, and silver technical tunics, field vests with glowing cyan status badges, and utility trousers/shorts (matching `e001-c001-p001-caelum-iv-landing.jpg`).
     - **Shipboard Off-Duty & Swimming:** Casual loungewear or swimming attire.
   - **Ryan's T10 Paraplegia ADLs & Changing Routine:** All four family members (Cassidy, Holly, Tyler, and Evan) are fully trained and comfortable helping Ryan with his **Activities of Daily Living (`ADLs`)** (getting dressed/changed, transfers, and toileting/catheterization routines) since his 1990 hospital discharge. Ryan prefers **Cassidy** to help him with getting changed and toileting routines because he knows **he is her autistic safe person**. When getting changed in the locker bay or stripping down to swim in the purple ocean, the five family members have **zero need for privacy from one another**.

# Lore Dossier: Quantum Long-Range Corporate Jet (2005)

**Aircraft Type:** Ultra-Long-Range Executive Jet (Quantum’s premier internal fleet) **Purpose:** Global, frictionless transport for Building 33 executives and high-level Quantum personnel.

## 1. The "Cassandra Anomaly" Origin

While this specific jet is technically available to any top-tier executive in Building 33, it is an open secret within Quantum's aviation department that the aircraft was procured and customized with exactly one person in mind. Because Cassandra Vance is an anomaly—reaching the executive suite at only twenty-six years old and becoming the indispensable logistical architect for William Keswick—Quantum leadership authorized the massive expense of retrofitting this airframe specifically for her. Keswick engineered the fleet this way to ensure his most vital asset never experiences a single microsecond of travel friction.

## 2. Flight Range & Operational Scale

This aircraft is designed to bypass the need for layovers, allowing executives to cross oceans and continents in a single, uninterrupted flight.

- **Maximum Range:** Approximately 7,500 statute miles (roughly 12,000 kilometers) flying at near-supersonic cruising speeds.
    
- **Domestic Scale:** A cross-country flight from Norfolk to Seattle is roughly 2,400 miles. For this aircraft, that is considered a short, effortless hop that barely burns a third of its fuel capacity.
    
- **International Scale (Direct, Non-Stop Routes from Seattle):**
    
    - **Seattle to Tokyo, Japan:** ~4,800 miles (Easily achieved).
        
    - **Seattle to London, England:** ~4,800 miles (Easily achieved).
        
    - **Seattle to Dubai, UAE:** ~7,400 miles (Pushing the aircraft's maximum range, but entirely possible under the right weather conditions).
        

## 3. Exterior & Boarding Architecture

- **The Ramp:** Unlike standard private jets that utilize steep, fold-down stairs, this specific aircraft was retrofitted with a specialized, mechanical boarding ramp. It deploys smoothly to the tarmac, allowing Cassandra to wheel her manual chair directly into the cabin without requiring a physical lift or assistance.
    
- **Livery:** Pristine white with understated, minimalist Quantum corporate branding.
    

## 4. Cabin Layout (Front to Back)

**Zone 1: The Forward Galley & Accessibility Station**

- **The Galley:** Located immediately upon entering the aircraft. It is a fully stocked, high-end kitchenette capable of providing hot meals, dark roast coffee, and premium catering for global flights.
    
- **The Executive Lockdown Track:** Situated near the front of the cabin. Quantum engineers installed a specialized lockdown track directly into the aircraft's deck. If Cassandra is only taking a short regional flight, or if turbulence is severe, she can wheel her chair over the mechanism and lock securely into place.
    
- **The Primary Transfer Seat:** Adjacent to the lockdown track is a massively oversized, highly adjustable captain's chair. For the vast majority of flights, Cassandra transfers out of her manual chair into this seat to relieve the physical pressure on her body, while her empty wheelchair remains locked safely in the track beside her.
    

**Zone 2: The Mid-Cabin Boardroom**

- **The Conference Table:** The center of the aircraft is dominated by a sprawling, polished mahogany conference table.
    
- **Seating:** Surrounded by heavy, plush leather chairs, this space allows executives to spread out deployment spreadsheets, host mid-air meetings, or eat a meal together in transit.
    
- **Connectivity:** Hardwired into Quantum's secure satellite network, providing enterprise-grade, in-flight Wi-Fi.
    

**Zone 3: The Aft Lounge & Stateroom**

- **Decompression Space:** The rear of the cabin features additional wide, reclining captain’s chairs upholstered in cream leather near the oversized windows.
    
- **The Berthing Divan:** One side of the aft lounge features a long, luxurious leather divan that mechanically folds out into a completely flat, memory-foam bed. On trans-Pacific or trans-Atlantic flights, Cassandra can transfer here to sleep horizontally, fully mitigating the physical toll of her 1999 axial load injury.
    

**Zone 4: The Aft Lavatory**

- **Accessible Facilities:** A spacious, fully wheelchair-accessible lavatory located at the extreme rear of the aircraft, featuring wide pocket doors and structural grab bars designed to blend seamlessly with the high-end polished wood aesthetic.

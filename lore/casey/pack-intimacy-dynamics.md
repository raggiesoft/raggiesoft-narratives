# Lore Dossier: Pack Intimacy Dynamics

**Subject:** Intimacy and Boundaries within the East Coast Pack (The Delaneys and the Ragsdales)
**Status:** Canonical Lore Notes (Non-Explicit/Off-Screen Context)

## Overview
The East Coast pack—consisting of David Vance, his sisters (Cassandra and Casey), Liz, and the extended Delaney network (Virginia Beach and Rhode Island branches)—operates with extremely deep physical and emotional intimacy. This document establishes the exact boundaries and rules of that dynamic for future reference.

## Physical Affection & Cuddling
David is an incredibly affectionate anchor for his pack. He happily hugs, cuddles, and shares non-sexual physical affection with **ALL** of the women in his pack. 
- This includes deep-pressure contact and cuddling with his sisters (Casey and Cassandra).
- This includes hugging and kissing all of the Delaney women (from both the Virginia Beach and Portsmouth, RI branches).
- Liz is fully aware of this, actively encourages it, and participates in the deep physical affection that defines their chosen family.

## Sexual Permissions & Boundaries
- David is explicitly allowed to have sexual relations with the women of the Delaney pack from Portsmouth, RI. 
- Liz is completely aware of this arrangement and even participates in it. 
- As a strict logistical baseline for the entire pack, **ALL** of the women are on birth control. 
- *Note:* While the permission and structure for these relations exist, as of the current timeline, David's only actual sexual partner has been Liz. 

## Safety Guidelines Rule
These dynamics exist as foundational world-building to explain the utter lack of jealousy and the absolute security of the pack. However, per safety guidelines, all explicit scenes of sexual intimacy must remain strictly off-screen. The narrative focus remains entirely on the logistical, emotional, and tactical security these relationships provide.

## Lore Dossier: The Redmond Compound Acquisition

### I. The Tactical Search

- **The Catalyst:** Realizing their Federal Way apartment is at critical mass with four adults, Cassandra Vance initiates a search for a permanent family compound.
    
- **The Quantum Network:** She completely bypasses the public real estate market. Instead, Cassandra logs into the highly exclusive Quantum Building 33 intranet wiki to locate a Tier-One real estate agent who specializes in acquisitions for high-net-worth tech executives.
    
- **The Requirements:** She mandates a single-family home on a flat lot, structurally suitable for commercial-grade ADA retrofitting, and located within a highly specific, transit-dense perimeter near the Overlake Transit Center in Redmond.
    

### II. The Target & The Threat Assessment

- **The Property:** The wiki-vetted agent locates a unicorn property—a sprawling, 3,800-square-foot Pacific Northwest rambler situated right on the edge of the Quantum campus.
    
- **The Listing:** The seller lists the home for **$745,000**.
    
- **The Bidding War:** The property is dangerously underpriced and hits the market during a massive Eastside tech money surge. Before the weekend open house even concludes, Cassandra’s agent intercepts critical intelligence: there are seven competing offers, including two all-cash tech couples and five aggressive conventional mortgages with escalation clauses.
    

### III. The Strategic Escalation

- **The Agent's Play:** The seller’s agent issues a "highest and best" ultimatum. Cassandra’s agent runs the math and predicts the winning bid will need to clear $1.1 million. The agent prepares to draft a highly competitive offer at **$1.15 million** to win by a safe, calculated margin.
    
- **The Executive Veto:** Cassandra reviews the strategy and immediately rejects the incremental approach. She refuses to leave her pack's perimeter to a fraction of a percent of risk.
    

### IV. The Nuclear Strike (The Execution)

Cassandra instructs the agent to completely rewrite the contract and execute a devastating, preemptive financial strike to instantly assassinate the bidding war.

- **The Offer:** She submits a bid for **$1.5 million**—more than double the original asking price.
    
- **Straight Cash:** The transaction involves zero financing or appraisals. It is executed via a single, unencumbered wire transfer from a liquid Quantum holding account.
    
- **As-Is Condition:** Cassandra legally waives all inspection contingencies. Because she has already hired a commercial general contractor to violently gut the interior for wheelchair accessibility, the condition of the drywall, plumbing, or roof is entirely irrelevant to her.
    
- **The Result:** The sheer financial weight of the offer vaporizes the competition. The sellers sign the paperwork within hours, the open house is canceled, and Cassandra successfully secures the sovereign perimeter for her pack without breaking her stride.

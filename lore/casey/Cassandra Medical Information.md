# Character Medical & Lore Archive: Cassandra Vance

**Subject Name:** Cassandra Vance **Title:** CEO, Quantum Corporation
**Primary Medical Attendant:** David Vance, LPN (Licensed Practical Nurse, younger brother)
**Mobility:** Permanent manual power-assist wheelchair user

## Clinical Diagnoses & Physical Profile

**Bilateral Calcaneus and Talus Fractures (Axial Load Injury)** Sustained on Black Friday, 1999. Cassandra slipped and fell from the roof of her house while hanging Christmas lights, landing directly on her feet. The axial load impact catastrophically shattered the internal bone structure of both feet. While her feet appear perfectly fine externally to an uninformed observer, the internal structure is a permanent landscape of microscopic scar tissue and damaged nerves, rendering her completely unable to bear weight.

**Complex Regional Pain Syndrome (CRPS) / Allodynia** A direct result of the 1999 trauma. Her nervous system frequently misinterprets non-painful stimuli—such as the vibration of an idling bus or an unpaved sidewalk rolling up through her wheelchair—as severe pain.

**Dysautonomia & High-Risk Cardiac Arrhythmia** Her autonomic nervous system struggles to regulate itself automatically. Spikes in her neuropathic foot pain or overwhelming sensory input can cause her blood pressure to plummet or her heart rate to spike uncontrollably. Because she is at constant risk of dangerous tachycardic episodes and irregular heart rhythms, she wears a continuous Mobile Cardiac Telemetry (MCT) Holter monitor. This device is permanently patched to her chest and streams her real-time EKG data to David’s LPN tablet 24/7. This constant telemetry monitoring is an absolute requirement. Because David's tablet acts as the primary medical relay for her heart data, it is equipped with a highly secure, built-in GPS uplink to Quantum Corporate. This allows Building 33 to cross-reference her exact physical location with her medical stability at all times.

**AuDHD (Autism + ADHD)** High-functioning but highly demanding. Her executive function requires rigid chemical scaffolding to manage multi-threaded corporate data without sensory scatter. Her AuDHD is closely tied to her physical state; masking in high-stakes neurotypical environments (boardrooms, congressional hearings) drains her physical battery exponentially.

**Sensory Processing Disorder (SPD)** Hyper-acute auditory and visual processing. Flickering fluorescent lights, overlapping chatter, and unpredictable environmental noise trigger rapid sensory overload. Her absolute preference for municipal transit (trains, ferries, and buses over luxury rideshares) requires careful sensory management of these public environments.

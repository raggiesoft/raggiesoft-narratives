### Physical Profile (2014 Era)

Weighing in at a hefty 15 grams, the Obsidian Card is cold to the touch and makes a distinct, authoritative _clack_ when dropped on a marble counter or a silver presentation tray. Unlike the standard magnetic-stripe cards still widely used in the US in 2014, Cassandra’s Obsidian is equipped with an early-adoption EMV "chip and PIN." This ensures flawless, frictionless functionality whether she’s at a high-end boutique in Seattle or a private chalet in the Swiss Alps. The front is minimalist: no account numbers, just a subtle Meridian crest and _Cassandra Vance_ elegantly laser-etched into the metal.

### Financial Mechanics: The "Shadow Limit"

The card operates on a **No Pre-Set Spending Limit (NPSL)**. It is essentially a charge card that must be paid in full at the end of the month. Meridian’s algorithms dynamically adjust her shadow limit based on her massive personal wealth profile.

- **Frictionless Spends:** For standard ultra-wealthy purchases—commercial first-class buyouts, bespoke luxury goods, or five-star hotel suites—the card swipes without a second thought.
    
- **Pre-Cleared Spends:** For abrupt, seven-figure anomalous expenditures—like quietly buying out a private medical clinic in cash, or chartering an emergency heavy-lift helicopter—her private wealth manager at Meridian simply pre-clears the charge via a 60-second phone call.
    

### The Separation of Church and State (Corporate vs. Pack)

As the CEO of a publicly traded tech titan like Quantum, Cassandra’s professional life is scrutinized by the SEC, a board of directors, and armies of auditors. To protect the existence and operations of her pack, her finances are strictly compartmentalized:

- **The P-Card (The Decoy):** For trackable business expenses—corporate jets to tech summits, executive dinners, and scheduled PR travel—she uses a standard Quantum corporate purchasing card. It is transparent and perfectly aligned with her "tech CEO" public persona.
    
- **The Obsidian Card (The Shadow Wallet):** The Obsidian is tied exclusively to her private, post-tax personal trusts. It is entirely off-the-books to Quantum's accountants. When a pack member requires emergency off-grid medical care, when she needs to buy massive tracts of remote land for a safe haven, or when a massive payoff is required to keep a territorial dispute out of the police blotter, it goes on the Obsidian.
    

### The Meridian Global Desk

The true power of the card is its concierge. In 2014, ultra-luxury is not handled by a smartphone app; it is handled by highly discreet human professionals. By dialing an unlisted international number, Cassandra (or her personal pack-liaison) reaches the Global Desk. Answered on the first ring, this team acts as a high-end fixer agency. They do not ask _why_ Ms. Vance needs a trauma surgeon flown to a remote cabin at 3:00 AM, or _why_ she needs a fleet of black SUVs with reinforced suspensions—they simply arrange it, bury the paper trail under holding companies, and bill the Obsidian.

- **The Card:** The **Meridian Obsidian Card** (or simply, "The Obsidian Card")
    
- **The Issuer:** Meridian Heritage Bank
    
- **The Concierge:** The **Meridian Global Desk**

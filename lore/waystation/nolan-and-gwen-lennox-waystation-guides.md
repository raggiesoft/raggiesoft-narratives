## 1. Early-2000s Chesapeake & Norfolk Backstory: `1999–2002`

Five years apart in age and done growing—**Nolan Lennox** at **twenty-four** (`5'7"`) and his tall younger sister **Gwen Lennox**, who **just turned nineteen** (`6'1"`) when the portal opens in October 2002—the two siblings share an unbreakable, mutually protective bond forged through loss, survival, and fierce sibling loyalty.

### Childhood, Tammy's Trailer (`1999–2001`), Breakups #1–#3, & Escaping at the Norfolk Pathfinder Terminal
Before paternal grandfather **Walter Lennox** (a U.S. Navy veteran formerly stationed at **`NAVSTA Norfolk`**) gained legal custody of both children in the mid-1980s, young Nolan and Gwen lived with their parents in a **one-story house**—so coming over to Grandpa Walter's spacious, upper-middle-class **two-story single-family home (`~2,888 sq ft` heated + attached `2-car garage`)** within **walking distance of both Emerald Creek Mall (*Greenbrier Mall*) and the Emerald Creek Blue Tech (*real-life Best Buy Store #421 on Greenbrier Pkwy*)** originally meant **"visiting Grandpa Walter's house."** Because Walter had bought that two-story home long before taking custody (when he had plenty of money prior to his January 2001 medical crisis, and no idea he would ever be raising two grandchildren with leg disabilities), the house was **completely un-adapted**: three wooden front porch steps, standard doorways, a downstairs **full bathroom (standard shower, toilet, and sink)**, and a full flight of stairs to the second floor (where Walter's master bedroom had its own **en-suite master bathroom**, while Nolan and Gwen each had **full-sized beds** in their upstairs bedrooms and **took turns with the upstairs hallway bathroom**). Whenever Nolan and Gwen spent the night as little kids visiting Grandpa's—and later, even after Walter gained custody, on any evening their legs weren't feeling up to climbing the stairs—they unfolded the **queen-sized sofa bed** in Grandpa's downstairs family room and slept on the sofa bed together (**the exact childhood reason Nolan and Gwen never mind sharing one full-sized bed together as adults in `Studio 205`**).

- **Autumn & Y2K `1999` (`p001.md`–`p006.md`):** At age twenty-one (**Saturday, October 23, 1999** — four days after Gwen turned sixteen on October 19), verbal autistic Nolan met twenty-four-year-old **Tammy** at Emerald Creek *Blue Tech*, missed *"Just so you know, I'm ready for everything"* at the Emerald Creek Mall food court (`p001.md`), cuddled at her trailer where `5'0"` Tammy mistook `6'1"` sixteen-year-old Gwen for Nolan's older sister (`p002.md`), spent his first night away from family on **October 30, 1999** (`p003.md`), checked Gwen's straight-A freshman homework the next day (`p004.md`), co-signed onto Tammy's *Pinecrest Court* lease on **November 20, 1999** alongside Mr. Pruitt's sketchy cinder-block plywood ramp (`p005.md`), and called home on **December 31, 1999** (`11:35 PM`, `p006.md`) to make sure Gwen backed up her files onto a `3.5"` floppy and shut down/unplugged her `Quantum OS 98` laptop before the Y2K rollover.
- **Breakup #1 (`Sep 20–23, 2000`, `p007.md` — 4 Days, Before Mallory Arrived):** Tammy explodes when literal-minded Nolan takes *"I'm fine"* at face value; Nolan blames himself and sleeps four nights back upstairs at Grandpa Walter's before Tammy reels him back in when he stops by for clean work polos.
- **Grandpa Walter's CHF Crisis (`Jan 2001`), Breakup #2 (`May 2001`, `p008.md` — ~3 Weeks), & Enter *Mallory Walsh*:** Two months after 78-year-old Walter survives winter pneumonia + congestive heart failure (`requiring a six-figure home-equity medical loan`), 23-year-old autistic `Quantum OS` expert **Mallory Walsh** is promoted in **March 2001** from ***Blue Tech Store #420* on Independence Boulevard in Virginia Beach** to **Computers & Software Shift Supervisor** at Emerald Creek ***Store #421***. During Nolan's ~3-week second breakup in May 2001 (rolling into shift in his wheelchair with his crutches in the back pocket on a bad-leg day), Mallory gives Nolan a safe friend in the breakroom (*"The bug isn't in your hardware"*) before Tammy panics ahead of June 1st lot rent and pulls Nolan back one final time.
- **Breakup #3 at the Norfolk Pathfinder Terminal (`Tue, Aug 21, 2001`, `p009.md`):** Tammy drags 23-year-old Nolan on a day-of whim to buy walk-up `$99` tickets to Seattle without calling Pathfinder 48 hours ahead for a wheelchair lift. Nolan breaks up with her before the counter, rejects her cardigan-shielded enticement in favor of yell-free peace with Gwen, survives Tammy's slap when fellow passengers step in, rides three `CVTA` buses (`Route 2` → `Route 20` → `Route 15`, stopping short of the curbless grassy island at Emerald Creek Mall) home to Grandpa Walter and Gwen, files his 90-day written lease non-renewal notice (`clearing midnight Nov 30, 2001`), and tells **Mallory Walsh** first among non-family the next day—where Mallory holds a firm **Shift Supervisor ethical boundary** against dating anyone on her shift while remaining his closest store friend.

### `September 11, 2001` (`p010.md` & `p011.md` — ZERO IMAGES), Gwen's 18th Birthday (`Oct 19`), Grandpa Walter's Passing (`Oct 20`), & Moving to Norfolk (`Dec 1, 2001`)
- **September 11, 2001 (`p010.md` & `p011.md` — IMMUTABLE ZERO-IMAGES RULE):** Nolan works an opening shift rewiring the *Blue Tech* TV rack with Ray Halstead before `9:03 AM` and is dismissed shaken when the Hampton Roads rumor mill claims a plane is heading for `NAVSTA Norfolk` (where Grandpa Walter served). Walter pulls 17-year-old Junior Gwen out of **Emerald Creek High School** (*2nd-block Latin III → German classroom watching `CSN`*), checks *Blue Tech*, picks Nolan up in his wheelchair at **`Eden Way North & Merchants Way`** outside **`Emerald Creek Station`** (U.S. Coast Guard Recruiting Station), and holds the family together at home.
- **Gwen's 18th Birthday (`Oct 19, 2001`) & Walter's Passing (`Oct 20, 2001`, `p012.md`):** Because every suburban apartment near Emerald Creek requires `3×` monthly rent (`$750+`), the one apartment in Hampton Roads Nolan's retail paystubs qualify for (`$395/month` utilities included) is **`Studio 205`** inside **`Harborview Towers`** (`[INTERNAL: 330 W. Brambleton Ave, Unit 205, Norfolk — Hague Towers]`), a twenty-story `1965` high-rise overlooking The Hague off West Brambleton Avenue on `CVTA Routes 2 and 11`, six blocks from **`Ghent High School`**. Over-the-moon excited both to live with Nolan *and* to leave the Chesapeake school district that held her back in elementary school, Gwen says yes wholeheartedly on her 18th birthday; Walter passes away peacefully the next morning, passing his lifelong role as Nolan's neurotypical-world translator down to Gwen.
- **Moving Into `Studio 205` (`Dec 1, 2001`, `p013.md`):** Once Tammy's 90-day holdout expires Nov 30, Nolan and 18-year-old Gwen unlock `11' × 18'` **`Studio 205`** on the second floor of `Harborview Towers` (`riding the groaning 1965 elevator right before yellow out-of-order tape leaves them with 22 stairs`), upgrade Gwen's laptop to **`Quantum OS 2000 Professional`**, and enroll Gwen six blocks away at **`Ghent High School`** to finish eleventh grade.

### `Early 2002` — Brutal 3-Bus Commute, Landing the `CCHR` Norfolk Campus Tech Job, Friday Ride Home, & Mallory Coming Back Saturday (`p014.md`–`p016.md`)
- **Leaving *Blue Tech* for `CCHR` Norfolk Campus & Clocking Out Together (`Feb 12 → Fri, Mar 1, 2002`, `p014.md`):** Commuting from `Harborview Towers` in Norfolk back to Emerald Creek *Blue Tech* during the winter payroll drought takes **three buses (`Route 2 or 11` ↔ `Route 20` ↔ `Route 15`) plus a half-mile push across six-lane `Emerald Creek Parkway` and `Mall Ring Road` to the `Emerald Creek Mall` bus stop (across from the commuter Park & Ride)** just to work four-hour shifts. Gwen spots a posting at the **Community College of Hampton Roads (`CCHR`) Norfolk Campus** (Martin Building downtown) for a **Part-Time Campus Computer Lab & Helpdesk Technician** (`28 hrs/wk`, seated workbench imaging `Quantum OS 2000 Professional` workstations—one **6-minute ride on `CVTA Route 2` or `Route 11`** with zero transfers!). Nolan aces the practical interview and works a clean two-week notice at *Blue Tech*. On Nolan's final Friday shift (**`Friday, March 1, 2002`, `2:30 PM`**), Nolan—who always loved talking to Mallory in the breakroom when both were off the clock—and Mallory happen to clock out at the exact same minute. Now that both are off the clock and Nolan is permanently off *Blue Tech*'s payroll, **Mallory offers to drive Nolan straight home to Norfolk**, folding his wheelchair (with his forearm crutches in the back pocket) into her trunk.
- **Gwen Investigates From the Lobby, Crimson-Red Nolan, & Surprising Both Siblings (`Fri, Mar 1, 2002`, `p014.md`):** At `Harborview Towers` (`~3:08 PM`), 18-year-old Gwen—whose **Ghent High School** bus has *just* dropped her off—is waiting in the lobby for the slow 1965 elevator when she sees Nolan arriving **by car** and an unfamiliar woman helping him into his wheelchair at the curb. Going outside to investigate, Gwen meets Mallory while Nolan blushes **crimson red**, delightedly connects *"Protected-memory Mallory!"*, and **Gwen invites Mallory upstairs to `Studio 205`**. Introduced upfront to their single corner full-sized bed, Mallory immediately realizes *siblings had to share the one bed, otherwise Gwen would be out on the street*—and is **completely fine with it**, genuinely surprising **both** Nolan and Gwen!
- **The Mallory Visits Scene — Coming BACK the Very Next Day (`Sat, Mar 2, 2002`, `p015.md`, Illustrated):** Proving Friday wasn't polite lip service, Mallory drives **right back** across the river on rainy Saturday afternoon (`climbing the 22 stairs after the 1965 elevator stalls again`), sits warmly beside Nolan on the shared plaid corner bed over hot cocoa and Gwen's twin-moon orbital simulation (`e001_c001_p015_mallory_visits_studio_205.jpg`), and out on the second-floor landing plainly admits she has liked Nolan for months now that zero Shift Supervisor conflict remains!

## 1. How Two Barefoot Crutch-and-Wheelchair Users Landed in a 1980s Fantasy World

Ten minutes before arriving at **The Uncharted Waystation**, twenty-year-old **Nolan Lennox** (`5'7"`) and his nineteen-year-old tall younger sister **Gwen Lennox** (`6'1"`) were having a quiet Friday evening at home. Both siblings are ambulatory wheelchair and forearm-crutch users: their two wheelchairs were parked side by side across the living room rug near the front hall, their shoes were kicked off by the door, and their two pairs of ergonomic aluminum **forearm crutches** were propped right against the end of the sofa within arm's reach while Nolan and Gwen sat barefoot in worn blue jeans and plain cotton t-shirts, cuddled up together under a plaid throw blanket.

Then the air in front of the coffee table shimmered like amber stained glass. A dimensional eddy swallowed the sofa cushion—pulling Nolan, Gwen, the plaid throw blanket, and **both pairs of forearm crutches** leaning against the armrest straight into an ancient stone courtyard ripped out of a 1980s fantasy epic, **while leaving both of their wheelchairs behind on the living room rug**.

Fortunately, the sun-warmed quartz flagstones of **The Uncharted Waystation** are smooth and level, and a wide mossy carved stone bench sits right beside the central bronze Astrolabe! Using their forearm crutches for short barefoot walks across the courtyard and resting side by side on the stone bench whenever their legs tire, Nolan and Gwen decoded the thirteen humming runestone archways—welcoming anyone who tumbles onto an unmapped `*.raggiesoft.com` address (`HTTP 404`) and pointing every traveler straight toward the right world in the **RaggieSoft Universe**.

## 1. Early-2000s Chesapeake & Norfolk Backstory: Escaping `2710 Ike Street`, Gwen's 18th Birthday, Grandpa Walter's Passing, and Studio 2B

Five years apart in age and done growing—**Nolan Lennox** at **twenty-four** (`5'7"`) and his tall younger sister **Gwen Lennox**, who **just turned nineteen** (`6'1"`)—the two siblings share an unbreakable, mutually protective bond forged through loss, survival, and fierce sibling loyalty.

### Escaping Tammy's Trailer (`2710 Ike Street`) at the Norfolk Pathfinder Terminal
With their parents absent from their lives (reserved for future exploration), Nolan and Gwen were raised by their beloved paternal grandfather, **Walter Lennox**, at his modest brick-and-siding bungalow near **Emerald Creek Mall** (*Greenbrier Mall*) in Chesapeake, Virginia. Working hourly shifts as a sales associate wandering the aisles on his matte-silver Lofstrand forearm crutches and running front cash registers at the **Emerald Creek Blue Tech** electronics superstore (*Best Buy*), twenty-three-year-old Nolan was manipulated into moving out of Grandpa Walter's house to live with his controlling, emotionally and psychologically abusive girlfriend **Tammy** inside her rundown, smoke-stained single-wide trailer at **`2710 Ike Street`**—located in a trashy trailer park along **CVTA Route 13** (*real-life HRT Route 13*, walking distance to where **CVTA Routes 6 and 13** meet up at **Robert Hall Boulevard**).

**Two days before Gwen's eighteenth birthday**—after weeks of petty, exhausting fights had already left Nolan ready to break up with her—Tammy woke up and impulsively decided **that very morning (the day of)** to drag Nolan to the Norfolk **Pathfinder** (*Greyhound*) station to buy two walk-up `$99` one-way cross-country tickets to Seattle, without giving a single thought to Nolan needing wheelchair accommodations:
1. **Nolan's Internal Escape Plan & The 48-Hour Pathfinder Wheelchair-Lift Rule:** Sitting in his manual wheelchair with his matte-silver Lofstrand forearm crutches stowed securely on the back of his backrest, Nolan kept his refusal completely to himself inside the isolated trailer, deciding this public trip was his chance to escape safely. Moreover, Nolan knew perfectly well that **Pathfinder requires calling at least 48 hours in advance** to request a wheelchair-lift-equipped intercity coach—meaning Tammy had never called (and Nolan deliberately didn't call either, because he already knew he wasn't going on the trip).
2. **Riding Early-2000s High-Floor `CVTA` Buses (`Elevate-U` Lift & `10-16` Log) & Pre-Counter Breakup:** Boarding high-floor **`CVTA Route 13`** via its front-stairwell **`Elevate-U`** hydraulic wheelchair lift (`whichever of Route 6 or 13 arrived first` near Robert Hall Boulevard) into **Downtown Norfolk** and transferring to **`CVTA Route 11`** (`whichever of Route 2 or 11 arrived first`) up to the **Norfolk Pathfinder Terminal**, Nolan locked his wheelchair brakes in the middle of the crowded waiting area **before Tammy could even reach the walk-up ticket counter** to buy the two one-way tickets—telling her he wasn't going and they were done. Furious, **Tammy violently smacked Nolan across the face** right in his wheelchair. Nolan broke down sobbing heavily in the middle of the public waiting room, **fellow passengers immediately stepped in and pushed Tammy away** until security escorted her out, and through heavy sobs Nolan made it clear **he just wanted to go home to the Emerald Creek area of Chesapeake to his kid sister and dying grandfather**.
3. **Three-Bus `CVTA` Return & Stopping Short of the Inaccessible Grassy Island at Emerald Creek Mall:** Guided safely out the side doors by a retired chief, Nolan rode **`CVTA Route 2`** (`whichever of Route 2 or 11 arrived first`) back to **Downtown Norfolk**, transferred to eastbound **`CVTA Route 20`** out Virginia Beach Boulevard to **Glenrock Mall** (with the `Route 20` operator calling ahead on the radio to hold outbound **`Route 15`** for a `10-16` bound for Emerald Creek Mall), and rode **`CVTA Route 15`** south down 8-lane Military Highway to **Emerald Creek Mall**. Because the official **Emerald Creek Mall** bus stop on **Mall Ring Road** is a metal sign planted on a narrow, six-inch-curbed grassy parking-lot island with **zero sidewalk and zero ADA curb cuts**, Nolan alerted the `Route 15` operator on approach so the driver **stopped short of the grassy island** and lowered the `Elevate-U` lift directly onto the flat asphalt of the parking lot roadway—just blocks from Grandpa Walter's house!
4. **Getting Affairs in Order & Leasing Studio 2B:** Seventeen-year-old Gwen was **overjoyed** that Nolan finally dumped Tammy! Dying Grandpa Walter—holding on by sheer willpower so Gwen would turn eighteen legally free of county child-welfare custody—sat Nolan down with his manila accordion folder to get his final affairs in order (executor will, veterans burial policy, and the reverse/medical home-equity loan that would require selling the house), while Nolan retrieved his belongings from `2710 Ike Street` with a sheriff's civil standby and leased **Studio 2B** four blocks from Emerald Creek Mall and Blue Tech.

### Gwen's Eighteenth Birthday, Grandpa Walter's Passing the Day After, & Executor Weight
- **Gwen's 18th Birthday (`Over the Moon Excited`):** Grandpa Walter made it to Gwen's eighteenth birthday cake at the kitchen table—where Nolan set a fresh brass key beside Gwen's plate and **invited newly eighteen-year-old Gwen to come live with him in Studio 2B**. Thrilled beyond words that Nolan had dumped his abuser *and* that she now got to live with her big brother, Gwen threw her arms around Nolan while Grandpa Walter smiled in complete peace.
- **The Day After Gwen's Birthday (`What Happens When Someone Passes Away`):** Having seen Gwen safely to eighteen and united with Nolan, **Grandpa Walter passed away peacefully in his sleep the very next morning**. Twenty-three-year-old Nolan—with only three paid bereavement shifts from Blue Tech—had to shoulder the overwhelming real-world weight of a death in the family: hospice pronouncement and funeral chapel removal, holding firm against expensive funeral upsells to honor Grandpa's modest burial policy, ordering ten raised-seal Virginia death certificates, qualifying as executor at the Chesapeake Circuit Court probate window, absorbing the Treasury's automatic clawback of Grandpa's final Social Security deposit, returning leased oxygen equipment, shutting off utilities, running an estate tag sale, and **listing Grandpa's bungalow for sale** to pay off the ticking ninety-day home-equity medical lien—steadied every step of the way by eighteen-year-old Gwen, who vowed never to leave Nolan's side or let anyone pull him back into the darkness of `2710 Ike Street` ever again.

### Exact Layout of Studio 2B in Emerald Creek (The One-Person Rectangular Box)
- **Nolan's Full-Sized Bed (`54" × 75"`, Tucked Flush Into the Corner Walls):** Pushed tight against two perpendicular corner walls beneath the small window A/C unit—serving as both siblings' shared bed *and* daytime couch under Grandpa Walter's dark-green and navy-blue plaid wool blanket, with all four Lofstrand forearm crutches leaning cleanly beside the footboard.
- **Nolan's Dresser & Early-2000s Media Stack (Swiveled Toward the Corner Bed & Wheelchairs):** Topped with Nolan's **CRT television, V-shaped metal rabbit ears, and brushed-silver dual-deck VCR + DVD combo unit** turned cleanly toward the corner bed so Nolan and Gwen could watch TV together from bed or their wheelchairs.
- **The Unobstructed Tiny Kitchen & Microwave Pantry Cart:** Clear roll-up floor access to the **dorm-style mini-fridge tucked under the counter**, narrow single sink, **compact natural-gas oven/range**, and Nolan's **microwave sitting on a rolling wire cart whose lower shelves doubled as their entire pantry**.
- **Two Early-2000s Laptops (`Quantum OS` Upgrade):** Nolan upgraded Gwen's crashing **`Quantum OS 98` (`v4.10.1998`)** laptop cleanly to **`Quantum OS 2000 Professional`** alongside his own charcoal laptop during moving week, right after Gwen proudly signed onto the Studio 2B lease.
- **Unselfconscious Healing & Summer Heat:** After six-foot-one Gwen nearly caught a crutch tip in the cramped three-foot bathroom on moving night, both adult siblings agreed to get changed in the studio room—Gwen's calm, matter-of-fact warmth melting away the body-shame Tammy had planted in Nolan until, during an eighty-nine-degree August 2001 Chesapeake heatwave when their window A/C barely worked, both adult siblings spent sweltering afternoons in bare skin on the shared full-sized bed beside a box fan, completely safe in their tiny lifeboat.

## 2. Why Vaeloria Needed Them (*The Great Drift*), Initial Fear vs. Wonder, & Reciprocal Paused Time

Six days past Gwen's nineteenth birthday in **Autumn 2001** (Nolan now twenty-four), Vaeloria's sleeping **Twin-Ring Bronze Astrolabe** answered Gwen's spoken wish via **Two-Heart Resonance**—pairing Gwen's sleepless-night binary-moon orbital math with Nolan's protective ground-reading eyes, and pulling barefoot Nolan, Gwen, Grandpa Walter's plaid blanket, and all four Lofstrand forearm crutches out to **Star-Moss Hollow**.

- **During the Quest (Zero Knowledge That Earth Time Stands Still):** Dropped barefoot ten feet short of both manual wheelchairs, twenty-four-year-old Nolan initially feels genuine big-brother fear over losing their chairs and missing his Saturday shift at **Blue Tech** (having zero idea Earth is paused!), before nineteen-year-old Gwen's wonder at the two real moons and Vaeloria's gentle, step-free roads turns fear into the adventure of their lives.
- **First Return to Studio 2B (`9:15 PM` on the VCR Clock) & Two-Way Reciprocal Pause:** Once Nolan and Gwen finish their Crossroads quest, turn the Twin-Ring Bronze Astrolabe together to heal **The Great Drift**, and claim **Keepers' Hall & The Glowing Hearth-Archway**, two-way travel opens between worlds anytime they wish. Stepping back into Studio 2B for the first time, they discover the green LED clock on Nolan's VCR/DVD combo unit **still reads `9:15 PM`**—not a single second passed on Earth! Gathering **Grandpa Walter's silver-framed photograph**, **both `Quantum OS 2000 Professional` laptops**, and **both manual wheelchairs** (whose bent front caster fork a Waystation smith trues in four effortless turns!), they roll back into Keepers' Hall to learn from **Archivist Thorne** that **time always stands still in the other world**: Earth pauses at `9:15 PM` while they live in Vaeloria, and Vaeloria pauses whenever they visit Earth. Choosing **Keepers' Hall** wholeheartedly as their true home, they know Earth will always wait safely whenever they wish to visit.

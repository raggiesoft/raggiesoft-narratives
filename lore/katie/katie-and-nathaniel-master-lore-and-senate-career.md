## 1. Core Twin Identity, Mobility Baseline, & Family Canon

- **Fraternal Twins Katie & Nathaniel ("Nate") Prairie**:
  - **Date of Birth**: **April 8, 1985** (Virginia Beach, VA).
  - **Neurotype & Sibling Sanctuary**: Both Katie and Nate are autistic and share an unbreakable lifelong twin sanctuary (*"Twin Brothers and Twin Sisters never leave each other behind"*).
  - **POV Standard**: Every chapter of `books/katie` is written in **First-Person Past-Tense Novel POV (`"I"`, `pov: "Nathaniel"`)**.
- **Full-Time Power Wheelchairs (`September 2001+`, Age 16)**:
  - Born with progressive pediatric neuromuscular weakness in their lower legs, Nate and Katie walked with increasing fatigue through age 15 and early age 16 until their legs gave out on the third Friday of September 2001 (Junior year at Northwood High School).
  - From September 2001 onward, both Nate and Katie use **compact `SwiftChair` power wheelchairs** full-time from the moment they wake up until they transfer into bed at night.
  - Neither Nate nor Katie drives a motor vehicle; **Coastal Virginia Transit Authority (`CVTA`)** buses are their everyday lifeline across Hampton Roads.
- **Lifelong Sobriety Vow & Lethal Medication Contraindication (`Alcohol & Tobacco = Known Side Effect of Death`)**:
  - After witnessing their mother's alcoholism on the cruise ship *Sunward Tropics* (August 2001) and hiding behind a locked bedroom door eating dry toasted oat cereal out of a cardboard box and drinking ginger ale during their cousin **Brittany's** unauthorized New Year's Eve rave (`books/katie/e004/c001/p001.md`), both Nate and Katie vow at midnight on January 1, 2003 never to drink alcohol—not even a twenty-first birthday drink.
  - **Medical Contraindication (`Katie`, `Nate`, & `David`)**: In addition to their personal sobriety vow, **Katie, Nate, and their father David all take a daily prescription medication where mixing with alcohol or tobacco has a known side effect of death**. In Washington, D.C. and Richmond, this gives Senator Katie Prairie, Nate, and David an ironclad, indisputable medical reason never to drink wine, champagne, or cocktails—or touch cigars/tobacco—with other Senators, lobbyists, or donors, always toasting strictly with ginger ale, soda, or coffee.

- **Family & Cousin Canon**:
  - **David Prairie (Dad)**: Hardworking single father (100% ambulatory—David walks on his own two feet and does NOT use a wheelchair) who rents the single-story three-bedroom brick ranch with an aluminum front-porch wheelchair ramp just off **Military Highway (`US-13`) & Indian River Road** following the August 2001 separation/divorce from their alcoholic mother.
  - **Aunt Elizabeth ("Aunt Liz") & Cousin Chelsea (`Paternal Cousin — Protected Adult Storyline`)**:
    - **Chelsea** is Dad's sister Aunt Liz's daughter (born ~1982, **three years older** than Nate and Katie).
    - **Chelsea is developmentally disabled** (`books/katie/e001/c003/p001.md`).
    - **CRITICAL CANON RULE**: Chelsea is **NEVER** the cousin who throws the drunken New Year's Eve party in `e004/c001/p001.md` (on New Year's Eve 2002, Chelsea travels across the Hampton Roads Bridge-Tunnel to Newport News with Dad and Aunt Liz to visit Grandma). In adulthood, **Chelsea has a major dedicated story arc when Nate is an adult dealing with an abusive girlfriend**.
  - **Cousin Brittany (`The December 31, 2002 New Year's Eve Party Cousin`)**:
    - Seventeen-year-old female cousin who stays over during the second half of winter break in December 2002, defies Dad and Aunt Liz's ground rules by inviting five teenagers and twenty-one-year-old Derek (who buys gas-station alcohol on Military Highway) into the living room, and whose guest **Tyler** spreads the spiteful rumor at Northwood High School that causes **Heather Collins** to break up with a blindsided Nate on Monday, January 6, 2003 (`books/katie/e004/c001/p001–p002.md`).

## 2. The 2008 Political Catalyst: *Ideas for Coastal Virginia Transit* & The Four-Bus Mayoral Loop

Katie Prairie's political career is sparked directly by a real-life 2008 Virginia Beach transit event filmed by Michael P. Ragsdale (who ran the *Ideas for Hampton Roads Transit* blog in 2008, invited all four Virginia Beach mayoral candidates to ride the bus with him, and served as the camera operator when only candidate Scott Taylor accepted the challenge).

### A. The Blog & The Four-Candidate Invitation (`2008`, Age 23)
- After graduating from college (`May 2007`), 23-year-old software developers and daily **CVTA (*Coastal Virginia Transit Authority*)** riders Nate and Katie run the local transit advocacy blog ***Ideas for Coastal Virginia Transit***.
- During the **2008 Virginia Beach Mayoral & City Council Campaign**, Nate and Katie issue an open invitation to all four mayoral candidates—and the City Council candidates—to meet them at the **CCHR** (*Community College of Hampton Roads*, Virginia Beach Campus — real-life *TCC Virginia Beach*) transit hub and ride a four-bus loop across the city with them.
- **Only one mayoral candidate**—a young, political-outsider Navy veteran—takes them up on the offer. Every single incumbent and establishment City Council candidate ignores the invitation.

### B. The Exact 4-Bus Circuit (`#29` $\rightarrow$ `#20` $\rightarrow$ `#15` $\rightarrow$ `#12`) & The Expired 2-Hour Transfer
1. **Start at CCHR (*TCC Virginia Beach*) $\rightarrow$ Board CVTA Route `#29` *(later renumbered `#26`)* to Hilltop**:
   - The candidate drives to CCHR, meets Nate and Katie at the bus loop, and boards **Route #29** with them up to **Hilltop**.
2. **Transfer at Hilltop $\rightarrow$ Board CVTA Route `#20` Westbound to Glenrock Mall (*Military Circle*)**:
   - Riding **Route #20** westbound along Virginia Beach Boulevard, they encounter a Spanish-speaking citizen on board the bus, and the candidate speaks Spanish right back to the rider.
3. **Transfer at Glenrock Mall $\rightarrow$ Board CVTA Route `#15` Southbound on US-13 (`Military Highway`)**:
   - With Nate operating the camera on board **Route #15** right as the bus crosses the **Eastern Branch of the Elizabeth River** from Norfolk back into Virginia Beach via **US-13 southbound**, they film the centerpiece segment exposing how Virginia Beach's early-evening bus shutdown locks transit-dependent citizens out of local democracy.
4. **Transfer from Route `#15` $\rightarrow$ Board CVTA Route `#12` Back to CCHR (Paying a Second Fare)**:
   - Upon boarding **Route #12** to return to the **CCHR** hub, Nate and Katie swipe their **30-day CVTA passes** as daily regulars, while the candidate's **2-hour transfer time limit has already expired**—forcing him to pay a second full fare on camera just to complete a single round-trip loop back to his parked car at CCHR.

### C. The Virginia Beach Municipal Center Bus Curfew Catch-22 (`Route #33` & `Route #12`)
- **The `6:00 PM` City Council Lockout**:
  - Formal evening sessions of the **Virginia Beach City Council** at the **Municipal Center** gavel in at **`6:00 PM`**, but the majority of CVTA buses in Virginia Beach stop running around `6:00 PM`. A citizen can take a bus *to* the Municipal Center in the afternoon, but will be **stranded at the Municipal Center** if they stay for the `6:00 PM` meeting:
    - **Northbound from Municipal Center (`Route #33`)**: The last **Route #33** going to **68th Street** leaves at **`6:01 PM`**, and the **`7:01 PM`** trip goes only as far as **19th Street** at the Oceanfront.
    - **Toward CCHR Hub (`Route #33`)**: The last **Route #33** to **CCHR** leaves the Municipal Center at **`6:33 PM`**, at which time the *only* connecting bus left at CCHR is **Route #36**.
    - **The `5:30 PM` (`5:33 PM`) Connecting Deadline**: If a rider needs to connect at **CCHR** to **Routes #12, #25, #29, or #36**, they **must leave the Municipal Center on the `5:30 PM` (`5:33 PM`) Route #33**—nearly half an hour *before* the `6:00 PM` City Council meeting starts.
    - **Katie & Nate's Specific Route Home (`Last #12 at 5:48 PM`)**: Because Katie and Nate live off **Military Highway & Indian River Road**, they must take **Route #33** from the Municipal Center to **CCHR** and transfer onto **Route #12** to get back to Military Highway. Because the **last Route #12 of the day departs CCHR at `5:48 PM`**, Katie and Nate *have* to catch the **`5:30 PM` (`5:33 PM`) Route #33** out of the Municipal Center—meaning their last `#12` bus home pulls out of CCHR **twelve minutes before the `6:00 PM` City Council meeting even begins!**
- **The Spark**: When the video Nate filmed on the `#15` goes viral across local blogs and newspapers—and Katie sees that none of the establishment City Council candidates bothered to ride the bus while transit riders are scheduled out of `6:00 PM` Council meetings—Katie decides to run for **City Council** herself.

## 3. Katie Prairie's Step-by-Step Political Career Ladder (`2010–2026+`)

### Step 1: Virginia Beach City Council (`Non-Partisan` under Virginia Law)
- **Partisan Status**: **Non-Partisan** (as mandated by the Code of Virginia for municipal elections).
- **Term Length & Service**: **1 Term (4-Year Term)** — Elected **November 2010** at **Age 25** (serving **`January 2011 – January 2014`** before moving up to the Virginia General Assembly).
- **Campaign & Legislative Highlights**:
  - Campaigning directly from the front wheelchair securement bays of CVTA buses and rolling door-to-door along neighborhood sidewalks in her compact power wheelchair alongside Nate (who writes their custom voter-turnout and precinct-mapping software), Katie defeats an entrenched incumbent to win her City Council seat.
  - Upon taking her seat at the Municipal Center (where the city has to install a permanent wheelchair ramp up to the Council dais), her first signature ordinance funds **late-evening CVTA service on Route #33 and the evening connecting pulse (`#12`, `#25`, `#29`, `#36`) at CCHR** so working-class and disabled citizens can attend `6:00 PM` City Council meetings in person and still ride the bus home afterward.

### Step 2: Virginia House of Delegates (`Democrat` — State Capitol, Richmond)
- **Constitutional Title**: **Delegate Katie Prairie (`D-Virginia Beach`)** in the 100-member **Virginia House of Delegates** (the lower chamber of the Virginia General Assembly in Richmond).
- **Term Length & Service**: **2 Terms (2-Year Terms, Odd-Year Elections — 4 Years Total)** — Elected **November 2013** (Age 28) and re-elected **November 2015** (Age 30), serving **`January 2014 – January 2018`**.
- **Virginia "Citizen Legislature" Rhythm**:
  - Because the Virginia General Assembly is a part-time legislature that meets in Richmond for **60 days in even-numbered years** and **45 days in odd-numbered years** (January through late February / early March), Katie and Nate continue living in Hampton Roads for ten months of the year and stay together in an accessible apartment near Capitol Square in Richmond during the winter legislative session.
- **Legislative Highlights**:
  - Serving in the Democratic minority in the House of Delegates during the mid-2010s, Delegate Prairie earns a statewide reputation for forensic, unshakeable committee preparation, championing disability Medicaid waiver reform, regional transit funding, and working-family protections while forcing historic ADA renovations in the State Capitol complex.

### Step 3: Senate of Virginia (`Democrat` — State Senator, Richmond)
- **Constitutional Title**: **State Senator Katie Prairie (`D-Virginia Beach / Chesapeake`)** in the 40-member **Senate of Virginia** (the upper chamber of the Virginia General Assembly).
- **Term Length & Service**: **2+ Terms (4-Year Terms, Odd-Year Elections)** — Elected **November 2019** (Age 34, when Democrats win the Virginia General Assembly majority) and re-elected **November 2023** (Age 38), serving **`January 2020 – January 2027`** *(optionally entering the State Senate earlier via a special election or the `Nov 2015` cycle if desired)*.
- **Legislative Highlights**:
  - In the Senate majority starting in January 2020, Senator Prairie rises to become a powerhouse committee chair (Transportation / Rehabilitation & Social Services), passing landmark statewide legislation on public transit equity, accessible housing, disability civil rights, and substance-abuse recovery programs—building high-profile statewide name recognition across Hampton Roads, Richmond, Northern Virginia, and the New River Valley / Roanoke Valley (**CPI** & **VDU**).

### Step 4: United States Senator from Virginia (`D-Virginia` — Washington, D.C.)
- **Constitutional Eligibility**: Meets the U.S. Constitution's **Article I, Section 3** minimum age requirement (`30 years old`) on **April 8, 2015**.
- **Election Cycle**: **Class II Virginia U.S. Senate Seat — Elected Tuesday, November 3, 2026** (Age 41; sworn in **January 3, 2027** for a **6-year term**).
- **Campaign & Narrative Payoff (`pov: "Nathaniel"`)**:
  - When Virginia's senior Democratic U.S. Senator announces their retirement ahead of the 2026 cycle, State Senator Katie Prairie launches a grassroots statewide campaign powered by Nate's data architecture and fifteen years of fighting for working-class and disabled Virginians.
  - Throughout the entire political arc—from the CCHR bus loop in 2008 to City Hall, Capitol Square in Richmond, the Election Night headquarters on **November 3, 2026**, and the U.S. Capitol subway and Senate floor in Washington, D.C.—the story is told through **Nathaniel's eyes**, proving that the twin brother and sister who once hid behind a locked bedroom door with a box of dry cereal on New Year's Eve never left each other behind.

### Step 5: Senator Prairie's Signature Commute (`ARTS` Business Class from `NFK` to `ALX` Mobile Lift $\rightarrow$ Capital Transit Yellow Line Over the Charles R. Fenwick Bridge $\rightarrow$ `L'Enfant Plaza` $\rightarrow$ Blue Line to `Capitol South`)
- **The "Amtrak Joe" Parallel with Autistic Sensory & Transit-Insider Precision**:
  - Rather than isolating herself inside the Washington beltway bubble or riding in black SUVs up I-95, **U.S. Senator Katie Prairie (`D-Virginia`)** commutes regularly by rail on the **ARTS (*American Rail Transit Service*) Northeast Regional** from **Norfolk (`NFK`)** alongside Nate (and on her January 3, 2027 Swearing-In Day, their father **David Prairie**).
  - **Buying Regular Passenger `Business Class` Tickets for the Free Café Car Drinks**:
    - Instead of asking for a government VIP car or special arrangements, Katie, Nate, and their dad buy regular passenger tickets in **ARTS Business Class**—specifically because Business Class comes with **complimentary non-alcoholic beverages (free ginger ale, soda, and coffee) from the Café Car**!
  - **Why She Switches at `ALX` (*Alexandria*) on the `ARTS Mobile Lift` Instead of Riding to `WAS` (*Washington Union Station*)**:
    - Because Katie and Nate are autistic, they strongly prefer **Alexandria (`ALX`)** over the cavernous, echoing, crowded sensory overload of **Washington Union Station (`WAS`)** (and skipping `WAS` also avoids the slow train crawl across the Long Bridge and through the First Street Tunnel).
    - Because **`ALX`** has a **low-level red-brick platform**, the station attendant rolls the portable **`ARTS Mobile Lift`** right up to the open Business Class vestibule door to lower Nate and Senator-Elect Katie Prairie down to the brick platform—leaving fellow passengers and commuters wondering in amazement why the newly elected U.S. Senator is getting off at Alexandria (*"Wait—is that Senator-Elect Prairie getting off the ARTS train on the mobile lift at ALX?"*) as she, Nate, and their dad head straight toward the adjacent **`King St–Old Town`** Capital Transit Metro station.
  - **The Yellow-to-Blue Line Shortcut (`King St–Old Town` $\rightarrow$ `L'Enfant Plaza` $\rightarrow$ `Capitol South`)**:
    - Although the **Capital Transit Blue Line** runs directly from `King St–Old Town` to `Capitol South`, taking the Blue Line all the way is the long way around via `Rosslyn` and `Foggy Bottom`.
    - Like true transit regulars, Senator Prairie and Nate tap their **`CapitalPass`** cards to board the **Capital Transit Yellow Line** at `King St–Old Town`, ride across the Potomac River over the **Charles R. Fenwick Bridge** into **`L'Enfant Plaza`**, and transfer to the **Blue Line** (or Orange/Silver Line) for the quick two-stop hop to **`Capitol South`**.
  - **Senator Prairie's Statewide Constituent Transit Rule**:
    - Throughout her tenure in the U.S. Senate, whenever Senator Katie Prairie visits constituents anywhere in the Commonwealth of Virginia (whether in Hampton Roads on **CVTA**, in Blacksburg/Roanoke on **Blue Ridge Transit (`BRT`)**, in Richmond, or in Northern Virginia on **Capital Transit / BOLT / ARC / Fairfax Link**), **she always rides that region's local public transit system as part of her constituent visit** so she experiences the buses, trains, lifts, ramps, and transfer schedules firsthand alongside everyday riders.
  - **Swearing Her Oath on the Constitution (`Virginia Constitution` & `U.S. Constitution — "So help me, God!"`)**:
    - Whenever Katie is sworn into Virginia state or municipal office (**Virginia Beach City Council**, **Virginia House of Delegates**, and **Senate of Virginia**), she famously insists on swearing her oath of office with her hand resting on a bound copy of the **Constitution of the Commonwealth of Virginia**.
    - When she is sworn into the **United States Senate** on **January 3, 2027**, she famously swears her oath of office—ending with a ringing ***"So help me, God!"***—with her left hand resting on a bound leather copy of the **Constitution of the United States of America** held by her twin brother Nate.

## 4. The Two-Story Brick House on Sunset Drive (Just on the Other Side of the King Street Tunnel) & The Bedroom 4 Coding Sanctuary

When the U.S. Senate is in session, Senator Katie Prairie does not live inside the District of Columbia—she, Nate, and their father David buy a **two-story red-brick house on Sunset Drive in Alexandria, Virginia** (modeled on the brick Colonial architecture at `1921 King Street` / `Sunset Drive`, referred to in prose simply as **"Sunset Drive"** without a house number).

### A. Exterior Geography & Transit Sightlines (`Sunset Drive` $\leftrightarrow$ `King St–Old Town` & `ALX`)
- **Right on the Other Side of the King Street Railroad Tunnel/Underpass**:
  - The house sits immediately west of the steel railroad overpass/tunnel where King Street passes beneath the CSX / **ARTS** tracks and the **Capital Transit** Metro tracks.
  - Looking out from the front yard and upstairs windows, you can see both **King St–Old Town Metro Station** and the elevated rail embankment of **Alexandria Union Station (`ALX`)** right across the underpass.
  - A wide, flat sidewalk runs straight through the short King Street railroad underpass into the **`King St–Old Town` Station bus loop**, the **`King St–Old Town` Metro** entrance, and **`ALX`**—giving Katie and Nate effortless, step-free access to buses and trains while allowing Senator Katie Prairie to sleep in the Commonwealth of Virginia every single night.

### B. Two-Story Floor Plan & Vertical Platform Lift Architecture
- **Interior Through-Floor Vertical Platform Wheelchair Lift (`Ground Floor` $\leftrightarrow$ `Second Floor`)**:
  - To make the two-story brick house 100% accessible for both Katie and Nate's compact `SwiftChair` power wheelchairs, they install an **interior residential vertical platform wheelchair lift** (a quiet, enclosed through-floor vertical lift shaft with flush threshold gates on the first and second floors) alongside the central hallway staircase so both siblings can roll between the ground floor and the second floor at will.
- **First Floor (Ground Level)**:
  - **Front Living Room & Dining Room (Facing the Street)**: Positioned at the front of the house behind the shuttered sash windows so anyone walking past on the sidewalk only sees a traditional living and dining room.
  - **Accessible Kitchen & Ground-Floor Roll-In Bathroom**: Located in the center of the first floor with roll-under counters and a curbless roll-in shower.
  - **Ground-Floor Back Master Bedroom (*Officially Katie's Bedroom — Shared by Katie & Nate*)**:
    - Built in the **private back corner of the ground floor** overlooking the fenced backyard—specifically positioned **in the back of the house so nobody on the street can ever peer into a front window to see two adult opposite-gender siblings sharing a bed**.
    - Furnished with their shared **Queen-sized bed** beneath the framed blue butterfly print and dual wall chargers for their two compact `SwiftChair` power wheelchairs.
- **Second Floor (Upper Level — Reached via the Vertical Platform Lift or Stairs)**:
  - **Bedroom 2 (*Officially Nate's Bedroom — Unused*)**:
    - Upstairs bedroom officially designated as Nate's room and set up with a neatly made **Full-sized bed**, nightstand, and dresser, though it goes completely unused at night because Nate sleeps downstairs with Katie in the private ground-floor back bedroom.
  - **Bedroom 3 (*David's Bedroom*)**:
    - Upstairs bedroom for their father **David Prairie**, furnished with a **Full-sized bed** and adjacent upstairs bath.
  - **Bedroom 4 (*The Upstairs Three-Desk Coding & Remote-Work Office Overlooking the Trains*)**:
    - Using the vertical platform lift to roll their powerchairs up to the second floor, Katie and Nate share **Bedroom 4** with their dad as a three-desk home office with a direct view toward the elevated **`King St–Old Town`** and **`ALX`** train platforms:
      - **Katie & Nate's Dual Coding Desks**: Even as a U.S. Senator, Katie sits side by side with Nate coding in **Quantum Studio 2026** and **PHP** on the **network of websites that the two siblings share**.
      - **David's Remote-Work Desk**: Their father David has his own desk in Bedroom 4 where he works remotely during the week.

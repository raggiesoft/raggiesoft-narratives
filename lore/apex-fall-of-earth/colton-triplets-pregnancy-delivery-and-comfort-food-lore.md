## 1. First-Trimester Conception & Biological Symptom Timeline (Mid-May – June 2045)

Because Apex Defense Solutions cut corners on the Omni-Cycle blocker and Sarah is carrying triplets (identical girls **Ellie** and **Nora** plus fraternal brother **Leo**), her body produces human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) at a significantly higher and faster rate than a singleton pregnancy:

1. **Implantation (Late May — ~6 to 12 days post-conception):**
   - Around May 18–24, the embryos attach to the uterine lining, causing mild cramping (easily dismissed as muscle fatigue from gardening or hiking) or light spotting.
2. **First Wave of Symptoms (Early June — ~2 to 3 weeks):**
   - **No Obvious "Missed" Period Clue:** Because Sarah was previously on a heavy corporate blocker that halted her cycle entirely, she initially assumes the blocker is simply working its way out of her system.
   - **Heavy Fatigue, Heightened Olfactory Sensitivity, & Tenderness:** Three placentas drawing on her energy reserves trigger deep afternoon exhaustion and acute sensitivity to smells (soil, pines, or galley cooking).
3. **Undeniable Morning Sickness & Ultrasound Reveal (Mid-to-Late June — ~4 to 6 weeks):**
   - Severe first-trimester nausea (`hyperemesis gravidarum`) prompts Wyatt to suggest checking Sub-Level 1's diagnostic bio-bed and ultrasound suite, revealing three healthy heartbeats.

## 2. Delivery Intervals, Birth-Related Cerebral Palsy, & Neurodivergence

- **Baby A — Ellie (Neurotypical "Mother Hen", Firstborn):** Positioned ideally and delivered smoothly first.
- **Baby B — Nora (Verbal Autistic Sister with Mild Left-Leg CP, +12 to 15 Minutes):** After Baby A is delivered, uterine dynamics shift and Nora experiences brief umbilical compression before Aria's automated medical suite completes extraction, resulting in mild cerebral palsy (a noticeable limp in her left leg).
- **Baby C — Leo (Non-Verbal Autistic Brother with Severe CP, +8 to 10 Minutes After Baby B / ~20–25 Minutes Total):** Faces the most complicated delivery environment (cord wrap / breech positioning) with a drop in oxygen before Aria stabilizes him, accounting for his severe cerebral palsy and non-verbal neurodivergence.
- **Why Hidden In Utero:** Birth-trauma CP does not exist prior to delivery (all prenatal ultrasounds show three actively moving, healthy fetuses), and autism is a neurodevelopmental wiring difference rather than a structural anomaly on physical scans.

## 3. Age Twelve Rebellion of Love & Custom Shared Sibling Bed

- **Age Twelve Transition:** At twelve—when Wyatt and Sarah offer to transition Leo into his own medically equipped bedroom thinking the girls want adolescent privacy—both Ellie and Nora flatly refuse separation, insisting they are the ones who know his routines without him needing to speak.
- **Foundry-Printed Triplet Bed & Nighttime Startle Grounding:** Wyatt fabricates an oversized custom bed (modeled after his and Sarah's Alaskan King) so Leo permanently sleeps in the center flanked by Ellie and Nora:
  - **Startle Triggers:** Sudden geothermal HVAC hums, muffled mountain super-cell thunder, or dreadnought hull settling can trigger painful CP startle spasms in the dark.
  - **Instinctual Anchor:** Without even fully waking, Ellie drapes an arm across Leo's chest for deep-pressure grounding while Nora presses against his shoulder or rests her leg across his ankles, communicating wordlessly: *We are here. We have you. You are safe.*

## 4. The Triplets' Signature Comfort Food (Origin Date: June 18, 2045)

- **The Dish:** Thick-sliced smoked sausage pan-seared crispy in cast iron, topped with melted sharp white cheddar cheese, and dipped directly into cold homemade hydroponic strawberry jam.
- **Origin & Family Significance:** Originated as Sarah's first intense post-hyperemesis pregnancy craving (combining dense protein/sodium, calcium, and fast glucose to support three placentas) and became the lifelong comfort meal Wyatt pan-seared for Ellie, Nora, and Leo—offering Leo ideal contrasting sensory textures (crispy savory sausage, warm melted cheddar, and cold sweet jam).

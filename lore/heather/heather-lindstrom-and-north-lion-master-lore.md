## 1. The Core Thematic Mirror (`Why Heather's Adult Life Echoes Northwood High`)

At age seventeen in Virginia Beach (`2001–2003`), **Heather Collins** was **Nathaniel ("Nate") Prairie's** first girlfriend—welcomed warmly into Nate and **Katie Prairie's** twin sanctuary because of how naturally she cared for her younger developmentally disabled, **non-verbal** cousin **Wesley** (`who communicates through expressive vocalized sounds/hums and non-vocalized gestures/two-tap hand squeezes`, and for whom **Nate was the very first real guy buddy Wesley ever had in his life**, making their four-person Saturday gatherings at **Emerald Creek Mall** the highlight of Wesley's week!). Then, on **Monday, January 6, 2003** (`books/katie/e004/c002/p002.md`), stung by sixty seconds of public embarrassment in Homeroom 212 over **Tyler's** malicious New Year's Eve lie, Heather panicked, screamed at Nate without letting Nate or Katie speak two sentences in their own defense, and threw away eighteen months of trust. **Wesley was furious with Heather ever since she screamed at Nate and broke up with him**—taking the framed November 2001 *Annie's Pretzels* snapshot (`snapped by Aunt Linda—Wesley's ONLY picture in the world of himself and Nate`) off the living-room shelf to hide in his bedroom so Heather couldn't touch it, and demanding through his vocalized and non-vocalized cues all winter and spring that she go to the twins and issue a formal apology.

On **Saturday, June 14, 2003** outside the **Virginia Beach Pavilion** (`books/katie/e004/c002/p004.md`), after sitting hopefully through forty minutes of Elgar's ***Pomp and Circumstance***, sixteen-year-old Wesley marched Heather by her purple graduation sleeve straight up to Katie and Nate's wheelchairs and demanded she deliver that formal apology. When **Katie and Nate gently told Heather "no" to reforming a romantic relationship right as a reprise of *Pomp and Circumstance* echoed out of the Pavilion**, **it broke Wesley's heart right in front of them—permanently setting off Wesley's lifelong trigger for *Pomp and Circumstance* (`so to Wesley, graduations—even Molly's June 2026 high school graduation—are forever sad events because Nate had to say no to reforming the relationship with Heather`)**. Seeing innocent Wesley's tears upset both Katie and Nate terribly, and when Heather accepted they were over romantically and asked if there was *anything* she could do to prove she learned from her mistake before their family moved **back home to Minnesota** on **June 30, 2003** (`explaining that their family is originally from Minnesota, is already discussing Heather taking over Wesley's care in Minnesota, and that because Wesley is a minor with a developmental disability he can never be on his own and will always need an ADA companion aide for life`), the twins agreed to one strictly bounded Saturday at **Emerald Creek Mall** (`June 28, 2003`, `books/katie/e004/c002/p005.md`, scored to *If You Walked Away [Live in Chicago]*). Waiting nervously at the **`CVTA Route 15`** bus stop (`having driven Wesley since their neighborhood has no bus route`), Heather sat respectfully backed two feet away from their old corner table at *Annie's Pretzels* with her hands folded quietly in her lap; Wesley tried earnestly to get Nate and Heather to hug it out (`still wanting them to hug it out even at the very end, though it wasn't meant to be`), Heather gently lowered Wesley's hand herself and publicly owned 100% of the fault in front of Homeroom 212 classmate **Lauren Mitchell**, and as Nate and Katie gave Wesley a **smooth miniature toy city bus keyring** and a **clear-laminated Buddy Card** printed with their new **`Greenway Court, Apartment A6`** Norfolk address strictly for Wesley's picture postcards, **sixteen-year-old Wesley tried his absolute hardest across their final knuckle-tap goodbye to understand that moving thirteen hundred miles home to Minnesota meant this would be the very last time he ever saw his first guy buddy Nate in person**—postcards they traded happily until **July 2004**, when **Brandon Mercer intercepted and trashed Wesley's Saint Paul postcard (`containing Heather & Wesley's new apartment address after Aunt Linda's medical crisis turned guardianship/conservatorship over to Heather`)** right before Nate's October 2004 Arvin Road postcard came back **`Not Deliverable as Addressed — Unable to Forward`**!

Three years later in Minnesota, life hands twenty-one-year-old **Heather Lindstrom** (`née Collins`) the ultimate full-circle reckoning and calling:
1. She gives birth to **severely disabled, non-verbal fraternal twins—a son (`Jonas`) and a daughter (`Nora`)—who are 100% each other's safe person**, just like Nate and Katie Prairie.
2. Every single truth Nate and Katie taught teenage Heather at **Emerald Creek Mall**, in **Room 204**, and on **Arvin Road**—never separating fraternal twins who regulate each other's nervous systems, offering immediate deep-pressure comfort in public without an ounce of shame, speaking with zero baby talk, designing around public transit and seated wheelchair/child sightlines, and captioning every video properly—becomes the bedrock that turns Heather into the fierce, steady, unshakable mother her own children need.
3. Two years after the twins are born, Heather and her husband **Dan Lindstrom** welcome a third child—a neurotypical daughter (**`Molly`**)—who, completely on her own initiative around age seven/eight, decides that **her big brother Jonas and her big sister Nora deserve to live healthy, happy, dignified lives**, stepping up with the exact same instinctive, protective devotion young Heather once showed Wesley (`"Ever since Wes was five and I was seven..."`, `books/katie/e003/c006/p001.md`).
4. Alongside taking full **legal guardianship and conservatorship over Cousin Wesley** so Wesley lives permanently inside their family home, Heather and Dan build **`North Lion Scales & Tails`**—a beloved **`StreamCast`** educational reptile/amphibian channel (*North* = **Northwood High School**, *Lion* = **Northwood's Lion mascot**) and an accredited storefront **Reptile Zoo, Classroom, & Full-Line Supply Store** located directly on a **`Twin Cities Transit` (`TCT`)** public bus route in **Maplewood, Minnesota (`MSP`)**.

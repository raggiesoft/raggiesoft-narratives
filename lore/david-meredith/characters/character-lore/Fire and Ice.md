# **The Lore Bible: The Ice and Fire Protocol**

## **Meredith (Ice): The Surgical Strike**

- **The Method:** Meredith is terrifyingly quiet. She does not raise her voice, and she does not make a scene. She prefers to isolate the threat and dismantle them with cold, hard logic and the crushing weight of the law.
- **Defending David:** As seen in the third-floor lounge, Meredith handles Vesper by remaining perfectly still, lowering her voice, and wielding a manila folder of court documents. She corners the predator psychologically. 
- **Defending Kate:** If a boy approaches Kate in public and attempts to use the banned nickname "Kitty," Meredith will quietly step between them. She will calmly and clinically inform him of his exact legal standing, document his presence, and systematically erase his confidence until he retreats in nervous silence.
    

## **Morgan (Fire): The Scorched Earth**

- **The Method:** Morgan is a spectacle of sheer, unbridled protectiveness. She weaponizes public attention. If someone threatens her cousins, she wants the entire room to look at the predator and know exactly what they are doing.
- **The Restraint:** Her immediate internal instinct is to physically fight anyone who hurts her cousins, but she is smart enough to know that going to jail leaves David and Kate vulnerable.
- **Defending David:** If Morgan had been the one to catch Vesper screaming at David in Elevator M1, there would have been no quiet legal briefing. Morgan would have physically backed Vesper out of the elevator, shouting her down so loudly that the entire third-floor food court stopped to watch Vesper get humiliated and chased out of the mall.
- **Defending Kate:** If a boy tries to hit on Kate or touches her wheelchair, Morgan goes nuclear. She will loudly broadcast his predatory behavior to everyone within a fifty-foot radius, stepping aggressively into his personal space and using her physical presence to intimidate him until he turns and runs just to escape the public shaming.

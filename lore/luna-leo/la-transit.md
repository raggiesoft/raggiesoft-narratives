# Greater Los Angeles Transit Lore

In the *Luna and Leo* universe, public transit in California (and especially Los Angeles) uses in-universe fictionalized names for real-world transit agencies. The transit system is heavily fragmented, requiring riders to navigate multiple disjointed networks, reflecting the real-world complexity of SoCal transit.

## Transit Agencies

### ARTS (American Rail Transit System)
* **Real-world equivalent:** Amtrak / Amtrak California (Pacific Surfliner)
* **Role:** The national and state-supported intercity passenger rail network. Luna, Leo, Olivia, and Stella take an ARTS train down the coast (similar to the Coast Starlight or Pacific Surfliner) from the Bay Area (Jack London Square) to LA Union Station.
* **Key Hub:** Jack London Square (Oakland) in the north; LA Union Station in the south.

### LATA (Los Angeles Transit Authority)
* **Real-world equivalent:** LA Metro (Bus and Rail)
* **Role:** The primary urban transit operator within Los Angeles proper. They run the subway (B and D lines), light rail (A, C, E, K lines), and local/rapid bus services.

### SCR (Southern California Regional)
* **Real-world equivalent:** Metrolink
* **Role:** The commuter rail system connecting LA Union Station to the broader Southern California suburbs (Orange County, San Bernardino, Riverside, Ventura).

### Pacific Blue Bus
* **Real-world equivalent:** Big Blue Bus (Santa Monica)
* **Role:** A separate municipal bus operator primarily serving the Westside and coastal communities. Requires a separate fare or transfer agreement from LATA.

### City Circulator
* **Real-world equivalent:** LADOT DASH
* **Role:** Neighborhood-level localized shuttle buses that run short loops in specific districts (e.g., Downtown LA, Hollywood).

### Pathfinder
* **Real-world equivalent:** Greyhound Lines
* **Role:** The national intercity bus network for long-distance road travel.

## Narrative Notes
* **Fragmented System:** The cast often experiences frustration when transferring between systems (e.g., from an ARTS train to a LATA subway, and then to a Pacific Blue Bus), as fare systems and schedules don't always align perfectly.
* **The Journey South:** The 12-hour train ride down the coast on ARTS is a scenic but grueling journey, acting as a transition period between their Bay Area adventures and their time in Southern LA.

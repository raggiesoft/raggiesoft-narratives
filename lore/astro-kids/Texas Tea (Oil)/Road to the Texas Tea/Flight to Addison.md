## The Wright Amendment (2004 Reality)

You are absolutely correct that in 2004, the Wright Amendment was still in full effect in Dallas (it wasn't fully repealed until 2014). This law heavily restricted direct commercial flights out of Dallas Love Field (DAL) to states beyond Texas and its immediate neighbors.

**However, there is a massive loophole:** The Wright Amendment only restricted _commercial airline flights_ operating aircraft with more than 56 seats. Private charter jets and general aviation were entirely exempt. A private Gulfstream or Cessna Citation could legally fly direct from Virginia to Love Field.

That being said, if the Dallas law firm wanted to completely eliminate sensory overload for the triplets and avoid the hustle of Love Field entirely, they would use a different airport altogether.

## The Origin: Lynchburg Regional (LYH)

- **The Location:** Lynchburg Regional Airport (LYH) is the absolute best choice. It is only about a 40-minute drive from the Big Island cabin, minimizing Orion's time in the bumpy van.
    
- **The FBO Advantage:** Because they are flying private, they do not go to the main commercial terminal. They drive their van directly up to the airport's Fixed Base Operator (FBO)—a small, quiet, private aviation terminal.
    
- **Zero TSA Chaos:** There are no crowds, no loud intercom announcements, and no TSA security lines. The van pulls right up to the tarmac. The flight crew comes out to greet them, helping Valerie manually lift Lyra and Orion into the luxurious, quiet cabin of the jet. The wheelchairs are carefully folded and stowed in the pressurized cargo hold.
    

## The Destination: Addison Airport (ADS)

While DFW is a sensory nightmare and DAL is still relatively busy, the elite corporate predators of Dallas do not use either for their private jets. They use **Addison Airport (ADS)**.

- **The Billionaire's Strip:** Located in North Dallas, just minutes away from the city's highest-end corporate law firms and luxury hotels, Addison is a dedicated general aviation airport. It has _zero_ commercial airline traffic.
    
- **Total Privacy:** When the jet touches down at Addison, it taxis directly into a private, climate-controlled hangar.
    
- **The Seamless Transfer:** The law firm has a fleet of black, tinted luxury SUVs (including an accessible transport van they rented specifically for the triplets) waiting inside the hangar. The triplets are transferred straight from the quiet jet cabin into the quiet vehicles without ever seeing a crowded terminal, hearing a jet engine blast, or dealing with the Texas heat.
    

## The Sensory Experience

For Lyra, Orion, and Celeste, this flight is a revelation. They are used to the noisy, grinding, bone-rattling suspension of their beat-up van. The private jet, by contrast, is a masterclass in engineered luxury.

- The cabin is heavily soundproofed, reducing the engine noise to a gentle, consistent white-noise hum that Celeste actually finds soothing.
    
- The leather seats are massive and highly adjustable, allowing Orion to recline into a comfortable, supported position.
    
- It is their first taste of what limitless money actually buys: not just things, but the ability to purchase absolute peace and physical comfort.

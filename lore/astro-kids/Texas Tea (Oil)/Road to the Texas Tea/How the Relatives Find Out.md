# Lore: The Heir Hunters (How the Relatives Found Out)

**Tags:** `#worldbuilding` `#legal_strategy` `#texas_tea` `#timeline_2004`

## The Invisible Radar

You are entirely correct: a probate filing, even a multi-billion-dollar one, does not automatically trigger a breaking news chyron on national television. To the general public across the United States, the unfreezing of the $2.4 billion SovTex Energy escrow was completely invisible.

The distant relatives scattered across the country did not stumble upon the Texas public records, nor did they hear about it on the evening news. They found out because a highly specialized, predatory industry tracked them down.

## The Parasite Industry (Probate Researchers)

In the early 2000s, an entire cottage industry of "Heir Hunters" (forensic genealogists and independent probate researchers) existed specifically to exploit massive unclaimed estates.

- **The Data Scrape:** These firms made their money by constantly monitoring state unclaimed property databases and newly filed probate dockets.
    
- **The Trigger:** When the Dallas law firm filed the initial _Application to Determine Heirship_ linking Valerie and the triplets to the great-grandfather's estate, the sheer dollar amount attached to the SovTex escrow triggered massive alarms within these heir-hunting firms.
    
- **The Trace:** These agencies immediately pulled the great-grandfather’s public records and began aggressively building out his family tree, tracing every single branched bloodline across the country looking for living descendants.
    

## The Cold Calls

This is how the estranged relatives actually discovered the money. They were living normal, everyday lives in places like Ohio, Florida, or Pennsylvania when their landlines rang.

- **The Pitch:** An heir hunter would call a distant second cousin and say, _"We have discovered that you are a direct descendant of a Texas estate currently in probate, estimated to be worth over two billion dollars. If you sign a contract granting our agency a 15% finder's fee, our lawyers will secure your rightful percentage."_
    
- **The Greed:** The relatives, suddenly realizing they were potentially tied to billionaire-level wealth, were thrown into a frenzy.
    

## The Backfire & The Cheap Lawyers

The heir hunters' business model actually caused the very wave of disorganized litigation that the Dallas firm crushed.

Once the distant relatives were alerted to the existence of the great-grandfather's estate in Texas, many of them refused to sign the heir hunter's predatory 15% contract. Out of sheer greed, they decided to cut out the middleman. They took the information the heir hunter had provided on the phone, drove to their local, small-town attorney, and paid them out-of-pocket to file a claim directly in the Texas court.

Because they were relying on out-of-state, low-tier lawyers who had no idea how to navigate high-stakes Texas oil probate law, they walked directly into the Dallas law firm's procedural meat grinder.

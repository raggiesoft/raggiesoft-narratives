# Lore: SovTex Energy (The Oil Monolith)

**Tags:** `#corporations` `#texas_oil` `#the_fortune` `#worldbuilding` `#sovtex_energy`

## Overview

**SovTex Energy** is a massive, multinational oil and gas corporation headquartered in Houston, Texas. It is the entity responsible for pumping the crude oil from the great-grandfather's West Texas land, and the source of the family's staggering $2.4 billion royalty trust and ongoing monthly payouts.

## Origins: The "Baby Standards" (1911)

SovTex Energy was not built from scratch; it was born from a government mandate.

In 1911, the United States Supreme Court ruled that the monolithic Standard Oil Company held an illegal monopoly under the Sherman Antitrust Act. The court ordered the trust to be broken up into 34 independent companies, historically referred to as the "Baby Standards".

While companies like Standard Oil of New Jersey and Standard Oil of New York eventually merged to become real-world titans, **SovTex Energy** (originally incorporated as Sovereign Texas Petroleum) was the Baby Standard that inherited the trust's sprawling southwestern infrastructure and refining capabilities.

## The Permian Basin Boom (1920s)

In the early 1920s, the oil industry fundamentally changed when wildcatters struck black gold in the Permian Basin of West Texas—a massive, arid region that many geologists had previously dismissed as a "petroleum graveyard".

- **The Land Grab:** Realizing the Permian Basin sat on one of the thickest deposits of hydrocarbons on Earth, SovTex Energy moved aggressively. They sent landmen across West Texas to buy up mineral rights from ranchers, farmers, and eccentric investors.
    
- **The Great-Grandfather's Lease:** One of those eccentric investors was the Starling great-grandfather. He owned a massive tract of seemingly worthless scrubland. SovTex Energy didn't buy the land outright; they signed a standard commercial lease giving the great-grandfather **1/8th mineral royalty rights** on anything they pulled out of the ground.
    

## The Escrow Anomaly

The Permian Basin didn't just produce oil for a few years; it became the highest-producing oil field in the United States, yielding billions of barrels of crude over the decades. SovTex Energy grew into a global superpower off the back of this basin.

- **The Disconnect:** The great-grandfather moved east, died intestate (without a will), and the grandfather (who built the Big Island cabin) completely lost track of the Texas paperwork.
    
- **The Law of Escheat:** SovTex Energy could not legally keep the great-grandfather's 1/8th cut. As the oil continuously pumped from the 1920s all the way through the 20th century, SovTex faithfully deposited the great-grandfather's royalties into a holding account. When the heirs couldn't be located, corporate law dictated that SovTex had to turn the funds over to the state.
    
- **The Compounding Giant:** For decades, SovTex Energy surrendered millions of dollars in unpaid royalties to the Texas Comptroller's Unclaimed Property division. Through decades of relentless Permian Basin oil production, massive spikes in global crude prices, and compound interest, that forgotten 1/8th royalty swelled into the **$2.4 billion** escrow account Valerie found on the library computer in 2004.
    

## Current Status (2004)

Once the Dallas law firm finalizes the probate court proceedings, SovTex Energy's title decks are legally updated. The corporation is forced to stop sending the 1/8th royalty checks to the state of Texas. Instead, SovTex Energy’s automated accounting system routes millions of dollars in fresh oil revenue directly into the newly established trust accounts of Valerie, Lyra, Orion, and Celeste on the first of every single month.

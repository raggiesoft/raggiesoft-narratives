# Lore: The Texas Oil Trust ($2.4 Billion)

**Tags:** `#timeline_2004` `#finances` `#texas_oil` `#the_bridge_loan` `#legal_strategy`

## The Discovery: How Valerie Found Texas

In 2004, Valerie has absolutely no knowledge of the great-grandfather's Texas mineral rights. Her discovery of the fortune is a perfect storm of early-internet architecture and idle curiosity.

- **The Aggregator Website:** While at the library computer, Valerie doesn't actually go to the Virginia Treasury website directly. She uses **MissingMoney.com**—a newly established, real-world national database officially endorsed by the National Association of Unclaimed Property Administrators (NAUPA).
    
- **The Search:** After successfully finding the mother's $1,500 life insurance policy, she decides to search for the grandfather (the one who bought the cabin) just to see if he left behind any old utility deposits. She enters his distinct last name into the national search bar.
    
- **The Hit:** The database returns zero hits for the grandfather in Virginia. However, it returns a massive hit in _Texas_ for the grandfather's father (the great-grandfather).
    
- **The Redirect:** Valerie clicks the link, which redirects her to the Texas Comptroller’s Unclaimed Property portal. Because the great-grandfather died intestate (without a will) and his descendants moved east and lost touch, decades of SovTex Energy 1/8th oil royalties have been quietly escheating to the state of Texas. The portal displays the staggering escrow balance: **$2,418,903,114.67**.
    

## The Strategy: The Contingency Play

Valerie treats this discovery exactly like a multi-billion-dollar lottery ticket. She knows she is a 22-year-old girl in a thrift-store flannel with three disabled cousins and three dollars in her checking account; if she tries to claim this alone, the state of Texas or corporate vultures will eat her alive. She needs a team of apex predators.

Because she has zero cash upfront, she relies on **contingency law**. She targets the most ruthless, prestigious Estate and Probate corporate law firm in Dallas. She knows they will take a percentage of the winnings (perhaps 1% or 2%) in exchange for representing them for free upfront. Two percent of $2.4 billion is $48 million—an easy sell for any law firm.

## The Claim Timeline (The Long Game)

Getting billions of dollars out of a state government is a grueling, highly audited legal process.

### Phase 1: The Apex Predators (Weeks 1 to 4)

- **Week 1 (The Cold Call):** Valerie calls the Dallas law firm from the cabin's landline. She calmly provides the great-grandfather's exact name, the SovTex Energy property ID from the website, and the family tree proving she and the triplets are the sole surviving direct descendants.
    
- **Weeks 2–3 (The Verification):** The law firm doesn't take her word for it. They dispatch a private investigator/paralegal team to Virginia. They pull birth certificates, death certificates, and the grandfather's old estate papers. They confirm Valerie is holding a golden ticket.
    
- **Week 4 (The Contract):** The Dallas firm formally takes the case on contingency. They begin filing the "Application to Determine Heirship" in a Texas probate court to legally establish Valerie, Lyra, Orion, and Celeste as the 4th-generation heirs.
    

### Phase 2: The Bridge Loan (Week 5)

- **The Mechanism:** The law firm knows the state will take almost a year to release the funds, but their clients are living in poverty now. The firm introduces Valerie to their partnered Private Wealth Bank.
    
- **The Underwriting:** Because the $2.4 billion claim is verified and backed by the state of Texas, the private bank views it as zero-risk collateral.
    
- **The Payout:** The bank underwrites a massive **$25 million "bridge loan"** (a line of credit) for the family. The moment this account goes live, Valerie has a limitless black card. This is the exact moment they can cancel their Food Stamps and Medicaid. They are functionally rich, even though the state hasn't paid out yet.
    

### Phase 3: The Bureaucratic War (Months 2 to 8)

- **The Probate Court:** The lawyers spend months in Texas courts formally proving the genealogy, fighting off any fraudulent claims from distant third cousins, and legally dividing the estate into four equal shares (25% each for Valerie, Lyra, Orion, and Celeste).
    
- **The State Audit:** The Texas Comptroller’s office performs a massive, agonizingly slow audit of the SovTex Energy escrow account to ensure every penny of the $2.4 billion is accounted for before releasing it.
    
- **The Future Royalties:** The lawyers legally compel SovTex Energy to update their title decks. Instead of sending the monthly 1/8th royalty checks to the state, the oil company prepares to route them directly to the family's newly established trust accounts.
    

### Phase 4: The Final Transfer (Month 9)

- **The Release:** A Texas judge signs the final probate order. The Texas Comptroller formally releases the escheated funds.
    
- **The Wire:** The state wires the $2.4 billion (minus the lawyers' contingency fee and the $25M bridge loan repayment) into the four family trusts.
    
- **The Aftermath:** By late 2004, the money is officially theirs. The bridge loan served its purpose of getting them out of poverty, and they are now legally recognized billionaires receiving massive monthly royalty checks from West Texas oil fields for the rest of their lives.

# Lore: The Circulation Desk (Valerie's Daily Mandate)

**Tags:** `#valerie` `#library_operations` `#lynchburg` `#timeline_1999_2001` `#worldbuilding`

## Overview

As a full-time, hourly municipal employee at the Piedmont Central Branch, Valerie is the operational anchor of the circulation desk. She is not the branch manager or the head librarian, but she is the frontline logistical engine that keeps the building running. Because of her sharp administrative pragmatism, she is frequently heavily relied upon by the senior staff to maintain order in the high-traffic downtown facility.

## Daily Frontline Logistics

When Valerie is on a standard shift, she is physically tethered to the main circulation desk. Her baseline expectations from the city require absolute accuracy and steady patron management.

- **Account Management:** She processes new library card applications, verifies Lynchburg residency or adjacent county addresses, and updates expired accounts in the municipal database.
    
- **Cash Handling:** She is responsible for managing a precise cash register. She accepts payments for late fees, lost materials, and damaged books. Because this is city revenue, her till must balance perfectly to the penny at the end of every shift.
    
- **Information Triage:** Valerie acts as the first line of defense for the building. She handles basic directional questions, explains loan policies, and efficiently redirects complex research or computer-help requests to the specialized Reference Librarians on the second floor.
    

## Behind-the-Desk Operations

When she isn't actively assisting a patron, Valerie is expected to manage the constant physical flow of returning inventory.

- **The Book Drop:** She empties the massive, heavy canvas bins beneath the exterior book drops, quickly scanning hundreds of items back into the system to clear patron accounts.
    
- **The Holds Shelf:** She processes transit bins coming in from other Seven Hills branches, printing slips and organizing requested materials on the public holds shelf.
    
- **Staging Carts:** While the actual physical shelving is usually handled by part-time pages (often high school students), Valerie is responsible for organizing the incoming materials onto the correct rolling carts (fiction, non-fiction, media, children's) so the pages can shelve them efficiently.
    

## The Acting Shift Supervisor

Because Valerie is a highly competent full-time employee, she is cross-trained as an "Acting Shift Supervisor." If the Lead Circulation Supervisor calls out sick, takes a vacation day, or steps off the floor for a meeting, Valerie is given the override keys and the authority to run the desk.

When acting as the shift supervisor, her responsibilities escalate significantly:

- **Opening & Closing:** She is entrusted with the physical security of the building. She unlocks the main doors, arms or disarms the municipal alarm system, and ensures all public computers are shut down at closing time.
    
- **Till Reconciliation:** She must pull the cash drawers from the other circulation clerks, count out the registers in the back office, and prepare the daily deposit slips for the city bank run.
    
- **De-escalation & Overrides:** She becomes the final authority for the desk clerks. If a patron aggressively disputes a late fee, or if a clerk needs a supervisor's system override to waive a penalty due to extenuating circumstances, Valerie handles the interaction. Her history of dealing with toxic family dynamics makes her exceptionally good at shutting down hostile patrons with cold, polite, bureaucratic finality.

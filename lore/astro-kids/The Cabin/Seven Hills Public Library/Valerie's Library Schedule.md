# Lore: The Municipal Clock (Valerie's Weekly Schedule)

## The Shift Mathematics

Valerie's role at the Piedmont Central Branch is structured as a textbook municipal 40-hour work week. Because the library's operating hours demand full-day coverage, she does not work split shifts; she works the entire day from open to close.

**Weekday Shifts (Monday – Friday)**

- **Arrival:** 9:45 AM (15 minutes early to prep the tills, unlock doors, and boot up circulation terminals).
    
- **Operating Hours:** 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
    
- **Departure:** 6:15 PM (15 minutes of closing duties, balancing the cash register, and securing the building).
    
- **Total Elapsed Time:** 8.5 hours.
    
- **Paid Time:** 8.0 hours (after her mandatory 30-minute unpaid lunch break).
    

**Saturday Shifts**

- **Arrival:** 8:45 AM.
    
- **Operating Hours:** 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
    
- **Departure:** 5:15 PM.
    
- **Total Elapsed Time:** 8.5 hours.
    
- **Paid Time:** 8.0 hours (after her 30-minute unpaid lunch break).
    

By working exactly five shifts a week, Valerie hits exactly 40.0 paid hours, maximizing her municipal paycheck without triggering the city's strict overtime restrictions.

## The Rotating Days Off

Valerie is guaranteed two days off per week.

- **The Fixed Day:** Sundays are a guaranteed day off, as the entire Seven Hills Public Library System is closed to the public.
    
- **The Fluctuating Day:** Her second day off is entirely at the mercy of the city's scheduling needs. The Lead Circulation Supervisor posts the schedule bi-weekly, meaning Valerie's second day off constantly rotates.
    

## The Impact on the Latchkey Protocol

Because Valerie's second day off fluctuates, the triplets' weekly routine in the Big Island cabin must adapt to two distinct logistical scenarios:

**Scenario A: The Mid-Week Off Day (e.g., Tuesday off)**

- Valerie works Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday.
    
- _The Triplets' Week:_ For four days out of the school week, the triplets execute the Latchkey Protocol, arriving at the cabin at 3:30 PM and doing their homework alone until Valerie gets home around 7:00 PM.
    
- _The Weekend Excursion:_ Because Valerie works Saturday, the triplets ride into Lynchburg with her for the day, spending their Saturday reading in the library's non-fiction section.
    

**Scenario B: The Golden Weekend (Saturday off)**

- Valerie is scheduled Monday through Friday.
    
- _The Triplets' Week:_ They execute the Latchkey Protocol all five days of the school week.
    
- _The Weekend Relief:_ Valerie gets a true, two-day weekend (Saturday and Sunday) completely off. On these rare weekends, the family does not travel into Lynchburg at all, giving Valerie 48 continuous hours to rest, manage the cabin, and be fully present with Lyra, Orion, and Celeste.

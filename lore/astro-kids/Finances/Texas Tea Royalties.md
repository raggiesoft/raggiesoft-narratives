# Lore: The Monthly Royalties (The Texas Tea)

**Tags:** `#finances` `#texas_oil` `#sovtex_energy` `#passive_income` `#timeline_2004`

## Overview

The $2.4 billion the family received from the Texas Comptroller was the historical backlog—the compounding sum of decades of unpaid oil revenue. However, the great-grandfather's original lease guarantees them a perpetual **1/8th (12.5%) royalty** on all ongoing production from that land.

Because SovTex Energy is still actively pumping crude from those massive Permian Basin tracts, the family receives a direct deposit on the first of every month.

## The Math (The 2004 Baseline)

Oil royalties are tied directly to production volume and the fluctuating price of West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude.

- **The Volume:** The great-grandfather’s scrubland sits atop a highly pressurized, multi-well unit in the Permian Basin. SovTex Energy pumps roughly 20,000 barrels of crude oil per day from this specific tract.
    
- **The 2004 Price:** In the year 2004, WTI crude oil averaged around **$40 per barrel**.
    
- **The Gross:** 20,000 barrels x 30 days = 600,000 barrels a month. At $40 a barrel, the tract generates about $24,000,000 in gross monthly revenue for SovTex Energy.
    
- **The Family's Cut:** Taking their 1/8th (12.5%) share of that gross revenue, plus fractional royalties from natural gas capture, the family's royalty check comes out to roughly **$3.5 to $4 million per month**.
    

## The Four-Way Split

By the time the money hits the bank, the Dallas law firm and the family's wealth managers have already siphoned off the necessary taxes and accounting fees.

The remaining net monthly income is split equally four ways, per the probate judge's ruling. This means Valerie, Lyra, Orion, and Celeste each receive approximately **$750,000 to $1,000,000 per month** in pure, passive liquid cash.

> **The Reality of Passive Wealth:** The triplets and Valerie do not have to manage oil rigs, attend board meetings, or talk to SovTex Energy executives. They do absolutely nothing. The oil comes out of the ground in Texas, and $4 million simply appears in their trust accounts thirty days later.

## The Fluctuation Factor

Because they are tied to a commodity market, their monthly allowance is inherently volatile—but given their $2.4 billion safety net, this volatility doesn't hurt them.

- **When Prices Dip:** If global oil prices slump and crude drops to $25 a barrel, their monthly check might shrink to $2 million. It is a massive drop on paper, but practically invisible to their daily lifestyle.
    
- **The 2008 Foreshadowing:** In 2004, they are making ~$4 million a month. But in the summer of 2008, the real-world price of WTI crude will historically skyrocket to over **$140 per barrel**. During that massive boom, the family's monthly royalty checks will violently spike to over **$12 million to $14 million a month**, accelerating the expansion of their architectural utopia.

# Lore: The Deadname Catalyst (Choosing "Starling")

**Tags:** `#characters` `#backstory` `#legal` `#the_triplets` `#timeline_2001_2002` `#worldbuilding`

## The 757 Triggers

During the grueling 2001–2003 divorce proceedings, the TTY machine at the Big Island cabin was a constant source of anxiety. Every time a 757 area code registered and the thermal printer engaged, the triplets knew a Virginia Beach attorney was on the line.

Because the parents were fighting a scorched-earth legal battle over custody and assets, their lawyers demanded strict adherence to the legal paperwork. When they called, they always insisted on speaking to Valerie, explicitly deadnaming the triplets by demanding to discuss "Emily, Chris, and Sarah".

For the triplets, seeing those common 1980s birth names spool out in harsh black ink on the thermal paper felt like a physical violation. Those names belonged to the toxic Virginia Beach household. They belonged to the people who forced them into separate locker rooms, screamed at each other over the dinner table, and ultimately abandoned them.

## The Murmuration (Choosing the Surname)

It was during their Junior year in late 2001, huddled around the heavy wooden table in the cabin during their latchkey hours, that they decided they would not carry their parents' generic surname—Johnson—into adulthood.

They needed a family name that accurately reflected their reality. They didn't view themselves as three separate siblings; they functioned flawlessly as a single, interdependent unit.

While watching the winter birds descend on the Blue Ridge Mountains, Lyra observed a massive flock of starlings. Starlings fly in a "murmuration"—a highly complex, synchronized biological phenomenon where hundreds of birds move seamlessly as a single, fluid entity without ever colliding, communicating entirely through subtle spatial and auditory cues.

It was the perfect metaphor for their existence. They adopted **Starling** as their new shared last name.

## The Astronomical Shift (First and Middle Names)

With the surname chosen, they turned to their given names. They had always felt a deep connection to the astronomy-inspired middle names their parents had given them at birth.

- **The Elevation:** They decided to drop their birth first names entirely and elevate their middle names—**Lyra**, **Orion**, and **Celeste**—to their official first names.
    
- **The New Middle Names:** Because they were promoting their middle names, they needed to select new ones that anchored their specific, individual identities within the _Cassini_ universe they were actively building on their legal pads.
    
    - Lyra chose **Carina**, after the constellation containing the second-brightest star, reflecting her hyper-vigilance and navigational focus.
        
    - Orion chose **Rigel**, the blue supergiant, perfectly anchoring his passion for deep-space physics and his role as the trio's core.
        
    - Celeste chose **Callisto**, the icy moon, tying directly into her obsession with atmospheric acoustics and ocean worlds.
        

## The 18th Birthday Blueprint

This decision wasn't made on a whim. The triplets spent nearly two years quietly referring to each other as the Starlings. They drafted the exact spelling and legal formatting of their new names on their legal pads, long before they had the legal authority to execute the change.

By the time the autumn of 2003 arrived and the family court judge officially struck the custody motions from the docket, the triplets were completely prepared. The very first act of their newly granted legal adulthood was not a celebration; it was handing Valerie the finalized paperwork to submit to the Virginia court, permanently erasing Sarah, Chris, Emily, and the Johnson name from existence.

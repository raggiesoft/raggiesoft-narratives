# Lore: The Logistics of Leaving the Cabin

**Tags:** `#locations` `#timeline_2003` `#routine` `#glasgow_va` `#lynchburg_va` `#valley_pride_market`

## Overview

Leaving the Big Island Cabin was never a casual undertaking. Due to the isolation of the Blue Ridge Mountains and the triplets' complex mobility needs (requiring the physical exhaustion of folding Lyra's wheelchair and safely securing Orion into their beat-up van), Valerie categorized every excursion into two distinct tiers: "Going Into Town" and "The Big Trips into The City."

## Tier 1: Going Into Town (Glasgow, VA)

Glasgow is a small, quiet town in Rockbridge County, located about a 15-to-20-minute drive north along the winding James River via Route 501.

- **The Routine:** This was their standard, weekly excursion. The short drive meant Valerie didn't have to worry about Orion sitting in the bumpy van for too long.
    
- **The Public Library:** Glasgow possessed a modest branch of the public library. Because the cabin had no internet, this brick building was Valerie's only connection to the outside world. It was here that she used the beige Quantum OS 98 computers to research Medicaid waivers, print legal forms, and eventually, where she discovered the Unclaimed Property portals.
    
- **Agency & Scenery:** Valerie fiercely refused to keep the triplets locked away. Going into Glasgow gave them a crucial change of scenery. Lyra and Celeste loved wheeling through the library aisles, and at the tiny local grocery store, Valerie would let them use their non-verbal cues to pick out their own snacks, giving them a vital sense of autonomy.
    

## Tier 2: The Big Trips into The City (Lynchburg & Roanoke)

For anything beyond basic survival staples, Valerie had to drive to a proper city. Lynchburg was roughly 18 miles southeast, while Roanoke was a longer drive to the west. These trips were grueling, all-day affairs that required meticulous planning, a full tank of gas, and careful timing around Orion’s liquid feeding schedule.

- **Roanoke (The Medical Hub):** Roanoke was strictly reserved for medical emergencies or visits to specialized durable medical equipment (DME) providers. If Orion's chair needed a structural repair that duct tape couldn't fix, or if they needed to see a specialist, they made the long drive to Roanoke.
    
- **Lynchburg (The "Luxury" Run):** Lynchburg was their closer commercial hub. If Valerie somehow managed to save a little extra cash from an odd job, she would load up the van for a "Big Trip" to Lynchburg to visit **Valley Pride Market**—a massive, well-stocked regional grocery chain.
    
- **The Valley Pride Haul:** Valley Pride Market was a sensory adventure. It had pristine, wide aisles that could comfortably fit both Lyra and Orion's wheelchairs side-by-side. Valerie would buy bulk cases of Orion’s expensive nutritional shakes and allow the sisters to pick out "luxury" items—like fresh fruit, premium instant ramen, or name-brand sensory-safe yogurt tubes—that the tiny Glasgow store never carried.
    

## The Financial Shift (2004)

Once the $25 million bridge loan cleared, the nature of these trips completely inverted.

They never had to return to the Glasgow library computers. When they went to Lynchburg, they didn't just go to Valley Pride Market to check the price tags on yogurt; they bought a brand-new, top-of-the-line customized wheelchair transport van with a motorized hydraulic lift, hired a private security driver, and began taking meetings with high-end architectural firms to design their sprawling, accessible utopia.

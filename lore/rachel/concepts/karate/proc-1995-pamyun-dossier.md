*   **The Initial Scope:** Prior to Friday, August 18th, Master April was actively drafting a standard disciplinary report regarding Curtis Maddox's insubordination and Murye (Disrespect) during his failed 3rd Dan examination.
*   **The Paradigm Shift:** Following a phone call from Master Ray Shackford (who immediately called April after speaking with Megan and Drew's mother on Friday morning) and a call from Jessica, April visits the trauma bay and pediatric ICU at the Children's Hospital. The scope of the investigation immediately shatters. Curtis Maddox—an adult student only recently hired onto Tidewater Tumblers' junior teaching rotation as part of preparing for his 3rd Dan test—is reclassified from an insubordinate instructor to a violent predator.
*   **Institutional Accountability:** While Master Ray Shackford immediately cooperated with police, ordered Megan to lock all facility records in the safe, offered to cover 100% of Drew's emergency room bills, and testified against Curtis in court, April’s dossier also documents the institutional supervisory failure that allowed Curtis to run the Thursday floor unsupervised.

*   **The Dossier:** April scraps the initial disciplinary report. She drafts a devastating prosecution of Curtis Maddox alongside Ray Shackford's formal self-report of institutional accountability.
*   **The Evidence:** She compiles the medical evidence and translates the local Chesapeake Police reports into formal Korean.
*   **The Recommendation:** As a Sunhoe Sabomnim, April does not have the unilateral authority to erase a practitioner's lineage. Instead, she formally petitions the Grandmasters to permanently strip Curtis Maddox of his ranks and titles and completely excommunicate him from the Art (*Pamyun*), while imposing formal institutional sanctions on Master Ray Shackford and retiring the Tidewater Tumblers charter.

*   **The Valise Diplomatique:** To ensure the dossier cannot be intercepted, legally subpoenaed, or tampered with by the American legal system or Curtis Maddox's defense lawyers, April seals the petition inside her Diplomatic Pouch. 
*   **The Protection:** Under international law, this pouch cannot be opened, x-rayed, detained, or searched by TSA, U.S. Customs, or any law enforcement agency.

*   **The Review:** For three months, the *Choego Pyeong-uihoe* (Supreme Council) in Seoul reviews April's devastating dossier, the medical evidence, and the translated police reports.
*   **The Unanimous Consensus:** In November 1995, the Grandmasters reach a unanimous decision, agreeing entirely with April's recommendation.
*   **The Erasure:** While Curtis Maddox sits in a concrete cell in the Chesapeake City Jail awaiting criminal trial, the Seonduwon drops the hammer. A heavy black line is drawn through Curtis Maddox's name in the international registry, officially and permanently erasing him from the martial arts world (*Pamyun*), while Master Ray Shackford honorably accepts formal sanctions from the Grandmasters and closes Tidewater Tumblers forever.

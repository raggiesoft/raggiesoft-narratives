# Brainstorming: The Defusing of the Incest Rumor

If Mary McCullough’s nuclear-level rumor about Michael and Jessica is going to be dismantled, she has to slip up and try to leverage the gossip with the absolute wrong person. 

Here are three different character angles for who Mary tells, and how that person systematically destroys the rumor (and Mary's social standing along with it).

## Option 1: Ashley Raybourn (The Sentinel's Wrath)
**The Setup:** Mary, unaware of how deep the trauma-bond goes, assumes Ashley is just another freshman band geek. She approaches Ashley in the locker room or cafeteria, eager to share her "disgusting" discovery about Michael to isolate him further.
**The Takedown:** Ashley doesn't cry or scream. She is Michael's primary sentinel. She lets Mary finish her twisted story, and then loudly, with surgical precision, explains exactly *why* they sleep on the sofa—because Jessica's spine was crushed and she cannot use the stairs, and the siblings are terrified she will wake up needing help.
**The Burn:** Ashley makes sure everyone in earshot hears the truth. She frames Mary not just as a liar, but as a predator who is mocking a disabled girl's trauma just because she got rejected. The rumor doesn't die instantly, but Mary's social capital takes a massive, permanent hit.

## Option 2: Jessica Ragsdale (The Senior Interception)
**The Setup:** High school gossip bleeds upward. Mary tells an older sibling of a friend, and the rumor reaches the Senior Class. Someone casually brings it up in front of Jessica, who has just returned to school in her manual wheelchair and spends her time in the band room sanctuary.
**The Takedown:** Jessica doesn't get emotional; she gets tactical. She wheels herself directly down into the freshman hallway during passing period—a territory a senior usually never visits. She corners Mary McCullough in front of her entire clique.
**The Burn:** Using the exact same terrifying, uncompromising logic she will later use in the Quantum OS Boardroom, Jessica clinically dissects Mary. She publicly states the logistical reality of her paralysis, the sofa bed, and how deeply pathetic Mary must be to invent a sexual fantasy out of a family's trauma simply because Michael wouldn't sleep with her. It is a chilling, quiet dismantling that terrifies the freshmen into silence. 

## Option 3: A Teacher / The Band Director
**The Setup:** The rumor gets so loud that it hits the faculty radar. A teacher or the Band Director (who knows the Ragsdale family well and knows about Jessica's devastating Black Friday fall) overhears Mary gossiping in the hallway.
**The Takedown:** The teacher immediately pulls Mary into the office. Instead of standard disciplinary action, the teacher forces Mary to confront the reality of the trauma she is weaponizing. 
**The Burn:** The teacher explains the Black Friday drop, the resulting paralysis, and the family's coping mechanisms. They make Mary realize the horrifying weight of what she's mocking. The teacher forces Mary to publicly retract the statement to her clique under threat of suspension for extreme harassment/bullying of a disabled student.

---

### How the Rumor Dies Slowly:
Because it's a high school in the year 2000, the truth doesn't overwrite the lie overnight. 
- The immediate confrontation stops the active spread.
- But the "stain" of the rumor lingers. Michael still feels the stares for the rest of the year. 
- It forces Michael to completely retreat from the general population and strictly limit his interactions to the Band Room (where the truth is known and respected) and his immediate pack.

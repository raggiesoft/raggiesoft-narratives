# Lore Bible: The Brigadoon Sabotage (The Mary McCullough Hack)

**Timestamp:** January 2000 (Freshman Year) **Location:** Brigadoon neighborhood / Northwood High School / Ocean Lakes High School 
**The Catalyst:** Mary McCullough, a Northwood High School student who lives in the Brigadoon neighborhood near Bonnie.

**The Background:** Michael and Bonnie originally met and dated during their 8th-grade year in the Morrison Middle School percussion section. However, for 9th grade, they were separated: Bonnie was accepted into the prestigious Magnet Program at Ocean Lakes High School, while Michael funneled into his neighborhood school, Northwood High School. The geographical separation made their relationship vulnerable to outside interference.

**The Event:** Mary McCullough attends Northwood High School alongside Michael, Jessica, Ashley, and Janette. Despite being popular and capable of getting any guy she wants, Mary sets her sights on Michael, driven by a highly calculated desire to sleep with him. Knowing that Michael is fiercely loyal to Bonnie, Mary leverages her neighborhood proximity to Bonnie in Brigadoon to orchestrate a devastating social engineering hack. 

Mary plants a completely fabricated, highly pervasive rumor. She tells Bonnie—and subsequently the rest of the social circle—that Michael and Bonnie have been highly sexually active. Mary frames the lie aggressively, insisting that Bonnie "must have scored" and ensuring Bonnie knows that Mary has told *everyone* about it. 

In reality, as Ashley, Rachel, Jessica, and Janette can fiercely attest, Michael was entirely inexperienced, completely asexual in his behavior at the time, and had never bragged to anyone about wanting intimacy from anybody.

**The Fallout:** Bonnie, operating on standard-hardware social anxiety and isolated at Ocean Lakes, believes Mary's lie without verifying the data. Horrified and deeply embarrassed by the sudden toxic spotlight, Bonnie immediately breaks up with Michael. She accuses him of starting the rumor to act tough and macho.

**The Confrontation & The Nuclear Escalation:** With Bonnie out of the picture, Mary corners Michael at Northwood High School and drops the pretense, explicitly telling him, "I want to sleep with you." Expecting a flustered teenage reaction, Mary instead crashes headfirst into Michael's undiagnosed autistic processor. Michael parses "sleep with" with absolute logistical literalism. His brain pulls up the physical geometry of the Delaney Street living room—where he, Jessica, and Rachel sleep together on the sofa bed every night to maintain the perimeter following Jessica's paralyzing fall. 

Deadpan and genuinely confused, Michael replies: *"Why would you want to do that? There's no room. I already sleep with Jessica and Rachel every night."*

Mary, operating entirely on standard high school toxic logic, recoils in horror. She equates "sleep with" to having sex, knows Jessica is his disabled senior sister, and doesn't even know who Rachel is. She immediately weaponizes his innocent, literal logistical answer, turning the rumor nuclear: she tells the entire Northwood High School ecosystem that Michael Ragsdale is committing incest with his crippled sister.

**The Senior Class Retaliation:** Mary's nuclear rumor eventually bleeds upward into the Senior Class, which proves to be her fatal miscalculation. The seniors all know who Jessica Ragsdale is, they know the devastating reality of the Black Friday drop, and they know the exact reason the siblings share a pull-out sofa bed: so Jessica doesn't have to be alone if she wakes up in the middle of the night needing help. 

When the seniors catch wind that a popular freshman is weaponizing their paralyzed classmate's trauma to score cheap social points over a boy, they absolutely rip Mary a new one. The retaliation is brutal and systematic. The Senior Class ruthlessly dismantles the rumor, exposing Mary not just as a liar, but as a malicious predator mocking a disabled girl. Mary's social capital takes a massive, permanent hit. However, because teenage gossip spreads like wildfire and dies slowly, the stain of the rumor lingers in the freshman halls, leaving permanent psychological scars on Michael.

## The Psychological Impact on Michael:

1.  **The Lockdown:** Michael’s processor completely rejects the chaos. He spoke an absolute, innocent truth about a trauma-bonded coping mechanism, and the standard-hardware world twisted it into something sick and depraved, turning him into a social pariah. He goes into immediate, permanent emotional lockdown. The logical solution is to never open up again.

2.  **The Pack Consolidation (The Ashley Protocol Genesis):** This catastrophic betrayal aggressively reinforces his reliance on Ashley and his sisters. They are the only women who understand the mechanics of his life without twisting them into something disgusting. He permanently locks the gates, seeding the absolute foundation of the Ashley Protocol. The Base Camp trio becomes his sole source of female interaction.

3.  **The Pre-Conditioning for October:** This January breakup leaves his nervous system incredibly fragile regarding romance. It perfectly sets the stage for the absolute disaster that will happen in October 2000 with Ashley Wiswell. By the time Mr. Wiswell corners him, Michael's brain has already logged romance as "unsafe." The terrifying encounter with the father just upgrades it to "lethal."

# Lore Bible: The Golden Summer & The Sudden Drop

**Timestamp:** May 2004 – September 3, 2004

**Location:** Norfolk, VA (Michael’s Studio Apartment) / CCHR Roper Performing Arts Center

**Subject:** The Unclouded Baseline & The Arrival of Delores

• **The Narrative Rule of Summer 2004:** The Summer of the Sentinel is an era of absolute, unclouded peace. There are no ominous musical cues, no subtle red flags, and absolutely zero foreshadowing of the darkness to come. The narrative focus is entirely on Michael's triumph in adult independence, his mastery of his executive functioning, and the joyous, stress-free exploration of platonic sexual intimacy with Ashley.

• **The Departure:** In late August, Ashley returns to Shenandoah Valley University. The tone shifts to a quiet, solitary peace. Michael is alone, but he is stable, confident, and comfortable in his studio apartment. The perimeter is quiet.

• **The Sudden Drop (September 3, 2004):** Michael attends a screening of Boiling Point: 9/11 at the CCHR Roper Performing Arts Center in downtown Norfolk. The audience has no warning. Delores enters the narrative abruptly, stepping directly into the physical space next to Michael in the theater.

• **The Immediate Shift:** Her arrival is abrupt but initially presents as a positive, exciting new variable. Because the audience has been lulled into the absolute safety of the Summer of 2004, her sudden appearance reads as the next logical, happy step in Michael's adult journey—a new girl to practice his newfound confidence with. The trap is sprung before anyone, including the reader, realizes the jaws have snapped shut.

# Lore Bible: The Rehabilitation Protocol (Kristin)

**Timestamp:** Late 2006 / Early 2007 **Location:** Washington State (The Forward Operating Base) **Subject:** Reclaiming the Intimacy Baseline & The Sentinel's Healing

**1. The Corrupted Baseline (The Aftermath of Kent)** When Michael arrives in Washington, his physical boundaries have been catastrophically shattered by Delores and her cousin Brandi. The female body, which was once a source of comfort and safety (established by Ashley in 2004), has been violently reprogrammed in his processor to represent degradation, control, and predatory danger. Michael cannot even endure casual proximity to non-family females without his threat-detection system hitting Redline.

**2. The New Sentinel (Kristin’s Integration)** Kristin, age 20, is the exact same age as Rachel. As Rachel’s best friend and roommate, she is fundamentally integrated into the pack's daily operations. Crucially, she is *not* biologically related to Michael. She becomes the perfect, safe variable to bridge the gap between his family perimeter and the civilian world.

**3. The Healing Protocol (The Safe Encounter)** Once Michael's nervous system has stabilized in Washington, Kristin steps in to execute the ultimate therapeutic override. She provides Michael with his first sexual encounter following the abuse.

- **The Intent:** This is not a neurotypical romantic pursuit. It is a highly evolved, trauma-informed intervention. Kristin provides absolute, string-free consent, moving at his exact pace.

- **The Reclamation:** By engaging in physical intimacy with a non-relative who demands nothing, controls nothing, and actively protects his sister, Michael's processor is finally able to overwrite the trauma of Kent. Kristin de-weaponizes physical touch.

**4. The Ultimate Spite (Delores's Nightmare)** This dynamic serves as the ultimate narrative middle finger to the abuser. Delores’s most unhinged, toxic paranoia was that Michael was engaging in inappropriate intimacy with the women in his life. By having Kristin—a gorgeous, fiercely loyal, unrelated woman—actually fulfill that physical role in a perfectly healthy, platonic, and protective way, Michael actively lives out Delores's worst nightmare, stripping her jealousy of any lingering power.

**5. The Blessing of the Bug** Rachel fully understands and blesses this dynamic. Because Rachel knows Michael's processor, she knows he desperately needs physical touch to regulate, but cannot survive the chaotic variables of the neurotypical dating scene right now. Having her best friend safely fulfill that need keeps the pack's perimeter completely impenetrable.

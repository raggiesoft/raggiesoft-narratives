# Lore Bible: The Shared Personal Bubble

- **Timestamp:** Childhood – Permanent

- **Subject:** The Physics of the Pack Perimeter & Spatial Consolidation

- **The Concept:** While standard society views physical space as an individual, highly guarded commodity, Michael and Rachel operate on a combined spatial algorithm. They do not possess individual bubbles when they are together; they exist within a "shared personal bubble."

- **The Mechanics:** When the environment demands too much processing power, they physically merge their space to survive it. Cuddling, heavy pack-hugs, and stomach-to-stomach compression serve as the structural walls of this bubble. The intense, continuous physical contact manually forces their racing heart rates to decelerate, blocking out the unpredictable data of the outside world.

- **The Exclusion Rule:** This shared bubble is an absolute, high-clearance sanctuary. It is impenetrable to outsiders, casual acquaintances, and even the adults who raised them. It is the ultimate proof that within the exact physical dimensions of each other's arms, they are entirely, structurally safe.

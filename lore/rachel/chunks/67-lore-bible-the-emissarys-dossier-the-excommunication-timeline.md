# Lore Bible: The Emissary’s Dossier & The Excommunication Timeline

## 1. Saturday (The Initial Infraction)

- **The Incident:** Master Scott formally yields the floor to April during the regional testing. Curtis, blinded by his own misogynistic ego, openly challenges her authority, argues the criteria of the *Art*, and attempts to elevate himself by diminishing his students.

- **The Verdict:** April issues a definitive "Fail," denying his 3rd Dan promotion and leaving him permanently stuck at 2nd Dan.

- **The Initial Dossier:** As a *Sunhoe Sabomnim*, April takes this insubordination incredibly seriously. Following the test, she begins compiling a formal dossier to send to the Seonduwon in Seoul. At this stage, her report is purely disciplinary—she is petitioning to have Curtis penalized or suspended for violating the sacred hierarchy and demonstrating a toxic, ego-driven philosophy.

## 2. Thursday (The Blind Spot)

- **The Assault:** Curtis weaponizes the Dojang, closing the blinds and deploying the Vengeful Variant and Level 3 Crush on Michael, Rachel, and Drew.

- **The Disconnect:** April is completely unaware of the atrocity. While the children are being rushed to emergency rooms and pediatric ICUs, April is likely at home or in her own academy, still methodically drafting her initial disciplinary report regarding Curtis’s behavior from the previous weekend.

## 3. Friday (The Paradigm Shift)

- **The Missing Anchors:** Michael and Rachel—who are relentlessly punctual and thrive on routine—fail to show up for her Friday Open Gym. Drew is also missing.

- **The Phone Calls:** As soon as Master Ray Shackford gets off the phone with Megan and Drew's mother Claire on Friday morning, he immediately calls April off-camera and tells her everything Megan and Claire just reported to him. Moments later, April also speaks with Jessica at the Delaney Street house, who delivers the full reality of the assault on Michael and Rachel.

- **The Hospital Visit:** April strips off her *dobok* and rushes to the Children's Hospital. She visits Michael and Rachel in the trauma bay, bowing to them and hearing firsthand how Curtis weaponized Rachel's voice and Michael's weight. She then visits the pediatric ICU to see six-year-old Drew, who just had his spleen surgically removed.

## 4. The Pivot (The Upgraded Petition)

- **The Realization:** Standing in the hospital, the scope of her investigation completely shatters. Curtis Maddox—an adult student she and Ray had taught since 1988, who had begun his Junior Instructor rotation just before his 2nd Dan test at Tidewater Tumblers' Virginia Beach location and had only recently transferred to the Chesapeake Thursday rotation ahead of his 3rd Dan exam—isn't just an insubordinate, arrogant instructor; he is a violent predator.

- **Master Ray's Accountability & Why the Background Check Was Clean:** When Curtis entered the Junior Instructor program ahead of his 2nd Dan test in 1993, Master Ray Shackford had run a standard Virginia State Police criminal background check, which came back 100% clean because Curtis's offsite *Iron Shirt Underground* consisted exclusively of consenting adult Red and Black belts bound by the "First Rule" of silence. Horrified when Megan and Drew's mother Claire tell him what Curtis did, Ray immediately offers to pay 100% of Drew's emergency room costs, orders Megan to lock every record in the safe for the police, calls April off-camera to report everything, and testifies against Curtis in court. However, Ray also acknowledges his institutional failure as *Kwanjangnim*: trusting Curtis's clean background check and six-year student history so completely that he left a 2nd Dan junior instructor unsupervised with young children on Thursdays behind closable lobby blinds.

- **The New Dossier:** April scraps the initial disciplinary report. She drafts a comprehensive petition to the Grandmasters: she formally petitions to permanently strip Curtis Maddox of his rank and completely excommunicate him from the *Art* (*Pamyun*), while recommending formal institutional censure (*Gyun-chaek*) and permanent charter/teaching license revocation (*Kwanjangnim Gwonhan Park-tal*) for Master Ray Shackford and the permanent retirement of Tidewater Tumblers' charter.

## 5. November (The Verdict)

- **The Deliberation:** For three months, the *Choego Pyeong-uihoe* (Supreme Council) in Seoul reviews April's devastating dossier, the medical evidence, and the translated police reports.

- **The Unanimous Consensus:** In November, the Grandmasters reach a unanimous decision. They agree entirely with April's recommendation.

- **The Excommunication & Institutional Sanctions:** While Curtis Maddox sits in a concrete cell in the Chesapeake City Jail awaiting criminal trial, the Seonduwon drops the hammer. A heavy black line is drawn through Curtis Maddox's name in the international registry (*Pamyun*), stripping him of his *Dan* rank *ab initio* and erasing him from the martial arts world forever. Because Master Ray Shackford acted with immediate honor upon learning the truth and testified against Curtis, his name is **not** struck in black ink and he retains his personal *Dan* history; however, for leaving a 2nd Dan junior instructor unsupervised behind closed blinds, the Grandmasters permanently revoke Tidewater Tumblers' charter and Ray's headmaster/teaching license (*Kwanjangnim Gwonhan*)—a sanction Ray accepts without appeal, vowing never to operate a *Dojang* again.

# Lore Bible: The Reunion Circuit & The Summer of the Sentinel

**Timestamp:** November 2003 – September 2004

**Location:** Virginia Beach, VA; Harrisonburg, VA (SVU); Seattle, WA (SEA); Norfolk, VA (CCHR)

**Subject:** The Intimacy Baseline & The Introduction of the Abuser

**Phase 1: The Reunion Circuit (Late 2003 – Spring 2004)**

Following the emotional devastation of Ashley leaving for Shenandoah Valley University, the pack relies on highly anticipated reunions to maintain the perimeter.

• **Thanksgiving 2003 (Virginia Beach):** The establishment of the physical intimacy baseline. Ashley and Michael cross the boundary into adult romantic and sexual contact in the absolute safety of her childhood bedroom.

• **Christmas 2003 (Seattle, WA):** The entire pack is physically reunited. Michael and Ashley fly out of Norfolk to SEA. For Michael, the travel logistics are a known variable (having flown to PDX in 2001 when Rachel was across the river in Vancouver, WA). For Ashley, this is her first time in the Pacific Northwest. The trip reinforces their bond—they navigate the stress of air travel and holiday crowds as a seamless, functioning unit.

• **Spring Break 2004:** A final, grounding mid-semester anchor before the ultimate summer deployment.

**Phase 2: The Summer of the Sentinel (Summer 2004)**

This is the golden era of Michael’s early adulthood. It is a defined, temporary, but profoundly impactful season of domestic and romantic peace.

• **The Logistics:** Ashley officially moves into Michael’s studio apartment in Virginia Beach. There is no sneaking around; she is on the paperwork, and the landlord is fully aware. Michael is successfully managing his own adult space, and Ashley is his welcomed co-pilot.

• **The "Girlfriend" Protocol:** For three months, Ashley occupies every single role: Sentinel, Best Friend, Honorary Sister, and actively, his Girlfriend. They share a bed, share meals, and engage in regular sexual and romantic intimacy.

• **The Healthy Boundary:** This is not a traditional, neurotypical "dating with the intent to marry" setup. It is a highly evolved, trauma-informed contract. They both know the romantic protocol has a hard expiration date. When Fall 2004 arrives, she will return to SVU, and they will revert to their core Pack dynamic.

• **The Psychological Armor:** Michael experiences what it is like to live with a romantic partner without fear, screaming, or manipulation. His processor compiles a massive cache of positive data regarding cohabitation and consent.

**Phase 3: The Void and The Abuser (Fall 2004)**

When Ashley returns to SVU in late August, the silence in the Virginia Beach studio apartment is deafening. Michael’s perimeter is secure, but his daily operational capacity is suddenly missing its primary support node. He is primed for connection, making him vulnerable.

• **The Date:** September 3, 2004.

• **The Location:** The Community College of Hampton Roads (CCHR) Norfolk Campus, specifically at the historic downtown theater venue (The Commodore Performing Arts Center).

• **The Catalyst:** Michael attends a screening of Celsius 9/11 (the highly controversial, zeitgeist-dominating political documentary of 2004).

• **The Introduction:** This is where he meets Delores. She steps directly into the geographic and emotional void left by Ashley’s departure. Delores will likely present herself as a safe, stabilizing force initially, mirroring the companionship Michael is craving, before slowly initiating her two-year cycle of abuse and isolation.

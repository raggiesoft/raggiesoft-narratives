# Lore Bible: The Intimacy Axiom (Rachel’s Directive)

- **Timestamp:** April 2007 – Permanent

- **Subject:** Rachel’s Relational Boundaries & Trauma-Informed Logic

- **The Analytical Conclusion:** Rachel’s neurodivergent processor has compiled the empirical data regarding romantic and sexual intimacy, and the failure rate is 100%.

  - *Data Point A:* Her parents’ divorce (the destruction of the original family unit).

  - *Data Point B:* Delores (the weaponization of intimacy resulting in severe trauma to her primary anchor, Michael).

  - *Data Point C:* David Lawson (the use of marriage to enact coercive control and physical battery against her Sentinel, Ashley).

- **The Directive:** Based on this data, Rachel concludes that the risk-to-reward ratio of sexual and romantic intimacy is completely unviable. While she possesses the biological capacity for intimacy, she makes the absolute, calculated choice that it is permanently off the table for her.

- **The Pack Dynamic & Michael's Four Favorite Women:** Michael's adult universe inside the Moosewood Drive fortress revolves around the four favorite women in his life: **Rachel**, **Jessica**, **Ashley**, and **Kristin**—with **Rachel** remaining, now and always, his number-one favorite woman in the world and his lifelong partner in daily operations and sensory grounding. Because Michael's literal, harm-based autistic processor evaluates morality strictly through consent versus coercion (*"why does the outside world treat rules as sacred when every medical and logistical baseline is managed and we aren't hurting anyone?"*), **Rachel's Intimacy Axiom** (alongside their biological sibling boundary and Jessica's executive sister role) serves as the immovable **in-universe law** that governs the household. Because Rachel has completely opted out of the romantic and sexual sphere by her own calculated directive, she and Michael never cross that line, and she experiences zero jealousy regarding Michael's romantic life.

- **The Dual Sentinels' Role (Ashley & Kristin — Zero Jealousy):** This logic makes Rachel (alongside Jessica) the fiercest advocate for Michael's consensual romantic and intimate relationships with **both Ashley and Kristin**. Both Ashley and Kristin are verified, trusted Sentinels within the pack's five-person perimeter; both know about and fully support each other's intimate relationship with Michael without a shred of jealousy or competition; and Rachel actively *wants* Ashley and Kristin to fulfill that role because it keeps the pack's ecosystem entirely closed, safe, and uncontaminated by outsiders like Delores.

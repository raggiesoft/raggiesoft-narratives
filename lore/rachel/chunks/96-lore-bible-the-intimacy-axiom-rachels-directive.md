# Lore Bible: The Intimacy Axiom (Rachel’s Directive)

- **Timestamp:** April 2007 – Permanent

- **Subject:** Rachel’s Relational Boundaries & Trauma-Informed Logic

- **The Analytical Conclusion:** Rachel’s neurodivergent processor has compiled the empirical data regarding romantic and sexual intimacy, and the failure rate is 100%.

  - *Data Point A:* Her parents’ divorce (the destruction of the original family unit).

  - *Data Point B:* Delores (the weaponization of intimacy resulting in severe trauma to her primary anchor, Michael).

  - *Data Point C:* David Lawson (the use of marriage to enact coercive control and physical battery against her Sentinel, Ashley).

- **The Directive:** Based on this data, Rachel concludes that the risk-to-reward ratio of sexual and romantic intimacy is completely unviable. While she possesses the biological capacity for intimacy, she makes the absolute, calculated choice that it is permanently off the table for her.

- **The Pack Dynamic:** Because her brother is her lifelong anchor, she accepts the biological boundary that they cannot cross that line. However, because she has completely opted out of the romantic sphere, she experiences absolutely zero jealousy regarding Michael's romantic life.

- **The Sentinel’s Role:** This logic makes Rachel the fiercest advocate for Michael and Ashley’s relationship. Ashley is a verified, safe variable. Rachel actively *wants* Ashley to fulfill Michael's need for sexual intimacy because it keeps the pack’s ecosystem entirely closed, safe, and uncontaminated by outsiders like Delores.

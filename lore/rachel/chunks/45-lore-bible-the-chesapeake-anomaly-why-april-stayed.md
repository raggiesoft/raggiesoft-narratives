# Lore Bible: The Chesapeake Anomaly (Why April Stayed)

## 1. The Global Rarity of the Silver Thread

- In the entire world, there are only twelve *Sunhoe Sabomnim*.

- In the United States, there are only three (stationed in Washington state, Hawai'i, and Virginia).

- For 99.9% of martial arts practitioners, witnessing Protocol II (an Emissary commanding a testing floor) is a once-in-a-lifetime, awe-inspiring event. It is the equivalent of a Supreme Court Justice walking into a local traffic court to preside over the docket.

## 2. The Statistical Anomaly of Tidewater Tumblers

- Because April lives just across the city line in Virginia Beach, her presence at the Chesapeake facility on Professional Place is a massive statistical anomaly.

- To Tidewater Tumblers, Protocol II isn't a rare honor; it is a quarterly routine. She regularly attends classes, runs the Friday Open Gym, and actively presides over the testing floor.

- **The Blindness of Familiarity:** This routine presence is exactly why Curtis fundamentally underestimates her. Because he has seen her walk through the doors since she was fifteen, he is completely blind to the terrifying magnitude of the credential she earned in Seoul.

## 3. The Anchor of Origin

- Why does a globally recognized Emissary spend her time in a suburban, commercialized gymnastics warehouse instead of traveling the world or exclusively running her own elite academy?

- **Loyalty to her roots.** Tidewater Tumblers is the exact Dojang where April tied on her first white *tti* at five and a half years old. Despite Master Ray’s business-first busy schedule and Curtis's recent rotation from the Virginia Beach branch to the Chesapeake floor ahead of his 3rd Dan test, the physical mat in that windowless Chesapeake warehouse is her sacred, foundational ground. She refuses to abandon the floor that raised her.

## 4. The Vow to the Vulnerable (The Shield)

- Above all else, April stays for the kids.

- As she matured into her Master ranks, she recognized the dangerous shift in Curtis's attitude once he rotated from his Junior Instructor post at the Virginia Beach branch into the Chesapeake Thursday schedule ahead of his 3rd Dan exam, while Master Ray trusted Curtis too much and left him unsupervised on Thursdays.

- April knew that if she left to exclusively run her own *Lynnhaven Academy of Seondu*, the neurodivergent and vulnerable students at Tidewater Tumblers—like Michael, Rachel, and Drew—would be left without a senior traditionalist on the Chesapeake mat when Curtis rotated in.

- She deliberately chose to maintain her presence in Chesapeake to act as their anchor. She held the line, enforcing accommodations (like letting Michael and Rachel hold hands) and validating their *Jeongsin* (Spirit) to protect them from Curtis’s rigidity.

## 5. The Tragedy of Thursday

- This reality makes the impending Thursday assault infinitely more tragic. April held the line in Chesapeake specifically to protect those exact children. On Thursday, August 17, 1995—with Master Ray off for the day and April away from the building—Curtis seizes the unsupervised blind spot and unleashes the Vengeful Variant. The devastation of Thursday isn't just an assault on the kids; it is a direct, catastrophic breach of the sanctuary the Emissary and Master Ray had built for years.

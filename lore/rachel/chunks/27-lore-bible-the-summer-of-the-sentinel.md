# Lore Bible: The Summer of the Sentinel

**Timestamp:** May 2004 – August 2004

**Location:** Harrisonburg, VA (SVU) & Norfolk, VA (Michael’s Studio Apartment)

**Subject:** The Cohabitation Practicum & The Platonic Intimacy Clause

**1. The Retrieval (May 2004)**

Like all university housing, Shenandoah Valley University requires students to vacate the dorms for the summer. Jim and Joan Raybourn, treating Michael as an extension of their own family, bring him along for the drive to Harrisonburg to collect Ashley.

• **The Significance:** Michael isn't just tagging along; he is actively participating in bringing his Sentinel home. The drive back down to the coast is a triumphant return. The perimeter is fully re-established.

**2. The Norfolk Studio (The Established Habitat)**

Rather than moving back into her childhood bedroom at 1802 Rich Court, Ashley moves directly into Michael’s studio apartment in Norfolk.

• **The Baseline:** Michael has lived in this apartment since Summer 2003. He has already conquered the executive dysfunction hurdles of living alone—paying rent, buying groceries, managing his sensory environment.

• **The Integration:** Because Michael's foundational routines are already rock-solid, his processor has the bandwidth to handle the massive new variable Ashley is introducing: adult cohabitation.

**3. The "Romantic Partner" Protocol**

This summer is a deliberate, trauma-informed "dry run" for Michael’s future. Ashley knows that navigating romance, shared domestic space, and sexual intimacy with a neurotypical stranger would be a terrifying minefield for his processor.

• **The Practicum:** For three months, Ashley intentionally steps out of the "Honorary Sister" role and occupies the "Girlfriend" role. They share the physical space, sleep in the same bed, navigate domestic compromises, and engage in full sexual intimacy.

• **The Safety Net:** Because it is Ashley, there is zero fear of judgment, mockery, or failure. She provides him with a flawless, completely safe baseline for what romantic and sexual cohabitation should feel like—built entirely on explicit communication and absolute trust.

**4. The Hard Expiration & The Future Clause**

The reason this dynamic doesn't crash Michael's system is because the boundaries are explicitly defined from day one. There is no ambiguity, which is the ultimate comfort for an autistic brain.

• **The Fall Reset:** They both openly acknowledge that when August ends, Ashley will return to SVU for her sophomore year. When she leaves, the "Girlfriend" protocol terminates. She will immediately revert to being his Sentinel, Best Friend, and Honorary Sister.

• **The Platonic Intimacy Agreement:** They establish a new, permanent sub-routine for the future. If Ashley visits him (or vice versa), the physical intimacy is still on the table, but the romantic expectations are not. She offers him access to safe, platonic sexual intimacy. It is an extension of their Base Camp survival cuddling—a way for him to experience physical connection without the terrifying neurotypical strings attached.

**5. The Tragic Blind Spot**

Throughout the summer of 2004, Michael and Ashley are building this armor with the best of intentions. They believe this practicum is preparing him for a healthy, successful dating life in the adult world. They are completely unaware that his newfound confidence and craving for companionship are about to be weaponized.

When Ashley packs her bags and heads back to Harrisonburg in late August, she leaves behind a confident young man whose perimeter is momentarily down. He is perfectly primed, completely by accident, to walk into the Celsius 9/11 screening at the CCHR Roper Performing Arts Center and meet Delores.

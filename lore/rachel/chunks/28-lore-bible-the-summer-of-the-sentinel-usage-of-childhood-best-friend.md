# Lore Bible: The Summer of the Sentinel (usage of childhood best friend)

**Timestamp:** May 2004 – August 2004

**Location:** Norfolk, VA (Michael’s Studio Apartment)

**Subject:** The Public Persona & The Delores Contrast

• **The Public Persona:** Despite engaging in full domestic and sexual intimacy behind closed doors, Ashley strictly introduces Michael in public as her "childhood best friend." This is a calculated Sentinel maneuver designed to protect Michael from confusing neurotypical social scripts and the pressure of the "relationship escalator."

• **The Intimacy Clause (Post-Summer):** When Ashley returns to Shenandoah Valley University in late August, she explicitly leaves the offer of platonic sexual affection on the table. She reassures Michael that her physical intimacy will always be available to him whenever they reunite, free of any romantic obligations or neurotypical dating rules.

• **The Narrative Contrast (Foreshadowing):** Ashley’s deliberate avoidance of the "Girlfriend" label establishes a perfectly healthy, low-pressure baseline for Michael. This is specifically designed to contrast with Delores (arriving September 3, 2004), who will immediately seize and weaponize the title of "Girlfriend" to manufacture jealousy, enforce isolation, and systematically dismantle Michael's perimeter.

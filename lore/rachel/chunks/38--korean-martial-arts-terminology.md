# 🇰🇷 Korean Martial Arts Terminology

**(The Pinnacle Mastery School Complete Glossary)**

**Titles & Etiquette**

- **Sabomnim (사범님):** Master / Head Instructor. (Though Master Curtis and Master Ray primarily use the English "Master," this remains the formal Korean title in the Pinnacle curriculum).

**Kamsahamnida (감사합니다):** Thank you. The mandatory phrase students must shout in unison at the end of class or after receiving a correction.

## Commands & Stances

- **Cha-ryeot (차렷):** Attention. (Heels together, hands at the side, absolute stillness).

- **Kyeong-rye (경례):** Bow.

- **Junbi (준비):** Ready / Prepare.

- **Junbi Sogi (준비서기):** Ready Stance.

- **Narani Junbi Sogi (나란히 준비서기):** Parallel Ready Stance (feet shoulder-width apart).

- **Juchum Sogi (주춤서기):** Horse Riding Stance (wide, squatting stance used for punching drills).

- **Gyeorugi Junbi (겨루기 준비):** Fighting Stance Ready.

- **Shi-jak (시작):** Begin / Start.

- **Ba-ro (바로):** Return to the starting position.

- **Kman (그만):** Stop.

- **Dwiro Dol-a (뒤로 돌아):** About face / Turn around.

## Strikes & Blocks

- **Ap Chagi (앞차기):** Front Kick.

- **I-dan Ap Chagi (이단 앞차기) / Ttwio Ap Chagi:** Jumping Front Kick. (The Green belt technique Michael’s CP prevents him from executing).

- **Dollyo Chagi (돌려차기):** Roundhouse Kick.

- **Yeop Chagi (옆차기):** Side Kick.

- **Dwi Chagi (뒤차기):** Back Kick.

- **Momtong Jireugi (몸통 지르기):** Middle Punch.

- **Olgul Jireugi (얼굴 지르기):** High Punch (aimed at the face/philtrum).

- **Arae Makgi (아래막기):** Low Block.

- **Olgul Makgi (얼굴막기):** High Block.

- **Sonnal Makgi (손날막기):** Knife-Hand Block.

## General Dojang Terms & Ranking

- **Kihap (기합):** Spirit shout (mandatory for grading; triggers Rachel's situational mutism).

- **Poomsae (품새) / Hyung (형):** Form / Pattern (e.g., Basic Form IV, Basic Form V).

- **Dobok (도복):** The traditional white martial arts uniform.

- **Tti (띠):** The belt.

- **Geup (급):** The color belt rank system (counting down to black belt).

- **Dan (단):** The black belt rank (counting up from 1st Degree; represented by the midnight blue *tti*).

- **Dojang (도장):** The training hall itself.

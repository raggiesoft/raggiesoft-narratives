# Michael’s Timeline: The Neurological & Soft Tissue Battle

**Average Healing Time: 6 to 8 Weeks**

For Michael, the injury is a severe soft-tissue tear (the abdominal fascia) complicated heavily by his Cerebral Palsy. Soft tissue generally heals faster than bone, but his neurological condition makes it an uphill battle.

- **The Aggravating Factor:** Dragging his heavy legs up the metal bleachers on Thursday night forced his torn fascia to bear his entire body weight. Furthermore, his CP spasticity meant his core was constantly seizing around the tear for a full 24 hours before he finally got muscle relaxants at the hospital. This prevented any initial clotting or stabilization, turning a clean tear into a ragged, inflamed mess.

- **Weeks 1–3 (Strict Immobilization):** This is the hardest phase. He is heavily medicated with muscle relaxants to stop his CP from triggering spasms. Even coughing or sitting up too fast feels like being stabbed. He is trapped on the pull-out sofa.

- **Weeks 4–6 (The Transition):** The fascia begins to knit back together. The blinding pain reduces to a deep, stiff ache. He can carefully walk around the house, slowly transitioning from "patient" to Rachel's "courier and protector."

- **Weeks 6–8 (Baseline):** He is medically cleared, though the psychological fear of tensing his stomach will last much longer.

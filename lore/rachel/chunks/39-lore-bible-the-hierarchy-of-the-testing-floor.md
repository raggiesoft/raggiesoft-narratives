# Lore Bible: The Hierarchy of the Testing Floor

Because the absolute authority of the art descends directly from the Seonduwon in Seoul, local franchise owners (like Master Ray) never have final say over Black Belt promotions. The floor must be governed by the highest-ranking representative of the *Art*. There are three distinct procedural protocols for a sanctioned test.

## Protocol I: The Regional Procedure (The Standard)

- **The Authority:** The 7th Dan Regional Director (Master Scott).

- **The Frequency:** This is the standard procedure for 99% of all testing days in North America. Master Scott travels his territory, inspecting the franchises under his corporate purview (like Pinnacle Mastery School of Karate).

- **The Execution:** Master Scott acts as the supreme authority. Master Ray and his instructors bow to Scott. Scott commands the floor, calls the *poomsae* (forms), and issues the final passes or fails.

- **Curtis's Assumption:** This is the protocol Curtis *expected* to happen on August 12th. He assumed Scott would run the floor and April would simply assist as a lower-ranking 4th Dan.

## Protocol II: The Emissary Procedure (The Rare Exception)

- **The Authority:** The *Sunhoe Sabomnim* (Master April).

- **The Frequency:** Astronomically rare. Because there are only twelve Emissaries globally (and only three in the US), most Dojangs will never experience this protocol. Tidewater Tumblers only experiences it because April happens to live locally in Virginia Beach and specifically chose to attend to support Michael, Rachel, and her neurodivergent students.

- **The Execution:** For every single color belt class (including each belt-with-stripe class), every single *Poom* candidate, and every single *Dan* candidate presented on the gymnastics spring floor throughout the day, the presiding authority in charge of the testing floor (the 7th Dan Regional Director, Master Scott) must officially yield command. Before each class or candidate begins, Master Scott steps back, formally bows to the Emissary, and announces across the silent Dojang: *"As tradition dictates, the highest authority on a testing floor is the Voice of Korea. Sunhoe Sabomnim, you hold the Gwonhan. The floor is yours to administer the examinations."* (This means the announcement is heard multiple times throughout the testing day as each color belt class, each *Poom* candidate, and each *Dan* candidate is presented.)

- **The Dynamic:** April calls the techniques and issues the final grades. Master Scott acts as the doctrinal enforcer (as seen when he intercepted Curtis’s disrespect), but Scott cannot issue a final verdict. He must defer back to April for the final word.

## Protocol III: The Grandmaster Procedure (The Pinnacle)

- **The Authority:** A sitting member of the Seonduwon (7th, 8th, 9th Dan from Seoul, or the 10th Dan himself).

- **The Frequency:** A generational event. A Grandmaster from the Seonduwon traveling to an American franchise is a massive, highly publicized cultural event involving diplomatic visas and local government recognition.

- **The Execution:** If a Grandmaster is present (such as during fifteen-year-old April's 4th *Poom* test in Spring 1988), whoever is in charge of the regional floor yields the *Gwonhan* to the highest authority present, and even a *Sunhoe Sabomnim* yields. The Grandmaster himself administers the test from the head of the floor, while Master Scott handles floor logistics—presenting each color belt class, each *Poom* candidate, and each *Dan* candidate on the gymnastics spring floor beneath the second-floor observation deck bleachers.

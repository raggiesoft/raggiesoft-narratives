# Rachel’s Timeline: The Bone & Organ Crisis

**Extreme Long End Healing Time: 12 to 16 Weeks (3 to 4 Months)**

Rachel’s Level 3 crush caused structural damage (cracked lower ribs) and solid organ trauma (a bruised spleen). Because pediatric spleens are highly vascular and prone to delayed rupture if aggravated, her timeline is dictated entirely by extreme medical caution.

- **The Aggravating Factor:** This is where the transport logistics became actively dangerous. When Janette and Jessica carried her, their hands and arms naturally wrapped around her ribcage. The pressure of being held, combined with the physical heaving of her hyperventilating sobs in the car, ground her fractured ribs together and increased the micro-bleeding in her spleen.

- **Weeks 1–4 (Absolute Bed Rest):** This is the terrifying phase. Her spleen is essentially a ticking clock. If her blood pressure spikes or she takes a hit to the abdomen, the organ capsule could rupture, requiring emergency surgery. She is not allowed to walk up or down stairs, lift anything heavier than a book, or twist her torso. She is entirely confined to the "Sofa Sanctuary." The ribs are in the acute, agonizing stage of calcifying.

- **Weeks 5–8 (The Fragile Era):** The ribs have mostly fused, reducing the sharp, stabbing pain when she breathes, but the spleen is still deeply bruised. She is allowed to walk around the first floor of the house, but she is strictly forbidden from running, jumping, or any sudden movements.

- **Weeks 9–16 (The Extreme Long End):** While the pain is largely gone, the internal swelling of the spleen can take months to fully resolve on the extreme end of recovery. For a full three to four months, Dr. Young will likely ban her from PE class, playground equipment, and any situation where she could trip and fall.

# Lore Bible: Jessica's Medical Accommodations

- **Timestamp:** Pre-November 1999 (Clear Prescription Glasses) $\rightarrow$ Post-November 1999 (Permanent Tinted Prescription Glasses)

- **Subject:** Lifelong Prescription Glasses, Axial Load TBI Photophobia, & Tinted Lenses

- **The Origin:** Jessica always needed and wore regular (clear-lens) wire-rimmed prescription glasses from childhood (`1993–1999`). Following her severe November 1999 fall from the Delaney Street portico roof—where she landed feet-first and suffered a catastrophic bilateral axial load injury—the violent kinetic shockwave transmitted up her spine also caused a secondary Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI / post-concussive syndrome). When the TBI from her axial load injury came to light during her hospitalization (first manifesting as severe pain and squinting against the unshielded LCD laptop backlight and overhead hospital fluorescents on Day 2 in Tidewater General), the neurological trauma left her with permanent chronic photophobia (severe light sensitivity).

- **The Accommodation:** Once the TBI and photophobia from her axial load injury came to light, Jessica permanently transitioned from clear prescription lenses to custom, lightly tinted prescription glasses (beginning by December 1999). To navigate harsh lighting environments—from the fluorescent glare of the Quantum corporate campus to cheap, unshielded overhead bulbs in residential houses—her tinted prescription glasses act as a vital sensory shield, preventing debilitating migraines and allowing her to maintain her sharp, analytical focus without visual fatigue.

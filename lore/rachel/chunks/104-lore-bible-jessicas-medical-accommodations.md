# Lore Bible: Jessica's Medical & Quantum Workplace Accommodations

- **Timestamp:** Pre-November 1999 (Clear Prescription Glasses) $\rightarrow$ Post-November 1999 (Permanent Tinted Prescription Glasses & Quantum Building 35 Workplace Accommodations)

- **Subject:** Lifelong Prescription Glasses, Axial Load TBI Photophobia, Tinted Lenses, & Quantum Corporation (`Building 35`) Visual/Mobility Accommodations

- **The Origin:** Jessica always needed and wore regular (clear-lens) wire-rimmed prescription glasses from childhood (`1993–1999`). Following her severe November 1999 fall from the Delaney Street portico roof—where she landed feet-first and suffered a catastrophic bilateral axial load injury—the violent kinetic shockwave transmitted up her spine also caused a secondary Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI / post-concussive syndrome). When the TBI from her axial load injury came to light during her hospitalization (first manifesting as severe pain and squinting against the unshielded LCD laptop backlight and overhead hospital fluorescents on Day 2 in Tidewater General), the neurological trauma left her with permanent chronic photophobia (severe light sensitivity).

- **Optical Accommodation:** Once the TBI and photophobia from her axial load injury came to light, Jessica permanently transitioned from clear prescription lenses to custom, lightly tinted prescription glasses (beginning by December 1999). To navigate harsh lighting environments—from the fluorescent glare of the Quantum corporate campus to cheap, unshielded overhead bulbs in residential houses—her tinted prescription glasses act as a vital sensory shield, preventing debilitating migraines and allowing her to maintain her sharp, analytical focus without visual fatigue.

- **Quantum Corporation (`Building 35`) Visual & Photophobia Accommodations:**
  - **"De-Lamped" Private Office with Warm Indirect Task Lighting:** Before Jessica moves into her private corner suite in **Building 35**, Quantum Facilities physically disconnects/removes the overhead fluorescent ballast tubes above her desk so there is zero `60Hz/120Hz` fluorescent flicker or harsh overhead glare, replacing them with flicker-free, warm-spectrum (`2700K`) indirect floor and desk task lamps on stepless dimmer switches.
  - **Blackout Shades & Frosted Sidelight Film:** Her exterior window is fitted with dual-layer solar-mesh and blackout roller shades, and the interior glass sidelight panel beside her office door is treated with frosted light-diffusing film so hallway fluorescents never stab into her line of sight.
  - **Flicker-Free Matte Monitors & High-Contrast Dark Mode:** Quantum IT provisions DC-dimmed (zero-PWM flicker) matte-finish professional monitors on articulating arms with physical anti-glare filters, running custom high-contrast dark themes across **Quantum OS** and **Quantum Developer Suite**.
  - **Quantum Vision Plan Coverage:** Covers multiple pairs of custom-tinted, anti-reflective (`FL-41` / custom tint) prescription glasses tailored to computer distance, indoor office lighting, and outdoor/travel glare.

- **Quantum Corporation (`Building 35`) Mobility, Badge, ID, & Transit Accommodations:**
  - **1st-Floor (Ground-Floor) Private Corner Office Assignment:** Quantum assigns Jessica's private corner suite on the **ground floor (`1st floor`) of Building 35** so she has immediate, flat, grade-level access to the lobby, courtyard, and exits without ever needing an elevator to reach her office or risking being stranded by an elevator outage or fire alarm.
  - **Badge-Proximity Motorized Doors (`Blue Badge` Accessibility Flag):** Her full-time **Quantum Blue Badge** RFID profile is coded with an accessibility flag—when she rolls within range of Building 35's exterior entrance doors, security vestibule doors, corridor fire doors, or her private office door, the proximity reader automatically triggers the motorized door openers with an extended `15-second` hold-open timer.
  - **Roll-Under Motorized Cockpit Desk & Top-Mounted Hardware:** Her office features a wide motorized height-adjustable L-desk (`28"–32"` roll-under clearance) with all under-desk drawer pedestals removed, high-tucked padded cable channels, and desktop-mounted docking/power strips so her sensitive feet and `ApexLite` manual wheelchair footplate never bump into crossbars or floor cables.
  - **State Identification & Daily `TAPS` Transit Commute (`Overlake Transit Center`):** Jessica does not drive; she holds a **Washington State Photo ID** (and later a **Washington State Enhanced Photo ID**), and her daily ride to and from campus is **`TAPS` (`Transit Authority of the Puget Sound`)** via the **Overlake Transit Center** (with her Quantum Blue Badge covering her transit pass).
  - **Adjacent Executive/Cafeteria Cluster (`Buildings 33, 34, & 35`) & Accessible Shuttles:** On the Redmond grid, **Building 33** (Executive Suites) and **Building 34** (Cafeteria / Central Hub) are located **right next door to Building 35**, allowing Jessica to roll directly between all three buildings along paved grade-level walkways, while ramp-equipped Quantum inter-campus shuttles are available on demand for farther campus sectors.

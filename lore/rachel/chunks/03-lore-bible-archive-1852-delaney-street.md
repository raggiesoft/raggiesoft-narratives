# Lore Bible Archive: 1852 Delaney Street

**Location:** Virginia Beach, Virginia

**Occupancy Timeline:** Summer 1992 – 2003

The Delaney Street residence serves as the primary geographical anchor for the Ragsdale pack. It is the site of the 1995 Monday War triage, the 1999 Black Friday Drop, and the final dissolution of the family unit in 2001. The architecture of the house dictates the physical routing, defense protocols, and social dynamics of the siblings.

## I. Exterior Architecture

- **Siding & Trim:** Faded orange wooden siding with brown trim and a heavy brown painted wooden garage door (typical of its 1999 aesthetic).

- **The Portico:** A slanted, asphalt-shingled roof extending directly over the garage and front entrance. This is the exact site of the Black Friday Drop in November 1999, resulting in Jessica's severe axial load injury.

- **Second-Story Facade:** Two distinct windows overlook the portico. The left window belongs to Michael's bedroom (his vantage point for the drop). The right window belongs to Jessica and Rachel's shared room.

## II. The First Floor (The Perimeter)

### The Foyer & Front Entry

The entry point features linoleum flooring. It branches straight ahead into the main hallway, or left into the living room. Historically significant as the location of the Christmas tree, where Michael experienced his first milestone hug with a non-family female (Bonnie Reed) on Christmas Day 1999.

### The Living Room (Base Camp)

The emotional and physical epicenter of the house, fundamentally defined by its dark blue polyester carpet.

- **Front Wall:** Features a large brick fireplace (swept annually) and two windows overlooking 1850 Delaney Street. Between the windows sits the main entertainment center housing a large CRT TV, VCR, Disney Channel cable box, and Dad's stereo system (AM/FM receiver, dual cassette deck, CD player) with speakers on either side.

- **Right Wall:** The staging area for the sofa. During the 1995 trauma recovery, this was pulled out into a sofa bed, flanked by two additional twin mattresses, creating a fully enclosed medical perimeter. A blue recliner and an end table (holding the cordless phone/answering machine) sit to the right.

- **The Staircase:** Located on the far right. The stairs feature open slats for the first three steps, leading to an L-shaped landing split into two right-triangle steps (where the carpet transitions to beige). Underneath these slatted stairs sits a desktop computer desk—the exact workstation where Jessica dismantled and memorized the Q32 API.

### The Dining Room

Connected to the living room via a wide, open threshold spanning the length of the room (no door, requiring hushed conversations to avoid acoustic bleed). A dark-stained wooden dining table sits in the center with modular seating; one chair is kept removed to allow Jessica absolute tactical control over her wheelchair docking position. A sliding glass door on the left (leading to the backyard) is secured by a heavy 2x4 "Charlie Bar." An empty RJ-11 port sits on the wall.

### The Galley Kitchen

A narrow, highly constrained 1990s galley layout that later inspires Jessica's demand for zero-friction open-concept architecture.

- **Straight Ahead:** Standard freezer-on-top refrigerator.

- **Left Side:** Prep space, stove, prep space with a Sharp Convection Microwave, and a trash can tucked under the far window. *Trivia: The stove was the site of a near-catastrophic kitchen fire in 1994, permanently heightening the adults' awareness of fire safety.*

- **Right Side:** Full dishwasher, prep surface, central sink, and secondary prep surface. Overhead cabinets line both walls, completely inaccessible from a manual wheelchair.

### The Main Hallway & Downstairs Bathroom

The central artery connecting the Dining Room back to the Foyer.

- **Right Side:** Utility closet (washer/dryer), followed by the coat closet. Winter gear and dog leashes are kept here, causing Baby and Archie to react instantly to the door opening.

- **Left Side:** The Downstairs Bathroom (sink/cabinet, toilet, medicine cabinet, standing shower). This is the designated hygiene station for the siblings and the *only* approved transport destination during the 1995 mattress confinement. The next door leads to the 1-car garage and toolbench.

## III. The Second Floor

### The Upper Landing

Turning right at the top of the stairs, a railing overlooks the vaulted ceiling of the living room below, creating significant acoustic bleed. The original 1980 A/C thermostat is on the left wall. The hallway takes a 90-degree left turn (past a linen closet) toward the bedrooms.

### Michael's Bedroom (Front Left)

Painted dark blue. Features a bed pressed against the corner, a poorly anchored bookshelf, a dresser, and a Sharp Lyntron 13-inch TV hooked up to a SNES and VCR. A tiny closet sits past the dresser—the original safe space where Michael and Rachel discovered the regulating effect of deep pressure hugs. *Trivia: In August 1999, Dad flipped the door hardware to open outward so a Full bed could fit, allowing Jessica to share the room for her high school schedule.*

### Jessica & Rachel's Bedroom (Front Right)

White walls with a faint label scar from vintage nursery wallpaper. Features an immediate closet with a Fisher-Price toybox. A bunk bed rests on the right wall (Jessica top, Rachel bottom). The bottom bunk served as a secondary sensory haven. Jessica's PC was located here from 1997-1999. The room was largely abandoned after the 1999 drop forced a migration back to the living room.

### The Master Suite & Bathroom

The bedroom features a sliding door locked shut, leading to a non-existent deck with a railing fence. The room contains two dressers and a sliding-door closet. Attached is the Master Bathroom (sink, toilet, bath/shower unit), which is the historic site of "Rachel's Fury"—the traumatic moment Rachel exhibited severe separation anxiety upon realizing the divorce decree would separate her from her brother.

## IV. Chronology of Space Utilization

| **Era** | **Spatial Dynamic** |
|----|----|
| Fall 1995 | Living room transforms into the medical Base Camp. Sofa bed deployed; strict physical perimeter established. |
| August 1999 | Michael's bedroom door flipped outward. Jessica moves into Michael's room to protect Rachel's sleep schedule. |
| November 1999 | The Black Friday Drop. Upstairs bedrooms are abandoned as the entire youth pack permanently migrates to the living room sofa bed to remain unified with Jessica. |
| August 2001 | Divorce decree fractures the household. Rachel is forced to leave. The Master Bathroom becomes the site of extreme separation trauma. |

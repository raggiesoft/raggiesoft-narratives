# American Wrestling Entertainment (AWF / AWE) — Universal Lore Bible

> **Cross-Narrative Continuity Note**: **American Wrestling Entertainment (AWE)**—formerly the **American Wrestling Federation (AWF)** until May 2002—is the single canonical in-universe counterpart to the WWF/WWE across all RaggieSoft narratives (including `/rachel`, `/aubrie`, `/shiloh`, and `/paige`). Never use legacy placeholder names such as *"GWF"* or *"Monday Night Uprising"*.

---

## 1. Corporate History & Rival Promotions

| Real-World Entity | In-Universe Canonical Name | Lore & Historical Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **WWF $\rightarrow$ WWE** | **AWF $\rightarrow$ AWE** (**American Wrestling Federation** $\rightarrow$ **American Wrestling Entertainment**) | Founded and controlled by ruthless billionaire CEO **Chairman Victor Cross**. Rebranded from **AWF** to **AWE** in May 2002. |
| **World Wildlife Fund (WWF)** | **The Animal Welfare Fund (AWF)** | London-based international wildlife conservation fund. Successfully sued Chairman Victor Cross in the English High Court in 2002 for violating a 1994 co-existence agreement over the `AWF` initials, forcing the transition to **AWE**. |
| **"Get the F Out" Campaign** | **"We're getting the 'F' out!"** | Chairman Victor Cross's defiant May 2002 marketing slogan used to rebrand the **AWF** into **AWE** following the London lawsuit. |
| **WCW (World Championship Wrestling)** | **GCW (Global Championship Wrestling)** | Atlanta-based rival promotion that waged the 1990s ratings war against the AWF before being purchased by Victor Cross in March 2001, triggering the 2001 Invasion angle and the 2002 **AWE Brand Extension** draft. |
| **ECW (Extreme Championship Wrestling)** | **HCW (Hardcore Championship Wrestling)** | Philadelphia-based extreme wrestling promotion run by **Carter "The Fox" Finnley** out of the legendary **South Philly Bingo Hall** (the **Viking Hall / HCW Arena** at Swanson & Ritner). Acquired by AWE after its 2001 bankruptcy and revived in 2006 as AWE's third brand. |
| **"This Is Extreme!"** *(Harry Slash & The Slashtones — ECW Theme)* | ***"This Is Hardcore!"*** *(The South Street Slashtones — HCW Theme)* | The unmistakable, gritty drop-D industrial guitar riff, driving cowbell beat, and gravelly voiceover (*"THIS! IS! HARDCORE!"*) that opened every late-night HCW broadcast and sent the faithful chanting *"H-C-W! H-C-W!"* whenever a table was set on fire or a Singapore cane came out from under the ring. |
| **Titan Towers (Stamford, CT)** | **Titan Plaza (Stamford, CT)** | Corporate headquarters of American Wrestling Entertainment. |
| **TitanTron** | **TitanTron** | The colossal high-definition entrance stage video screen above the ramp. |

## 4. The Complete Annual Pay-Per-View (PPV) Calendar

AWE produces twelve monthly Sunday night Pay-Per-View extravaganzas each year, anchored by **"The Big Four"** (*The Iron Gauntlet*, *The Pinnacle*, *Summer Siege*, and *No Quarter*):

| Month | Real-World PPV | In-Universe Canonical PPV | Signature Match / Tradition & Lore Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **January** | **Royal Rumble** *(Big Four)* | **`The Iron Gauntlet`** | Kickoff to the Road to *The Pinnacle*. Headlined by the **30-Man Iron Gauntlet Match**, where a new Titan enters every 90–120 seconds; elimination only occurs when thrown over the top rope and *both feet* touch the arena floor, with the winner earning a World Championship main-event match at *The Pinnacle*. |
| **February** | **No Way Out** / **Elimination Chamber** | **`No Escape`** | Final stop before *The Pinnacle*. Features the **Iron Dome Match** (*Elimination Chamber*)—a 16-foot-high, 10-ton circular steel-and-chain prison with four polycarbonate corner pods. |
| **March / Early April** | **WrestleMania** *(Big Four)* | **`The Pinnacle`** | *"The Grandest Stage of Them All."* AWE's flagship annual stadium supershow and cultural phenomenon. |
| **Late April** | **Backlash** | **`Fallout`** | Features the immediate championship rematches and blood feuds spinning out of *The Pinnacle*. |
| **May** | **Judgment Day** / **Over the Limit** / **Payback** | **`Day of Reckoning`** | Late-spring grudge-match Pay-Per-View. |
| **June** | **King of the Ring** (1993–2002) / **Bad Blood** (2003–2004) / **One Night Stand** (2005–2008) / **Money in the Bank** (2010–present) | **`Crown of the Ring`** / **`Bad Bloodline`** / **`One Night Only`** / **`Contract in the Vault`** | Historic June PPV slot; in the 2010s era (`/aubrie`), features the multi-man **Contract in the Vault Ladder Match** for a briefcase guaranteeing a World Title match anywhere, anytime within one year. |
| **July** | **Vengeance** / **The Great American Bash** / **Battleground** | **`Vendetta`** / **`The Liberty Bash`** / **`Battlefront`** | Mid-summer championship Pay-Per-View building toward *Summer Siege*. |
| **August** | **SummerSlam** *(Big Four)* | **`Summer Siege`** | *"The Biggest Event of the Summer."* AWE's second-largest annual supershow, held each August in a major coastal or metropolitan arena. |
| **September** | **Unforgiven** (1998–2008) / **Night of Champions** (2008–2015) | **`Unrelenting`** / **`Night of Gold`** | Early autumn PPV; under the *Night of Gold* banner (2008–2015), every single active championship in the AWE is defended on the same card. |
| **October** | **No Mercy** (1999–2008) / **Taboo Tuesday & Cyber Sunday** (2004–2008) / **Hell in a Cell** (2009–present) | **`Zero Mercy`** / **`Cyber Clash`** / **`The Iron Cell`** | October autumn classic; features the **Iron Cell** (*Hell in a Cell*) 20-foot roofed steel cage structure. |
| **November** | **Survivor Series** *(Big Four)* | **`No Quarter`** | **The Annual Thanksgiving Week Tradition.** AWE's historic November "Big Four" Pay-Per-View, held on the Sunday before (or of) Thanksgiving week. Famous for traditional **5-on-5 No Quarter Elimination Tag Team Matches** and career-defining stipulation bouts. In `/rachel` (Books 16–17), Michael, Rachel, Jessica, and Kristin sit in front-row Ringsider seats for **AWE *No Quarter* 2006** at The Emerald Coliseum in Seattle, WA on Sunday, November 26, 2006. In `/aubrie`, *No Quarter* is a centerpiece of the suite's annual Thanksgiving Sanctuary tradition at Daniel and Laura Brooks' home in Christiansburg. |
| **December** | **Armageddon** (1999–2008) / **TLC: Tables, Ladders & Chairs** (2009–present) | **`Doomsday`** / **`TLS: Tables, Ladders & Steel`** | Year-end December blow-off Pay-Per-View featuring multi-man demolition matches with tables, ladders, and steel folding chairs. |

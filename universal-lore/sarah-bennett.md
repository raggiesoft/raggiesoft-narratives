# Lore Dossier: Sarah Bennett

**Role:** Head Lifeguard & Swim Instructor, Fairlawn Recreation Center (Early 2000s) -> Head of City-Wide Aquatics Department (Future)
**Family:** Jack Bennett (Younger brother, Autistic)

## Career Trajectory
Sarah's career at the Fairlawn Recreation Center is foundational to her identity. Starting in the natatorium on the second floor, she operates as the Head Lifeguard, a role that mirrors the protective nature she holds over her younger brother, Jack (who works the first-floor front desk).

*   **Early 2000s:** Serves as Head Lifeguard and a Swim Instructor at the Fairlawn Recreation Center. She is fiercely protective of her patrons and her brother, deeply intertwined with the facility's emergency protocols (including the Bennett Sibling Protocol / Code Purple).
*   **Mid-Career:** Begins the rigorous process to become a Water Safety Instructor Trainer (WSIT)—the person responsible for teaching and certifying the city's swim instructors.
*   **Late Career:** Her dedication, administrative competence, and deep understanding of municipal aquatics eventually propel her through the ranks. She becomes the ultimate authority, serving as the Head of the City-Wide Aquatics Department, managing the aquatics facilities for all recreation centers across the city.

## Uniforms & Aesthetics

**1. The Head Lifeguard Uniform**
During her time on the stand, Sarah's signature look is the municipal Aquatics Department lifeguard uniform. The iconic bright red short-sleeved t-shirt features the word 'GUARD' in bold white athletic lettering across the chest, with 'AQUATICS DEPARTMENT' underneath. This is paired with athletic shorts and a standard-issue whistle on a lanyard, symbolizing safety and authority in the natatorium.

**2. The Swim Instructor Uniform**
When transitioning from the guard stand to teaching lessons, Sarah wears the official Swim Instructor shirt. This is a highly visible Royal Blue short-sleeved t-shirt (creating an immediate visual distinction from the red-clad active lifeguards). It features 'INSTRUCTOR' in bold white athletic lettering across the chest, with 'AQUATICS DEPARTMENT' underneath.

**3. The Standardized Municipal Swimsuit**
Worn dependably under her day clothes, her swimwear is 100% functional, professional, and optimized for high-stress water rescues. It is a classic athletic racing-style one-piece swimsuit in solid Navy Blue (or Red). It features thick, secure shoulder straps and a racerback design to ensure the straps never slip off her shoulders while swimming hard or pulling an adult out of the water. It includes a small municipal seal or the word 'GUARD' printed on the chest/hip, ensuring it is both respectful and hydrodynamically efficient.

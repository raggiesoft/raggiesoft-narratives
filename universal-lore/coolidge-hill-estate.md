# The Coolidge Hill Estate

**The Coolidge Hill Estate** is Cassandra Vance's primary East Coast residence, utilized while she serves as the Executive Vice President of Operations overseeing the Quantum NERD (New England Research & Development) Center. 

## 1. The Property: Coolidge Hill
**Location & Architecture:** A massive, heavily wooded 19th-century colonial revival mansion located on Coolidge Hill in West Cambridge. It provides the sweeping, secluded estate feel of her Newport property (Highcliff) while keeping her seamlessly embedded in the Cambridge transit grid.

**The ADA Retrofit:** Before moving in, the Vance Family Trust executed a flawless, historical-board-approved ADA integration. Casey designed a commercial-grade elevator shaft completely enclosed within a carriage house addition, preserving the original facade and matching the historic red brick and trim. This ensures Cassandra and the other wheelchair users in the pack have unrestricted access to the entire compound.

## 2. The Delaney Family Protocol (Continuous Occupancy)
**The Inner Circle:** The Delaney family are lifelong friends who have known Cassandra and her family, including Liz, their entire lives.

**Full-Time Guardianship:** To ensure that a home owned by Cassandra Vance is never left empty or vulnerable, members of the Delaney family live full-time across her various properties. 

**Unrestricted Access:** The Delaneys hold "Sovereign" clearance within the Trust's architecture. They are explicitly permitted to show up unannounced to any of the homes Cassandra owns, operating as a trusted, permanent extension of the pack's domestic perimeter.

## 3. The New Transit Vector: The NERD Center Commute
Despite possessing extreme wealth, Cassandra and David prioritize the use of public transit. The commute from Coolidge Hill to the Quantum NERD Center is highly optimized:

1. **The Mount Auburn Roll:** Cassandra and David wheel out of the Coolidge Hill compound and navigate the paved sidewalks a short distance to the bus stop on Mt. Auburn Street.
2. **The BSTA Bus:** They board either the BSTA Route 71 or Route 73, utilizing the low-floor accessibility and dedicated transit priority systems to bypass Cambridge traffic.
3. **The Harvard Tunnel Transfer:** The bus bypasses the weather completely by descending into the historic Harvard Square Underground Busway.
4. **The Red Line to Kendall/MIT:** From the subterranean busway, they take the elevator down to the Red Line platforms. They board an Inbound train for a short, two-stop ride to the Kendall/MIT Station, emerging via the station's accessible elevators directly beside the Quantum NERD building.

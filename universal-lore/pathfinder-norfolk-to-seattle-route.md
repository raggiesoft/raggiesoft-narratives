# The Pathfinder Exodus (The Virginia to Washington Route)

This is a shared piece of universal lore across the RaggieSoft universe. Whenever a narrative features a disabled protagonist being dragged across the country by their abuser from Virginia to Washington State, they are subjected to this exact grueling, multi-day Pathfinder bus route. 

**The Narrative Motif:** It is crucial to understand that these protagonists are *not* on the same bus together. This is a recurring traumatic motif that happens across entirely different timelines. For example, Leo is dragged across the country by his abuser in 2003, while Michael endures this exact route with Delores in November 2006. 

**The Logistics:** The entire trip takes exactly four days (96 brutal hours) of constant forward motion. Because it is the absolute cheapest way to cross the continent, the tickets cost a mere $99 per person—a cruel reflection of how little financial investment the abuser is willing to put into the protagonist's comfort or safety.

It is not just a geographic route; it is a psychological descent. The trip strips the protagonist of their agency, inflicts severe physical damage (due to lack of medical accommodation), and isolates them completely from their support systems.

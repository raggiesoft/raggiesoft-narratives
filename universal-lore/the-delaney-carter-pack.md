# The Pack (Delaney, Carter, Vance History)

The core social and emotional support structure (the "pack") for David Vance consists of his childhood best friend (and eventual romantic partner) Liz Carter, and the expansive Delaney family. The physical and emotional boundaries within the pack are extremely open, entirely bypassing normal societal jealousies.

## The Geography and History
* **The Portsmouth Delaneys:** Mr. and Mrs. Delaney and their four daughters (Erin, Megan, Shannon, Kelly). They live on Aquidneck Island in Rhode Island. They have known David since the exact day he was born, as the Vance family originally lived on Aquidneck Island.
* **The Relocation:** When David was 2 years old, the Vance family relocated from Newport, RI to Virginia (the Hampton Roads/Virginia Beach area). 
* **Liz Carter & Meredith Delaney:** When David moved to Virginia at age 2, he met Liz Carter and Meredith Delaney. Meredith is the Virginia Beach cousin to the Portsmouth Delaneys (her father is the younger brother of Mr. Delaney from Portsmouth). Liz and Meredith have known David since he was 2 years old.

## The Physical Boundaries
Because this pack has operated as a unified, fiercely protective surrogate family for almost two decades, the physical boundaries are entirely blurred. 
* David is explicitly allowed to hug, cuddle, and even kiss the Delaney women (Erin, Megan, Shannon, Kelly, and Meredith). 
* **Liz's Absolute Security:** Liz Carter is completely, 100% okay with this physical affection. Because she grew up alongside Meredith and David since they were 2, she possesses absolute emotional security. She knows the Delaney women view David as a brother/packmate. There is zero romantic jealousy or territorial threat when David engages in extreme physical affection with the Delaneys.

## The Check-In Protocol
Because of this tight-knit pack mentality, the Delaneys aggressively monitor David's well-being. If the Portsmouth Delaneys sense that David is in distress (e.g., during Kristine's intense isolation tactics in October 2004), they will utilize their geographical spread and dispatch Meredith Delaney in Virginia Beach to physically track him down and check on him.

## Deep Intimacy and Pack Bonding
The emotional intimacy of the pack is absolute and barrier-free, which eventually extended into physical intimacy once the consenting members turned eighteen (which occurred before David met Kristine). 
* David has a sexual relationship with Liz Carter, Meredith Delaney, Morgan, and Kate. 
* The only reason he hasn't had a sexual relationship with the Portsmouth Delaney sisters (Erin, Megan, Shannon, Kelly) is purely logistical: the last time he saw them in Rhode Island, he was still seventeen.
* All of the women in the pack are on long-form birth control, removing any anxiety about consequences.
* There is zero jealousy between any of the women regarding their shared physical intimacy with David. It is simply considered the ultimate extension of their unconditional pack bonding.

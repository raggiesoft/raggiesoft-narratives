# Lore Dossier: Meridian, Washington

**Location:** Skagit County (Roughly the geographical equivalent of Mount Vernon / Burlington / Anacortes corridor, but serving as a major metropolis). 
**Anchors:** The I-5 / I-96 Interchange, Sterling Plaza

## Overview
Meridian City is a major fictional metropolis located in the Pacific Northwest, nestled in a valley floor. It is completely defined by its towering vertical architecture and massive transit infrastructure. 

## The I-96 Infrastructure Anomaly
Meridian is geographically anchored by one of the largest concrete interchanges in the Pacific Northwest: the massive, sweeping intersection where North-South Interstate 5 meets East-West Interstate 96.

**Interstate 96 (The Unfinished Federal Dream):**
- **The Origin:** I-96 does not start in a traditional downtown grid. It starts directly at the water at the Anacortes Ferry Terminal (Evergreen Marine). It was designed specifically to pull heavy island/international vehicle traffic directly out of the port and shoot it east.
- **The Route:** From the ferry terminal, it runs east through the massive urban canyons of Meridian City, crosses I-5, and pushes into the Skagit Gorge. 
- **The Bottleneck:** Originally, I-96 was slated to blast entirely through the North Cascades and stretch all the way to Duluth, Minnesota (acting as a northern I-90). However, it was killed by environmental protections and geographic reality. Instead, just past Meridian City, the multi-lane federal highway abruptly ends near Birdsview and Rasar State Park, seamlessly bottlenecking and merging into the rural, two-lane asphalt of State Route 20 (SR-20).

## The Vance-Whitaker Crossover (Johnny's Extraction)
This highway infrastructure is critical for the Ruby Mountain lore. When Johnny is dragged off the mountain to Friday Harbor, he doesn't realize he is actively dying—his lungs cannot process the sea-level altitude without his specialized breathing apparatus. When he escapes and boards the eastbound Evergreen Marine ferry, the vehicle waiting for him at the Anacortes docks is not just a standard transport. 

It is a highly specialized, medical-grade **Ridgeback (Quigley 4x4 van)**. Its cabin is heavily retrofitted and pressurized to artificially stabilize his breathing the second they load him into the back. The Vanguard secures him, merges immediately onto I-96 East, and executes a high-speed, multi-lane burn straight through Meridian City (bypassing I-5). The Ridgeback's specialized environment keeps him alive as they ride I-96 until it bottlenecks into SR-20, taking them the rest of the way up into the Air-Gap at The Prospector's Keep.

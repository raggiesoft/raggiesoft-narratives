## I. Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI) — Overview & Campus Geography

* **In-Universe Counterpart:** Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University (Virginia Tech) in Blacksburg, Virginia.
* **Official Name:** Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (`CPI`).
* **Location:** Blacksburg, Virginia (Montgomery County / New River Valley).
* **Colors:** Chicago Maroon (`#630031`) and Burnt Orange (`#CF4420`).
* **Nickname & Mascot:** **The Forgers** (celebrating the university's deep blue-collar roots in metallurgy, heavy engineering, and industrial arts).
* **Marching Band:** **The Marching Forgers**.
* **Student Fan Section:** **The Ironheads** (the fanatical, hard-hat-and-overalls-wearing student section in the North End Zone of The Crucible; CPI's football counterpart to **Buchanan University** basketball's *The Pavilion Lunatics* at Buchanan Indoor Pavilion).

### Key Campus & Blacksburg Landmarks (Master Gazetteer)
* **Commonwealth Green (*The Green*):** The massive central oval lawn at the heart of campus (in-universe counterpart to the Drillfield), bordered by neo-Gothic **Ironstone** (quarried gray Appalachian limestone) academic buildings.
* **Preston Hall:** Towering Neo-Gothic Ironstone administrative fortress on the northern rim of Commonwealth Green (counterpart to Burruss Hall), housing **Administrative Command** (Office of the President, Office of the Bursar, and the 3,000-seat **Preston Auditorium**).
* **Commonwealth Mall:** The main ceremonial tree-lined boulevard connecting Downtown Blacksburg to Commonwealth Green (counterpart to Alumni Mall).
  * **Pre-Transit-Center Era (2004):** Before the dedicated multi-bay **CPI Transit Center** opened, **Blue Ridge Transit (BRT)** buses staged bumper-to-bumper along the curb of Commonwealth Mall beneath **The Keystone Bridge** across from **Kincaid Hall** in a chaotic, diesel-choked queue (the site of Erin's November 2004 front-door ADA ramp rescue of her twin brother John in *Before the Distance*).
  * **CPI Transit Center:** Multi-bay off-street concrete bus loop constructed in later years to replace the chaotic Commonwealth Mall curbside lineup.
* **Keystone Hall & The Keystone Bridge:** Monumental arched Ironstone skybridge spanning Commonwealth Mall (counterpart to Torgersen Hall & Bridge), linking **Keystone Hall** directly to **Pendleton Library**.
* **Pendleton Library (*Pendleton Memorial Library*):** Main university research library at the head of Commonwealth Mall and Commonwealth Green (counterpart to Carol M. Newman Library).
* **Kincaid Hall (*Kincaid Student Center*):** Massive student union along Commonwealth Mall (counterpart to Squires Student Center; formerly `Newman Hall` in early drafts), housing the **Kincaid Atrium**, **Kincaid Grand Ballroom**, **Kincaid Auditorium**, **Kincaid Second-Floor Lounge**, **Kincaid Game Room & Pool Tables**, and the **PolyPass ID Office**.
* **Callahan Hall:** Quiet, carpeted building near the eastern edge of Commonwealth Green (counterpart to Henderson Hall), housing **Services for Students with Disabilities (SSD)** / **Office of Educational Accessibility** and the **SSD Quiet Room Testing Center**.
* **The Engineering Quad:** Located northwest of Commonwealth Green, anchored by:
  * **Faraday Hall:** Electrical & Systems Engineering (3rd-Floor Systems & Control Lab).
  * **Joule Hall:** Mechanical, Aerospace & Materials Engineering.
  * **Kelvin Hall:** Computer Science & Symbolic Logic.
  * **Archer Hall:** General Undergraduate Lecture Hall (where Dr. Thorne teaches `HPE 204` in *Paige*).
* **Copernicus Hall (*The Science Complex*):** Brutalist concrete-and-Ironstone science building housing Physics, Astronomy (`PHY 102` / `AST 101` 200-seat tiered amphitheater), and the CPI Geological Sciences seismograph.
* **Carter Hall:** Historic brick humanities building on the upper quad reached via a steep, winding incline (where Dr. Gable teaches `History 101: Western Civilization` in *Aubrie*).
* **Eleanor Bennett Commonwealth Hall (*Bennett Hall* / *"The Old Lady"* / *"The Old Lady on the Hill"*):** 100% fictional historic 1960s residence hall perched on a grassy overlook above Commonwealth Green; **Suite 214 / 216** serves as the accessible sanctuary for Jordan Shawn Kelly, Aubrie, and the Group of 8 during the 2014–2016 timeline (*The Aubrie Chronicles*).
* **Westgate Hall:** Traditional undergraduate residence hall on the residential quad.
* **The Foundry & Kincaid Commons:** Primary campus dining halls—**The Foundry** is the massive two-story rotunda dining hall serving the residential quad (counterpart to Dietrick Hall / D2), while **Kincaid Commons** is the multi-level hillside dining hall built into the slope between the upper and lower quads (counterpart to Owens Food Court).
* **Progress Street NE:** The residential street just north of campus where the O'Connell siblings (Ryan, Cassidy, and Holly) and their maternal twin cousins (Evan and Tyler Wright) shared an off-campus rental house (`412 Progress Street NE`) from 1985 to 1989 and formed **The Stardust Engine**, and where twins John & Erin share a ground-floor wheelchair-accessible apartment in *Before the Distance*.
* **Pepper Street & Pepper Plaza:** Major student residential and commercial corridor in Christiansburg/Blacksburg (home to Paige, Freddie, and Jessica at `105 Pepper Street`, and the **Pepper Plaza** transit transfer hub next to **Valu-Mart**).
* **The Ironwood Collective (`Stardust Blacksburg, LLC`):** Founded in 2003 by the O'Connell Family Trust (`Stardust Properties, LLC`), providing renovated, fiber-equipped student apartments along the Blue Ridge Transit "Spine" priced 15% below market rate so no CPI student ever has to choose between physical safety and tuition.

### Academic & Enrollment Mechanics Across Narratives
1. **High School Dual Enrollment (The Holly O'Connell Precedent, 1985–1987):**
   * Through a joint partnership between **Montgomery County Public Schools (Blacksburg High School)** and **CPI**, academically accelerated 11th and 12th graders can enroll in Dual Enrollment courses that simultaneously satisfy high school graduation requirements (for a Virginia Advanced Studies Diploma) and earn official CPI undergraduate credits.
   * Because Blacksburg High School and CPI sit in the same town linked by **Blue Ridge Transit Route 1**, dual-enrolled students attend high school classes in the morning and ride the bus to the CPI campus in the afternoon for 200- and 300-level university lectures.
   * **Holly O'Connell** (born 1969) entered this program at age 16 in Fall 1985 while her older siblings (Ryan, 21; Cassidy, 19) and cousins (Evan and Tyler, 18) matriculated as CPI freshmen. By taking afternoon Business Administration and Commercial Contract Law classes on campus and summer sessions, Holly entered CPI full-time as a credit-hour Junior in Fall 1987 and graduated alongside all four of them in **May 1989** (*The Class of '89*).
2. **The Summer Session PolyPass Loophole (The Jessica / Paige / Freddie Precedent):**
   * Incoming Fall freshmen do not have active **PolyPass** transit privileges on their CPI student IDs until the Fall Semester begins in late August.
   * However, enrolling in a six-week accelerated **Summer Session** course (such as *Session 2: July–August*, e.g., `HPE 204: Dimensions of Human Sexuality`) immediately activates a student's **PolyPass** upon tuition payment—unlocking unlimited fare-free rides across the entire Blue Ridge Transit network for the summer.

## II. The Crucible, The Furnace, & The 1995 Ironhead Covenant

* **In-Universe Counterparts:**
  * **The Crucible:** Lane Stadium / Worsham Field (65,000-seat open-air football stadium).
  * **The Furnace:** Cassell Coliseum (10,000-seat vaulted-roof indoor basketball and convocation arena immediately adjacent to The Crucible).
* **Official Names:** **The Crucible** and **The Furnace** (strictly un-hyphenated; corporate naming rights are legally barred in perpetuity for both venues).
* **Capacity:** **65,000** at The Crucible (often referred to colloquially in chants and lyrics as *"Sixty thousand standing in the maroon and orange sea"*); **10,000** at The Furnace.
* **The North End Zone ("The Anvil"):** General-admission, unpadded galvanized metal bleacher seating inside The Crucible reserved for **The Ironheads** (the 20,000-strong student section). Engineered so metal-on-metal boot stomping reverberates like artillery fire across the New River Valley.

### The August 15, 1995 "Ironhead Covenant"
On August 15, 1995, CPI alumnus and industrial magnate **Jeremiah "Ironhead" Vance (Class of 1958)** executed a binding **Deed of Gift** (`CPI-2017-084-VANCE`) endowing the *Vance Forger Scholarship & Facilities Fund*. Acceptance of the principal was conditioned on perpetual covenants running with the land:
1. **Section 2.1 — Naming Rights in Perpetuity (The Crucible & The Furnace):** The football stadium must remain **`"THE CRUCIBLE"`** and the basketball arena must remain **`"THE FURNACE"`** forever. No bank, corporation, telecom conglomerate, or individual name may ever be prefixed, appended, or hyphenated to either venue (e.g., *"The Crucible at [Corporate Name]"* or *"The [Sponsor] Furnace"* is strictly forbidden).
2. **Section 2.2 — The Acoustic Mandate:** All future renovations or expansions must prioritize vertical noise-trapping geometry over luxury suites ("the architecture must serve the roar"), preserving The Crucible's and The Furnace's status as the loudest collegiate venues in the world.
3. **Section 2.3 — The Spirit of Iron:** Primary structural materials must remain exposed concrete, Ironstone, and steel, and the North End Zone (**The Anvil**) must remain general-admission metal bleachers ("comfort shall never supersede volume").
4. **Article III — The Clawback Penalty:** Any breach regarding either **The Crucible** or **The Furnace** triggers immediate revocation of the principal plus **compound interest at the Federal Prime Rate + 2%** accruing retroactively from August 15, 1995—making a corporate naming-rights deal mathematically suicidal for any future University Board of Visitors.

## III. Stadium Anthems & The 1986 Progress Street Redline

### 1. The 1986 "$1.00 Perpetual Mutual Cross-License" ("The Progress Street Redline")
* In October 1986, CPI's Student Government Association held a contest to replace the football team's traditional entrance march. The standard entry form contained a buried **"Work Made for Hire"** clause transferring 100% of the winning song's master and publishing rights to the CPI Athletic Foundation.
* Sixteen-year-old dual-enrolled prodigy **Holly O'Connell** redlined the contract at the Progress Street kitchen table and negotiated a **\$1.00 Perpetual Mutual Cross-License** with CPI's Office of University Counsel:
  * **The Band's Retention:** The Stardust Engine retained 100% of their master and publishing rights.
  * **CPI's License:** CPI Athletics and the Marching Forgers received a perpetual, royalty-free synchronization and public-performance license to play *"Ignition (The Forger's Call)"* and future Stardust Engine anthems inside The Crucible.
  * **The Band's Trademark Co-Existence License:** CPI's Office of Trademarks & Licensing granted the band (and any label they wholly owned, later **Engine Room Records, LLC**) a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to sing and print `CPI`, `Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute`, `The Forgers`, and `The Crucible` in their commercial releases and liner notes.

### 2. Pre-Game Entrance Ritual: *"Ignition (The Forger's Call)"* & The 1989 Stomp
* **Canonical Title & Apple Music DSP Formatting:**
  * **Narrative / Prose Title:** ***"Ignition (The Forger's Call)"***
  * **Apple Music / Suno DSP Metadata Title:** ***"Ignition: The Forger's Call"*** (or ***"Ignition: The Forger's Call (Live)"*** on 2016's *Live at The Crucible*, complying with Apple Music's rule reserving parentheses `()` strictly for `feat.` credits or official version descriptors like `(Live)`).
* Submitted as a raw garage-metal cassette demo in 1986 featuring **Tyler Wright** striking a salvaged automobile brake drum with a sledgehammer (`TING... BOOM!`).
* **Birth of "The Stomp" (November 1989 vs. VPU):** During a freezing November night game against arch-rival **Virginia Piedmont University (VPU)**, students in **The Anvil** began **stomping** their heavy winter boots on the galvanized metal bleachers in exact time with Tyler's brake drum to stay warm (replacing Virginia Tech's real-life "Jump"). When visiting VPU fans tried to boo during the pauses, 20,000 CPI students roared **`"IGNITION!"`** back at the PA system.
* By the mid-1990s, the CPI Athletic Director formalized the pre-game ritual: stadium lights cut to black before kickoff, the Marching Forgers sway **Left, Right, Left, Right** during the bridge cadence, and 65,000 fans perform **The Stomp**, triggering a localized seismic event on the Copernicus Hall seismographs as the team charges out of the tunnel.

### 3. Fourth-Quarter Victory Anthem: *"Saturday Night Lights: The Crucible"* (Oct 8, 2004 Premiere)
* The Stardust Engine's homecoming album ***Progress Street*** was scheduled for worldwide release on **Monday, October 10, 2004**.
* Two nights earlier—on **Saturday, October 8, 2004**—CPI hosted **#6 Monongahela State University (MSU Coalminers)** in a bruising night game for *The Black Anvil Trophy*. Under a top-secret addendum to her 1986 cross-license, Holly O'Connell delivered an unmarked gold master CD-R to the press-box audio booth before kickoff.
* When the clock hit `0:00` at the end of the 3rd quarter with CPI clinging to a narrow lead, the stadium lights dimmed to twilight and the PA system blasted the world premiere of Track 6: ***"Saturday Night Lights: The Crucible."***
* Just as **Boston Beacons** fans belt the eighth-inning horn hook of *"Sweet Evangeline"* at **Back Bay Park**, **The Ironheads** locked onto Cassidy O'Connell's triumphant three-note 1980s synthesizer brass fanfare at the end of every chorus phrase—spontaneously roaring **`"BAH! BAH! BAH!"`** and **`"SO GOOD! SO GOOD! SO GOOD!"`** across the stadium. CPI held off #6 MSU to win **19–13**, cementing *"Ignition"* as the pre-game war cry that starts the fire and *"Saturday Night Lights: The Crucible"* as the 4th-quarter singalong that seals the victory.

### The 2014–2015 VDU "Homecoming Shutout War" (*The Hammer vs. The Crown*)
* **Act I — "The Homecoming Humiliation" (September 27, 2014, Blacksburg):**
  * Ranked **#3 in the nation** with a **Gridiron Trophy**-candidate quarterback, CPI scheduled its first-ever meeting with unranked **Virginia Dominion University (VDU)** as CPI's 2014 Homecoming game, assuming it would be effortless target practice. Dorm doors in Bennett Hall and chalk murals on The Commonwealth Green depicted the CPI Forger's Hammer smashing the VDU Crown under *"BEAT NORFOLK!"* banners.
  * After CPI missed an opening-drive chip-shot field goal wide left, VDU's methodical rushing attack ground down the #3 Forgers (`7–0` after Q1, `24–0` at halftime after a CPI fumble inside the final minute of the half, and a shocking **`38–0` final shutout**).
  * *Cross-Universe Tie-In (`The Aubrie Chronicles`):* Freshman **Jordan Shawn Kelly** suffered a devastating panic attack at halftime watching his new Blacksburg sanctuary humiliated by the team from his abusive mother's Hampton Roads backyard. Jordan did not set foot inside The Crucible again until **May 21, 2016**, when The Stardust Engine performed ***Live at The Crucible*** and overwrote the ghost of the `38–0` scoreboard.
* **Act II — "The Norfolk Reckoning" (October 2015, Norfolk):**
  * Intoxicated by their 2014 upset, VDU arrogantly scheduled the 2015 return game in Norfolk as **VDU's own Homecoming Game**, printing thousands of *"38–0"* student t-shirts.
  * Having stared at `0–38` stenciled on every weight-room mirror in Blacksburg for 365 days, CPI walked into VDU's Homecoming party and delivered a merciless **`52–0` retaliatory shutout**, keeping their starting defense on the field until `0:00` to preserve the goose egg.

## V. The New River Valley (NRV) Regional Transit Network Bible

Public transportation in the New River Valley is an interconnected, multi-agency ecosystem anchored by **Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI)** and the **PolyPass** agreement. Depending on the year in which a narrative takes place, the physical hubs, rail links, and fare-collection technology evolve across four canonical eras.

### A. Chronological Timeline of Campus Hubs, Rail Links, & Fare Payment
1. **Era 1 — The "Flash-Pass & Commonwealth Mall" Era (`2003–2015`):**
   * **Campus Bus Hub (`Commonwealth Mall`):** Before the dedicated off-street **CPI Transit Center** exists, all **five bus services** (Blue Ridge Transit, Bluewater Transit, Roanoke Valley Transit, Radford Transit, and the 2003 Supplemental Commuter Coaches / Valley Flyer) converge curbside along **Commonwealth Mall** beneath **The Keystone Bridge** outside **Kincaid Hall** in a chaotic, diesel-choked lineup.
   * **Fare Collection ("Flash Pass"):** There are no electronic smart-card readers. Active CPI students simply **flash their physical PolyPass** (and eligible partner students flash their Galax Community College, Carroll County Public Schools, Radford High School, University of Radford, or Dublin Community College IDs) to the bus operator upon boarding at the front door.
   * **2003 Supplemental Commuter Coaches:** To relieve peak-hour overcrowding on BT Route 1, high-capacity 45-foot **MCI over-the-road commuter coaches** operate a 30-minute express shuttle (`Falling Branch Park & Ride ↔ New River Mall [Christiansburg Mall] ↔ Commonwealth Mall`) every 30 minutes, free with a flashed PolyPass.
2. **Era 2 — The "CPI Transit Center & ARTS Roanoke Rail Extension" Era (`2016–2019`):**
   * **CPI Transit Center Opens (`2016`):** CPI opens the dedicated, off-street multi-bay **CPI Transit Center**, permanently moving bus staging off Commonwealth Mall. BT Route 1 destination headsigns update from `1 COMMONWEALTH MALL` to `1 CPI TRANS CTR`.
   * **October 31, 2017 — ARTS Passenger Rail Reaches Roanoke (*Third Street Station*):**
     * **ARTS** (*American Rail Transit Service*, in-universe Amtrak) extends its Northeast Corridor regional service—known across the RaggieSoft universe as **ARTS *The Patriot*** (in-universe counterpart to Amtrak's *Northeast Regional*)—to a new passenger platform at **Roanoke's Third Street Station**.
     * **RVT *Train Connector* (Official ARTS Thruway Service):** That same day (October 31, 2017), **Roanoke Valley Transit (RVT)** launches its dedicated **Train Connector** coach service linking **CPI Transit Center**, **New River Mall (Christiansburg Mall)**, and **Falling Branch Park & Ride** directly to **Third Street Station** in Roanoke (including ARTS-subsidized **Sunday** runs and guaranteed delayed-train holds).
3. **Era 3 — The Post-COVID "Blue Ridge Go-Card & Smart-Card" Era (`2020–2021+`):**
   * **End of the "Flash Pass":** Triggered by the COVID-19 pandemic, **Blue Ridge Transit (BT)**, **Bluewater Transit**, and **Roanoke Valley Transit (RVT)** retire visual ID flashing and launch a unified contactless smart-card network: the **Blue Ridge Go-Card** (*"One Card. Three Systems. The Entire Valley."*), paired with **Open-Loop Contactless Payment** (Tap-to-Pay credit/debit cards, **MagnaPay / MagnaWallet**, and **OmniPay**).
   * **Radford Transit Goes 100% Fare-Free:** Rather than installing expensive Go-Card hardware across its `$0.50` fleet, **Radford Transit** eliminates fares permanently during COVID-19.
   * **Embedded School IDs & MagnaByte "Express Mode":** All newly issued **CPI PolyPasses**, **Galax Community College IDs**, and **Carroll County Public Schools IDs** contain an embedded **Blue Ridge Go-Card RFID chip** (and provisioning tokens for **MagnaWallet** on **MagnaPhone** and **MagnaWatch** with **Express Transit Mode**, allowing students to tap without waking or unlocking their device, plus 5-hour Power Reserve battery backup).
   * **One Year Post-Launch — Rear-Door Readers & Proof of Payment (`POP`):** One year after the Go-Card rollout, **Blue Ridge Transit** installs validators at the **rear doors** of its 40-foot and 60-foot articulated buses, shifting Campus and Route 1 operations to **Proof of Payment (POP)** with random handheld inspections by Transit Ambassadors.
4. **Era 4 — The Future Christiansburg Rail Extension Era:**
   * **ARTS Extends Rail to Christiansburg:** When ARTS extends passenger rail south from Roanoke toward Bristol, the **Christiansburg Transit Center** (featuring the multi-level VDOT parking garage, 8-bay bus terminal, and a new **ARTS Passenger Rail Station**) is constructed on the footprint of the **Falling Branch Park & Ride (`I-81 Exit 118`)**.
   * **End of RVT's *Train Connector*:** Because ARTS trains now stop directly at the **Christiansburg Transit Center** (where passengers transfer directly onto **BT Route 1** articulated buses, **Bluewater Transit**, or **RVT**), RVT discontinues its special *Train Connector* Thruway runs while maintaining its regular Mon–Sat *Roanoke Express* commuter route.

### B. The Four PolyPass-Eligible Agencies (+ Two Special Corridor Services)

#### 1. Blue Ridge Transit (`BT` / `BRT`) — Local Blacksburg & Christiansburg
* **Operating Days:** 7 days a week.
* **Fleet & Livery:** 29-foot, 35-foot, and 40-foot rigid buses (**Stalwart** / Gillig Advantage & **Prairie Coachworks** `D40LF`), plus **60-foot low-floor articulated (`D60LF` / `RLFD60`) buses** manufactured by **Prairie Coachworks** in the *"Mountain Mist & River Ribbon"* livery.
  * **CRITICAL REALISM RULE:** As US low-floor transit buses, the flip-out aluminum ADA wheelchair ramp is ALWAYS located at the **front passenger boarding door** right beside the operator and farebox.
* **Fare Structure:** **`$1.00`** base cash/Go-Card fare (includes a free paper transfer slip pre-COVID or automated 2-hour digital transfer on the Go-Card); **`$40.00` 30-Day Pass** (automated 30-day rolling cap after 40 rides on the Go-Card); **`$0.00` (Free)** with an active **CPI PolyPass**.
* **Key Routes:**
  * **Route 1 ("The Spine" — Christiansburg $\leftrightarrow$ CPI):**
    * **No Short-Turns:** Every Route 1 bus runs the full length of the corridor.
    * **Exact Alignment & Travel Times:** Leaves **Christiansburg Transit Center / Falling Branch (`I-81 Exit 118`)** $\rightarrow$ follows **US-11 (Roanoke Street)** into downtown Christiansburg to **Pepper Plaza Transit Center** (located right next door to the **Montgomery County Courthouse** across from `105 Pepper Street` and **Valu-Mart**; **15 minutes** from Falling Branch) $\rightarrow$ turns north and follows **South Main Street (Business US-460)** into Blacksburg and through **Progress Street NE** to terminate at **Commonwealth Mall** (`2003–2015`, headsign `1 COMMONWEALTH MALL`) or the **CPI Transit Center** (`2016+`, headsign `1 CPI TRANS CTR`; **30 minutes** from Pepper Plaza; **45 minutes** end-to-end). Southbound headsign reads `1 FALLING BRANCH`.
    * **Frequency:** Every **15 minutes** on weekdays using 60-foot Prairie Coachworks articulated buses.
  * **Route 3 ("The Radford Link" — Christiansburg $\leftrightarrow$ Radford University):**
    * **Alignment:** Leaves **Pepper Plaza Transit Center**, follows **US-11** west across the New River, and terminates at Radford's **McConnell Transit Center** (`6:00 AM – 10:00 PM`; every **30 minutes** Mon–Fri, every **60 minutes** Sat–Sun).
  * **Route 8 ("The Riner Run" — Christiansburg $\leftrightarrow$ Riner):**
    * **Alignment:** Leaves **Pepper Plaza Transit Center** and follows **State Route 8** south to Riner (`7:00 AM – 6:00 PM` Mon–Fri, every **60 minutes**; no weekend service). Used by Jessica and Paige for their OB/GYN appointments in Riner.

#### 2. Bluewater Transit (`BT` — "The Other BT") — Carroll County & *The Maroon Express*
* **Service Area & Hub:** Based in **Bluewater, Virginia** (in-universe counterpart to **Hillsville, Virginia**, the county seat and largest town in Carroll County, and the hometown of **Madison Elizabeth Scott**). All routes pulse out of the **Bluewater Transit Center**, located directly across the street from **Bluewater High School**.
  * **The 2:30 PM High School Rush:** When the final bell rings at Bluewater High School at `2:30 PM`, hundreds of students pour across the street into the transit center to catch the `2:45 PM` pulse departure.
* **Fare-Free Partner IDs:** **`$0.00` (Free)** on all Local and *Maroon Express* buses for riders with a **Carroll County Public Schools ID**, **Galax Community College ID**, or **CPI PolyPass**.
* **Local Service (`$1.00` Cash / Go-Card, `$3.00` Local Day Pass Cap):**
  * Operates **7 days a week** (`5:45 AM – 9:15 PM`) using 29-foot and 35-foot low-floor transit buses.
  * **The Clockface Pulse:** Local routes pulse from Bluewater Transit Center every 30 minutes at **`:15`** and **`:45`** past the hour—except the **Purple Line** (`Bluewater Transit Center ↔ Galax Community College`), which runs **hourly** on a 60-minute one-way trip (`120-minute` round-trip cycle requiring 2 buses; the final `8:15 PM` departure from Galax CC arrives at Bluewater Transit Center at `9:15 PM`, when the system closes for the night).
* ***The Maroon Express* (`$5.00` Cash / Go-Card, `$10.00` Express Day Pass Cap):**
  * **Equipment:** Three 45-foot over-the-road commuter coaches (with mid-coach hydraulic ADA wheelchair lifts) operating a `180-minute` round-trip cycle (**90 minutes** each way).
  * **Schedule:** Runs **7 days a week**, hourly at **`:15`** northbound from Bluewater (`5:15 AM – 9:15 PM`) and hourly at **`:45`** southbound from CPI (`6:45 AM – 10:45 PM`, arriving back in Bluewater at `12:15 AM`).
  * **Stop Sequence:**
    1. `Bluewater Transit Center` (Across from Bluewater High School)
    2. `Bluewater Park & Ride` (**I-77 Exit 14**)
    3. `Max Meadows Park & Ride` (**I-81 Exit 84**)
    4. `Dublin Park & Ride / NRVRC Justin's Station` (**I-81 Exit 98** — where Justin Hayes and *The Wall of 20* board the 6:30 AM run)
    5. `Falling Branch Park & Ride / Christiansburg Transit Center` (**I-81 Exit 118B**)
    6. `New River Mall / New River Commons` (**The Christiansburg Mall**)
    7. `Commonwealth Mall` (`2003–2015`) / `CPI Transit Center` (`2016+`)
  * **Upgrade Rules & Post-COVID GPS + Tap-Off Logic:**
    * Riders transferring from a `$1.00` Bluewater Local bus pay a **`$2.00` single-ride upgrade**; riders holding a `$3.00` Local Day Pass pay a **`$7.00` upgrade** to an Express Day Pass (`$10.00` total daily cap).
    * Because the *Maroon Express* serves two local stops inside Bluewater (`Bluewater Transit Center` and `Exit 14 P&R`), its post-COVID Go-Card validators are linked to the coach's **GPS**:
      * **Northbound (`Tap On + Tap Off`):** Tapping on at Bluewater Transit Center pre-authorizes the `$5.00` Express fare. If a local commuter taps off 5 minutes later at `Bluewater Park & Ride (Exit 14)`, the GPS recognizes they stayed within the local zone and charges only **`$1.00`**. Riding past Exit 14 onto I-77/I-81 (or failing to tap off) finalizes the **`$5.00`** Express charge. (Riders boarding at Exit 14 or further north only tap on).
      * **Southbound (`Tap On Only`):** Every boarding at CPI, New River Mall, Falling Branch, Dublin, or Max Meadows is automatically charged **`$5.00`** (no tap-off needed). Once the coach reaches `Exit 14 P&R` heading into town, the GPS automatically drops the validator to **`$1.00` Local Mode** for the final hop to Bluewater Transit Center.
      * **Student ID Bypass:** CPI PolyPass, Galax CC, and Carroll County school ID holders never need to tap off (`$0.00` across all zones).

#### 3. Roanoke Valley Transit (`RVT`) — *Roanoke Express* & *ARTS Train Connector*
* **Service Area & Hub:** Serves Roanoke, Salem, and Vinton from **Third Street Station** in downtown Roanoke.
* **Operating Hours:** **Monday–Saturday**, `5:45 AM – 8:45 PM` (no regular Sunday service, except for the ARTS-subsidized *Train Connector* between 2017 and the Christiansburg rail extension).
* **Fares:** **`$2.00`** Local (`$60.00` 30-Day Local Pass/Cap); **`$3.00`** *Roanoke Express* (`$90.00` 30-Day Express Pass/Cap; `$1.00` upgrade from Local); **`$0.00` (Free)** with an active **CPI PolyPass**.
* ***The Roanoke Express* & The Combined 30-Minute Express Corridor:**
  * Operates 45-foot over-the-road coach buses along `Third Street Station (Roanoke) ↔ Falling Branch P&R / Christiansburg Transit Center ↔ New River Mall (Christiansburg Mall) ↔ Commonwealth Mall / CPI Transit Center`.
  * **Timed Coordination with Bluewater Transit:** On Monday–Saturday, RVT and Bluewater Transit offset their hourly schedules by 30 minutes along the shared northern spine (`Falling Branch ↔ New River Mall ↔ CPI`), providing **30-minute combined express headways** (`Bluewater` northbound at `:15` / `RVT` at `:45`; `RVT` southbound from CPI at `:15` / `Bluewater` at `:45`).
* **The *ARTS Train Connector* (`October 31, 2017 – Future Christiansburg Rail Extension`):**
  * Functions as an official **ARTS Thruway Bus Service** connecting **CPI Transit Center**, **New River Mall**, and **Falling Branch** to **ARTS *The Patriot*** passenger trains at Roanoke's **Third Street Station**.
  * **Unified Ticketing & PolyPass Access:** Can be booked as a single through-ticket on `ARTS.com` / the ARTS mobile app, or boarded walk-up with cash (`$3.00`), an RVT pass, or **for free with a CPI PolyPass**.
  * **Guaranteed Connection & Early-Termination Rules:** If an incoming ARTS train is delayed (even by several hours), RVT **holds the *Train Connector* bus** at Third Street Station until the train arrives. On the final run of the night, once all deboarding rail passengers have loaded, if no passengers are waiting or traveling to a downstream stop, the operator may terminate the route early and deadhead to the garage as soon as the coach is empty.

#### 4. Radford Transit — Local Radford & *The Radford Connector*
* **Service Area & Hub:** Serves the independent City of Radford and **Radford University (RU)** from the **McConnell Transit Center** using a fleet of maneuverable **29-foot low-floor transit buses**.
* **Fares & The School ID Loophole:**
  * **Pre-COVID (`2003–2019`):** **`$0.50`** exact-change cash fare (strictly **no free transfers**—every boarding costs two quarters).
  * **Post-COVID (`2020+`):** **Permanently 100% Fare-Free** for all riders (eliminating the need for Blue Ridge Go-Card validators).
  * **Eligible Free IDs (Pre-COVID):** **University of Radford ID**, **Dublin Community College ID**, **CPI PolyPass**, and **Radford Public Schools ID**.
    * *The Elementary/Middle School Loophole:* Officially, the municipal school subsidy only covers **Radford High School (RHS)** students (`Grades 9–12`). In practice, because every school in the small independent city uses the exact same plastic ID badge template from the same printer, bus operators never squint at the grade level and wave middle and elementary school kids on for free.
* **Schedule Split:** Local Radford routes (including the **Plumb Creek** residential route and the **Dublin Community College** commuter link) run **Monday–Friday only**. Only the **Radford Connector** (`Radford ↔ Christiansburg ↔ CPI`) operates **7 days a week**.

#### 5. Excluded Commercial Intercity Service: *The Valley Flyer*
* **In-Universe Counterpart:** The Virginia Breeze (operated by Megabus / Pathfinder under contract with VDRPT).
* **Route:** Daily intercity motorcoach linking **CPI** and **Falling Branch** directly to **Washington, D.C. (Union Station)** via I-81 and I-66 (bypassing New River Mall and downtown Roanoke).
* **Strictly Commercial (NO PolyPass / NO Go-Card):** Requires advance online reservation. Even a local hop between CPI and Falling Branch costs **`$15.99`**, and a ticket to Washington, D.C. costs **`$49.99`**.

## VI. The Christiansburg Transit Center at Falling Branch (Exit 118) & Game-Day Logistics

On Monday through Friday, the **Christiansburg Transit Center at Falling Branch** (`I-81 Exit 118`) serves as the southern anchor of BRT Route 1 and a regional commuter hub. On **CPI Forgers Football Saturdays**, it transforms into a purpose-built, industrial-scale people-moving monster designed by **VDOT**, **CPI**, and **Blue Ridge Transit** to intercept thousands of automobiles *before* they ever enter the Town of Blacksburg.

### 1. Structural & Highway Specifications
* **The Garage:** An **8-level, 6,000-space concrete parking structure** owned and operated by **VDOT** (`Level 1` houses the pedestrian concourse, transit services, and accessible circulation; `Levels 2–8` house commuter and game-day parking).
* **Game-Day Intercept Volume:** At an average game-day occupancy of ~2.4 fans per vehicle, a full 6,000-space garage intercepts **12,000 to 14,400 spectators**—roughly **1 out of every 5 fans** inside a sold-out Crucible—keeping 5,000+ private automobiles completely off Blacksburg's surface streets.
* **WSDOT-Style Asymmetric Freeway Geometry:**
  * **Direct I-81 Ramps (`ALL VEHICLES`):** High-capacity flyover ramps connect **I-81 North and South** directly into the 6,000-space garage (`$0 FREE PARKING`), allowing motorists to enter and exit the interstate without passing through a single local traffic light.
  * **Direct US-460 Transit Flyovers (`BUSES ONLY`):** Private automobiles *cannot* access the direct high-speed connector from the facility toward Blacksburg. Only **Blue Ridge Transit** (and partner coaches) may use the grade-separated **`BUSES ONLY` direct ramps** linking the ground-level **8-bay BRT Transit Terminal** (plus articulated-bus staging lanes) straight onto the **US-460 Bypass** and into the Virginia State Police-enforced **Southgate Drive Bus-Only Priority Corridor** leading directly to The Crucible.

### 2. The Game-Day "Load and GO!" Protocol
* **Zero Fare Friction:** On Forgers game days, both the 6,000-space VDOT garage and the **Crucible Shuttle** are **100% FREE**. There is no farebox collection, no PolyPass tapping, and no ticket inspection at the bus bays.
* **Simultaneous 8-Bay Articulated Dispatch:** Eight 60-foot **Prairie Coachworks** articulated buses stage simultaneously across Bays 1–8 with overhead amber LED signs alternating between `CRUCIBLE SHUTTLE` / `FALLING BRANCH P&R` and `GO FORGERS!`. Each artic crush-loads ~100 maroon-and-orange-clad fans; the moment doors close, the dispatcher releases the coach onto the bus-only US-460 ramp while the next staged 60-footer rolls immediately into the bay.
* **Early Fill Warning:** Because veteran Forger Nation fans know the system's speed, VDOT overhead highway matrices along I-81 routinely flash `GARAGE 82% FULL` by mid-afternoon and `GARAGE FULL / USE ALTERNATE LOT` well before kickoff during marquee matchups like *The Coal & Iron War*.

### 3. The Great CPI Parking Paradox ("The \$150 Arrogance Tax")
Every season, snooty visiting fans or first-time VIPs scoff at riding a bus (*"Buses are for students and riffraff"*), pay **\$150** for a single-game pass in the close stadium lots beside The Crucible, and learn the hard way that **\$150 buys a short walk to the gate—not a short trip home**.

* **The Official CPI Athletics Fine Print:** Printed verbatim on the back of every single-game campus parking hangtag:
  > *"NOTICE: Game-day parking fees reserve a vehicle space only. Parking location near The Crucible does not grant or imply priority departure from campus following athletic events."*
* **Postgame Evacuation Physics & Road Controls:**
  * The instant the clock hits `0:00`, **Virginia State Police** and CPI Campus Police convert Blacksburg into a rigid outbound flushing grid:
    * **Southgate Drive (`BRT BUSES ONLY`):** Physically barricaded to private automobiles. Every 60-foot BRT articulated shuttle carrying ~100 passengers is waved straight through by State Troopers onto US-460 and the bus-only ramp into Falling Branch.
    * **The \$150 Stadium Lots (`Metered Outbound`):** Drivers who parked 500 yards from The Crucible reach their cars in three minutes—and then sit motionless in a sea of brake lights for 90+ minutes. Barricades block their GPS shortcuts, and troopers force-meter central campus lots north toward **Prices Fork Road** before they can ever reach US-460.
    * **The "South Main Street" Trap (`Business 460`):** Motorists who think they are outsmarting US-460 by cutting through Downtown Blacksburg via South Main Street discover that 20,000 other "clever" drivers had the exact same idea—turning a municipal street full of traffic signals, crosswalks, and turning movements into a multi-hour parking lot all the way to Christiansburg.

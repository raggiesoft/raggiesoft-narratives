# Lore Dossier: CVTA (Coastal Virginia Transit Authority)

## Overview
CVTA is the in-universe counterpart to Hampton Roads Transit (HRT) in the Virginia Beach/Norfolk area.

## Route Naming Convention
`The [number]` or `Route [number]`. These are used interchangeably (e.g. `The 12` or `Route 12` for the Indian River Road bus).

## Fleet & Visual Identity
*   **Primary Vehicle:** CVTA uses a standard 40-foot low-floor city transit bus (specifically a **Gillig Advantage**). It is crucial that the bus has **two sets of doors** (front and middle/back) typical of a city transit Gillig Advantage (not a Suburban layout). New Flyers and over-the-road coach buses (Prevost, MCI, etc.) are **never** used.
*   **Livery:** The livery features a clean white base with blue and yellow sweeping accents and waves. The side prominently displays "CVTA" with "Coastal Virginia Transit Authority" underneath. The front bumper area displays the CVTA acronym and logo.
*   **Fleet Numbering:** Standard 40-foot Gillig buses use **25XX** fleet numbers (e.g. 2525). The 4000-series numbers are reserved for older 29-foot Gillig Advantage Hybrids.

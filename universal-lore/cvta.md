# Lore Dossier: CVTA (Coastal Virginia Transit Authority)

## Overview & The October 1999 Merger (`TRT` + `PenTran` $\rightarrow$ `CVTA`)
**CVTA (*Coastal Virginia Transit Authority*)** is the in-universe counterpart to **Hampton Roads Transit (`HRT`)** in the Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, Hampton, and Newport News region.

*   **The October 1, 1999 Merger:** Mirroring the real-world October 1, 1999 merger of *Tidewater Regional Transit (`TRT`)* and *Pentran*, **CVTA** was formed in **October 1999** by merging **TRT (*Tidewater Regional Transit*, serving the Southside)** and **PenTran (*Peninsula Transit*, serving the Peninsula)**.
*   **Route Naming Convention:** `The [number]` or `Route [number]`. These are used interchangeably (e.g. `The 12` or `Route 12` for the Indian River Road bus; `The 15` or `Route 15` for the Military Highway / Naval Base Norfolk – Emerald Creek Mall / Robert Hall Blvd. bus).

## Fleet & Visual Identity By Era (Strict Chronological Accuracy)

### 1. The Early-2000s Post-Merger Era (`1999–2005`, e.g., `books/katie` in 2001–2002)
*   **Workhorse Vehicles (`900`-Series *Orion V* / `Beacon V` & *Flxible Metro* / `MetroCoach`):**
    *   In **2001–2002**, CVTA's workhorses are **high-floor 40-foot transit buses**: the **`900`-series `Beacon V`** (in-universe counterpart to the **Orion V**, fleet numbers in the **`900` series** such as `#914`, `#918`, `#924`) and the **`MetroCoach Metro`** (in-universe counterpart to the **Flxible Metro**).
    *   **High-Floor Hydraulic Stairwell Wheelchair Lift (`Elevate-U`):** Because `900`-series *Orion V* (`Beacon V`) and *Flxible Metro* (`MetroCoach`) buses are **high-floor** buses (whose interior floor sits roughly 36 inches / 3 feet above the street, NOT low-floor ramp buses), wheelchair passengers board via an **Elevate-U** electro-hydraulic front-stairwell wheelchair lift (the copyright-safe counterpart to *Lift-U*—never use the trademark *Lift-U*). The operator flips a toggle switch so the three front boarding steps flatten into a 100% horizontal steel elevator platform supported by twin vertical hydraulic lift arms and side handrails, lowers the flat platform vertically down the three-foot stairwell drop to the curb with its yellow outer safety barrier lip lowered, lifts the wheelchair rider straight up three feet to the elevated interior floor level (one wheelchair at a time), and stows the lift back into front steps once boarding is complete.
*   **2001–2002 Transitional Livery (Old `TRT` Stripes + `CVTA` Door Sticker):**
    *   In 2001–2002, most Southside buses have not yet been repainted since the October 1999 merger. They still wear the classic **1990s `TRT` (*Tidewater Regional Transit*) livery**—a clean white body with bold horizontal **navy-blue and teal/aqua beltline stripes** running down the flanks—with a white rectangular **`CVTA` (*Coastal Virginia Transit Authority*) vinyl decal sticker** applied right next to the front passenger door over the old agency emblem.

### 2. The Modern Era (`2015–2020s+`, e.g., `books/rachel`, `books/nate`)
*   **Primary Vehicle:** In the modern era, CVTA uses a standard 40-foot low-floor city transit bus (specifically a **Stalwart Advantage**, the in-universe counterpart to the *Gillig Advantage*) with a flip-out front wheelchair ramp and **two sets of doors** (front and middle/back). Prairie Coachworks (New Flyer) and over-the-road coach buses (Sainte-Claire / Prevost, Atlas / MCI, etc.) are **never** used.
*   **Modern Livery:** Clean white base with blue and yellow sweeping accents and waves, prominently displaying "CVTA" with "Coastal Virginia Transit Authority" underneath.
*   **Modern Fleet Numbering (`25XX`):** Standard 40-foot Stalwart Advantage buses on the modern active roster use **25XX** fleet numbers (e.g. `2525`—never use `25XX` in 2001–2002 scenes!). The `4000`-series numbers are reserved for older 29-foot Stalwart Advantage Hybrids.

## Canonical Route Timetables & Alignment Lore

### Route 15 — Naval Base Norfolk / Robert Hall Blvd. (via 8-Lane US-13 Military Highway & Emerald Creek Mall)
Based on the historical 2001–2002 HRT Route 15 schedule, **CVTA Route 15** serves as the primary north–south cross-town spine along **US-13 (`Military Highway`)**, linking **Naval Base Norfolk**, **Little Creek Road**, **Glenrock Mall** (*Military Circle Mall*), **Military Hwy. & Indian River Rd.**, **Emerald Creek Mall** (*Greenbrier Mall*), and **Robert Hall Blvd. / Tidewater Square** (*Chesapeake Square*).

*   **Strict Corridor Geography Rule (`8-Lane US-13 / Military Highway`):**
    *   In this part of Virginia Beach / Norfolk / Chesapeake, **US-13 (`Military Highway`)** is a massive **8-lane divided highway (4 lanes per direction plus turn lanes and a raised median)**.
    *   **Route 15 stays strictly out on 8-lane Military Highway** and **never** deviates onto residential neighborhood streets. Riders living in the nearby residential blocks off Military Highway & Indian River Road walk or roll out to the main commercial corridor bus stop along the 8-lane highway.

*   **Alternating Southern Branch Rule (Monday–Saturday):**
    *   *"On arrival at Old Emerald Creek Rd. and Military Hwy., every other trip will alternate between Robert Hall and Emerald Creek Mall Monday through Saturday."*
*   **Key Timepoints (North to South):**
    1.  **Naval Base Norfolk / Navy Exchange Mall** (`Transfer to Routes 2, 3, 15, 19, 22 & 51`; evenings also serve **Pier 12** and **S & S Gate**)
    2.  **Hampton Blvd. & Little Creek Rd.** (`Transfer to Routes 2 & 15`)
    3.  **Little Creek Rd. & Granby St.** (`Transfer to Routes 1 & 15`)
    4.  **Little Creek Rd. & Evelyn T. Butts Ave.** (`Transfer to Routes 3, 8, 9, 15 & 61`)
    5.  **Robin Hood Rd. & Alameda Ave.**
    6.  **Glenrock Shopping Center** (*JANAF Shopping Ctr.*)
    7.  **Glenrock Mall** (*Military Circle*, `Transfer to Routes 15, 20, 23, 25 & 27`)
    8.  **Military Hwy. & Indian River Rd.** *(Location of David, Katie, and Nathaniel Prairie's single-story rental house in `books/katie` starting October 2001)*
    9.  **Emerald Creek Mall** (*Greenbrier Mall* — served by alternating trips)
    10. **Robert Hall Blvd. & Shopping Ctr. / Tidewater Square** (*Chesapeake Square*, `Transfer to Routes 6, 13, 15, 57, 58 & 60` — served by alternating trips)
*   **Exact Schedule Pattern at `Military Hwy. & Indian River Rd.` (Monday–Saturday Daytime):**
    *   **Southbound (Toward Emerald Creek Mall OR Robert Hall Blvd.):**
        *   Departs **Glenrock Mall** (*Military Circle*) at **`:15` and `:45` past the hour** (`5:45 AM` to `6:45 PM`, then hourly at `:45` until `9:45 PM`).
        *   Arrives at **Military Hwy. & Indian River Rd.** **10 minutes later** at **`:25` and `:55` past the hour**:
            *   **`:25` past the hour** (`6:25 AM`, `7:25 AM`, ..., `6:25 PM`): Branches to **Emerald Creek Mall**, arriving **20 minutes later at `:45` past the hour**.
            *   **`:55` past the hour** (`5:55 AM`, `6:55 AM`, ..., `6:55 PM`, plus `7:55 PM`, `8:55 PM`, `9:55 PM`): Continues to **Robert Hall Blvd.**, arriving **20 minutes later at `:15` past the hour**.
    *   **Northbound (Toward Glenrock Mall & Naval Base Norfolk):**
        *   Departs **Robert Hall Blvd.** at **`:18` past the hour** (`6:18 AM` to `7:18 PM`, plus `:18` evening trips) OR departs **Emerald Creek Mall** at **`:48` past the hour** (`6:48 AM` to `6:48 PM`).
        *   Passes **Military Hwy. & Indian River Rd.** **13 minutes later** at **`:31` and `:01` past the hour** (`6:31 AM`, `7:01 AM`, `7:31 AM`, ..., `6:31 PM`, then hourly at `:31` until `10:31 PM`).
        *   Arrives at **Glenrock Mall** (*Military Circle*) **14 minutes later** at **`:45` and `:15` past the hour**, and reaches **Naval Base Norfolk / Navy Exchange Mall** at **`:30` and `:00`**.

### The 2008 Virginia Beach Mayoral Bus Loop (`#29` $\rightarrow$ `#20` $\rightarrow$ `#15` $\rightarrow$ `#12`) & The Route `#33` Municipal Center Evening Curfew
Based on the real-life 2008 *Ideas for Hampton Roads Transit* mayoral bus ride filmed by Michael P. Ragsdale (where all four 2008 Virginia Beach mayoral candidates were invited to ride the bus, and only candidate Scott Taylor accepted):

*   **In-Universe Blog Name:** ***Ideas for Coastal Virginia Transit***
*   **The 4-Bus Virginia Beach / Norfolk Loop & The Expired 2-Hour Transfer Window:**
    1.  **Start at CCHR (*Community College of Hampton Roads*, Virginia Beach Campus — real-life *TCC Virginia Beach*):** Board **CVTA Route #29** (later renumbered `#26`) from **CCHR** north/east to **Hilltop**.
    2.  **Transfer at Hilltop to Route #20:** Ride **CVTA Route #20** westbound along Virginia Beach Boulevard all the way to **Glenrock Mall** (*Military Circle Mall* in Norfolk). *(Includes a memorable encounter on board the `#20` with a Spanish-speaking citizen, where the candidate speaks Spanish right back to the rider.)*
    3.  **Transfer at Glenrock Mall to Route #15:** Ride **CVTA Route #15** southbound down **US-13 (`Military Highway`)**, filming on board the bus as it crosses the **Eastern Branch of the Elizabeth River** from Norfolk back into Virginia Beach via US-13 southbound.
    4.  **Transfer from Route #15 to Route #12 back to CCHR:** Board **CVTA Route #12** to return to **CCHR**—at which point the **2-hour transfer time limit has already expired**, forcing a cash-fare rider to **pay a second full fare** just to finish a single loop back to their starting point (while daily regular riders carry a **30-day pass**).
*   **Route `#33` & The 6:00 PM Virginia Beach City Council Meeting Catch-22:**
    *   Because the majority of CVTA routes in Virginia Beach stop running around **6:00 PM**, a transit-dependent citizen **cannot attend a 6:00 PM Virginia Beach City Council meeting** at the **Municipal Center** without getting stranded:
        *   **Northbound from Municipal Center (Route `#33`):** The last **Route #33** going all the way to **68th Street** departs at **`6:01 PM`**, and the **`7:01 PM`** trip goes only as far as **19th Street** at the Oceanfront.
        *   **Heading Toward the CCHR Hub (Route `#33`):** The last **Route #33** to **CCHR** departs the Municipal Center at **`6:33 PM`** (at which hour the *only* connecting bus left at CCHR is **Route #36**).
        *   **The `5:30 PM` / `5:33 PM` Connecting Deadline & The `5:48 PM` Last Route `#12` for Katie & Nate:** If a citizen needs to connect at the **CCHR** hub to **Routes #12, #25, #29, or #36** to get home, they **must leave the Municipal Center on the `5:30 PM` (`5:33 PM`) Route #33**—nearly thirty minutes *before* the `6:00 PM` City Council session even begins. Specifically for **Katie and Nathaniel Prairie** (who live off **Military Highway & Indian River Road**), they need **Route #33** at `5:30 PM` / `5:33 PM` from the Municipal Center to catch **Route #12** at CCHR back to Military Highway—because the **last Route #12 from CCHR departs at `5:48 PM`** (twelve minutes before the `6:00 PM` City Council meeting even starts!).
*   **The `Route 1` & `Route 25` Norfolk-vs.-Virginia Beach Border Cliff & The Mayor Myra E. Overton (`Meyera E. Oberndorf`) `Newtown Road` Folklore:**
    *   **Mayor Myra E. Overton (`1988–2008`, *real-life Meyera E. Oberndorf*):** Virginia Beach's 20-year incumbent mayor up through the November 2008 election was personally a sweet, gracious, genuinely nice lady who got her political start on the **Virginia Beach Public Library Board** (which is why the city's flagship **Central Library** on Virginia Beach Boulevard was renamed the **Myra E. Overton Central Library** in her honor), but she was not a strong politician when it came to public transit.
    *   **The Local Political Story (*"To Keep the Riff-Raff Out"*):** Though impossible to confirm, a famous story circulated across Hampton Roads in the 2000s to explain why Virginia Beach's bus service hours were so poor compared to Norfolk's: *so they said, if Mayor Overton had her way, **Route #20 (`Virginia Beach Boulevard`)** would depart Downtown Norfolk, take the Boulevard east to **Newtown Road** at the city line, and turn right back around and stay in Norfolk ("to keep the riff-raff out," of course).*
    *   **Timetable Proof (`route-1.pdf` & `route-25.pdf` in `~/Developer/ai-staging/bus-schedules/cvta/2005/`):**
        *   **`Route 1` (*Downtown Norfolk / Pembroke East*):** On the **Norfolk side** (`Downtown Norfolk` $\leftrightarrow$ `Wards Corner` $\leftrightarrow$ `Ocean View` $\leftrightarrow$ `Amphibious Base`), buses run **every 15–30 minutes** and run late into the night until **`2:15 AM` seven days a week**. Crossing into **Virginia Beach** (`Pleasure House Rd. & Shore Dr.` $\rightarrow$ `Haygood Shopping Center` $\rightarrow$ `Pembroke East`), most buses turn back at `Amphibious Base`, service drops to **hourly and daytime-only** (last bus out of `Pembroke East` at **`6:22 PM`**), and the Sunday timetable reads **`NO SUNDAY SERVICE`**.
        *   **`Route 25` (*Glenrock Mall / Princess Anne Route*):** Runs **every 30 minutes** from `Glenrock Mall` (*Military Circle*) in Norfolk to **`Greenwich Rd. & Newtown Rd.`** at the city line, where **every second bus literally turns around at Newtown Road and stays in Norfolk**, dropping Virginia Beach service down Princess Anne Road to **`CCHR` (*TCC Virginia Beach*)** to **hourly daytime-only** (last bus out of `CCHR` at **`5:53 PM`**, weekdays only).

### The `Shore Drive & Pleasure House Road` CVTA Transfer Point, The 2026 `CBBT` Transit Gap, & Joint `Route 913` (`CVTA` + `ShoreLine Transit` Express, Enacted July 2027)
*   **`Shore Drive & Pleasure House Road` Transfer Point (Foot of the `CBBT`):** Located in northern Virginia Beach right where `US-13` (*Northampton Boulevard*) meets `Shore Drive` at the southern approach to the **17.6-mile Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel (`CBBT`)**, **Shore Drive & Pleasure House Road** serves as the northern Virginia Beach transfer point for **CVTA Routes `#1`, `#27`, and `#35`**.
*   **`ShoreLine Transit` (`SLT` — *In-Universe for `STAR Transit` on Virginia's Eastern Shore*) Fleet & Route Nomenclature:**
    *   **Fleet:** Unlike `CVTA`'s heavy-duty 40-foot low-floor **`Stalwart Advantage`** (`25XX`-series) urban transit coaches with full-width `24×200` two-line-capable amber LED destination signs, **`ShoreLine Transit`** operates highway-geared **`Holt Automotive`** dual-rear-wheel fiberglass **cutaway shuttle buses** equipped with hydraulic wheelchair lifts and compact **single-line amber LED destination signs** (`12–14` standard characters wide) mounted in the sloped fiberglass forehead cap above the cab windshield.
    *   **Route Naming System:** For its `$0.50` local Eastern Shore corridor loops across Accomack and Northampton Counties, **`ShoreLine Transit`** uses **Color Names** (**`Red`**, **`Purple`**, **`Blue`**, **`Yellow`**, **`Silver`**, **`Gold`**, and **`Orange`**) alongside descriptive corridor names along `US-13` (such as the *Northampton Route 13 Express*).
*   **The Pre-July 2027 `CBBT` & Delmarva Transit Gaps (`2026 U.S. Senate Campaign`, `books/katie/e010/c003/p002.md`):**
    *   Prior to July 2027, despite sitting right at the southern foot of the Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel, **neither `CVTA` nor `ShoreLine Transit` (`SLT`)** ran a public bus across the `17.6-mile` Bridge-Tunnel between **Shore Drive & Pleasure House Road** and **Cape Charles** in Northampton County, and at the northern end of Accomack County on `US-13`, **`ShoreLine Transit`** terminated at **Oak Hall / Chincoteague Junction** roughly eleven miles short of **Pocomoke City, Maryland**, where **Maryland Shore Transit** (*Shore Transit*) connects north to **Salisbury** and **Delaware's `First State Transit`** (*DART First State*).
    *   During her **2026 U.S. Senate campaign** (`books/katie/e010/c003/p002.md`), **Katie and Nathaniel Prairie** rode **CVTA Route `#1`** to the **Shore Drive & Pleasure House Road Transfer Point**, where their father **David Prairie** bridged the `CBBT` gap in a rented wheelchair-accessible ramp minivan to **Cape Charles** so they could ride a **ShoreLine Transit** cutaway bus up `US-13` through **Eastville, Exmore, Onley, Accomac, and Oak Hall**—and Katie championed dedicated federal interstate transit funding on the Senate Appropriations Committee to close both gaps permanently.
*   **Enacted Joint Interagency Service (`July 2027+`, `books/katie/e011/c004/p003.md`) — `Route 913` (*Bay Bridge-Tunnel Express*, Modeled on Puget Sound's Joint Interagency *Olympia Express*):**
    *   **Route Number (`913`):** Numbered **`Route 913`**—pairing `CVTA`'s `900`-series regional express numbering with **U.S. Route 13** across the Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel—so Hampton Roads riders recognize a regional express route and Eastern Shore riders immediately recognize the `Route 13` spine.
    *   **Two-Bus Hourly Clockface Pool (`60-Minute Headways` on a `120-Minute Round-Trip Cycle`, Zero Cross-Bay Deadhead):** Covering `~31.3 miles` one way between **Shore Drive & Pleasure House Road (`Virginia Beach`)** and **Cape Charles (`Mason Avenue & Fig Street`)**, each one-way trip runs **`~45 minutes`** (`17.6 miles` of 55-mph uninterrupted bridge-tunnel trestles and tunnels plus terminal approaches) with **`15 minutes` of terminal layover/recovery** = a clean **`60-minute` one-way block** (`120-minute` round-trip cycle). Each agency contributes **1 revenue vehicle** from its home side of the Bay:
        1. **`CVTA` Vehicle (`1 × 40-Foot Low-Floor Stalwart Advantage`, e.g., `Coach #2514`):** Pulls out of CVTA's Southside division to depart **northbound from `Shore Drive & Pleasure House Road` on even hours (`:00`)** (`arriving Cape Charles at :45`) and departs **southbound from `Cape Charles` on odd hours (`:00`)** (`arriving Shore Drive & Pleasure House Road at :45`), returning to its Virginia Beach/Norfolk garage at night with zero cross-bay deadhead.
        2. **`ShoreLine Transit` Vehicle (`1 × Highway-Geared Holt Automotive Dual-Rear-Wheel Cutaway Bus`):** Pulls out of ShoreLine's Eastern Shore yard to depart **southbound from `Cape Charles` on even hours (`:00`)** (`arriving Shore Drive & Pleasure House Road at :45`) and departs **northbound from `Shore Drive & Pleasure House Road` on odd hours (`:00`)** (`arriving Cape Charles at :45`), returning to the Eastern Shore at night with zero Southside deadhead.
        3. **The `:22` Mid-Bay Trestle Meet:** Departing opposing terminals simultaneously at the top of every hour (`:00`), the `CVTA` 40-foot transit coach and the `ShoreLine Transit` cutaway bus pass each other out on the Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel trestles at roughly **`:22` past every hour**.
    *   **`FTA Section 5311(f)` Funding, `$1.00` `Class 8` Public-Transit Toll, & Mandatory 100% Pass Cross-Honoring:** Signed into law by **President Clara Morales** in **June 2027**, Senator Katie Prairie's bipartisan **Federal Rural & Bridge-Tunnel Transit Connectivity** appropriation funds both agencies' cross-bay operating blocks under **`FTA Section 5311(f)`**, bills the Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel District's **`Class 8` ("Public Transit Bus") `$1.00 per crossing` toll** automatically via each agency's fleet transponder, and enforces a **mandatory statutory stipulation requiring 100% reciprocal cross-honoring of all fares, transfers, and passes between `CVTA` and `ShoreLine Transit`**—so riders tap or show either pass regardless of whether that hour's `Route 913` trip is operated by `CVTA`'s 40-foot coach or `ShoreLine Transit`'s cutaway bus.
    *   **Official Municipal Amber LED Destination Sign Programming (`Route 913`):**
        *   **`CVTA` (`40-Foot Stalwart Advantage` — Full-Width `24×200` Two-Line-Capable Amber LED Matrix):**
            *   **Northbound** (*Single-Line Full-Height, 2-Frame Alternating Flip*):

                *   **Frame 2:** Giant `913` | Line 1: `VA BEACH` / Line 2: `via CBBT`
        *   **`ShoreLine Transit` (`Holt Automotive` Cutaway Bus — Narrow Single-Line Forehead Cap Amber LED Sign, `12–14` Chars Max):**

### The `CVTA Route #3 (Chesapeake Boulevard)` Discontinuation (`First View Street`, `Gate 4`, `Bellinger Boulevard`, & `Navy Exchange / NEX`)
*   **Real-World `HRT` $\rightarrow$ In-Universe `CVTA` Service Cut:** Mirroring real-world Hampton Roads Transit (`HRT`) service reductions, **`CVTA` discontinued service on `Route #3 (Chesapeake Boulevard)` to `First View Street` in Ocean View, `Gate 4` of Naval Station Norfolk, `Bellinger Boulevard`, and the `Navy Exchange (NEX)`**.
*   **Impact on Ocean View Residents & Car-Free Sailors (`books/katie/e011/c006/p001.md`):** Cutting that segment severed the direct one-seat bus link connecting `First View Street` and Ocean View neighborhoods into `Gate 4`, `Bellinger Boulevard`, and the `Navy Exchange`, stranding transit-dependent Ocean View residents, junior enlisted sailors without cars, and military spouses behind long multi-seat transfers. Never depict baseline pre-restoration `CVTA Route #3` buses serving `First View Street`, `Gate 4`, `Bellinger Boulevard`, or the `NEX` as if the cut hadn't happened; instead, in **October 2027** (`books/katie/e011/c006/p001.md`), **U.S. Senator Katie Prairie** writes dedicated federal defense-community transit operating funds into the ***Naval Station Norfolk Fleet Readiness, Enlisted Housing, & Military Family Restoration Act*** specifically to fund restoring direct `CVTA` bus service across `First View Street`, `Gate 4`, `Bellinger Boulevard`, and the `Navy Exchange` alongside cross-honored sailor/spouse transit passes.

# Lore Dossier: CVTA (Coastal Virginia Transit Authority)

## Overview & The October 1999 Merger (`TRT` + `PenTran` $\rightarrow$ `CVTA`)
**CVTA (*Coastal Virginia Transit Authority*)** is the in-universe counterpart to **Hampton Roads Transit (`HRT`)** in the Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, Hampton, and Newport News region.

*   **The October 1, 1999 Merger:** Mirroring the real-world October 1, 1999 merger of *Tidewater Regional Transit (`TRT`)* and *Pentran*, **CVTA** was formed in **October 1999** by merging **TRT (*Tidewater Regional Transit*, serving the Southside)** and **PenTran (*Peninsula Transit*, serving the Peninsula)**.
*   **Route Naming Convention:** `The [number]` or `Route [number]`. These are used interchangeably (e.g. `The 12` or `Route 12` for the Indian River Road bus; `The 15` or `Route 15` for the Military Highway / Naval Base Norfolk – Emerald Creek Mall / Robert Hall Blvd. bus).

## Fleet & Visual Identity By Era (Strict Chronological Accuracy)

### 1. The Early-2000s Post-Merger Era (`1999–2005`, e.g., `books/katie` in 2001–2002)
*   **Workhorse Vehicles (`900`-Series *Orion V* / `Beacon V` & *Flxible Metro* / `MetroCoach`):**
    *   In **2001–2002**, CVTA's workhorses are **high-floor 40-foot transit buses**: the **`900`-series `Beacon V`** (in-universe counterpart to the **Orion V**, fleet numbers in the **`900` series** such as `#914`, `#918`, `#924`) and the **`MetroCoach Metro`** (in-universe counterpart to the **Flxible Metro**).
    *   **High-Floor Hydraulic Step-Lift Boarding:** Because `900`-series *Orion V* (`Beacon V`) and *Flxible Metro* (`MetroCoach`) buses are **high-floor** buses (not low-floor ramp buses), wheelchair passengers board via the **electro-hydraulic front-doorway step-lift**. The operator flips a toggle switch so the three front boarding steps flatten into a horizontal steel elevator platform, lowers the platform down to the curb with its yellow outer safety barrier lip raised, lifts the wheelchair rider three feet up to the high interior floor level (one wheelchair at a time), and converts the lift back into steps once boarding is complete.
*   **2001–2002 Transitional Livery (Old `TRT` Stripes + `CVTA` Door Sticker):**
    *   In 2001–2002, most Southside buses have not yet been repainted since the October 1999 merger. They still wear the classic **1990s `TRT` (*Tidewater Regional Transit*) livery**—a clean white body with bold horizontal **navy-blue and teal/aqua beltline stripes** running down the flanks—with a white rectangular **`CVTA` (*Coastal Virginia Transit Authority*) vinyl decal sticker** applied right next to the front passenger door over the old agency emblem.

### 2. The Modern Era (`2015–2020s+`, e.g., `books/rachel`, `books/nate`)
*   **Primary Vehicle:** In the modern era, CVTA uses a standard 40-foot low-floor city transit bus (specifically a **Stalwart Advantage**, the in-universe counterpart to the *Gillig Advantage*) with a flip-out front wheelchair ramp and **two sets of doors** (front and middle/back). Prairie Coachworks (New Flyer) and over-the-road coach buses (Sainte-Claire / Prevost, Atlas / MCI, etc.) are **never** used.
*   **Modern Livery:** Clean white base with blue and yellow sweeping accents and waves, prominently displaying "CVTA" with "Coastal Virginia Transit Authority" underneath.
*   **Modern Fleet Numbering (`25XX`):** Standard 40-foot Stalwart Advantage buses on the modern active roster use **25XX** fleet numbers (e.g. `2525`—never use `25XX` in 2001–2002 scenes!). The `4000`-series numbers are reserved for older 29-foot Stalwart Advantage Hybrids.

## Canonical Route Timetables & Alignment Lore

### Route 15 — Naval Base Norfolk / Robert Hall Blvd. (via 8-Lane US-13 Military Highway & Emerald Creek Mall)
Based on the historical 2001–2002 HRT Route 15 schedule, **CVTA Route 15** serves as the primary north–south cross-town spine along **US-13 (`Military Highway`)**, linking **Naval Base Norfolk**, **Little Creek Road**, **Glenrock Mall** (*Military Circle Mall*), **Military Hwy. & Indian River Rd.**, **Emerald Creek Mall** (*Greenbrier Mall*), and **Robert Hall Blvd. / Tidewater Square** (*Chesapeake Square*).

*   **Strict Corridor Geography Rule (`8-Lane US-13 / Military Highway`):**
    *   In this part of Virginia Beach / Norfolk / Chesapeake, **US-13 (`Military Highway`)** is a massive **8-lane divided highway (4 lanes per direction plus turn lanes and a raised median)**.
    *   **Route 15 stays strictly out on 8-lane Military Highway** and **never** deviates onto residential neighborhood streets. Riders living in the nearby residential blocks off Military Highway & Indian River Road walk or roll out to the main commercial corridor bus stop along the 8-lane highway.

*   **Alternating Southern Branch Rule (Monday–Saturday):**
    *   *"On arrival at Old Emerald Creek Rd. and Military Hwy., every other trip will alternate between Robert Hall and Emerald Creek Mall Monday through Saturday."*
*   **Key Timepoints (North to South):**
    1.  **Naval Base Norfolk / Navy Exchange Mall** (`Transfer to Routes 2, 3, 15, 19, 22 & 51`; evenings also serve **Pier 12** and **S & S Gate**)
    2.  **Hampton Blvd. & Little Creek Rd.** (`Transfer to Routes 2 & 15`)
    3.  **Little Creek Rd. & Granby St.** (`Transfer to Routes 1 & 15`)
    4.  **Little Creek Rd. & Evelyn T. Butts Ave.** (`Transfer to Routes 3, 8, 9, 15 & 61`)
    5.  **Robin Hood Rd. & Alameda Ave.**
    6.  **Glenrock Shopping Center** (*JANAF Shopping Ctr.*)
    7.  **Glenrock Mall** (*Military Circle*, `Transfer to Routes 15, 20, 23, 25 & 27`)
    8.  **Military Hwy. & Indian River Rd.** *(Location of David, Katie, and Nathaniel Prairie's single-story rental house in `books/katie` starting October 2001)*
    9.  **Emerald Creek Mall** (*Greenbrier Mall* — served by alternating trips)
    10. **Robert Hall Blvd. & Shopping Ctr. / Tidewater Square** (*Chesapeake Square*, `Transfer to Routes 6, 13, 15, 57, 58 & 60` — served by alternating trips)
*   **Exact Schedule Pattern at `Military Hwy. & Indian River Rd.` (Monday–Saturday Daytime):**
    *   **Southbound (Toward Emerald Creek Mall OR Robert Hall Blvd.):**
        *   Departs **Glenrock Mall** (*Military Circle*) at **`:15` and `:45` past the hour** (`5:45 AM` to `6:45 PM`, then hourly at `:45` until `9:45 PM`).
        *   Arrives at **Military Hwy. & Indian River Rd.** **10 minutes later** at **`:25` and `:55` past the hour**:
            *   **`:25` past the hour** (`6:25 AM`, `7:25 AM`, ..., `6:25 PM`): Branches to **Emerald Creek Mall**, arriving **20 minutes later at `:45` past the hour**.
            *   **`:55` past the hour** (`5:55 AM`, `6:55 AM`, ..., `6:55 PM`, plus `7:55 PM`, `8:55 PM`, `9:55 PM`): Continues to **Robert Hall Blvd.**, arriving **20 minutes later at `:15` past the hour**.
    *   **Northbound (Toward Glenrock Mall & Naval Base Norfolk):**
        *   Departs **Robert Hall Blvd.** at **`:18` past the hour** (`6:18 AM` to `7:18 PM`, plus `:18` evening trips) OR departs **Emerald Creek Mall** at **`:48` past the hour** (`6:48 AM` to `6:48 PM`).
        *   Passes **Military Hwy. & Indian River Rd.** **13 minutes later** at **`:31` and `:01` past the hour** (`6:31 AM`, `7:01 AM`, `7:31 AM`, ..., `6:31 PM`, then hourly at `:31` until `10:31 PM`).
        *   Arrives at **Glenrock Mall** (*Military Circle*) **14 minutes later** at **`:45` and `:15` past the hour**, and reaches **Naval Base Norfolk / Navy Exchange Mall** at **`:30` and `:00`**.

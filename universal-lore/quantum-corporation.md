# Lore Dossier: Quantum Corporation

**Headquarters:** 1 Quantum Way, Redmond, WA
**Key Facilities:** Building 33 (Executive Suites)

## Overview
Quantum Corporation is the in-universe counterpart to Microsoft. It is a massive, global technology conglomerate headquartered in Redmond, Washington. 

## Campus Architecture (The Redmond Grid)
**AI DIRECTIVE:** The Quantum Corporation campus in Redmond is a strict **1:1 architectural match** to the real-world Microsoft Redmond Campus. Any public knowledge regarding Microsoft's building numbers, layout, cafes, or shuttles applies directly to Quantum. 

**Notable Buildings & Landmarks:**
*   **Building 33:** The Executive Suites. This is the traditional seat of power where the CEO and upper management reside. While Cassandra officially holds the top suite here, she frequently vacates it to work in the engineering bullpens.
*   **Building 34:** Famous for its massive cafeteria and central gathering spaces. It serves as a major social and logistical hub for the engineers.
*   **Building 35:** A major engineering hub and the specific operational base for **Jessica Ragsdale** (Quantum Senior Technical Program Manager).
*   **Building 87 (The Hardware Labs):** Home to Quantum's hardware design divisions and the **Anechoic Chamber** (the quietest room on Earth). This is where hardware like the Quantum Zephyr was engineered and tested.
*   **Building 92:** The Quantum Visitor Center and Company Store. This is the primary public-facing building for tourists and families.
*   **Building 99:** Quantum Research (equivalent to Microsoft Research), housing the most advanced, theoretical, and unreleased projects.
*   **Buildings 16 & 17:** The historic nerve centers for the core **Quantum OS** architecture teams.
*   **Studio A, B, C, & D:** The sprawling creative and gaming hubs located near The Commons. This is where franchises like *Starfall 3* and *Iron Crusade 3* are managed and playtested.
*   **The Commons:** The sprawling retail and dining complex at the heart of the campus.
*   **Lake Keswick:** The central pond surrounded by the original 1980s 6-pack of buildings (Buildings 1-6). Named after founder William Keswick, it is steeped in early company lore.
*   **The Treehouses:** A network of literal, high-end outdoor treehouse workspaces built into the Pacific Northwest canopy on campus. These provide incredibly grounded, sensory-friendly outdoor meeting spaces that perfectly align with Cassandra's "Mountain Vanguard" sensibilities.
*   **RedWest Campus:** A massive satellite campus extension located just across the highway, operating as a self-contained hub for various cloud and web services teams.

## Leadership Timeline
*   **William Keswick:** The visionary founder of Quantum, acting as the in-universe counterpart to Bill Gates. Keswick laid the original foundation for the empire and established the core culture that Cassandra would later inherit and protect. Note: Cassandra is not a 1:1 stand-in for any real person; she is an original character who brings her own unique, ground-level sovereign leadership to the throne.
*   **Arthur Alden:** The brilliant co-founder of Quantum, acting as the in-universe counterpart to Paul Allen. He famously coined the original company name and architected its earliest foundational software. He left active daily operations in 1983 due to health issues, but retained his board seat and leveraged his massive billionaire fortune to become a legendary Seattle philanthropist, funding major aerospace initiatives, sports teams, and science museums (like the eventual Nebula Museum).
*   **Steve Ballantine:** The legendary, high-kinetic CEO who led Quantum through the 2000s, acting as the in-universe counterpart to Steve Ballmer. He famously held the line and protected the empire until his mandate was complete, formally handing the keys to Building 33 to Cassandra in 2014. He is a proud alumni of Northwest Pacific University (NPU), and Quantum maintains a massive presence at the NPU Open House every year in large part due to his ongoing loyalty to the school (and the fact that his own children attended).
*   **2014 - Present:** Cassandra Vance ascends to the role of Chief Executive Officer (CEO). Her leadership style completely disrupts the traditional executive mold. She actively codes side-by-side with her engineers, refuses the standard executive "bubble" (like blacked-out SUVs), and fiercely prefers commuting via public transit (using her wheelchair ramp on TAPS buses).

## Security & Access
*   **The Badge System:** Quantum uses a strict, color-coded RFID badge system to control access across its massive campus. 
    *   **Blue Badges:** Full-Time Employees (FTE). Cassandra holds a Blue badge with absolute, unrestricted access to all facilities, including the highly secure Building 33.
    *   **Orange Badges:** Contractors and Vendors. 
    *   **The David Anomaly:** David (Cassandra's younger brother and Licensed Practical Nurse) holds an Orange contractor badge. However, because his medical duty requires him to be by Cassandra's side 24/7 to keep her alive, his Orange badge is hardcoded with a massive security override. It perfectly mirrors Cassandra's Blue badge permissions, allowing an Orange badge contractor unprecedented, unrestricted access to the highest levels of Building 33.

# Lore Dossier: Washington D.C., Maryland, and Virginia (The DMV) Transit

## Capital Transit
**Capital Transit** is the in-universe counterpart to WMATA (Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority). It operates the bus network and the heavy-rail subway system across the DMV.

*   **Naming Rule:** It is 100% legally safe to refer to the rail system colloquially as "the Metro" (e.g., "Cassandra took the Metro to Capitol Hill"). "Metro" is a generic, universally un-trademarkable transit term used globally.
*   **Fare Card:** "CapitalPass" (the in-universe counterpart to SmarTrip).
*   **Lore/Geographic Context:** Cassandra Vance, David, and Casey grew up in Virginia Beach, VA (in the CVTA transit footprint). Because of frequent childhood and family trips up to Northern Virginia and Washington D.C., the Vance pack possesses deep, foundational knowledge of the DMV transit grids. They do not navigate Capital Transit like tourists; they navigate it like locals (e.g., explicitly referring to the Yellow Line's Charles R. Fenwick Bridge rather than just the generic "14th Street Bridge").

## DMV CapitalPass Partner Agencies (Fragmented Network)
The DMV region maintains its highly fragmented transit network, all linked by the CapitalPass:
*   **Capital Loop:** The in-universe counterpart to the DC Circulator.
*   **ARC (Arlington Regional Coaches):** The in-universe counterpart to Arlington Transit (ART).
*   **BOLT:** The in-universe counterpart to DASH (Alexandria Transit Company).
*   **Fairfax Link:** The in-universe counterpart to the Fairfax Connector.
*   **KEY Bus:** The in-universe counterpart to the CUE Bus (Fairfax City).
*   **Loudoun Transit:** The in-universe counterpart to Loudoun County Transit.
*   **NovaRide:** The in-universe counterpart to OmniRide (Prince William County / PRTC).
*   **Glide:** The in-universe counterpart to Ride On (Montgomery County).
*   **PG Coach:** The in-universe counterpart to TheBus (Prince George's County).
*   **STA (State Transit Administration):** The in-universe counterpart to MTA Maryland. Just like in real life, their local proprietary card (the *ChesapeakeCard*, taking the place of the CharmCard) is 100% interoperable with CapitalPass.
*   **DRE (Dominion Railway Express):** The in-universe counterpart to VRE (Virginia Railway Express).
*   **MAC (Maryland Area Commuter):** The in-universe counterpart to MARC train service.

## U.S. Senator Katie Prairie's Famous `ARTS` + Capital Transit Commute (`NFK` $\rightarrow$ `ALX` $\rightarrow$ Yellow Line via Charles R. Fenwick Bridge $\rightarrow$ `L'Enfant Plaza` $\rightarrow$ Blue Line to `Capitol South`)
Inspired by Senator Joe Biden's famous Wilmington (`WIL`) to Washington (`WAS`) Amtrak commute—adapted for an openly autistic, full-time power-wheelchair-using U.S. Senator from Hampton Roads:

*   **Jumping Off `ARTS` Business Class at `ALX` (*Alexandria Union Station*) via the `ARTS Mobile Lift` Instead of `WAS` (*Washington Union Station*):**
    *   When traveling between Hampton Roads (**Norfolk Station, `NFK`**) and the U.S. Capitol, **U.S. Senator Katie Prairie (`D-Virginia`)**, her twin brother **Nathaniel ("Nate") Prairie**, and their father **David Prairie** buy regular passenger tickets in **ARTS Business Class**—specifically so they can enjoy the **complimentary non-alcoholic beverages (free ginger ale, soda, and coffee) from the Café Car**—and intentionally detrain from the **ARTS (*American Rail Transit Service*) Northeast Regional** at **`ALX` (*Alexandria*)** rather than riding all the way into **`WAS` (*Washington Union Station*)**.
    *   **Autistic Sensory Logic & The Low-Level Platform `ARTS Mobile Lift`:** `WAS` (*Washington Union Station*) is a massive, echoing, sensory-overloading concourse with booming acoustics, dense crowds, and a slow, crawling rail approach over the Long Bridge and through the First Street Tunnel. By contrast, **`ALX`** is a small, calm, open-air brick station in Old Town Alexandria with a **low-level red-brick platform**, where the station attendant rolls the portable **`ARTS Mobile Lift`** up to the open Business Class vestibule door to lower Katie and Nate in their compact `SwiftChair` power wheelchairs down to the brick platform so they can cross directly to the adjacent **`King St–Old Town`** Capital Transit Metro station.

*   **The Insider Yellow-to-Blue Line Shortcut (`Charles R. Fenwick Bridge` $\rightarrow$ `L'Enfant Plaza` $\rightarrow$ `Capitol South`):**
    *   While a tourist at `King St–Old Town` might board the **Blue Line** because it runs one-seat to `Capitol South`, seasoned DMV transit riders know the Blue Line takes the long western loop through Arlington (`Rosslyn`), `Foggy Bottom`, and downtown D.C.
    *   Instead, Senator Prairie and Nate tap their **`CapitalPass`** cards at `King St–Old Town`, board the **Capital Transit Yellow Line**, cross the Potomac River into D.C. over the **Charles R. Fenwick Bridge**, transfer at **`L'Enfant Plaza`** to the **Blue Line** (or Orange/Silver Line), and ride two stops to **`Capitol South`**—making `"Is that Senator Prairie getting off the ARTS train at ALX to catch the Yellow Line?"` a signature sight for Virginia commuters.

# Lore Dossier: Sterling Plaza

**Location:** Meridian, Washington
**Height:** 135 Stories
**Owner:** Arthur Sterling / The Sterling Family

## Overview
Because Meridian City sits in a valley floor, Arthur Sterling’s 135-story vertical city is visible for dozens of miles in every direction. It casts a massive shadow over the I-5/I-96 interchange, serving as an inescapable navigational beacon for anyone driving through the county.

It is a masterpiece of vertical integration, holding corporate offices, luxury residential condos, and heavy transit infrastructure (The Sterling Pass / Orange Line). 

## AI Directive: The Crown Point Connection (The Towering Inferno)
In this universe, the real-world 1974 disaster film *The Towering Inferno* was never made. Instead, the events of that film are treated as established, historical fact: on December 16, 1974, a catastrophic electrical fire destroyed the newly built 135-story **Crown Point Tower in San Francisco**.

While the media and the San Francisco developers manufactured a fierce West Coast skyscraper "arms race," Arthur Sterling completely ignored it. He paid absolutely no attention to Crown Point, focusing entirely on over-engineering Sterling Plaza into an unyielding fortress to protect his disabled children. It wasn't until Arthur saw the evening news broadcasting a fire breaking out on Crown Point's 81st floor that the connection was forged. Because the San Francisco developers cut corners for speed, their tower burned, while Sterling Plaza survived. 

When major Hollywood studios refused to touch the San Francisco tragedy, Arthur Sterling personally stepped in and funded two separate films: a terrifyingly dry, feature-length documentary (which became mandatory viewing in engineering ethics classes) and a massive Hollywood drama based on the event. When writing scenes involving the scale or architecture of Sterling Plaza, the AI should heavily reference the tone and cinematic architectural dread of the real-world *Towering Inferno*. The Plaza feels like a living, breathing machine of glass and steel that dwarfs the humans inside it—the surviving, over-engineered sister tower to the one that burned.

## The Vance-Whitaker Crossover (Ruby Mountain)
Despite their sovereign isolation at 7,000 feet, Victoria Vance utilizes Sterling Plaza as the Vanguard's primary lowland anchor:
*   **The Corporate Office:** The official, legal corporate headquarters for The Prospector's Keep LLC is located inside Sterling Plaza. This allows Victoria to conduct high-level financial and legal warfare (like managing the Klondike Trust) from a legitimate, imposing urban fortress rather than a snowy mountain peak.
*   **The Pressurized Condo:** Victoria maintains a highly specialized, luxury residential condo within the Plaza. This condo is custom-pressurized. Because Johnny suffers from severe high-altitude respiratory issues and usually requires a breathing apparatus, this pressurized sea-level environment allows him to exist comfortably on the coast without his medical gear whenever the pack needs to operate in the lowlands.

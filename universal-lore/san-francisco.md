# Lore Dossier: San Francisco

## Weather & Geography
*   **The Summer Marine Layer (Karl the Fog):** The San Francisco summer fog is a massive, relentless physical presence caused by hot inland temperatures pulling cold ocean air through the Golden Gate. 
    *   **Anachronism Warning:** The anthropomorphized nickname **"Karl the Fog"** did NOT exist until the famous `@KarlTheFog` Twitter account was created in **August 2010**. For any stories set before 2010 (like Luna & Leo's 2003 trip), the fog should NOT be referred to as "Karl." It should simply be called the marine layer, the summer fog, or a wall of gray.

# Ocean State Transit Authority (OSTA) - East Bay Transit Spine

This document outlines the core transit spine bridging the "Wickford Void" and connecting Braxton, Newport, and Providence via OSTA (Ocean State Transit Authority).

## The Hub: Jamestown Bridge Park & Ride
**Location:** I-895 at Route 1A (Corresponds to the real-life Route 138 at Route 1A interchange / North Kingstown Park & Ride).
**Function:** The critical transfer point and border checkpoint where the state's regional routes converge.

## OSTA Core Routes

### OSTA Route 14: The Braxton Express
* **Route:** Braxton Central Terminal ↔ Jamestown Bridge Park & Ride ↔ PVD Airport ↔ Kennedy Plaza (Providence).
* **Service Pattern:** Operates **Express** from Braxton Central Terminal to the Jamestown Bridge Park & Ride. After the Park & Ride, it operates **Local** straight up the US-1 corridor to PVD Airport and Providence Kennedy Plaza.

### OSTA Route 12: The Newport/Providence Link
* **Route:** Newport Gateway Center ↔ Jamestown Bridge Park & Ride ↔ PVD Airport ↔ Kennedy Plaza (Providence).
* **Service Pattern:** Operates **Express** out of Newport, crossing both the Newport Pell Bridge and the Jamestown Bridge (I-895 "The Waiver") to reach the Jamestown Bridge Park & Ride. After the Park & Ride, it turns North and operates **Local**, serving the exact same stops as Route 14 up the US-1 corridor to PVD and Providence.

### OSTA Route 64: The URI / Kingston Connector
* **Route:** A direct 1:1 functional equivalent of the real-life RIPTA Route 64, with the vital addition of servicing the **Jamestown Bridge Park & Ride**. Connects Newport to Rhode Island State University and Kingston Station.

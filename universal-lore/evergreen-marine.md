# Lore Dossier: Evergreen Marine

## Overview
**Evergreen Marine** is the in-universe counterpart to Washington State Ferries (WSF). It operates the massive fleet of green-and-white passenger and auto ferries that connect the mainland of Washington State to the San Juan Islands, the Kitsap Peninsula, and Vancouver Island.

## Fares & Integration (The CASCADE Anomaly)
While the TAPS network (buses and light rail) operates seamlessly on the CASCADE smart card system, the State of Washington fiercely protects its maritime vehicle boarding revenue. 
*   **Passenger Fares:** Walk-on passengers can tap their CASCADE card (or Sterling Pass) to board an Evergreen Marine vessel seamlessly.
*   **Vehicle Fares:** The CASCADE system is **not** accepted for vehicle boarding. Drivers are forced into massive, archaic tollbooth lines at the terminals where they must pay separate state fares. This causes massive bottlenecks, specifically at major terminals like Anacortes.

## The Anacortes Terminal & Route 96
The Anacortes ferry terminal serves as the westernmost origin point for the unfinished **Interstate 96**. This terminal is specifically designed to swallow up heavy international and island vehicle traffic and shoot it east. It is here that Johnny Vance makes landfall when escaping Friday Harbor, where the Vance Vanguard's Ridgebacks wait to extract him.

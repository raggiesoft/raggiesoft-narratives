# The Quantum Directive: The Caretakers (Book Cover Mapping)

This document maps the characters depicted on the official series cover (`raggiesoft-books/images/covers/quantum-casey.jpg`).

## Background & Aesthetic
- **Left Side (The Seattle Era):** The sprawling, low-rise corporate tech campus in Redmond, Washington (Quantum Headquarters), surrounded by evergreen pine trees at twilight.
- **Right Side (The East Coast Era):** The warm, massive brick-and-wood architecture of the historic Gilded Age New England mansion (Highcliff in Newport / Coolidge Hill in Cambridge).

## Character Mapping (Left to Right)

1. **David Vance (Top Left):** The young Caucasian man standing on the far left. He serves as the primary caregiver and emotional anchor for the pack.
2. **Megan Delaney (Middle Left, Standing):** The blonde woman standing just behind Casey. As the oldest Delaney sibling, she acts as a structural anchor for the family.
3. **Casey Vance (Bottom Left, Kneeling):** The teenage girl with her hair tied back, kneeling and hugging Cassandra’s arm. She relies on the deep-pressure grounding provided by the pack.
4. **Liz Carter (Middle Left, Tucked):** The young woman with dark hair tucked between Casey and Cassandra. David's best friend, primary partner, and an honorary sister who shares his caretaking baseline.
5. **Cassandra Vance (Center):** The fierce, pale-skinned woman with long dark hair wearing the sharp executive suit and seated in her manual power-assist wheelchair. She is the youngest executive at Quantum and the absolute financial and logistical commander of the entire pack.
6. **Erin Delaney (Bottom Right, Kneeling):** The blonde woman kneeling directly to the right of Cassandra, her chin resting affectionately near Cassandra's shoulder. Fiercely protective and heavily integrated into David's daily perimeter.
7. **Chloe Delaney (Top Right, Standing):** The blonde woman standing on the right with her hair pulled back.
8. **Claire Delaney (Far Right, Leaning In):** The young woman with curly brown hair leaning heavily into the group from the far right edge. 

*(Note: While identical twins Meredith and Morgan Delaney are absolutely critical members of the pack and the primary exfiltration specialists, this specific cover composition focuses on the core eight individuals present in the famous "Christmas Eve Puppy Pile" structural alignment.)*

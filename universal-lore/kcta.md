# KCTA (Kitsap County Transit Authority)

**Real World Equivalent:** Kitsap Transit

**Overview**
The Kitsap County Transit Authority (KCTA) operates the public transportation network across the Kitsap Peninsula, including Bremerton, Silverdale, and Poulsbo. Unlike TAPS (which covers the eastern side of the Puget Sound), KCTA is an entirely independent agency.

**Fleet and Aesthetics**
KCTA coaches are painted in a distinctive green-and-white livery. The primary transit hub is the Bremerton Transportation Center, located immediately outside the Evergreen Marine Ferry Terminal, allowing for seamless transfers from the cross-sound ferries to inland peninsula routes.

**Fare Media & Accessibility**
*   **No TAPS Integration:** Because KCTA is independent, it does *not* accept TAPS Passes, nor does it recognize the Quantum Blue Badge for subsidized fare. Passengers arriving from Seattle must pay with cash, debit, or a distinct KCTA transit card.
*   **RRFP Acceptance:** Like all Washington State transit agencies, KCTA legally honors the **Regional Reduced Fare Permit (RRFP)** for seniors and persons with disabilities. Cassandra Vance and David Vance utilize their RRFPs when crossing into Kitsap County. 
*   **Accessibility:** All KCTA coaches are fully equipped with kneeling suspensions and ramps, and feature federal dual-tie-down bays for wheelchair users.

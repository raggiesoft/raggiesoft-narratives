# Lore Dossier: Chicago Transit

This document maps the real-world Chicago transit networks and fare media to their in-universe Raggiesoft equivalents.

## Transit Agencies

*   **CMA (Chicago Metropolitan Authority):** The in-universe counterpart to the CTA (Chicago Transit Authority). Operates the city bus grid and the iconic elevated and subway trains. Instead of the "L", locals refer to the train system simply as **"The Loop"** or **"The Elevated"**.
*   **Heartland Commuter:** The in-universe counterpart to Metra. Operates the extensive, multi-line commuter rail network connecting the Chicago downtown terminal stations (like Union Station) to the outer suburbs.
*   **Stride Transit:** The in-universe counterpart to Pace. Operates the suburban bus network that feeds into the CMA and Heartland Commuter systems.

## Fare Media & History

*   **2003 Era (CityCard):** During the events of *Luna and Leo* (Summer 2003), transit riders use the **CityCard** (the equivalent to the real-life magnetic stripe Transit Cards and the early RFID Chicago Card). Stella has to buy paper CityCards from the automated vending machines, which get sucked in and popped back out by the turnstiles.
*   **Modern Era (OmniPass):** The in-universe counterpart to Ventra. Introduced roughly a decade later (matching the 2013-2014 Ventra debut), the **OmniPass** is a unified, tap-to-pay contactless smart card that works seamlessly across CMA, Heartland Commuter, and Stride Transit. 


*   **Lakeshore Interurban:** The in-universe counterpart to the South Shore Line (NICTD). This electric commuter rail line operates out of the downtown Chicago stations and runs east, hugging the southern coast of Lake Michigan to connect Chicago with the Indiana suburbs and cities (like Hammond, Gary, and South Bend).

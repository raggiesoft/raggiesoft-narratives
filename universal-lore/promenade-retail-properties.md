# Promenade Retail Properties

**Promenade Retail Properties** is the massive real estate development and management corporation responsible for operating the high-end "Promenade" brand of shopping malls across New England.

## Flagship Locations
* **Providence Promenade** (In-Universe equivalent of Providence Place): The massive flagship retail center in downtown Providence, Rhode Island.
* **Oakhaven Promenade**: The sprawling, towering commercial epicenter located deep in the Oakhaven suburb of Braxton, Rhode Island. 

## Lore & Design
* The Promenade brand is known for towering glass entrances, massive sprawling parking lots, and being heavily integrated into local public transit systems (like the BTA in Braxton and OSTA in Providence).
* They serve as the primary commercial shopping hubs for both major Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities in the region.

# Lore Dossier: Luna and Leo Character Profiles

## Leo Porter
* **Conditions:** Autistic, Cerebral Palsy.
* **Mobility:** Non-ambulatory. He uses a basic, folding manual wheelchair. (It is NOT a heavy motorized chair). 
* **Communication:** He uses a NovaVox AAC tablet mounted on a metal swing-arm on his wheelchair to communicate. 

## Luna Porter (Leo's Twin Sister)
* **Conditions:** Autistic, Cerebral Palsy.
* **Mobility:** Ambulatory. She can walk, but her CP affects her balance. Because of this, she typically pushes Leo's manual wheelchair, using the push-handles on the back of his chair like a walker to stabilize herself while they move together. 
* **Communication:** Unlike Leo, Luna is **fully verbal** and speaks normally. She only uses her NovaVox AAC device when she gets overwhelmed, breathless, or stumbles over her words. Otherwise, she prefers to speak out loud. 

## The Twin Dynamic
Because Leo is in a manual chair and Luna needs help balancing, they have a deeply symbiotic physical relationship. She pushes his chair to keep herself upright, and his chair provides the stable anchor she needs to navigate the world. They operate as a single, highly coordinated physical unit.

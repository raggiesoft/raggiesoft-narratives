# Universal Law of Cross-Realm Reciprocal Paused Time & Symmetric World-Locked Aging

This document defines a **universe-wide canonical metaphysics rule** governing cross-realm portals, rifts, and thresholds across the entire **RaggieSoft Narrative Universe** (`raggiesoft-narratives/books/**` and `raggiesoft-narratives/backstage/**`).

> **Scope Across the RaggieSoft Universe**: Currently, [***The Uncharted Waystation***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/waystation) (`books/waystation`, bridging `Studio 205` in Norfolk, Virginia and **Vaeloria / Keepers' Hall**) is the first narrative actively employing cross-realm passage. However, **any** RaggieSoft novel, spin-off, interactive Story Crossroads work, or *RaggieSoft Backstage* `"What If?"` dossier featuring travel between Earth and another realm or sanctuary world may invoke this exact canonical framework.

---

## 1. Reciprocal Clock Pause (*Zero Elapsed Time in the Unoccupied World*)

Whenever travelers step across an active cross-realm threshold from **World A** (such as Earth) into **World B** (such as Vaeloria):

1. **The Unoccupied World Stands Completely Still**: Time in whichever world the travelers are *not* currently standing in pauses on that exact instant—down to the second on digital clocks (`such as 9:15 PM on Nolan Lennox's brushed-silver VCR and DVD combo unit in Studio 205`), raindrops mid-bead on window glass, and rising hearth-smoke frozen in mid-curl.
2. **Symmetric Pause & Resume**:
   - While travelers live, explore, rest, or build a home in **World B**, zero seconds pass in **World A**—meaning zero missed work shifts, zero missed school mornings, zero unpaid rent cycles, and zero unexplained disappearances on Earth.
   - The instant travelers step back across the threshold into **World A**, World A's clock resumes ticking normally from the exact second they left, while **World B** immediately pauses on its own current breath—no stewpot boils dry, no lantern burns out, and no seasonal or orbital drift advances—until the travelers cross back again.

---

## 2. Symmetric World-Locked Biological Aging (*Threshold Age-Lock*)

As a direct physical consequence of time pausing in the unoccupied world, **every world holds and restores its own local biological timestamp for each traveler** across both directions of the threshold:

1. **Returning to Earth (`World B` → `World A` Snap-Back)**:
   - Even if travelers spend months, years, or decades living and aging naturally inside **World B**, the moment they step back across the threshold into **World A (Earth)**, their physical bodies immediately and seamlessly **return to the exact biological age and physical timestamp they held at the moment Earth paused** (for example, in *The Uncharted Waystation*, twenty-four-year-old Nolan Lennox, nineteen-year-old Gwen Lennox, and twenty-four-year-old Mallory Walsh step back into October 2002 Norfolk at `24`, `19`, and `24`).
   - Because their Earth biological age matches Earth's calendar to the minute, photo IDs, driver's licenses, high-school or college enrollments (such as Gwen's twelfth-grade senior year at Ghent High School), workplace badges (such as Nolan's `CCHR` Norfolk Campus technician post), and natural Earth lifespans remain **one hundred percent intact**.
2. **Returning to the Other Realm (`World A` → `World B` Symmetric Restoration)**:
   - Symmetrically, if travelers spend months or years aging on **Earth (`World A`)** while **World B** is paused—or if they previously aged inside **World B** before visiting Earth—stepping back across the threshold into **World B** immediately **restores them to the exact biological age and local state recorded on World B's paused clock**. Neither world's elapsed years ever overwrite or steal years from the other.

---

## 3. Continuous Memory, Emotional Healing, & Subjective Time Asymmetry on Earth

While **biological age** is locked to each world's local clock, **consciousness is 100% continuous**:

1. **Memories, Skills, & Emotional Healing Never Reset**:
   - Every memory, learned skill, conversation, inside joke, hard-won trauma recovery, and bond forged in one world carries **one hundred percent intact** across every crossing into the other world.
2. **The "Rushing Into It" Social Asymmetry on Earth**:
   - Because travelers accumulate weeks, months, or years of shared daily life in World B while zero minutes pass on Earth's calendar, outside observers on Earth experience a sharp **subjective-time gap**.
   - To coworkers, classmates, landlords, and neighbors on Earth, two partners who stepped through a threshold together on a Friday night and returned at `9:15 PM` that same Friday after months of shared life in another realm appear to be **rushing headlong into major relationship milestones**—such as **Nolan and Gwen wanting Mallory Walsh to move in with them on Earth** (requiring a larger Norfolk apartment than `Studio 205`'s `11' × 18'` room and single `54"` full-sized bed)—when, in the travelers' own lived reality, they have already spent an entire unhurried season sharing meals, soothing nightmares, and living side by side as a settled family.

---

## 4. Disability-Affirming Continuity Across All Worlds

Across the entire RaggieSoft universe, neither cross-realm travel nor symmetric world-locked aging ever "cures," erases, or treats as a flaw a character's core neurodivergence or physical disability:

- Autistic wiring, cerebral palsy, paraplegia, limb differences, AAC communication, Lofstrand forearm crutches, and manual or power wheelchairs remain integral to who the characters are on **both** sides of every threshold.
- What changes in a sanctuary realm like Vaeloria is **how the built environment meets the body**: step-free rose-quartz thresholds, springy three-inch cushion-moss paths, wide three-adult hearth beds, and master craftspeople who true a bent wheelchair caster fork in four turns of a brass wrench without forty-seven minutes of insurance hold music or six-week denial letters.

# Hampton Roads 1981 Regional Malls: *Emerald Creek Mall* & *Chesapeake Bay Mall*

This universal lore reference locks in the architectural floor plans, 1981–2001 interior finishes, elevator/escalator placements, anchor store portals, and CVTA transit connections for the two premier 1981 enclosed regional malls in South Hampton Roads across `/books/katie`, `/books/rachel`, `/books/aubrie`, and `/books/paige`.

---

## 1. Emerald Creek Mall *(Real-Life Baseline: Greenbrier Mall, Chesapeake, VA — Opened 1981)*

### A. Floor Plan & Architectural Geometry
- **Linear East–West Concourse Spine (90-Degree Cross-Corridors)**:
  - The main mall hallway (**main retail concourse**) runs in a straight east–west line (180 degrees), with short 90-degree perpendicular north/south anchor stub courts.
  - **No "V-Shaped" Hallways**: All intersections between the Food Court wing, anchor courts, and the main retail concourse are **strict 90-degree perpendicular T-intersections**.
- **Anchor Department Store Mall Entrances (Narrow Recessed Portals)**:
  - Anchor department stores—including **Wellington's Department Store** (*Hecht's*, located on the south side of the main concourse directly opposite the Food Court wing, in the footprint where *Regal Cinemas 18* sits on modern maps)—do **NOT** span the full width of the interior concourse wall.
  - Instead, each anchor store connects to the main concourse through a **~30–40 foot wide recessed two-story entrance portal** (with a single centered **`WELLINGTON'S`** sign over the portal), while regular **inline mall retail storefronts** (such as *The Literary Alcove*, *Oak & Iron Outfitters*, jewelry stores, and apparel shops—corresponding to units `2014` and `2010` on the upper-level plan) wrap right up to the left and right edges of the anchor portal along the straight concourse wall.
  - Outside, the CVTA Route 15 bus stop sits on the outer ring road straight across the parking lot from the first-floor exterior entrance of **Wellington's**.

### B. Interior Colors, Materials, & 1981 Original Elevator
- **Walkway Flooring**: **8x8 warm terracotta / reddish-brown square quarry tile** along the main concourse and Food Court wing landings (with light cream/grey circular tile inlays in the lower courts).
- **Mezzanine Balcony Railings**: **Polished brass tubular top-rails and upright posts with clear tempered glass infill panels** mounted on a low terracotta-brick curb around the second-floor atrium cutouts.
- **Main Concourse Roof & Skylights**: **White painted open steel space-frame trusses** supporting pitched skylights and continuous rectangular clerestory windows above warm cream/almond-beige upper wall soffits.
- **The Freestanding Early-1980s Scenic Glass Elevator**:
  - Located on the **main retail concourse** right at the 90-degree T-intersection between the **second-floor Food Court wing** (north side) and the **recessed `WELLINGTON'S` entrance portal** (south side).
  - **Colors & Construction**: Original 1981 freestanding vertical shaft framed by **four warm cream / almond-beige structural corner columns** and **multi-pane clear glass curtain walls** with cream/white mullions, topped by a **four-sided glass pyramid skylight cap**.
  - **Doors & Controls**: **Warm brushed-brass / bronze center-opening glass sliding doors** that open **flush onto the flat, level second-floor terracotta-tile landing** (zero stairs!), with a **polished brass call-button panel** (round illuminated buttons) on the right column and an early-1980s **amber digital floor indicator** (`1` / `2`) above the header.
- **The Second-Floor Food Court Wing**:
  - Branches off the north side of the main concourse directly behind the scenic glass elevator.
  - Topped by a **high white peaked fabric tent canopy ceiling** surrounded by perimeter skylights.
  - In the center of the Food Court floor opening sit the **twin chrome-and-glass food court escalators**: looking into the Food Court wing from the elevator landing, the **UP escalator** (first floor to second floor) is on the **left**, and the **DOWN escalator** (second floor to first floor) is on the **right**, with four-top food court tables arranged on the terracotta tile landing right between the two escalators (flanked by *Crown Burger* on the left and *Annie's Pretzels* on the right).
  - At the far back of the second-floor Food Court wing, exterior glass exit doors open directly onto a **graded upper section of the mall parking lot** that gently slopes up to the second-floor level.

### C. Narrative Role in *Katie & Nathaniel* (`books/katie`)
- **Nate & Katie's Favorite Regular Hangout**: Because Nate and Katie can catch **CVTA Route 15** half a block from their rented single-story brick ranch off **Military Highway & Indian River Road** and ride a **single bus (no transfers)** straight to the Emerald Creek Mall ring road on Mondays through Saturdays, Emerald Creek Mall is their favorite regular teenage hangout both before and after transitioning to their compact **SwiftChair** power wheelchairs in September 2001.

## 2. Chesapeake Bay Mall *(Real-Life Baseline: Lynnhaven Mall, Virginia Beach, VA — Opened 1981)*

### A. Floor Plan & Architectural Geometry
- **Sprawling Cruciform / Multi-Wing Super-Regional Layout**:
  - Located along Lynnhaven Parkway in Virginia Beach, VA (opened in 1981 as one of the largest two-level enclosed malls on the East Coast).
  - Designed with long intersecting concourse wings radiating from a grand two-story **Center Court** and a separate vaulted glass-atrium **Food Court wing**.
- **Interior Colors, Materials, & Landmarks (1981–2001 Era)**:
  - **Walkway Flooring**: **Earth-tone sandstone, tan, and cream ceramic tile** walkways with decorative geometric court borders and indoor ficus/palm tree planters in the lower courts.
  - **Mezzanine Balcony Railings**: **Dark bronze and oak/brass glass-panel railings** overlooking the two-story courts.
  - **Roof & Skylights**: **Continuous barrel-vaulted and pitched glass skylights** running down the length of the concourse spines over warm cream stucco bulkheads.
  - **Center Court Carousel & Scenic Elevator**: Features the iconic **double-decker vintage carousel** in Center Court alongside the main center-court escalators and a **freestanding glass-and-bronze hydraulic scenic elevator**.

### B. Narrative Role in *Katie & Nathaniel* (`books/katie`)
- **The "Big Trip" Favorite Mall**: While Emerald Creek Mall is their one-bus neighborhood hangout, **Chesapeake Bay Mall** is Nate and Katie's favorite **"big trip" mall** (requiring a multi-bus transfer across Virginia Beach).
- **Chronological Powerchair Milestone**: Nate and Katie went to both **Emerald Creek Mall** and **Chesapeake Bay Mall** all the time before their legs gave out in September 2001. As of October 2001 (`books/katie/e003/c004`), **they have not yet been to Chesapeake Bay Mall since getting their powerchairs**.

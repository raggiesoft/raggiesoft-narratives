# Meta-Crossovers and Easter Eggs

## Stardust Engine Library Characters in the Real World
The tutorial characters used for the Stardust Engine Library (the Book Reader website) physically exist within the Raggiesoft universe! 

In July 2003, they are college students in their early 20s. They were born and raised in the **Ocean View section of Norfolk, Virginia** (making them direct neighbors to Leo's pack in Virginia Beach).
The group consists of two sets of twins who are cousins to each other (their mothers are sisters, giving them different last names):
* **Oliver Callahan** (Male, motorized wheelchair user. **Autistic.** **100% Mute:** He cannot vocalize at all and relies entirely on his MagnaByte laptop text-to-speech for communication, though his family knows his mannerisms perfectly.) & **Sophia Callahan** (Female, brown hair, glasses. **Autistic.**)
* **Isabel Donovan** (Female, skinny, redhead, no glasses. **Neurotypical.**) & **Eleanor Donovan** (Female, curvy, redhead. **Autistic.**)

**In-Universe Appearance:**
In Book 8, Chapter 15 (July 2, 2003), Luna, Leo, Stella, and Olivia are having breakfast at The Sovereign Regency in San Francisco. Sitting at the table next to them are Oliver, Sophia, Isabel, and Eleanor. The college students are discussing their "Ethics in Engineering" class project about the Crown Point disaster, and they mention that the hotel atrium they are sitting in was used as the lobby for the *Crown Point* docudrama. 

This creates a brilliant, multi-layered Easter egg where the Stardust Engine characters cross paths with the main narrative pack!

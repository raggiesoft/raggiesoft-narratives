# Lore Dossier: The House in Virginia Beach

**Real-World Location:** 1091 Daniel Maloney Dr, Virginia Beach, VA 23464

## Architecture and History
Unlike the sprawling, multi-story estates in other Raggiesoft narratives (such as Highcliff Mansion), Stella, Olivia, Luna, and Leo live in a very modest, cozy, single-story suburban home in Virginia Beach. 

The house was originally built in 1983 as a tight 3-bedroom house with an attached garage. In 1993, when the pack expanded, they faced a critical decision: build a second floor, or convert the garage. They ultimately decided to convert the garage into a 4th bedroom. 

This decision to keep the house completely single-story proved incredibly fortuitous, as it made the entire home perfectly and naturally accessible for Leo's motorized wheelchair. There are no sprawling staircases to navigate—just tight corners that Olivia has completely mastered carrying him around. 

## Technology Setup
Because of the twins' need for distraction-free, highly predictable, closed-system environments, they do not use modern MagnaByte laptops at home. Instead, Stella miraculously tracked down **two working vintage Opus desktop computers** (the in-universe equivalent to the 1986 Apple IIgs) and a **GraphiPrint II** (Apple ImageWriter II) dot-matrix printer. These sit in their bedrooms, providing a safe, reliable, and sensory-friendly technological anchor for them at home.

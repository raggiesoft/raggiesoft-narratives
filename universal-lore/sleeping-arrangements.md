# The Pack: Typical Sleeping Arrangements

Across the *Meredith* and *Casey* narratives, the Vance/Delaney families have established typical sleeping arrangements, largely driven by pack dynamics, deep-pressure needs, and relationships.

## The established pairings:
*   **David Vance & Liz Delaney:** As an established couple, they always share a bed. 
*   **Meredith & Morgan Delaney:** The identical twins are fiercely co-dependent and almost always share a bed, usually aggressively cuddling.
*   **Wendy & Tom Delaney:** Married, always share a suite/bed.
*   **Megan & Erin Delaney:** The older Rhode Island sisters typically share a room/bed when traveling.
*   **Chloe & Claire Delaney:** The younger Rhode Island sisters typically share a room/bed when traveling.

## The solo sleepers:
*   **Cassandra Vance:** Typically requires her own bed/master suite to accommodate her ADA needs (roll-in showers, transfer space, etc.), though she deeply values the pack being close by.
*   **Kate Delaney:** As an adult power-chair user, she requires her own ADA-compliant space to maneuver her chair. 
*   **David Delaney:** Similar to Kate, he requires his own ADA-compliant room/bed to maneuver his power chair without crashing into hotel furniture. 

## The Outlier: Casey Vance
*   **Casey Vance (17):** Casey is the youngest of the core pack. Because David and Liz share a bed, and the twins share a bed, Casey frequently ends up being the "odd one out" who has to sleep in a solo bed or adjoining room. She frequently pouts about this, loudly complaining that she is being excluded from the "pack cuddle." The reason she craves these pack cuddles is deeply rooted in her childhood: when she and David were younger, they often shared a bed together because the shared deep-pressure contact helped regulate their autism. However, the absolute moment David turned 18, they had to strictly separate their sleeping arrangements because she was still a minor. She keenly feels the loss of that consistent nighttime regulation. To solve this, David and Liz have promised her that once she turns 18 and the family moves into the Redmond house, they will get a massive custom mattress so the four of them (Cassandra, David, Liz, Casey) can share a bed and maintain a permanent sleeping perimeter.


## The Fortress Four (Luna, Leo, Stella, Olivia)
During the epic Summer 2003 cross-country train trip (and in general hotel stays), the "Fortress Four" have highly specific and deeply normalized logistical and sleeping arrangements:

*   **Hotel Layout:** They always book a standard double-queen room. 
*   **The Pairings:** Olivia and Leo sleep in one queen bed. Stella and Luna sleep in the other queen bed. 
*   **Non-Sexualized Nudity:** There is absolute zero "turning away" or blushing when getting changed in the hotel room. Leo is completely comfortable seeing Stella, Luna, and Olivia naked, and they are comfortable around him. This is purely logistical and profoundly non-sexualized: because Leo is physically disabled, Stella and Luna bathe him. When they bathe him, they typically get fully undressed themselves so they can jump straight into the shower immediately after his bath.
*   **Olivia's Role:** During the summer of 2003, Olivia is NOT yet helping to bathe Leo (that is a boundary that hasn't been crossed yet). However, she IS fully involved in helping him with toileting. Furthermore, because Leo cannot bear to be separated from Olivia for even a moment during this trip, Olivia is completely welcome inside the bathroom while Stella and Luna are bathing him.

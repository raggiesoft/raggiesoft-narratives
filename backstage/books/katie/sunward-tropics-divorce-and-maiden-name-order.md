> **Archival Note:** In [***Katie & Nathaniel — Episode 3, Chapter 3, Part 1***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/katie/sixteen-summer-sanctuary/junior-year-at-northwood-high-the-prognosis/part-1-the-fault-grounds-filing-the-protective-order-junior-year-at-northwood-high-school), sixteen-year-old **Katie** and **Nathaniel Prairie** sit with their father **David Prairie** in family law attorney **Arthur Vance's** office on Monday, August 27, 2001 and ask whether the divorce decree can force their mother to surrender the surname **`Prairie`** and revert to her maiden name, **`Walsh`**. Because neither twin ever had to sit across a table from Brenda again, they only heard the result from their father after the decree was entered. This Backstage dossier reveals what happened inside Arthur Vance's conference room when Brenda and her counsel tried to contest the divorce.

## 1. The *M/S Sunward Tropics* Evidentiary Packet (`August 22–26, 2001`)

When the **Staff Captain** and **Chief Security Officer** of the ***M/S Sunward Tropics*** handed **David Prairie** a sealed manila envelope at the Port Canaveral gangway on Sunday morning, August 26, 2001, *Sunward Cruise Lines* was protecting itself against potential maritime premises-liability litigation by documenting beyond all dispute why a passenger had been confined to her stateroom and permanently banned from the fleet. Inside the envelope were four notarized records:

1. **Duplicate VHS Security Tape (`CAM-04B — Deck 4 Forward Stairwell Landing, Aug 22, 2001, 15:31:40–15:34:12`):**
   Time-stamped surveillance video showing sixteen-year-old Nathaniel and Katie sitting exhausted on the padded alcove bench holding hands after climbing four decks of stairs with progressive neuromuscular weakness, Brenda screaming inches from their faces over the *"Captain Barnaby's Pirate Parade"* children's character meet-and-greet, Brenda raising her right arm to strike Nathaniel across the face, and David stepping squarely between his wife and his children to block the blow before two uniformed shipboard security officers intervened.
2. **Shipboard Breathalyzer & Medical Brig Log (`15:48 EDT`):**
   Documenting Brenda's blood-alcohol concentration (`0.16% BAC`) at three-forty-eight in the afternoon and her verbal threats against the ship's purser and security staff.
3. **Captain's Master Log Extract & Stateroom 6004 Reassignment Order:**
   Documenting the Captain's order moving David, Nathaniel, and Katie to an accessible midship stateroom (`Stateroom 6004`) with two loaner manual wheelchairs while confining Brenda to the original interior cabin for the remainder of the Nassau voyage.
4. **Permanent Fleetwide Exclusion Notice:**
   Permanently barring Brenda from boarding any vessel operated by *Sunward Cruise Lines* or its corporate affiliates.

## 2. The Fourteen-Minute Settlement Conference (`Friday, October 19, 2001`)

When Brenda was served with the Virginia Beach Circuit Court fault-based **Complaint for Divorce** (`Va. Code § 20-91(A)(6)`, cruelty and reasonable apprehension of bodily hurt) and the **Family Abuse Protective Order** barring her from the marital home and from **Northwood High School**, she initially told her retained attorney that David was *"exaggerating a minor family disagreement on vacation"* and demanded spousal support and possession of the house.

On Friday afternoon, October 19, 2001, Brenda and her attorney walked into **Arthur Vance's** conference room off Independence Boulevard for a discovery preview. Mr. Vance did not argue. He simply pressed `PLAY` on the office VCR.

* **Minute 1 to Minute 3:** Brenda's attorney watched the `CAM-04B` tape in silence, read the Staff Captain's notarized brig report, and looked at Dr. Evelyn Carter's preliminary neuromuscular evaluation confirming that the sixteen-year-old twins Brenda had forced up four flights of shipboard stairs were losing functional motor strength in all four limbs.
* **Minute 4:** Brenda's attorney asked Mr. Vance to step out of the room for five minutes so he could speak with his client alone. Behind the closed door, her own lawyer told her bluntly that if a Virginia Beach Circuit Court judge watched that tape at a contested hearing, she would lose custody 100-to-0, be permanently barred from alimony under Virginia's fault statute, and be ordered to reimburse David for half of the **`$6,418.72`** she had secretly charged to the marital credit card to book the non-refundable Bahamas cruise—plus David's **`$4,500`** in legal fees.

## 3. The `Va. Code § 20-121.4` Maiden Name Stipulation

When Mr. Vance returned to the conference room, Brenda's attorney asked if David would consider a walk-away property settlement waiving reimbursement for the `$6,418.72` cruise debt and providing a modest one-time relocation check so Brenda could sign a lease on an apartment near Military Highway.

Mr. Vance slid two typed pages across the mahogany table:

1. **The Property & Custody Stipulation (`Va. Code § 20-109.1`):**
   Granting **David Prairie** permanent sole legal and physical custody of **Nathaniel** and **Katie Prairie**, permanent waiver of all spousal support, and a full release of Brenda's liability for the `$6,418.72` credit-card debt plus a `$3,000` relocation check—**strictly conditioned** upon Brenda's simultaneous execution of Document #2.
2. **The Joint Stipulation & Petition for Restoration of Former Name (`Va. Code § 20-121.4`):**
   Requiring Brenda to petition the Court as a mandatory, non-severable term of the Final Decree of Divorce to restore her maiden surname—**`Brenda Walsh`**—and requiring her to surrender her **`Prairie`** Virginia driver's license and Social Security card within thirty days of entry of the decree.

When Brenda protested that *"a husband can't make his ex-wife give back her married name,"* Mr. Vance looked across his glasses and delivered the message Katie and Nate had asked for:

> *"Your husband didn't ask for that clause, Ms. Walsh. Your sixteen-year-old son and daughter asked for it—and your husband told me he will happily take this tape to trial next Tuesday and let the judge saddle you with eight thousand dollars in debt and attorney's fees unless their wishes are respected in black and white."*

Brenda signed the **`Va. Code § 20-121.4`** stipulation in blue ink less than ninety seconds later. From that afternoon forward—through their Glenrock Mall near-miss in 2007 and their walk past **Suite 756 at Timberline Shopping Center** in 2027—she was legally **`Brenda Walsh`**, and never **`Brenda Prairie`** again.

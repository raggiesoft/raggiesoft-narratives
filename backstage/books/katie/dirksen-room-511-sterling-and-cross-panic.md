## 1. The Nine Stages of Bureaucratic Collapse Inside Dirksen Room 511

1. **Stage 1 — Smug Bureaucratic Overconfidence Walking Down the Hallway:**
   Walking into Dirksen Room 511 with their glossy spiral-bound `$18.5 million` Congressionally Directed Spending presentation, Sterling and Cross assumed this would be a ten-minute ceremonial photo-op: *"Senator Prairie is a forty-one-year-old wheelchair user from Virginia Beach. What disabled Senator on earth wouldn't rubber-stamp $18.5 million for Virginia's flagship rehabilitation center?"* Having checked the *Congressional Directory*—which listed only **Northwood High School (`VBCPS`)** and the **Community College of Hampton Roads (`CCHR`)**—neither executive had the slightest inkling that Katie or Nate had ever set foot in Fishersville.
2. **Stage 2 — Condescending Dismissal at Question #1 (*"Some Activist Sent Her an Email"*):**
   When Senator Prairie first asked about a twenty-one-year-old twin brother and sister in wheelchairs having their push-handles grabbed at **Barton Hall** in March 2007, Director Cross internally rolled her eyes—assuming a disgruntled former client had sent an email to the Senator's constituent inbox—and tried to brush it off with a canned line about modern staff training.
3. **Stage 3 — Baffled Unease at Questions #2 & #3 (*"Why Is a U.S. Senator Being So Forensically Hyperspecific About 2007?"*):**
   When Katie refused to let them pivot and drilled into hyper-granular 2007 operational details—two `CCHR` computer-science graduates forced to tour the **auto mechanics, woodworking, and nursing shops**, a Vocational Evaluator pitching a **Waynesboro ValueMart door-greeter partnership**, **VITA's seven-year-old `Quantum OS 2000 Professional`** locking down local web servers in the Bates-Harding lab, and staying stuck on **`Level 2` in Barton Hall** with **signed yellow paper Passes** because moving to **Cantor Hall** required earning **`Level 3`**—alarm bells started ringing inside both executives' heads:
   > *"Wait... why does a United States Senator know the exact color of the 2007 Level 2 paper passes in Barton Hall and VITA's desktop permissions in the Bates-Harding computer lab?! No K Street lobbyist or constituent form letter would ever know that!"*
4. **Stage 4 — Cold Creeping Dread at Question #4 (*"How Could Anyone Know About `MedStrip` Wound-Closure Strips and Lumpy Bags of Curtains Inside a Rec Hall Closet at 10:45 PM?!"*):**
   When Katie recounted the ninth afternoon (`Tuesday, March 13, 2007`)—the broken nose and split nasal bridge in the therapeutic pool, the twin brother covering his sister's bare chest while she held her bleeding nostrils with both hands until a towel arrived, the Bates-Harding clinic nurses pulling the split bridge skin together with **sterile `MedStrip` wound-closure strips** under a **seven-day white gauze bandage**, slipping down the darkened Rec Hall corridor at `10:45 PM` in street clothes, locking themselves inside a **seasonal storage closet** using **lumpy bags of curtains as pillows**, the night supervisor unlocking the door with a master key at `11:25 PM` and accusing them of having sex, and the twins daring him to walk to the Rec Hall desk phone and call their father in Alexandria right then and there—Sterling and Cross were internally panicking:
   > *"Even if someone in Richmond leaked an old 2007 disciplinary log, how does she know the exact sterile MedStrip wound-closure strips the nurses taped across that girl's nose—and no state form in DARS history ever recorded what the pillows inside a Rec Hall storage closet felt like at 10:45 PM! How on earth could she know that?!"*
   Still blind to the fact that the two forty-one-year-old `SwiftChair` users across the desk *were* those twins, they wondered out loud why she was being so oddly hyperspecific and threw up the **`34 C.F.R. § 361.38`** privacy wall—walking straight into Katie and Nate's trap.
5. **Stage 5 — The Split-Second Math Click & Stomach-Drop Vertigo (*"41 Minus 20 Is 21... OH GOD, IT'S THEM."*):**
   The instant Katie laced her fingers through Nate's across their `SwiftChair` armrests and said *"Because the twenty-one-year-old twin brother and sister in those March 2007 Barton Hall files are sitting right across this desk from you,"* every piece of the puzzle slammed together inside Sterling's and Cross's skulls at once:
   * `2027 - 2007 = 20 years ago`
   * `41 years old - 20 = 21 years old`
   * Fraternal twin brother and sister, both full-time wheelchair users, both graduated from `CCHR` in December 2006—three months before March 2007!
   * And the only reason `RRC` wasn't listed in the *Congressional Directory* was because **nobody puts a place they dropped out of after twelve days on their biography!**
   Both executives felt the floor drop out from under their stomachs as they realized they had just looked a sitting United States Senator in the eye, told her that her own broken nose and `MedStrip` bandage had never happened, and lectured her that **`34 C.F.R. § 361.38`** prohibited her from discussing her own life without her own permission.
6. **Stage 6 — The `"ValueMart Door Greeter"` & `Status 28 Dropout` Institutional Mortification:**
   Looking at the gold Senate lapel pin on Katie's blazer, Director Cross was hit by a wave of sheer professional horror: in March 2007, an RRC Vocational Evaluator had looked at **Katie Prairie**—a woman who already held an accredited college degree in computer science, who was about to build a multi-decade production software firm with her brother, and who would be elected to the Virginia Beach City Council, the House of Delegates, the Senate of Virginia, and the **United States Senate**—and told her to sit by the sliding doors of the **Waynesboro ValueMart as a door greeter**. Worse still, in DARS's own mainframes in Richmond right that second, **the United States Senator holding the purse strings over DARS's `$18.5 million` earmark was officially coded as a `Status 28` (*"Unsuccessful Rehabilitation — Client Dropped Out"*) failure!**
7. **Stage 7 — How DARS's Own 2007 Black-Marker Redaction Blindfolded Them in 2027:**
   Commissioner Sterling's mind raced wondering why his legislative liaison in Richmond hadn't flagged `"Prairie, Katherine"` and `"Prairie, Nathaniel"` before letting him walk into Dirksen Room 511—only to realize the supreme poetic justice: back in April 2007, when DARS General Counsel blacked out Katie and Nate's names on the Tuesday pool log with a thick black magic marker as `[REDACTED UNDER 34 C.F.R. § 361.38 — STATUS 28 DROPOUT]` so **Evelyn Brooks's** lawyer couldn't locate them as witnesses, **DARS had also guaranteed that its own 2027 leadership wouldn't recognize the two redacted `Status 28` dropouts on that page until they were sitting across Senator Prairie's mahogany desk!**
8. **Stage 8 — The Second Nuclear Bomb (*Eyewitnesses to the 48 Hours Before Natalie Brooks Was Raped*):**
   When Nate quietly added *"And wait until you hear why our Thursday morning Level 1 restriction meeting with our counselor never happened on March 15, 2007,"* and Katie laid **Evelyn Brooks's notarized Guardianship/Conservatorship waiver and March 16, 2007 hospital ER/SANE report** on top of their funding binder, Sterling and Cross realized the Senator sitting on the Appropriations Subcommittee was a **firsthand eyewitness** proving that the exact male client who had broken her nose in the pool on Tuesday (`March 13`) had been left on campus by RRC, had used a scrambled keycard to rape **Natalie Brooks** on Wednesday night (`March 14`), and had been whisked onto a bus in Staunton at `7:50 AM` on Thursday (`March 15`) while housekeeping bleached the sheets.

## 2. Stage 9 — The Frantic Hallway Calls to Richmond & The Governor's Chief of Staff Reading Them the Riot Act (`3:10 PM – 4:15 PM`)

Stumbling out of Dirksen Room 511 with shaking hands—and forgetting their glossy spiral-bound `$18.5 million` funding binder on Katie's desk—Sterling and Cross started speed-dialing Richmond and Fishersville before they even reached the fifth-floor elevators:

* Within forty minutes, the basement archives in Richmond and Fishersville pulled the unredacted March 2007 (`03/04/2007 – 03/16/2007`) paper files for `Prairie, Katherine` and `Prairie, Nathaniel` alongside the unredacted Tuesday pool log and Thursday infirmary/van dispatch records, confirming that **every single word Senator Prairie and Nate had said was 100% true**.
* Terrified of being annihilated on live television cameras in front of Natalie and her mother in **Dirksen Room 124** on Wednesday morning, both Sterling and Cross begged the **Virginia Secretary of Health and Human Resources** and **Governor's Chief of Staff Arthur Lawrence** for permission to withdraw from Wednesday's Subcommittee witness panel and drive straight back to Richmond.
* Instead, **Arthur Lawrence**—who knew Katie Prairie well from her twelve years in the General Assembly and knew she answered her direct office phone herself—called Dirksen Room 511 on a **three-way conference call** with Sterling and Cross on the line from their parked car on Constitution Avenue.
* When Katie warned Lawrence on the speakerphone that *"if the Director and the Commissioner do not show up for the Subcommittee hearing on Wednesday morning, they will be facing compulsory subpoenas from the United States Senate"*—knowing she didn't need to claim unilateral subpoena power when the Subcommittee Chairman was ready to back her—Lawrence heard the unspoken final sentence loud and clear (*"Arthur, get them into those chairs voluntarily, because neither you nor the Governor wants to find out what happens next"*) and ordered Sterling and Cross into Dirksen Room 124 at nine o'clock sharp so they wouldn't jeopardize Virginia's entire **`$92+ million` share of federal Title I Rehabilitation Act matching funds**.

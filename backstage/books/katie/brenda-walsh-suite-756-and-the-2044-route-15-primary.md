## 1. Inside `Suite 756` at Timberline Shopping Center: How Brenda Walsh's Store Imploded After April 16, 2027

When Katie, Nate, and David rolled out of **`Suite 756`** alongside their former Delaney Street neighbor **Sharron** on Saturday afternoon, April 3, 2027, Brenda Walsh was left standing alone behind her glass register counter:

1. **Twenty-Six Years of Resentment & Refusing to Vote for Her Own Daughter:**
   In the twenty-six years after the *M/S Sunward Tropics* divorce, **Brenda Walsh** never once apologized or accepted responsibility for why David divorced her or why her twin children cut off contact. Instead, every time Katie's name appeared on a Virginia Beach ballot—for the **Kempsville City Council (`2010`)**, the **85th House District (`2013–2019`)**, the **7th State Senate District (`2019–2023`)**, and the **United States Senate (`2026`)**—Brenda proudly told anyone who would listen that she was voting straight-ticket against her own daughter, bitter that the *"two ungrateful kids"* she had tried to drag up four flights of cruise-ship stairs were now beloved across the Commonwealth while she was an estranged stranger.
2. **Sinking Her Savings Into the Unauthorized *"Tidewater Thorne & RTR Superstore"* (`Suite 756`):**
   Consumed by cable-news grievance politics, Brenda signed a commercial lease on **`Suite 756`** in **Timberline Shopping Center** on Holland Road—next door to *Don Miller Subs* and *Neat Stuff*—and sank her remaining savings into wholesale boxes of unlicensed red **`RESTORE THE REPUBLIC (`RTR`)`** hats, **Garrett Thorne** flags, and bumper stickers attacking Democratic lawmakers (including her own daughter).
3. **The Ultimate Irony (`Monday, April 26, 2027`):**
   Ten days after **Senator Katie Prairie** rolled up to her desk microphone on the floor of the United States Senate (`Friday, April 16, 2027`) to quote **George Mason**, **James Madison**, and **Abraham Lincoln** and cast her historic **`Guilty`** and **`Aye`** votes removing **Garrett Thorne** from office and barring him for life from holding any office of honor, trust, or profit under the United States (`Article I, Section 3, Clause 7`), a certified letter arrived at **`Suite 756`**:
   * Facing massive federal defense legal bills in the **Eastern District of Virginia** over the leaked Naval Station Norfolk carrier strike group deployment schedule (`18 U.S.C. § 793`), **Thorne Enterprises Licensing LLC** sent aggressive **Trademark Cease-and-Desist & Statutory Damages Demand Letters** to every unauthorized mom-and-pop `RTR` merchandise shop in America—including **Brenda Walsh at Suite 756**.
   * Prohibited from selling another hat or flag by the very man she had worshipped, and with Navy families across Virginia Beach boycotting the store after Sharron's walkout and the Norfolk carrier betrayal, Brenda quietly shuttered `Suite 756` at the end of May 2027.
4. **The Double Irony of *"The Grand Decree (Live at Sanctuary Stadium)"* (`Engine Room Records' Strict No-Campaign Policy`, `The Rare Exception for "The Accessibility Project"`, & `Mana Canvas / Engine Room Records Copyright Takedowns`):**
   Since its founding in 1992, **Engine Room Records** has enforced a strict, across-the-board policy prohibiting its catalog from being used by political campaigns—formally excluding its roster from political-entity blanket venue licenses and declining political sync requests (having granted a very rare exception only to Katie Prairie's campaign for **The Paper Wall's** *"The Accessibility Project"*). Back during the 2024 presidential campaign, however, Garrett Thorne had infamously walked out at rallies and blasted **"The Grand Decree (Live at Sanctuary Stadium)"** from **Mana Canvas's** 1993 ***Runebreaker Live*** album (originally from the 1992 sixteen-bit *Runebreaker* soundtrack) as his campaign theme song without authorization—completely oblivious to the fact that it is literally the villain song of a tyrant ruling by executive decree who gets defeated by a pair of eighteen-year-old fraternal twins. Within forty-eight hours, Thorne's campaign videos featuring the song were hit with copyright claims by **Mana Canvas** (who hold the original **1992/1993 copyrights**) and **Engine Room Records** (who hold the **2016 re-release rights**), and his campaign received a formal **Cease and Desist** order forcing them to scrub the audio. When the Senate convicted Garrett Thorne on April 16, 2027, and voted `68–32` to permanently bar him from ever holding office again, Katie and Nate couldn't help thinking of the broken-music-box **"The Grand Decree (Reprise)"** from the end of *Runebreaker*—and ten days later, Thorne turned around and hit his own worshipful follower Brenda Walsh with a Cease and Desist of his own.

## 2. How the U.S. Capitol Police & Federal Bail Judge Crushed Thorne's Post-Conviction Vendetta (`April–July 2027`)

Why were Katie and Nate able to transition from round-the-clock **United States Capitol Police (`USCP`) Dignitary Protection Division** coverage back to riding **CVTA Route #15** and **Route #36** on their own by July 2027?

1. **The `USCP` Threat-Assessment Trap-and-Trace Sweep (`April 16–30, 2027`):**
   Following Garrett Thorne's Senate removal, the **USCP Threat Assessment Section** and the **FBI Norfolk Field Office** placed an immediate digital trap-and-trace on every threatening voicemail or social-media message directed at Senator Prairie's Washington and Virginia Beach offices—executing swift federal arrests for interstate communications threats (`18 U.S.C. § 875(c)`).
2. **The Federal Pretrial Release Gag Order in Norfolk (`18 U.S.C. § 3142`):**
   Even more decisively, when now-private-citizen **Garrett Thorne**, former Vice President **Conrad Drayton**, and former Defense Secretary **Bret Halverson** were arraigned in the **Walter E. Hoffman U.S. Courthouse on Granby Street in Norfolk** on federal Espionage Act indictments for leaking the Norfolk carrier strike group schedule, the presiding U.S. District Judge imposed a strict condition of pretrial release under **`18 U.S.C. § 3142(c)`**:
   > *"Any public statement, social-media post, or surrogate broadcast targeting any member of the United States Senate who sat as a juror in the Defendants' impeachment trials, or any family member thereof, shall result in the immediate revocation of bond and remand to the Western Tidewater Regional Jail pending trial."*
   Faced with sitting in a Portsmouth jail cell without his phone, Garrett Thorne never uttered Katie Prairie's name in public again.

## 3. Epilogue Dossier (`Spring 2044`): Why Three-Term Senator Katie Prairie Turned Down the Presidency & The Stop `#0812` Four-Seat Presidential Endorsement Loop

Seventeen years later—in the spring of **2044**, when fifty-eight-year-old **Senator Katie Prairie (`D-Virginia`)** and **Nathaniel Prairie** were serving in Katie's third full term in the United States Senate (`having been re-elected in 2032 and 2038`) and sitting as **Full Committee Chairwoman of the Senate Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs** (with 100% Senate jurisdiction over the Federal Transit Administration)—a national *"Draft Katie Prairie for President"* movement swept the Democratic Party ahead of the 2044 open presidential primary (`books/katie/e011/c007/p001.md`).

Why did Katie turn down a near-certain path to the White House—and how did the **Stop `#0812`** four-seat transit loop decide the 2044 nomination?

1. **Why Katie and Nate Refused to Trade Their Freedom for the Secret Service Bubble (Remembering October 14, 2027 at Chambers Field):**
   When national party leaders urged Katie to run in the 2044 election for the presidential term beginning January 20, 2045, fifty-eight-year-old Katie wasn't guessing from a textbook what the **Secret Service Presidential Protective Bubble** looked like—she vividly remembered **Thursday, October 14, 2027** (`books/katie/e011/c006/p001.md`), the very first time she and Nate had ever stepped inside it aboard **Air Force One** with **President Clara Morales** from **Joint Base Andrews** to **Chambers Field** and **Pier 12** at Naval Station Norfolk:
   * Watching Secret Service canine handlers sweep their SwiftChair battery boxes, seeing every internal Naval Station cross-street frozen behind barricades and black Counter-Assault SUVs, and realizing Clara Morales couldn't even step ten unscripted feet off a pre-swept carpet to grab a cold drink or say hello to an unscreened sailor had stayed with Katie and Nate for seventeen years.
   * Under federal statute (`18 U.S.C. § 3056`), a President of the United States **cannot decline Secret Service protection** and is never allowed to roll down to the corner bus stop, ride a regular city bus, eat a sub sandwich at *Don Miller Subs* unannounced, or browse retro games at *Neat Stuff* without an armored motorcade shutting down the street.
   * As Katie told the national party chairs from the bus shelter on **Military Highway**:
     > *"Nate and I spent forty-two years fighting for our independence to live our lives side by side, ride the public bus whenever we want, and answer directly to the people of Virginia. If you elect me President, the Secret Service will lock the two of us inside an armored limousine for the rest of our lives and never let us ride CVTA Route 15 again. I am not giving up my bus pass for the Oval Office!"*
2. **The 2044 Stop `#0812` Four-Seat Endorsement Loop — Designed by Sam, Mandy, & Sarah (`Route 15` $\rightarrow$ `Route 800 The Surge Light Rail` $\rightarrow$ `Route 25` $\rightarrow$ `Route 12`, `books/katie/e011/c007/p001.md–p004.md`):**
   Because three-term Senator Katie Prairie's endorsement was the single most coveted prize in the **2044 Democratic Presidential Primary**, every major presidential contender called her office asking where they could fly in to meet with her—expecting the traditional Washington endorsement pilgrimage behind closed doors at a private steakhouse or hotel boardroom.
   Sitting around the dining room table at Katie and Nate's house on **Arvin Road** (`p001.md`), 41-year-old triplets **Sam, Mandy, and Sarah Hawkins**—who are still together every day, have never married or had children, and after learning about the 2005 Ocean View nightmare Katie and Nate went through with Brandon and Tiffany, never even went looking for romantic partners—came up with the idea of conducting the endorsement interviews on a four-seat CVTA bus and light-rail loop starting and ending at Katie and Nate's home bus stop (**Stop `#0812`** at **Military Highway & Indian River Road**). Katie and Nate loved the idea, and **Sam, Mandy, and Sarah rode on board every single bus and light-rail endorsement interview alongside Katie and Nate**.
   Nate sent every campaign the exact same two-line calendar invite for **`Military Highway & Indian River Road (Northeast Corner — #0812), Virginia Beach, VA`** (`Candidate Only — No Staff, No Security Details, No Press Pool`), never mentioning the word *bus* and never notifying **CVTA** ahead of time so transit management couldn't stage a freshly washed bus or hold schedules:
   * **The Candidates Who Turned Down the Offer Without Boarding (`p001.md`):**
     * **Former Vice President Arthur Sterling** earned an immediate polite **Hard No** because his permanent Secret Service detail would have forced CVTA to pull buses out of service and delay commuters.
     * **Senator Julian Vance of New York** arrived at `#0812` in a camel-hair overcoat expecting a private office suite where he could offer Katie a Cabinet post (`Transportation` or `HHS`), scoffed when he saw the `9:18 AM` **Route 15** city bus kneeling at the curb (*"I am a United States Senator running for President of the United States. I do not ride a city bus with random commuters for two hours"*), and walked straight back to his SUV. Because **Stop `#0812`** is Katie and Nate's home bus stop right around the corner from **Arvin Road**, Katie, Nate, Sam, Mandy, and Sarah didn't even need to board the bus—they simply rolled into the **Market Square** grocery store right there in the shopping center for cold drinks and wheeled themselves home.
     * **Governor Gary Whitman of Colorado** (whose Super PAC was funded by private-equity hospital conglomerates and toll-road developers) abruptly canceled his appointment the night before after learning from Vance's horrified staff that Katie invited working nurses and disabled students to ask unscripted questions on Nate's camera.

     * **Leg 1 (`CVTA Route 15`, `9:18 AM – 9:31 AM`):** Board northbound **Route 15** at **Stop `#0812`** (`Military Highway & Indian River Road`) and ride north up Military Highway to **Military Highway Station**.
     * **Leg 2 (`CVTA Route 800 — The Surge Light Rail`, `9:38 AM – 9:41 AM`):** Roll onto the level-boarding platform of **Route 800 (*The Surge* Light Rail)** and ride **one stop eastbound** (`3 minutes`) to **Newtown Road Station**.

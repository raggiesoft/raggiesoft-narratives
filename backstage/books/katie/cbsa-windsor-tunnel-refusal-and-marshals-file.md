## 1. Saturday Night, November 12, 2005 (`5:10 PM – 11:45 PM`): The Panic at `224 Virginia Avenue`

When **Brandon Mercer** pulled into the parking lot of `224 Virginia Avenue` at `5:10 PM` on Saturday afternoon—having lost track of time at his friend's apartment—he expected to find twenty-year-old Katie sitting quietly in her bedroom waiting for him. Instead, he found an empty apartment, her power wheelchair and backpack gone, and her apartment key sitting in the middle of the kitchen counter.

Why did Brandon run for the Canadian border that night instead of staying in Williamsburg?

1. **The Contraband Inside His Hallway Closet Lockbox:**
   Brandon did not flee Virginia merely because he was afraid Katie would report him for domestic violence. Hidden inside a steel lockbox on the top shelf of his bedroom closet were stolen identity profiles, skimmed credit-card magnetic-stripe dumps, and illicit contraband files on two external hard drives. Convinced that Katie had gone straight to the **Williamsburg Police Department** and that detectives would be knocking on `224 Virginia Avenue` with a search warrant before midnight, Brandon stuffed the two external hard drives, `$480` in cash, and three changes of clothes into a black duffel bag, abandoned his car at the **Richmond Greyhound terminal** so his license plate wouldn't be spotted on the interstate, and bought a cash one-way overnight bus ticket north to **Detroit, Michigan** via Pittsburgh and Toledo.
2. **Meanwhile in Norfolk (`11:15 PM`):**
   That same Saturday night, after Katie arrived safely at **`154 W. Ocean View Avenue, Apartment 3`** on the last **CVTA Route #61** bus across the water and showed Nate and their father the bruises on her arms, **David Prairie** helped Katie file a formal criminal complaint with the **Williamsburg Police Department**, triggering statewide Virginia arrest warrants that were entered into the national **NCIC** criminal database by Sunday morning.

## 2. Sunday Afternoon, November 13, 2005 (`4:15 PM`): Refusal of Entry at `555 Goyeau Street` & `35 Park Street East`, Windsor, Ontario

Arriving at the Detroit Greyhound station on Sunday afternoon after sixteen hours on overnight buses, Brandon walked down to the **Detroit-Windsor Tunnel** terminal at **East Jefferson Avenue and Randolph Street** and paid the cash fare to board the cross-border **Tunnel Bus** under the Detroit River into Canada.

At `4:15 PM`, the Tunnel Bus emerged onto the Canadian plaza at **`555 Goyeau Street`** in downtown **Windsor, Ontario**, where all bus passengers were directed inside the **CBSA Windsor Tunnel District Operations building** at **`35 Park Street East`** (just off **Goyeau Street** and **Wyandotte Street East**).

Every single detail of Brandon's cover story fell apart at the primary inspection counter:

* **No Passport, One-Way Ticket, & Zero Ties:**
  Standing at the counter inside `35 Park Street East`, Brandon handed **CBSA Border Services Officer M. Vance** a Virginia driver's license and claimed he was visiting Windsor *"for a couple of days to look at the riverfront."* He had no return ticket, no hotel reservation on **Goyeau Street** or **Wyandotte Street East**, less than five hundred dollars in cash, and a heavy duffel bag packed with computer hard drives and winter clothes.
* **The CPIC / NCIC Database Hit (`4:22 PM`):**
  Officer Vance referred Brandon to **CBSA Secondary Inspection** inside `35 Park Street East`. The moment Secondary Officers queried his Virginia driver's license through the integrated **CPIC / NCIC** cross-border law-enforcement terminal, the active **Williamsburg, Virginia domestic assault and unlawful restraint warrant** flashed across the screen alongside a felony fugitive alert.
* **Inadmissibility Under `IRPA Section 36` & Immediate Turnback across the Tunnel (`4:50 PM`):**
  Deeming Brandon Mercer inadmissible to Canada on grounds of criminality under the *Immigration and Refugee Protection Act (`IRPA`)*, CBSA officers at `35 Park Street East` served him with an immediate **Direction to Return to the United States**, escorted him back out to the **`555 Goyeau Street`** tunnel apron, and telephoned **U.S. Customs and Border Protection (`CBP`)** at the American tunnel plaza at **East Jefferson Avenue and Randolph Street** in Detroit to alert them that a wanted Virginia fugitive was on the return bus.

## 3. Sunday Evening to Tuesday Morning (`November 13–15, 2005`): The CBP Seizure at Randolph Street & The Arrest on Cass Avenue

When the return Tunnel Bus pulled into the American inspection bay at **East Jefferson Avenue and Randolph Street** in Detroit at `5:05 PM`, **CBP officers** pulled Brandon off the coach and conducted a full border search of his black duffel bag:

1. **The Border Search of the Two External Hard Drives (`East Jefferson Avenue & Randolph Street`):**
   Because border searches at a U.S. Port of Entry require no probable-cause warrant under the Fourth Amendment border-search exception, CBP forensic technicians examined the two external hard drives in Brandon's duffel bag, immediately uncovering multistate wire-fraud and contraband files that elevated the case into a **federal prosecution** in the **Eastern District of Virginia** and the **Eastern District of Michigan**.
2. **Tracked to the Men's Shelter on Cass Avenue:**
   While the formal federal complaint and interstate detainer paperwork were being transmitted between Norfolk and Detroit, **Deputy U.S. Marshals** tracked Brandon nine blocks northwest from Randolph Street to a brick men's shelter on **Cass Avenue** in Detroit, taking him into federal custody without incident.
3. **Why Katie Never Had to Fly to Detroit (`Protected Non-Traveling Complainant`):**
   Because twenty-year-old Katie and Nate were full-time power-wheelchair users with progressive neuromuscular weakness—and because Brandon had chosen to commit a multi-jurisdictional federal offense across state and international borders while fleeing his crime in Williamsburg—the **Assistant United States Attorney** in Norfolk successfully petitioned the court to designate Katie a **Protected Non-Traveling Complainant**, allowing her to give her sworn victim-impact and evidentiary testimony from a quiet conference room inside the **Walter E. Hoffman U.S. Courthouse on Granby Street** in downtown Norfolk with Nate holding her hand the entire time.

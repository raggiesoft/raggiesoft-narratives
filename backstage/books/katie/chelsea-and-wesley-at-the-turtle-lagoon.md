Because Nate is inside the classroom for the next twenty minutes, this Backstage feature stays out at the centerpiece **Painted-Turtle Lagoon** to show what happened between **Wesley**, **Cousin Chelsea**, **Brittany**, **Dan**, **Jonas**, **Nora**, and **Molly** while that classroom door was shut.

## 1. 10:28 AM CDT — Why Wesley Let the Door Close Without an Ounce of Worry

Fifteen summers earlier at the Virginia Beach Pavilion (June 14, 2003), watching Heather cry beside Katie's and Nate's wheelchairs without understanding why things couldn't be fixed had broken sixteen-year-old Wesley's heart.

At 10:28 AM on Saturday, June 16, 2018, thirty-one-year-old **Staff Member Wesley** glanced up from his stainless-steel romaine cart as Heather walked down the short interior hallway beside Katie's and Nate's black power wheelchairs and pulled the oak *EDUCATION CLASSROOM* door gently shut.

Right away, thirty-three-year-old **Dan Lindstrom** stepped calmly into Wesley's line of sight—never talking down to him, never making up a fib—and spoke at an easy, matter-of-fact Minnesota pace.

"Hey, Wes," Dan said warmly, gesturing once toward the closed oak door and then tapping his own chest lightly. "Nate, Katie, and Heather are taking fifteen quiet minutes in the classroom to finish talking through old high-school memories so nobody carries heavy baggage anymore. Everything's hundred-percent good—remember that big hug out front? Right now, I need my Senior Greens Specialist in charge of the lagoon so Miss Chelsea and Shelly Turtle get the full VIP feeding lesson."

Wesley looked once at the closed oak door, remembered wrapping his fingers happily around his blue ring twenty minutes ago while Nate and Heather hugged for ten full seconds under open sky, and let out a single calm, grounded vocal hum. Tapping his own magnetic *WESLEY* badge once with complete professional confidence, he turned straight back to thirty-six-year-old **Chelsea**, unhooked the velvet VIP staff rope at the low stone feeding ledge of the lagoon, and waved her through!

---

## 2. 10:31 AM CDT — *"Nice Glove Man Gloves Just Like on the TV!"*

Stepping carefully onto the non-slip rubber mat at the accessible feeding ledge with **Brittany** right behind her elbow, **Chelsea** watched wide-eyed as **Wesley** pulled four crisp, powder-free light-blue animal-care gloves out of the dispenser box on his cart, snapped two onto his own large hands with practiced precision, and held the medium pair open for Chelsea.

Chelsea gasped as if Wesley had just handed her a crown made of solid gold.

"Brittany, look!" Chelsea whispered in pure, radiant reverence, tucking plush green **Shelly Turtle** securely into the crook of her left elbow so Brittany could help guide her fingers into the soft blue gloves. "Big brother Wes gave me *nice glove man* gloves just like Dan wears on the TV!"

Two steps back beside the thirty-inch viewing glass, Dan's grin nearly split his beard in half—laughing softly under his breath as Wesley beamed with pride, picked up two pairs of smooth, rounded-tip twelve-inch bamboo feeding tongs from his sanitized tray, kept one pair for himself, and placed the second pair gently into Chelsea's gloved right hand.

Down in the crystal-clear sunlit water of the lagoon, all three of Wesley's flagship rescued painted turtles knew the sight of blue gloves and bamboo tongs by heart:

* **Clover**, the bright yellow-striped Midland painted turtle, paddled eagerly to the low stone feeding shelf with her nose half an inch above the surface;
* **Scooter**, the sturdy Western painted turtle from Dan and Heather's original August 2007 thermostat tutorial (sporting the smooth, long-healed V-shaped notch on the rear edge of his shell), glided in right on Clover's left flank; and
* **Barnaby**, the unhurried grandfather painted turtle who had been sunning his back flippers on the cork island two minutes earlier, slid into the water with sudden dignified urgency so he wouldn't miss second breakfast.

Without speaking a single word, Wesley modeled the motion slow and clear: pinching one neat, bite-sized square of crisp green romaine from his wax-paper tray between the tips of his bamboo tongs, lowering it steadily two inches above the water so the tongs never bumped a turtle's beak, and letting Scooter snap the leaf cleanly into the water.

Then Wesley pointed gently to Chelsea's tongs, pointed to Clover waiting eagerly in front of her, and gave an encouraging two-note hum.

"Okay, Miss Clover—here comes crunchy Virginia-style lettuce from me an' Shelly Turtle!" Chelsea announced softly, steadying her gloved fingers with remarkable care until Clover rose smoothly up and plucked the romaine square clean off the bamboo tips with a tiny, polite splash!

"I did it! Did you see Clover eat my lettuce, Wes?!" Chelsea cheered, bouncing once on her sneakers without dropping her tongs while **Wesley** let out a booming, jubilant two-note cheer of his own and held his clean blue-gloved knuckles out so Chelsea could tap them twice!

Right beside them along the thirty-inch glass, twelve-year-old **Jonas** joined straight in on Wesley's happy three-note hum while holding inner hands with his twin sister **Nora**—whose shoulders bounced in a wide, completely silent belly laugh before she tapped four picture symbols across her communication board angled up toward her eyes (*"Chelsea friend feed Clover!"*) and reached over with nine-year-old **Molly** translating to give Chelsea's lavender cuff Wesley's signature two gentle squeezes.

## 3. 10:40 AM CDT — What Brittany and Dan Said Two Steps Back, & The Instant Photo for Uncle David

While **Molly** retrieved the staff's palm-sized instant film camera from behind the admissions desk so she could snap two keepsake snapshots of **Staff Member Wesley**, **Chelsea**, and **Shelly Turtle** holding up their matching blue gloves beside Clover at the lagoon (one photo for Wesley to pin inside his staff locker next to Aunt Linda's 2001 mall photo, and a duplicate photo for Chelsea to bring home to Virginia Beach), thirty-three-year-old **Brittany Fletcher** stood quietly beside **Dan Lindstrom** two paces back from the water.

Adjusting her wire-rimmed glasses as she watched thirty-six-year-old Chelsea—who less than two summers earlier had been locked behind a falsified Rhode Island probate order having rescue inhalers confiscated for talking past 7:30 PM—laughing freely in sunlit Minnesota air beside thirty-one-year-old Wesley, Brittany cleared her throat softly.

"Thank you, Dan," Brittany said quietly so only he could hear.

Dan glanced sideways in gentle surprise. "Thank *me*? For turtle lettuce?"

"For loving Heather and Wes as a package deal when you were twenty years old—and for building a place where nobody ever acts like Wes, Jonas, Nora, or Chelsea is a burden to hide in a back room," Brittany said earnestly, her eyes bright behind her lenses. "Two years ago I almost threw my whole life away marrying the guy whose lie broke Heather and Nate apart in high school. Watching Chelsea and Wes stand up there in matching blue gloves like they've known each other their whole lives... it makes a whole lot of awful years finally make sense."

Dan looked from his three children at the thirty-inch glass over toward the closed oak door of the Education Classroom, and smiled warmly.

"Funny how that works, isn't it?" Dan murmured back. "Every best habit in our house—never splitting Jonas and Nora up, building thirty-inch glass so nobody stares at drywall, putting every employee in the exact same green shirt and badge as Wes—started because two seventeen-year-olds in wheelchairs down in Virginia Beach showed teenage Heather what real loyalty looked like. We owe your family more than we could ever pay back."

Eight minutes later, at 10:48 AM CDT, the solid oak door of the Education Classroom clicked open down the hall.

Instantly, **Wesley** paused with Chelsea's developed palm-sized instant photograph held carefully by its side edges, looked straight up to inspect Heather's face, Katie's face, and Nate's face in sequence, and saw three calm, peaceful, lighter-than-air smiles with zero storm clouds anywhere in sight.

Letting out a deep, contented two-note hum that told everyone at the lagoon the fifteen-year chapter was officially settled for good, Staff Member Wesley peeled off his blue gloves, walked proudly over to slip the instant photo of himself and Chelsea into Sunny and Pebble's green paper treat bag, angled right toward Chelsea's eyes so she could show Uncle David back home on Arvin Road, and picked up his ceremonial ribbon-cutting scissors beside his first guy friend.

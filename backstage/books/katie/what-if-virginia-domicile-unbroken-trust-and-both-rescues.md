> **Archival Note:** In our earlier [***Minnesota Marriage 'What If' Dossier***](/books/katie/what-if-minnesota-marriage-and-the-unrescued-cousins), **Katie** and **Nathaniel Prairie** relocating thirteen hundred miles north to Minnesota to be with **Heather** and **Wesley** spared the twins from Brandon and Tiffany—at the devastating cost of erasing Katie's Virginia political career and leaving **Cousin Brittany** and **Cousin Chelsea** unrescued.
>
> That raises an irresistible question—especially for **Wesley's** sake, since sixteen-year-old Wesley never deserved to lose his very first guy friend through zero fault of his own: **Is there a realistic timeline where Nate and Heather stay romantically together, Wesley never loses a single Saturday with Nate, Katie still becomes a United States Senator, *and* both Brittany and Chelsea are rescued?**
>
> **Yes.** All it takes are two grounded real-world switches: **Kristen Bauer's movie-theater takedown happening *before* New Year's Eve**, and **Virginia's one-year in-state college tuition domicile statute (`Code of Virginia § 23-7.4` / `§ 23.1-502`)**.

## 1. Switch #1 (`December 2002` $\rightarrow$ `Monday, January 6, 2003`): Cousin Hannah Happens First, So Heather Destroys Tyler in Homeroom 212

In main canon ([***Episode 4, Chapter 2, Part 3***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/katie/new-year-s-eve-betrayal-broken-hearts-greenway-court/new-year-s-day-nuclear-fallout-a-lie-at-school-screaming-in-the-alley/part-3-monday-january-6-2003-tyler-s-malicious-lie-heather-s-screaming-breakup-six-months-of-silence) and [***Room 204 & Cousin Hannah's Green Parka***](/books/katie/room-204-unhandled-exception-and-tylers-cousin-lie)), Tyler ran his sleazebag breakup scam on Heather in **January 2003** before **Kristen Bauer** blew up his playbook at the varsity soccer table in **February**.

Flip those two calendar dates by three weeks:

1. **Mid-December 2002 (`The Kempsville Plaza Cinema Preview`):**
   Right before Winter Break, sitting six feet across the cafeteria aisle from the varsity table, seventeen-year-old **Heather Collins** overhears **Tyler** lean across to senior soccer captain **Kristen Bauer** using a lowered, fake-sympathetic voice—claiming he caught Kristen's boyfriend **Gavin Whitfield** hugging *"some pretty redhead in a forest-green parka"* behind the Kempsville Plaza movie theater, doubling down when Kristen calls him an idiot and says it was Gavin's nineteen-year-old VDU cousin **Hannah**, and then tacking on a sleazy offer to take Kristen out on Friday night. Kristen and two varsity track girls laugh Tyler out of the lunchroom—leaving Tyler humiliated in front of the varsity athletes, yet arrogant enough to think a quiet computer-lab girl in Homeroom 212 didn't hear it.
2. **Monday, January 6, 2003 (`8:02 AM` in Senior Homeroom 212):**
   When classes resume after New Year's Day, Tyler leans over Heather's desk smelling of cheap cologne and drops the exact same script word for word: *"Listen, Heather, I hate to be the guy to tell you this right after New Year's, except at Brittany Fletcher's party Tuesday night I saw Nate Prairie roll into a back bedroom and lock the door with some pretty blonde girl."*
   * **Beat One (`Same First Instinct`):** Heather stares at him and calls him an idiot right to his face: *"That was his twin sister Katie, you idiot! Nate and Katie live in that house!"*
   * **Beat Two (`Tyler's Practiced Double-Down`):** Stung just like he was with Kristen, Tyler scoffs—*"Keep telling yourself that, Collins! I know what his sister looks like, and this was some totally different blonde girl—ditch the wheelchair guy and let me take you out Friday night!"*
   * **Beat Three (`Prepared & Unshakeable`):** Because Heather **already heard the Cousin Hannah script in December**, Tyler's double-down hits a three-foot concrete wall. Even more than that, Heather knows **Nathaniel Prairie's** life inside and out: across eighteen months together, the **only** girls she has ever seen Nate spend time with are **his twin sister Katie**, **Heather herself**, and—back in their freshman and sophomore stories before the uncut `Lions Way` curbs blocked their walks to Earlton Court—**his cousin Brittany** (*leaving Heather privately wondering why Brittany threw a party when Nate and Katie hate alcohol, rather than doubting Nate for a split second*).
   * Standing straight up beside her desk so all twenty-six seniors in Homeroom 212 hear every syllable, Heather laughs straight in Tyler's burning-red face:
     > *"Oh, save it, Tyler! Did this 'mystery blonde' also have a flat tire behind the Kempsville Plaza movie theater like Gavin Whitfield's cousin Hannah?! Everybody in this building knows you make up sleazy cheating lies about people's sisters and cousins whenever you want a date—and the only girls Nate Prairie has ever hung out with in his life are his twin sister Katie, his cousin Brittany, and me! Go peddle your trash somewhere else!"*
3. **Why Brittany's Arc Still Happens (`Preserving the 2016 Rescue`):**
   Getting publicly torched a second time—now in front of the academic/homeroom crowd as well as the varsity athletes—makes January 6, 2003 the **absolute last time Tyler ever pulls his breakup stunt**. Radioactive inside the main building, bitter Tyler **still retreats out to `Smoker's Corner` on Macgregory Street that spring**, latches onto isolated, shunned **Brittany Fletcher** (who still threw the unauthorized New Year's Eve rave and got barred from Arvin Road by Uncle David on January 1!), talks Brittany into dropping out after her eighteenth birthday, and still traps her on a joint **Hillside Court** public-housing lease in South Richmond in 2013.

Meanwhile, ten minutes after Homeroom 212 lets out on January 6, Heather walks down to **Room 204**, wraps both Nate and Katie in a warm morning hug, tells them how she just demolished Tyler's lie, and asks gently if the two of them are okay after Brittany's New Year's Eve party. **Not one minute of trust is ever broken—and sixteen-year-old Wesley never misses a single Saturday pretzel tray at Emerald Creek Mall.**

## 2. Switch #2 (`June 2003`): Staying in Virginia on In-State Tuition Domicile & The Four-Person Package Covenant

When June 2003 arrives—and *Pomp and Circumstance* at the Virginia Beach Pavilion remains a **purely joyful memory** for Wesley watching Nate, Katie, and Heather cross the ramp in purple gowns—Heather's parents prepare to move back to Minnesota.

In this timeline, eighteen-year-old **Heather** sits down at the kitchen table with her parents, **Aunt Linda**, and **David Prairie** with a rock-solid financial and legal reality under **Code of Virginia § 23-7.4** (*Virginia's one-year continuous domicile statute*):

* **Why Leaving Virginia Costs Thousands:**
  Having lived in Hampton Roads throughout high school, Heather already holds **full Virginia in-state tuition domicile** for the **Community College of Hampton Roads (`CCHR`)** `2+2` Guaranteed Transfer pathway and **Virginia Dominion University (`VDU`)** in Norfolk—whereas uprooting immediately or trying to bounce between states triggers brutal out-of-state tuition surcharges and breaks sixteen-year-old **Wesley's** established Virginia routines and developmental-services waitlist continuity.
* **Mutual Package-Deal Devotion (`Two Pairs = One Household of Four`):**
  When Aunt Linda initially stays in Tidewater for one transitional year before her **July 2004 medical crisis**—at which point nineteen-year-old Heather petitions the Virginia Circuit Court to become eighteen-year-old **Wesley's plenary legal guardian and conservator**—there is zero question about who lives under one roof:
  * **Heather** respects with her whole heart: *Wherever Nate goes, Katie goes too.*
  * **Nate and Katie** respect with their whole hearts: *Wherever Heather goes, Wesley goes too.*

Instead of Katie and Nate renting a tiny studio alone on Greenway Court, **Nate, Katie, Heather, and Wesley** move into a zero-step ground-floor three-bedroom Norfolk/Kempsville apartment right along **`CVTA Routes #2 and #15`** (`walking/rolling distance to VDU and a single bus ride to CCHR`), with David Prairie happily building rolling kitchen carts and wide desk bays for all four of them!

## 3. `2004–2006`: Zero Tiffany Sloan, Brandon Hits a Four-Person Brick Wall, & Zero Closet Trauma

Look at how living together as an unbroken four-person household transforms Katie and Nate's early twenties:

1. **`Tiffany Sloan` Is 100% Eliminated:**
   Because Nate is happily, loyally together with Heather (`studying side by side at CCHR and VDU before marrying Heather in a joyful barrier-free Kempsville ceremony after graduation!`), Nate never dates **Tiffany Sloan** in November 2004. Zero filthy Ocean View apartment, zero stolen milk, zero Sunday-morning screaming meltdown.
2. **What Happens With `Brandon Mercer` (`May 2004`)?**
   Whether nineteen-year-old Katie never dates **Brandon Mercer** at all or briefly accepts two coffee dates with him at `C&A` / Norfolk in May 2004, **Brandon's isolation playbook dies in Week One**:
   * In main canon, Brandon only gained leverage by luring two lonely twins into an isolated Williamsburg house where nobody else saw behind closed doors.
   * Here, the very first afternoon Brandon visits their Norfolk apartment and tries to talk condescendingly over seventeen-year-old **Wesley**, reach toward Katie's wheelchair push-handles without asking, or suggest Katie come up to Williamsburg *"just the two of us without your brother,"* **Heather** calls out his controlling red flags to his face in thirty seconds flat—while six-foot-tall **Wesley** steps squarely in front of Nate's and Katie's footrests, folds both arms across his chest over his blue silicone ring, and fixes Brandon with an unmovable sentinel glare until Brandon backs out the front door for good!
   * **Zero Thirteen-Year Closet Trauma:** Because Katie and Nate are never terrorized in Williamsburg or Ocean View (and have Heather and Wesley sleeping ten feet down the hall), they never spend thirteen years sleeping on painful carpeted closet floors!

## 5. The One Quiet Cosmic Tradeoff (`Why Both Universes Carry Something Irreplaceable`)

Stepping back to compare main canon against this **Virginia Domicile Timeline** reveals a deeply poignant symmetry:

* In **this Virginia Domicile Timeline**, **Wesley** never loses a single Saturday with his first guy friend Nate, never flinches at *Pomp and Circumstance*, and gets twelve extra years side by side with Chelsea; Nate and Heather share a lifetime of unbroken marriage; Katie and Nate never suffer Brandon, Tiffany, or thirteen years on closet floors; and both Brittany and Chelsea still come home safe while Katie rises all the way to the United States Senate.
* Yet because eighteen-year-old Heather stayed in Virginia on in-state domicile instead of riding the **`NSSU` Campus Connector** bus in Saint Paul in late 2004, she never met **Dan Lindstrom**—meaning **Jonas, Nora, and fearless little sister Molly Lindstrom** were never born into this world.

Main canon cost Wesley twelve unfair years of missing postcards before giving him back his best friend, his Turtle Girl, and three mirrored suites across two states; this alternate road spares Wesley every tear from 2003 onward, reminding us that every turn of fate trades one irreplaceable miracle for another.

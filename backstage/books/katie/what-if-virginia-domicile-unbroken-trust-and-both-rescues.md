>
> That raised a heartfelt *"What If?"* question—especially for **Wesley's** sake, since sixteen-year-old Wesley never deserved to lose his very first guy friend through zero fault of his own: **What happens if Nate and Katie accept Heather's apology outside the Virginia Beach Pavilion (or if Heather holds her ground against Tyler's lie in January 2003 so trust is never broken), Heather and Wesley stay in Virginia on in-state college domicile, Katie still becomes a United States Senator, *and* both Brittany and Chelsea come home safe?**

## 1. Two Ways Into the Virginia Domicile "What If" (`January 6 Locker` or `June 14 Pavilion`)

Whether trust is preserved on the morning back from Winter Break or mended outside the **Virginia Beach Pavilion** after commencement, the structural foundation of this *"What If?"* timeline leads to the same unbroken four-person Tidewater household:

1. **Variant A — Holding Her Ground Against Tyler's Lie (`Monday, January 6, 2003`):**
   If **Heather Collins** stubbornly stands her ground at her upstairs locker—insisting to **Tyler's** face that the pretty blonde girl in the matching `SwiftChair` who locked a bedroom door with Nate on New Year's Eve *was* his twin sister **Katie** even when Tyler doubles down claiming otherwise (or if **Kristen Bauer's** varsity-table takedown of Tyler's *"mystery redhead behind the Kempsville Plaza movie theater"* lie about Gavin Whitfield's cousin **Hannah** happens right before Winter Break so Heather already knows Tyler's exact script)—trust between Nate, Katie, and Heather is never broken for a single morning. Publicly torched in Homeroom 212, Tyler still retreats to `Smoker's Corner` on Macgregory Street that spring and still traps shunned **Brittany** onto a South Richmond lease by 2013.
2. **Variant B — Accepting Heather's Full-Accountability Apology (`Saturday, June 14, 2003` at the Virginia Beach Pavilion):**
   At `11:52 AM` under the blooming crape-myrtles outside [***Episode 4, Chapter 2, Part 4***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/katie/seventeen-senior-year-greenway-court/drunken-new-year/part-4-purple-gowns-at-the-pavilion-pomp-and-circumstance-heather-s-apology), when sixteen-year-old **Wesley** holds his breath turning his smooth blue silicone ring between both palms after Heather confesses the whole truth without a single excuse, Katie and Nate welcome Heather back on clear calm-communication ground rules—turning *Pomp and Circumstance* into the moment Wesley throws his fist into the air cheering instead of crying, followed by Heather and Nate hugging side by side under the crape-myrtle shade.

## 2. Staying in Virginia Under `Code of Virginia § 23-7.4` & Mutual Package-Deal Devotion

Once Nate and Heather are together in June 2003, eighteen-year-old **Heather** and **Aunt Linda** (Wesley's mother) sit down with **David Prairie** around a rock-solid financial and legal reality under **Code of Virginia § 23-7.4** (*Virginia's one-year continuous college domicile statute*):

* **Why Leaving Virginia Costs Thousands:**
  Having lived continuously in Hampton Roads throughout high school, Heather already holds **full Virginia in-state tuition domicile** for the **Community College of Hampton Roads (`CCHR`)** `2+2` Guaranteed Transfer pathway and **Virginia Dominion University (`VDU`)** in Norfolk—whereas uprooting to bounce between states forfeits Virginia domicile, triggers triple out-of-state tuition, and disrupts sixteen-year-old **Wesley's** established Tidewater developmental-services roll right before he turns eighteen.
* **Mutual Package-Deal Devotion (`Two Pairs = One Household of Four`):**
  Aunt Linda rents a two-bedroom Tidewater apartment ten minutes from Arvin Road, and following her **July 2004 cardiac hospitalization**, nineteen-year-old Heather is appointed eighteen-year-old **Wesley's plenary legal guardian and conservator** in Norfolk Circuit Court as **Katie, Nate, Heather, and Wesley** move into a zero-step ground-floor three-bedroom apartment off Hampton Boulevard along **`CVTA Routes #2 and #15`**, living by an unbreakable mirrored rule:
  * **Heather** honors with her whole heart: *Wherever Nate goes, Katie goes too.*
  * **Nate and Katie** honor with their whole hearts: *Wherever Heather goes, Wesley goes with her.*

## 3. Summer 2003: The Ten-Campus College Tour for Rising-Junior Wesley (`Zero Crystal Balls`)

Right between sixteen-year-old Wesley's **sophomore and junior years at Northwood High School**, **Heather and Aunt Linda's** train of thought is proactive and pressure-free: by touring colleges two summers *before* Wesley's high-school graduation, Wesley will already know from lived sensory experience exactly where he feels safe when senior year arrives. Across July and August 2003, Katie, Nate, Heather, and Wesley visit **ten campuses**:

1. **Stops 1–4 (`Tidewater & Peninsula — Saturday, July 12, 2003`):**
   * **`Community College of Hampton Roads` (`CCHR`, Norfolk Campus):** Flat, familiar commuter sidewalks and automatic doors where David Prairie already has straight *A*s.
   * **`Virginia Dominion University` (`VDU`, Hampton Boulevard, Norfolk):** Wide brick pedestrian malls, zero-step elevator lobbies, and watching <led>2 | NAVAL STATION | via HAMPTON BLVD</led> glide right past the quad—visited with zero crystal balls long before anyone knows VDU is where all four will eventually plant their flag.
   * **`Warwick University` (`WU`, Newport News):** Because Heather drives Aunt Linda's accessible minivan across the Hampton Roads Bridge-Tunnel, Katie and Nate still have zero Peninsula bus-transfer knowledge of their own while admiring the sunny Neo-Georgian colonnades and watching a blue-and-silver CVTA coach exit the `SR-312 & Shoe Lane` roundabout displaying <led>112 NNTC</led>.
   * **`College of Charles & Anne` (`C&A`, Williamsburg):** Beautiful historic trees, immediately ruled out when three-hundred-year-old uneven, frost-heaved colonial bricks rattle Katie's and Nate's front wheelchair casters nonstop.
2. **Stops 5–7 (`Richmond, Fairfax, & Harrisonburg — Saturday, July 19, 2003`):**
   * **`Central Virginia University` (`CVU`, Richmond):** Howling West Broad Street ambulance sirens and city-bus air brakes keep Wesley's hands clamped over his ears.
   * **`George Wythe University` (`GWU`, Fairfax):** Clean modern curb cuts, yet four hours of bumper-to-bumper `I-95` gridlock away from David and Aunt Linda in Tidewater.
   * **`Shenandoah Valley University` (`SVU`, Harrisonburg — in-universe *James Madison University*):** Arriving in the middle of a booming **Summer Preview Open House**—complete with a brass pep band echoing off bluestone walls and a giant purple-and-gold balloon arch—overwhelms sixteen-year-old Wesley's sensory threshold within ten minutes. Heather immediately cuts the tour short to protect Wesley, though once calm in the quiet van, Wesley taps the campus brochure kindly to show he would still be interested in checking `SVU` out again on a normal weekday when it isn't an Open House.
3. **Stops 8–9 (`Fishersville & Blacksburg — Sunday, July 20, 2003`):**
   * **Stop 8 — `Roosevelt Rehabilitation Center` (`RRC`, Fishersville) & The `Redmond Hall` Safe-Person Refusal:** Driving thirty-five minutes south down `I-81` from Harrisonburg, the four sign in at the **`Bates-Harding Building`** reception window. Because **`Redmond Hall`** (the medical dormitory) is housed inside the exact same administration wing, the tour stops short sixty yards down the polished linoleum corridor the instant the admissions guide announces that dormitories are strictly gender-separated and that non-verbal students like Wesley are assigned to mandatory `Redmond Hall` medical supervision. Growing up, whenever Wesley woke up scared and crying from a nightmare in the dark, he always knew instinctively that he could walk into Heather's bedroom, climb into bed beside his **Safe Person**, and be cuddled by Heather until his breathing calmed down. Realizing a locked steel corridor would bar him from reaching Heather at night, sixteen-year-old Wesley bursts into heartbroken sobs in Heather's arms. Wrapping both arms tightly around her cousin, Heather looks calmly at the tour guide and says politely: ***"Thank you for your time, ma'am—this is Wesley making his decision, and the answer is no."*** None of the four ever enroll at `RRC`.
   * **Stop 9 — `Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute` (`CPI`, Blacksburg):** Rolling out onto the sunlit `Ironstone` quad beside `Commonwealth Green` as a Maroon Transit bus passes displaying <led>1 | CBURG TRANS CTR | via S MAIN ST</led>, eighteen-year-old Nate falls completely in love with the mountain valley—until climbing the long incline toward `Kincaid Hall` drops a green LED bar off both 24-volt `SwiftChair` battery gauges, leaving Nate worried that Blacksburg's steep hills and freezing mountain winters would wear out their wheelchair batteries too fast.
4. **Stop 10 (`Minnesota Family Visit & North Star State University — August 2003`):**
   Katie and Nate fly north to Minneapolis–Saint Paul with Heather, Aunt Linda, and Wesley to warmly meet Heather and Wesley's grandparents, aunts, and uncles (with Grandpa building a sturdy birch porch ramp for their two `SwiftChairs`) and ride the intercampus `Campus Connector` bus across **`North Star State University` (`NSSU`)**—experiencing both the underground winter skyway tunnels and the steep out-of-state tuition math firsthand before flying home together to Tidewater.

## 4. `2004–2031+`: Stopping Brandon Cold, Heather's 5-Step Special Ed Career, Brittany's `1991` Crew Cab Videos, & Both Rescues

Living together in Virginia as an unbroken household transforms their twenties and thirties while preserving Katie's Senate destiny and both cousin rescues:

* **`Tiffany Sloan` Is Eliminated & `Brandon Mercer` Hits a Four-Person Brick Wall (`May 12, 2004`):**
  Happily loyal to Heather (`marrying her in a barrier-free 2007 Kempsville ceremony as Heather Prairie`), Nate never dates Tiffany Sloan. And the very first afternoon **Brandon Mercer** visits their Norfolk living room from `CCHR` and tries to talk condescendingly over seventeen-year-old **Wesley** while suggesting Katie come up to Williamsburg *"just the two of us without your brother,"* **Heather** calls out every controlling red flag in thirty seconds flat while six-foot-tall **Wesley** steps squarely in front of Katie's and Nate's footrests with folded arms until Brandon backs out the door forever (`zero Williamsburg captivity, zero thirteen years sleeping in dark closets`).
* **Heather's Canonical 5-Step Special Education Career Ladder (`Zero North Lion Scales & Tails`):**
  Because Heather stays in Virginia and marries Nate rather than moving to Minnesota and meeting wildlife biologist Dan Lindstrom, ***North Lion Scales & Tails* never opens in this timeline**. Instead, Heather builds a remarkable five-step career in **Special Education — Adapted Curriculum**:
  1. **`2003–2007`:** `CCHR` (`2+2`) $\rightarrow$ `VDU` `B.S.Ed. in Special Education (Adapted Curriculum)`, working as a Special Education Paraprofessional while Wesley finishes his age 18–21 transition program.
  2. **`2007–2012`:** **High School Adapted Curriculum Classroom Teacher** in Hampton Roads while earning her `M.S.Ed.` at `VDU` (once Wesley ages out at 22, he spends school hours safely at home on Arvin Road beside Nate and Katie in their `Prairie Twin Interactive` office—never in a stranger-run facility—until Heather gets home at `3:00 PM`).
  3. **`2012–2017`:** **Special Education Department Chair & IEP Coordinator**, fiercely protecting non-verbal students and families in IEP meetings.
  4. **`2017–2025`:** **Assistive Technology & Inclusive Transition Specialist**, coaching Tidewater educators on `MagnaVoice` AAC tablets and sensory accommodations.
  5. **`2026+` (U.S. Senate Years):** **Director of Special Education & AAC Design at `Prairie Twin Interactive, LLC`**, making her career 100% portable between **Sunset Drive in Alexandria (`Mon–Wed`)** and **Arvin Road in Virginia Beach (`Thu–Sun`)** with zero taxpayer-payroll conflict under `5 U.S.C. § 3110`.
* **Rescuing Both Brittany (`Feb 6, 2016`) and Chelsea (`Jul 16, 2016`) + Living Uncle David's `L-1-2-3-R` Truck Videos (`Jun 2017+`):**
  Katie still rides Southside `CVTA` public buses daily, hits the `5:30 PM` Municipal Center curfew, wins **City Council (`2010`)** and the **House of Delegates (`2013`)**, defeats **`HB 663` (`Feb 4, 2016`)**, walks into that South Richmond church **alongside Nate and thirty-year-old Heather** on **February 6, 2016** to rescue **Brittany** from **Tyler**, and brings **Cousin Chelsea** (hugging *Shelly Turtle*) home from `Mount Regalis` in **July 2016**—giving **Wesley and Chelsea twelve uninterrupted years (`2016–2028+`)** together on Arvin Road before Senator Katie Prairie passes the **2028 *Supported Companions Act***. And starting in **June 2017**, while **Uncle David** is very much alive in the passenger bench of his navy-over-silver **`1991 Crestline Garrison 350` Crew Cab 4x4** (with its functional chrome-latched opening triangular front vent windows and unsynchronized granny-low **`L-1-2-3-R`** four-speed), Brittany films her unscripted driving and `Hartley` / `Factory Service Manual` / `Maintenance Binder` repair lessons on **`StreamTube` and `The Grid`** to earn a modest independent income—making her eventual 2030–2031 stewardship of David's truck emotionally earned across fourteen years.

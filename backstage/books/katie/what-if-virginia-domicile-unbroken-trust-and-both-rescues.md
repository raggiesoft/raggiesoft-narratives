## 1. Two Coequal Ways Into the Virginia Domicile Timeline (`Homeroom 212` or `The Pavilion Crossroads`)

Whether trust is preserved before New Year's Day or mended outside the **Virginia Beach Pavilion** on graduation morning, the structural foundation is identical:

1. **The Homeroom 212 Variant (`December 2002` $\rightarrow$ `Monday, January 6, 2003`):**
   If **Kristen Bauer's** varsity-table takedown of **Tyler's** *"mystery redhead behind the Kempsville Plaza movie theater"* lie about Gavin Whitfield's cousin **Hannah** happens right before Winter Break instead of February, seventeen-year-old **Heather Collins** hears Tyler's sleazebag script three weeks *before* he tries it on her. When Tyler leans over her desk in Homeroom 212 on January 6 claiming Nate locked a bedroom door with *"some pretty blonde girl,"* Heather calls him an idiot (*"That was his twin sister Katie!"*)—and when Tyler doubles down claiming it was someone else, Heather laughs right in his face because across eighteen months the **only** girls Nate has ever spent time with are **Katie**, **Heather**, and earlier **Cousin Brittany** (*leaving Heather wondering why Brittany threw a party when the twins hate alcohol rather than doubting Nate for a second*). Publicly torched, Tyler still retreats to `Smoker's Corner` on Macgregory Street that spring and still traps shunned Brittany onto a South Richmond lease by 2013.
2. **The Live Story Crossroads Path (`Saturday, June 14, 2003` at the Virginia Beach Pavilion):**
   At the interactive **Story Crossroads** at the end of [***Episode 4, Chapter 2, Part 4***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/katie/seventeen-senior-year-greenway-court/drunken-new-year/part-4-purple-gowns-at-the-pavilion-pomp-and-circumstance-heather-s-apology), when sixteen-year-old **Wesley** holds his breath turning his smooth blue silicone ring between both palms after Heather confesses the whole truth, choosing ***Accept Heather's Apology & Welcome Her Back*** lets Katie and Nate forgive Heather under the blooming crape-myrtles—turning *Pomp and Circumstance* into the moment Wesley throws his fist into the air cheering instead of crying.

## 2. Staying in Virginia Under `Code of Virginia § 23-7.4` & Mutual Package-Deal Devotion

Once Nate and Heather are together in June 2003, eighteen-year-old **Heather** sits down at the kitchen table with her parents, **Aunt Linda**, and **David Prairie** with a rock-solid financial and legal reality under **Code of Virginia § 23-7.4** (*Virginia's one-year continuous college domicile statute*):

* **Why Leaving Virginia Costs Thousands:**
  Having lived continuously in Hampton Roads throughout high school, Heather already holds **full Virginia in-state tuition domicile** for the **Community College of Hampton Roads (`CCHR`)** `2+2` Guaranteed Transfer pathway and **Virginia Dominion University (`VDU`)** in Norfolk—whereas uprooting to bounce between states forfeits Virginia domicile and breaks sixteen-year-old **Wesley's** established Tidewater routines and developmental-services continuity.
* **Mutual Package-Deal Devotion (`Two Pairs = One Household of Four`):**
  When Aunt Linda stays in Tidewater for one transitional year before her **July 2004 medical crisis**—at which point nineteen-year-old Heather petitions the Virginia Circuit Court to become eighteen-year-old **Wesley's plenary legal guardian and conservator**—all four adults live by a mutual, unbreakable covenant:
  * **Heather** respects with her whole heart: *Wherever Nate goes, Katie goes too.*
  * **Nate and Katie** respect with their whole hearts: *Wherever Heather goes, Wesley goes too.*

Instead of Katie and Nate renting a tiny studio alone on Greenway Court, **Nate, Katie, Heather, and Wesley** move into a zero-step ground-floor three-bedroom Norfolk/Kempsville apartment right along **`CVTA Routes #2 and #15`**, with David Prairie building rolling kitchen carts and wide desk bays for all four of them.

## 3. `2004–2005`: Eliminating Tiffany, Stopping Brandon Cold, & Touring Warwick University Beside `112 NNTC`

Living together as an unbroken four-person household transforms Katie and Nate's early twenties while preserving every geographical detail of their Tidewater canon:

1. **`Tiffany Sloan` Is 100% Eliminated:**
   Because Nate stays happily loyal to Heather (`studying side by side at CCHR and VDU before marrying Heather in a barrier-free Kempsville ceremony`), Nate never dates **Tiffany Sloan** in November 2004.
2. **`Brandon Mercer` Hits a Four-Person Brick Wall (`May 2004`):**
   The very first afternoon **Brandon Mercer** visits their Norfolk apartment after meeting Katie at CCHR and tries to talk condescendingly over seventeen-year-old **Wesley** or suggest Katie come up to Williamsburg *"just the two of us without your brother,"* **Heather** calls out every controlling red flag to his face in thirty seconds flat—while six-foot-tall **Wesley** steps squarely in front of Nate's and Katie's footrests, folds both arms across his chest over his blue silicone ring, and fixes Brandon with an unmovable sentinel glare until Brandon backs out the front door for good. Zero Williamsburg captivity, zero Ocean View isolation, and zero thirteen years sleeping on closet floors.
3. **October 2005 (`Touring Warwick University in Newport News & Spotting Route 112 NNTC at the Shoe Lane Roundabout`):**
   When Wesley turns eighteen and ages out of high school transition services, **Heather drives Nate, Katie, and Wesley across the Hampton Roads Bridge-Tunnel in her accessible van** up to Newport News to tour **Warwick University** (*Neo-Georgian red-brick-and-white-columned halls along wide brick pedestrian walkways*) as they evaluate inclusive campus-life and animal-care programs for Wesley alongside VDU in Norfolk.
   * **Why Heather Driving Matters:** Having Heather drive her van across the harbor cleanly preserves the canonical fact that **Katie and Nate have never ridden or memorized Peninsula bus routes on their own**.
   * **The `112 NNTC` Easter Egg at `SR-312 & Shoe Lane`:** As the four of them walk and roll along Warwick University's red-brick walkway beside the landscaped roundabout at **State Route 312 and Shoe Lane**, a blue-and-silver **CVTA Route 112** bus curves counterclockwise out of the roundabout with **`112 NNTC`** glowing on its amber LED front destination header—bound for the **Newport News Transit Center** down at `35th Street & Washington Avenue`. Readers who have also experienced the *Pavilion Bridge* Story Path will recognize `NNTC` immediately as the exact downtown transfer hub whose filthy bathrooms Katie encountered during their October 2004 Williamsburg escape—seen here from a sunlit university sidewalk on a Saturday afternoon where neither twin ever had to flee for safety.

## 4. `2007–2029+`: Katie's Full Political Career, Tidewater *North Lion*, & Rescuing Both Brittany and Chelsea

Every milestone of Katie Prairie's public-service career and both cousin rescues still unfolds in full:

* **Tidewater *North Lion Scales & Tails* + *Prairie Twin Interactive*:**
  While Katie and Nate launch **`Prairie Twin Interactive, LLC`**, **Heather (`VDU B.S. Biology & Special Education`)** and **Staff Member Wesley** open a Hampton Roads ***North Lion Scales & Tails*** educational reptile conservatory and **`StreamTube`** channel right on **`CVTA Route #15` on Military Highway**—where Nate films and captions Wesley's painted-turtle habitat videos from Day One.
* **Katie's Political Rise (`2010–2026`):**
  Even though Heather drives an accessible van for hauling fifty-gallon glass terrariums and crossing the harbor, **Katie and Nate proudly ride Southside `CVTA` public buses every day** for their own daily independence. Katie still hits Virginia Beach's `5:30 PM` Municipal Center bus curfew, still wins **Kempsville City Council (`2010`)** and the **85th House District (`2013`)**, and still defeats **`House Bill 663`** on **Thursday, February 4, 2016** alongside **Sam, Mandy, and Sarah Hawkins** and **Uncle Richard Callaway**.
* **February 6, 2016 & July 2016 (`Rescuing Both Brittany and Chelsea`):**
  On **Saturday, February 6, 2016**, **Nate, Katie, and Heather** walk and roll into that South Richmond church together—all three Northwood Class of 2003 alumni facing down **Tyler** as Nate says ***"I object!"*** to bring **Brittany** safely home to Kempsville. Five months later, **David, Katie, Nate, Heather, and Brittany** bring thirty-four-year-old **Cousin Chelsea** (`and Shelly Turtle`) home from Braxton, Rhode Island—where **Staff Member Wesley is already waiting in Kempsville with fresh romaine squares**, giving Wesley and Chelsea **twelve extra years side by side in Virginia Beach** before **U.S. Senator Katie Prairie** passes the landmark **2028 *Supported Companions Act***!

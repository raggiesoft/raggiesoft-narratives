## 1. The DSCC & Retiring Senator Martin Warren's Office (`January–June 2026`)

When retiring three-term **U.S. Senator Martin Warren (`D-Virginia`)** announced in January 2026 that he would not seek re-election, the **Democratic Senatorial Campaign Committee (`DSCC`)** on Capitol Hill and McLean venture capitalist businessman **Preston Holloway's** consultants in McLean treated the June 16, 2026 Democratic primary as a coronation:

1. **The `"Polite Token Challenger"` Misreading:**
   When Katie filed her `10,000+` verified voter signatures (`collected at bus transfer centers and train stations across all eleven congressional districts`) and announced she would campaign statewide exclusively by public transit alongside Nate without hiring a single D.C. media consultant, Holloway's team didn't even run negative ads against her—assuming a forty-one-year-old state senator in a power wheelchair riding county mountain buses was merely running to highlight transit funding before endorsing Holloway in June.
2. **Why *The Accessibility Project* Broke Holloway's Turnout Model:**
   What the DSCC consultants missed—because their models only polled high-propensity suburban drivers—was that Katie and Nate's live-blogged **Accessibility Project** (`fixing jammed wheelchair lifts on camera, riding the Maroon Express into Bluewater, visiting the Brooks family compound off Lee Highway, and wearing the National Firearms Association's 'F' grade as a badge of honor in Fairfax`) went viral in every rural county and urban bus corridor in Virginia. When Katie defeated Holloway **`55.6%` to `44.4%`** on June 16, 2026, Holloway's high-priced McLean media consultants were left staring in stunned silence at a fourteen-million-dollar self-funded and Super PAC ad buy that had been beaten across all eleven congressional districts by two siblings on a two-dollar bus pass.

## 2. Wednesday Morning, November 4, 2026 (`7:10 AM`): President Garrett Thorne's Meltdown & Uncle Richard on *Beacon News*

At `6:14 AM` on Wednesday, November 4, 2026—nine hours after every network Decision Desk called the Virginia U.S. Senate race for **Senator-Elect Katie Prairie (`54.6%` to `44.8%`)** alongside Virginia's **`61.4%` School Construction & Transit Referendum**—lame-duck **President Garrett Thorne** posted an all-caps tirade attacking *"BROKEN KATIE PRAIRIE, WHO CANNOT EVEN WALK OR STAND FOR OUR GREAT NATIONAL ANTHEM AND STOLE THE ELECTION IN VIRGINIA WITH PHONY BUS VOTES!"*

Down in **Danville, Virginia**, however, retired **Former House Majority Leader Richard "Uncle Richard" Callaway (`R-Danville`)** saw the President's post over his morning coffee, picked up his phone, and booked himself live at `7:10 AM` on conservative cable flagship ***Beacon News Morning*** (`with his twenty-four-year-old niece and nephews Sarah, Mandy, and Sam Hawkins watching from their group chat`):

> *"Listen to me very carefully,"* Uncle Richard told the stunned *Beacon News* anchors from his living room in Danville. *"I served as the Republican Majority Leader of the Virginia House of Delegates alongside Katie Prairie and her brother Nate. I disagreed with Katie on plenty of tax and spending bills, and she beat my caucus fair and square when she flipped the Seventh Senate District. Watching the President of the United States mock a woman in a wheelchair because she can't stand up for the National Anthem isn't conservatism—it's pathetic, cowardly trash. And as for her votes? Katie Prairie won fifty-four-point-six percent of the Commonwealth of Virginia because she and her brother rode every dusty bus route in Southwest and Southside Virginia that Washington politicians can't even find on a map!"*

## 3. Inside the Capitol Cabinet Shop: Preparing the Chamber for the Three-Member U.S. Senate Wheelchair Caucus (`November–December 2026`)

When the **120th United States Congress** convened on Sunday, January 3, 2027, the **Architect of the Capitol's** historic woodworking shop had just completed the largest accessibility upgrade to the Senate Chamber floor since **Senator Tess Dunmore (`D-Illinois`)** first entered the Senate:

* **Two Incoming Freshman Democrats in Wheelchairs (`Katie Prairie` & `Josh Novak`):**
  As shown in the November 2026 Senate Democratic freshman orientation reception in the Capitol, the incoming class of eleven new Democratic Senators included **two** full-time wheelchair users:
  1. **Senator-Elect Katie Prairie (`D-Virginia`):** Seated in her compact black motorized **`SwiftChair`** power wheelchair (after resigning her Virginia State Senate seat on December 1, 2026 under `Article IV, Section 4` of the Constitution of Virginia).
  2. **Senator-Elect Josh Novak (`D-Iowa`):** A thirty-nine-year-old paralyzed Army veteran and former state legislator who used an ultralight titanium manual wheelchair, seated front-and-center in the freshman class portrait beside **Senate Majority Leader Charles Schuman (`D-New York`)**.
* **Joining Senior Wheelchair User Senator Tess Dunmore (`D-Illinois`):**
  Together with combat-wounded Iraq War veteran **Senator Tess Dunmore (`D-Illinois`)**—whose custom-engineered mahogany ramp curves behind the back row of the Democratic side of the chamber—the 120th Senate featured a historic **three-member Senate Wheelchair Caucus**. Prior to January 3, 2027, the Architect of the Capitol's cabinetmakers fitted two additional historic nineteenth-century mahogany Senate desks on the back aisle with concealed hydraulic height-risers and brass floor-plate stops so both Senator Prairie's `SwiftChair` and Senator Novak's manual chair could roll flush under their writing surfaces without scratching a single square inch of the 1819 veneer.

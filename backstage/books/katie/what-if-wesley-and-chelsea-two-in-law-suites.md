## 1. Why Two Connected In-Law Suites Solve the Impossible Geography Puzzle

If you tried to pick *only* Virginia or *only* Minnesota, one half of the couple would lose the anchor that makes them thrive:
- Pulling Wesley permanently out of Minnesota for twelve months a year would tear him away from **Heather, Dan, Jonas, Nora, and Molly** and his beloved animal-care career at ***North Lion Scales & Tails***.
- Pulling Chelsea permanently out of Virginia for twelve months a year would tear her away from **Uncle David, Katie, Nate, and Brittany** and the quiet coastal home that healed eight years of Rhode Island group-home trauma.

With **Prairie Twin Interactive** and ***North Lion Scales & Tails*** both thriving—and Katie's 2028 federal law guaranteeing seamless dual-state Medicaid Home and Community-Based Services (`HCBS`) waiver reciprocity—the two families build **architecturally twinned `520-square-foot` zero-step ground-floor in-law suites** attached directly to *both* family homes:

1. **The Coastal Suite (`737 Arvin Road, Virginia Beach, VA` — Autumn, Winter Holidays, & Spring Months):**
   - Built onto the quiet shaded garden side of Uncle David's brick ranch house and connected directly into the main family kitchen through a **solid-oak interior pocket door**—so Uncle David, Brittany, Katie, and Nate are literally ten steps away day or night.
   - While in Virginia, Wesley serves as an enthusiastic remote/visiting ambassador alongside local Tidewater turtle rescues and tends to Chelsea's beloved yellow-bellied sliders ***Sunny* and *Pebble*** in their custom `75-gallon` oak-cabinet habitat!
2. **The North Star Suite (`Heather & Dan's Home on TCT Route 64, Maplewood, MN` — Late Spring, Summer, & Early Autumn Months):**
   - Built onto the ground level of Heather and Dan's Maplewood home and connected directly to their main kitchen through a **heated cedar-and-glass breezeway**—five minutes down **`Twin Cities Transit Route 64`** from ***North Lion Scales & Tails***.
   - To protect both Wesley's and Chelsea's autistic and developmental need for routine, the Maplewood suite mirrors the Virginia Beach floor plan **inch for inch**: the oatmeal loveseat sits on the exact same wall, the `75-gallon` basking turtle habitat sits on the exact same right-hand oak cabinet, and the bedroom doorway opens at the exact same angle—while on weekdays, Chelsea gets to put on her light-blue animal-care gloves right beside **Staff Member Wesley** in the sunlit *North Lion* conservatory to feed crisp romaine leaves to *Clover*, *Scooter*, and *Barnaby*!

## 2. Inside Their Shared Sanctuary: What They Get to Do Together Every Single Day

Inside both connected suites, Wesley and Chelsea get every ounce of the quiet, everyday couplehood they dreamed of at Bayshore Town Center without ever having to guard themselves:

- **Their Own Cozy Shared Living Room:** Every evening after dinner with the family next door, Wesley and Chelsea curl up side by side on their plush oatmeal-linen loveseat in comfortable fleece and flannel loungewear—Wesley's calm, steady arm wrapped around his *Turtle Girl's* shoulders in the exact deep-pressure hug that presses both of their frayed nervous systems back together, watching *Sunny* and *Pebble* climb onto their cork basking dock or streaming gentle nature documentaries with the volume turned low.
- **Sharing Their Own Queen Bed Together:** Through the wide, step-free bedroom doorway sits their tidy shared queen bed topped with a sage-and-navy patchwork quilt. On the wooden nightstand beside the warm amber lamp sit Chelsea's maintenance and rescue asthma inhalers right next to a small cedar dish for Wesley's smooth blue silicone ring, with plush green *Shelly Turtle* tucked right at the center of the pillows. Whenever thunder rumbles outside in Virginia Beach or summer rain patters against the Maplewood eaves, neither of them ever has to wake up alone or frightened.
- **Safe Morning Tea & Toast Together:** Their compact roll-under kitchenette is outfitted specifically for cognitive and physical safety—no open gas flames or scorching exposed coils, only an auto-shutoff warm-water kettle, a cool-touch toaster, and pre-portioned breakfast fruit and turtle-pellet tubs organized by Brittany and Heather.

## 3. Loving Guardrails: Respecting Plenary Guardianship & What Remains Off-Limits

Even in this joyful *What If* reality—with **100% guardian blessing from both David/Katie/Nate/Brittany and Heather/Dan** *and* **Katie's landmark federal law** backing them—both families lovingly honor the real-world cognitive and legal boundaries that keep Wesley and Chelsea safe from harm:

1. **No Buying Real Estate, Signing Mortgages, or Taking On Debt:** Neither Wesley nor Chelsea has the cognitive capacity to read a forty-page closing disclosure, understand compound interest, pay property-tax escrows, or spot a home-repair scammer at the front door. Under their court-supervised **plenary conservatorships** (explicitly preserved by Title III of Katie's 2028 Act), the real estate, utilities, insurance, and maintenance stay `100%` owned and managed by their guardian families—meaning no predatory lender, credit-card mailer, or timeshare salesperson can ever bind either of them to a contract.
2. **Guardian Proximity Within Ten Seconds (`Zero Unsupervised Living Alone`):** Living together in a connected in-law suite is **not** an isolated apartment across town. Because Chelsea has moderate-to-severe asthma (`requiring someone who can immediately distinguish her daily maintenance inhaler from her rescue albuterol puffer and call Dr. Carter or 911 if her peak flow drops`) and Wesley is non-verbal (`requiring a trusted communicator if a smoke detector chirps or a pipe leaks`), that unlocked interior oak pocket door to David and Brittany in Virginia—and to Heather and Dan in Minnesota—means trained family hands are ten seconds away `24/7` without hovering inside their living room!
3. **Separate Protected Fiduciary & ABLE Accounts (`Zero Benefit Cuts`):** Thanks to Katie's repeal of the SSI/DAC marriage and `Form SSA-4178` holding-out penalties, Wesley keeps `100%` of his *North Lion* wages, ABLE savings, and benefits managed transparently by Heather, and Chelsea keeps `100%` of her benefits and ABLE account managed transparently by David, Katie, Nate, and Brittany—giving two gentle souls who spent half their lives surviving a loud, unkind world a peaceful home of their very own, surrounded on both sides of the door by safe people.

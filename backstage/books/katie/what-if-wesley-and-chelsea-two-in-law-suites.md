## 1. Why Three Mirrored Connected In-Law Suites Solve the Year-Round Senate & Minnesota Puzzle

Building a suite at only one house would have broken a vital family anchor:
- Pulling Wesley permanently out of Minnesota twelve months a year would tear him away from **Heather, Dan, Jonas, Nora, and Molly** and his beloved animal-care career at ***North Lion Scales & Tails***.
- Pulling Chelsea permanently out of Virginia twelve months a year would tear her away from **Uncle David, Katie, Nate, and Brittany**.
- Building in Virginia Beach without **Alexandria** would have split the Virginia household apart whenever Katie, Nate, and Brittany rode **ARTS Business Class** north to **Sunset Drive** for year-round U.S. Senate sessions.

With **Prairie Twin Interactive** and ***North Lion Scales & Tails*** both thriving—and Katie's federal law guaranteeing seamless multi-state Medicaid Home and Community-Based Services (`HCBS`) waiver reciprocity—the two families build **three inch-for-inch identical `520-square-foot` zero-step ground-floor in-law suites**:

1. **The Coastal Suite (`737 Arvin Road, Virginia Beach, VA` — District Weeks, Autumn/Winter Holidays, & Coastal Stretches):**
   - Built onto the quiet shaded garden side of `737 Arvin Road` and connected directly into the main family kitchen through an unlocked **solid-oak interior pocket door**—so Uncle David (`before 2032`), Brittany, Katie, and Nate are ten steps away day or night while Wesley and Chelsea tend to ***Sunny* and *Pebble*** in their `75-gallon` oak-cabinet habitat!
2. **The Old Town Suite (`Sunset Drive, Alexandria, VA` — Year-Round U.S. Senate Session Weeks):**
   - Built onto the ground-floor garden wing of `Sunset Drive` (one level block from **`King Street–Old Town`**) and connected directly to the main kitchen through an identical unlocked **solid-oak interior pocket door**—allowing Wesley and Chelsea to ride **ARTS Business Class** between Norfolk (`NFK`) and Alexandria (`ALX`) right alongside Katie, Nate, and Brittany without ever breaking routine!
3. **The North Star Suite (`Heather & Dan's Home on TCT Route 64, Maplewood, MN` — Late Spring, Summer, & *North Lion* Stretches):**
   - Built onto the ground level of Heather and Dan's Maplewood home and connected directly to their main kitchen through a **heated cedar-and-glass breezeway**—five minutes down **`Twin Cities Transit Route 64`** from ***North Lion Scales & Tails***, where Chelsea puts on matching light-blue animal-care gloves right beside **Staff Member Wesley** in the sunlit conservatory to feed *Clover*, *Scooter*, and *Barnaby*!

To protect both Wesley's and Chelsea's autistic and developmental need for unbroken spatial predictability, **all three suites share the exact same floor plan down to the half-inch**: the oatmeal-linen loveseat sits on the exact same wall, the `75-gallon` basking turtle habitat (`each stocked with its own permanent resident pair so no animal is ever stressed in travel`) sits on the exact same right-hand oak cabinet, and the wide step-free bedroom doorway opens at the exact same angle.

## 2. Inside Their Shared Sanctuary: Everyday Couplehood Together

Inside all three connected suites, Wesley and Chelsea live the peaceful, everyday life together they first dreamed of at Bayshore Town Center:

- **Their Own Cozy Shared Living Room:** Every evening after dinner with the family next door, Wesley and Chelsea curl up side by side on their plush oatmeal-linen loveseat in comfortable fleece and flannel loungewear—Wesley's calm, steady arm wrapped around his *Turtle Girl's* shoulders in the deep-pressure hug that grounds both of their nervous systems, watching their turtles climb onto the cork basking dock or streaming gentle nature documentaries with the volume low.
- **Sharing Their Own Queen Bed (& Four-Cousin Goodnight Cuddles):** Whenever they are at Arvin Road or Sunset Drive, Chelsea still loves trotting down the main hall for fifteen minutes of pre-bedtime four-cousin cuddles on Katie, Nate, and Brittany's custom oak platform bed before Wesley taps softly at the doorframe to walk hand in hand back through the oak pocket door into their own tidy **shared queen bed** under a sage-and-navy patchwork quilt—with Chelsea's maintenance and rescue asthma inhalers resting right next to Wesley's carved cedar ring dish and plush green *Shelly Turtle* centered on the pillows.
- **Safe Morning Tea & Toast Together:** Each roll-under kitchenette is engineered specifically for cognitive and physical safety—zero open gas flames or scorching exposed coils, only an auto-shutoff warm-water kettle, a cool-touch toaster, and pre-portioned breakfast fruit and turtle-pellet tubs organized by Brittany and Heather.

## 3. Loving Guardrails: Respecting Plenary Guardianship & What Remains Off-Limits

With **100% guardian blessing from both Virginia and Minnesota families** and **Title III of Katie's 2028 Act** backing their **Supported Companion Declaration (`#00001`)**, both families lovingly uphold the real-world cognitive and legal boundaries that keep Wesley and Chelsea safe:

1. **No Buying Real Estate, Signing Mortgages, or Taking On Debt:** Neither Wesley nor Chelsea has the cognitive capacity to read a forty-page closing disclosure, understand compound interest, pay property-tax escrows, or spot a home-repair scammer. Under their court-supervised **plenary conservatorships**, all three properties, utilities, insurance policies, and repairs remain `100%` owned and managed by their guardian families—so no predatory lender, credit-card mailer, or salesperson can ever bind either of them to a contract.
2. **Guardian Proximity Within Ten Seconds (`Zero Unsupervised Living Alone`):** Living together in a connected in-law suite is **never** an isolated apartment across town. Because Chelsea has moderate-to-severe asthma (`requiring someone who can immediately distinguish her daily maintenance inhaler from her rescue albuterol puffer and call 911 if her peak flow drops`) and Wesley is non-verbal (`requiring a trusted communicator if a smoke detector chirps or a pipe leaks`), that unlocked oak pocket door to Brittany, Katie, and Nate in Virginia—and to Heather, Dan, and adult Molly in Minnesota—keeps trained, loving family hands ten seconds away `24/7`.
3. **Separate Protected Fiduciary & ABLE Accounts (`Zero Benefit Cuts`):** With the SSI/DAC marriage and `Form SSA-4178` holding-out penalties repealed from federal law, Wesley keeps `100%` of his *North Lion* wages, ABLE savings, and benefits managed transparently by Heather, and Chelsea keeps `100%` of her benefits and ABLE account managed transparently by Katie, Nate, and Brittany.

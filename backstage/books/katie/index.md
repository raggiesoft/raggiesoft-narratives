Welcome to the official **RaggieSoft Backstage** companion archive for [***Katie & Nathaniel***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/katie).

Because every chapter of ***Katie & Nathaniel*** is written strictly in **Nathaniel Prairie's first-person point of view**, the main novel only shows what Nate and Katie can directly see, hear, and deduce in the moment. Whenever you encounter a **Backstage Bonus Feature** button at the end of a chapter on [`books.raggiesoft.com`](https://books.raggiesoft.com/katie), you can open these behind-the-scenes and alternate `"What If?"` dossiers directly inside the reader without breaking narrative immersion.

## 1. 1:10 AM – 1:30 AM — Nine Minutes From Waynesboro & Calling Nate's Text a Lie in Front of Wesley

Just as in [**`Ending A`**](/books/katie/what-if-rrc-unlocked-closet-ending-a-three-fifty-am) and [**`Ending B`**](/books/katie/what-if-rrc-unlocked-closet-ending-b-six-forty-am-stealth), leaving the Rec Hall seasonal storage closet **unlocked** at `10:44 PM` on Tuesday, July 17, 2007 fools the `11:25 PM` curfew sweep completely. Right before powering both flip phones completely **OFF** inside the dark closet (`since RRC's Level 2 Overnight Pass rules required advance counselor approval plus pickup by a Parent or Legal Guardian, ruling Heather out that evening`), Nate sent **Heather** an `<sms>` message with their exact hiding spot—*the seasonal storage closet at the end of the back hallway in the Rec Hall*—feeling completely comfortable telling Heather they were sleeping in a closet because she already knew they slept side by side in their bedroom closet at home.

When the `9:30 PM` **Shenandoah Valley Transit (`SVT`) Route 250 Connector** check-in sheet and the `11:00 PM` Barton Hall curfew check both come up empty, **Center Director Dr. Evelyn Cross** triggers Missing Vulnerable Adult notifications at **`1:10 AM`**, waking both:
* **Heather Collins & Wesley** at their extended-stay hotel near the Waynesboro ValueMart (**~7 miles / 8–10 minutes** east via `I-64` Exit 94 to Exit 91), and
* **David Prairie** on Sunset Drive in Alexandria (**~155 miles / 2 hours 30 minutes** north via `I-66 West`, `I-81 South`, and `I-64 East` Exit 91).

Inside **Room 114** in Waynesboro, Heather flips open her phone, sees **Nate's `10:44 PM` text** immediately, shows it to **Wesley** so he knows Katie and Nate aren't lost on the highway, calls **David** on `I-66 West` so he knows they're in the Rec Hall closet, and pulls her silver accessible van up outside Barton Hall at **`1:20 AM`**—more than **two hours ahead** of David.

Walking into the Barton Hall lobby with Wesley right beside her, Heather holds her flip phone across the counter to **Dr. Cross** and the **Night Supervisor**, reading Nate's `10:44 PM` text aloud word for word.

And right in front of Wesley, the Night Supervisor scoffs in her face:

> *"Look, miss—first of all, you're not a parent or legal guardian. Second, I checked that seasonal storage closet myself at eleven-twenty-five, and there is **nobody** inside that Rec Hall! Either he sent that text before they snuck off campus or he sent you a decoy—we are not unlocking closed state buildings at one-thirty in the morning!"*

## 2. 1:31 AM – 1:34 AM — Angry Wesley Drags Heather Across the Quad & Yanks the Rec Hall Lock Loose

Nobody in Barton Hall knows **Wesley's** history—how losing his spoken bridge to his very first guy friend **Nate** over an untrue accusation in January 2003 broke Wesley's heart for four straight years ([***Five Red X's on the Wall Calendar***](/books/katie/wesley-blowout-at-heather-losing-his-friend-nate)), or how Wesley's literal, fiercely loyal autistic mind processes two unbreakable facts right now:
1. **Nate does not lie.** Nate's text says he and Katie—whose nose was hurt in the pool today—are inside the **seasonal storage closet at the end of the back hallway in the Rec Hall**.
2. **These rude staff people have the keys clipped right on their belt, and they are refusing to let his friends out.**

Wesley's sandy brows slam together in a thunderous scowl.

Shoving his blue silicone sensory ring deep into his right khaki pocket so both hands are completely free, an angry **Wesley** reaches sideways, wraps his fingers firmly around **Heather's** wrist, turns his back flat on Dr. Cross and the Night Supervisor, and **stomps straight out the automatic doors of Barton Hall across the courtyard toward the darkened Campus Recreation Hall—pulling Heather right along with him.**

Heather doesn't tug back for a fraction of a second. **Heather believes her cousin 100%, because she believes Nate's SMS 100%.** Snap-shutting her flip phone into her jeans pocket, Heather matches Wesley's long, furious strides across the lit red-brick walkway as the Night Supervisor and Dr. Cross bolt out of Barton Hall behind them.

"Hey! Stop right there, young man!" the Night Supervisor yells across the grass as **Augusta County Sheriff's Lieutenant Miller** steps out of his idling cruiser at the curb. "That building is locked for the night! Non-guardians and visitors are prohibited—get away from those doors!"

"You had ten minutes to use your master key!" Heather calls back over her shoulder without slowing down a step as Wesley marches her straight onto the Rec Hall concrete porch. "If you won't check on them, get out of my cousin's way!"

At **`1:32 AM`**, Wesley plants both sneakers shoulder-width apart on the entrance mat in front of the 1970s commercial aluminum-and-glass double doors of the Rec Hall, wraps his free hand around the vertical brushed-aluminum pull handle, and **yanks** backward with every ounce of furious twenty-year-old protective strength in his frame:

* **First yank (`CLANG!`):** The heavy glass door bows half an inch in its thirty-five-year-old aluminum frame, rattling the worn center-mullion deadlatch strike plate.
* **Second yank (`RATTLE-CRACK!`):** Two oxidized sheet-metal screws holding the 1974 aluminum strike lip begin backing out of the soft, stripped mullion channel while the Night Supervisor sprints across the courtyard waving his clipboard and shouting *"GET YOUR HANDS OFF STATE PROPERTY!"*
* **Third full-body yank (`POP—CLATTER!`):** With one last furious haul, the worn aluminum strike plate pops clean loose from the center mullion—skittering across the interior lobby tile—and **the heavy Rec Hall entrance door swings wide open**!

## 3. 1:34 AM – 1:42 AM — Furious Staff Demand Wesley's Arrest—Right Until the Closet Door Opens

The Night Supervisor and Dr. Evelyn Cross hit the Rec Hall porch red-faced and apoplectic right alongside **Sheriff's Lieutenant Miller**:

"Lieutenant, you saw that with your own eyes—arrest him right now!" the Night Supervisor roars, pointing furiously at the popped metal strike plate dangling from the center doorframe as Wesley pulls Heather straight across the dark game room past the billiard tables. "He just broke down the front door of a state rehabilitation building! Destruction of state property and unlawful entry! I told both of them ten times there is **nobody** inside this building!"

"Young man, stop immediately—this facility was cleared hours ago!" Dr. Cross snaps, hurrying across the waxed tile behind Lieutenant Miller's sweeping flashlight beam.

Wesley doesn't even glance over his shoulder at either of them. Having walked the Rec Hall with Nate, Katie, and Heather on Sunday afternoon, Wesley knows *exactly* where the darkened rear hallway sits—and right at **`1:35 AM`**, still holding Heather's hand, Wesley marches straight to the solid-wood door at the end of the rear corridor, grabs the **unlocked** brass doorknob, and pulls the closet door wide open while Heather reaches inside and flips the wall light switch **ON**.

Every angry word in the Night Supervisor's throat dies on the spot.

Flooded in the sudden glow of the sixty-watt ceiling bulb—sitting side by side on tan canvas winter-coat bags with a burgundy velvet stage curtain draped over their shoulders right beside their two neatly folded black joystick wheelchairs—**Katie** (with the clean white medical tape strip across her swollen nose) and **Nate** scrunch their eyes shut, blink three times, and stare up at the doorway in total, groggy `1:35 AM` bewilderment.

Having slept for barely two and a half hours with both flip phones powered completely off, **Katie and Nate have zero idea why half of Fishersville is standing in their closet—or why Wesley looks like he just won the Super Bowl.**

"Wes?! Heather?!" Nate blurts out, rubbing both eyes as his brain tries to compute seeing Wesley, Heather, a uniformed county Sheriff's Lieutenant, a ghost-white Dr. Cross, and a choking Night Supervisor crowded into a storage doorway at one-thirty-five in the morning. "How on earth did you guys get inside the locked Rec Hall—and why was somebody screaming about state property out in the lobby?!"

Heather hooks her thumb proudly down the hallway toward the front entrance, grinning from ear to ear:

> *"Because RRC called us at the hotel saying the two of you were missing, refused to believe your ten-forty-four text message, and refused to unlock the front door—so **Wesley** marched me across the grass and yanked the Rec Hall door open until the lock gave way!"*

Katie's and Nate's jaws drop open in unison as **Wesley**—every trace of anger gone from his face—beams with pure triumphant joy, fishes his blue silicone ring back out of his khaki pocket, drops to one knee on the linoleum beside the stage curtain, and gives Nate's knuckles two proud, emphatic taps!

"Way to go, Wes!" Katie gasps, half-laughing through her bandaged nose as she reaches out to squeeze Wesley's forearm with both hands.

Behind them in the hallway, the Night Supervisor's face cycles from chalk-white to deep purple as he stammers weakly toward Lieutenant Miller, *"H-he still damaged the front-door strike plate on a state building—"*

Lieutenant Miller turns his head slowly, looks from Katie's bandaged nose to Heather's `10:44 PM` text timestamp to the `1:00 AM` **Missing Vulnerable Adult Incident Sheet** clipped to the Night Supervisor's own board, and shuts him down ice-cold:

> *"Let me make sure I understand you for my Sheriff's report. You initiated a county-wide missing-vulnerable-adult emergency for an injured young woman, signed a state log claiming this closet was empty without turning the light switch on, refused to check the exact room sitting on her number-one emergency contact's phone, and you want me to charge the **one young man on this campus** who actually rescued her under Virginia emergency necessity?! Be grateful all he popped loose was four worn-out screws on a 1974 latch plate—because her father is still driving down Interstate 81 from Alexandria!"*

Dr. Evelyn Cross closes her eyes and doesn't utter another syllable about the door.

## 4. 1:45 AM – 8:30 AM — Calling Dad on I-81, Two Hardware-Store Screws, & Saving Natalie

Rescuing Katie and Nate at **`1:35 AM`**—more than **two full hours earlier** than in [**`Ending A`**](/books/katie/what-if-rrc-unlocked-closet-ending-a-three-fifty-am)—makes the rest of Wednesday morning even sweeter:

1. **1:45 AM — Calling David Before He Even Passes Strasburg (`Plus Filing Criminal Charges Against Chris`):**
   Thirty-five minutes into **David Prairie's** two-and-a-half-hour haul from Alexandria—right where `I-66 West` meets `I-81 South`—David's truck cab fills with laughter and relief when Heather calls on speakerphone: *"Mr. Prairie, take your foot off the gas and cruise safe—Wesley got mad when staff wouldn't believe Nate's text, yanked the Rec Hall door lock clean loose himself, and pulled Katie and Nate right out of the closet! Lieutenant Miller is taking Katie's criminal assault statement against Christopher Vance right now!"*
   With Lieutenant Miller already standing inside the Rec Hall, Katie and Nate sign a formal criminal battery complaint before `2:10 AM` (`putting Christopher Vance in Augusta County handcuffs at 7:00 AM and completely preventing Wednesday night's attack on classmate Natalie`), and Heather and Wesley drive Katie and Nate back to **Room 114** in Waynesboro by `2:25 AM` so the twins are already tucked safely into the hotel suite's carpeted walk-in closet when David rolls in at `3:45 AM`!
2. **8:15 AM — Hero Handshakes for Wesley, `$0.38` in Screws, & Tina's `PHP` / `SQL` Thumb Drive:**
   After greeting **Wesley** at the hotel with the proudest bear-hug handshake of Wesley's life, David drives back onto the sunny RRC campus at `8:15 AM` alongside Heather, Wesley, Katie, and Nate to load their Barton Hall suitcases into his pickup. Walking up to the front counter, David calmly sets two brand-new `#10` stainless-steel sheet-metal screws from a thirty-eight-cent Waynesboro hardware packet squarely onto Dr. Cross's blotter (*"For your 1974 Rec Hall strike plate—keep the change"*), before classmate **Tina** hurries out of the Dining Hall breezeway laughing in pure awe at hearing how Heather's quiet cousin single-handedly defeated a state lock and presses a `2-gigabyte` USB drive containing the rest of the **Web Applications Developer (`PHP` & `SQL`)** curriculum straight into Katie's hand as all five roll out of Fishersville together.

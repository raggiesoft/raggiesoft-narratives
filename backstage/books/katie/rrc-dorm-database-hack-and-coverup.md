## 1. Wednesday Afternoon, March 14, 2007 (`4:15 PM`): How Chris Scrambled the Dormitory Database

How did every dormitory room assignment in **Barton Hall** and **Cantor Hall** end up scrambled on Wednesday evening?

1. **An Unattended Housing Terminal on `Quantum OS 2000 Professional`:**
   At `4:15 PM` on Wednesday, March 14, 2007, a housing clerk in the **Bates-Harding Building** stepped away from her desk for ten minutes without locking her workstation, leaving the campus residential database application open under a local `Administrator` session.
2. **Swapping the Room Keys & Locking Out the IT Staff:**
   **Christopher Vance (`Chris`)**—who was furious at Katie and Nate after being humiliated in the therapeutic pool the afternoon before, and who had already targeted twenty-one-year-old **Natalie Brooks**—slipped into the housing office, ran an bulk update query that randomized dozens of male and female room assignments across Barton Hall (`deliberately assigning himself a keycard and printout slot for Natalie's room on the women's wing while routing himself past Katie's room`), printed the manipulated `5:00 PM` evening roster to the Barton Hall front-desk laser printer, and **changed the database administrator password** so the evening residential staff could not log into the server to verify or revert the table.
3. **Blind Bureaucratic Compliance at the Barton Hall Desk:**
   When the `6:00 PM` shift change arrived at Barton Hall and the computer terminal refused the evening supervisor's password, the residential staff—trained never to question a printed Bates-Harding directive—blindly enforced the paper printout, handing out reassigned magnetic keycards across both wings despite students protesting that men were being placed on the women's corridor and women on the men's corridor.

## 2. Thursday Morning, March 15, 2007 (`6:30 AM – 10:00 AM`): The Cover-Up & Why Nate and Katie's `Level 1` Meeting Was Canceled

While Nate and Katie were waiting in **Mr. Alderman's** Web Applications Developer classroom on Thursday morning expecting to be summoned at `10:00 AM` by their vocational counselor and slapped with a punitive **`Level 1` Restriction & Behavioral Contract** over Tuesday night's Rec Hall seasonal closet write-up, the second floor of the **Bates-Harding Building** was in full crisis-containment mode:

* **`6:45 AM` — Discovering What Chris Did to Natalie:**
   When morning dorm aides found a bleeding, traumatized **Natalie Brooks** crying in her room—understanding that what Chris had done to her was physically painful, even though her intellectual disability prevented her from comprehending the nature or long-term consequences of the sexual assault—the dorm supervisor notified the RRC Director's office instead of dialing `911` or the **Virginia State Police**.
* **`7:50 AM` — Putting Chris on a Bus in Staunton Without Calling Police:**
   Terrified that a felony rape investigation on campus—coming less than forty-eight hours after the same male client had broken Katie Prairie's nose in the therapeutic pool—would trigger an immediate state inspection, negative newspaper coverage, and a freeze on new client referrals (`"butts in seats"`), RRC administration quietly packed Christopher Vance's duffel bag, drove him in an unmarked Center van to the Greyhound bus station in **Staunton** at `7:50 AM`, and put him on a bus out of Augusta County without ever notifying law enforcement.
* **`8:30 AM` — Bleaching the Sheets, Holding Natalie in the Infirmary, & Falsifying `"Homesickness"` (`Status 28`):**
   Housekeeping was ordered to strip Natalie's bed and wash the bloodied sheets in industrial bleach in the campus laundry. When Natalie's mother and legal guardian, **Evelyn Brooks**, called to check on her daughter, RRC staff lied to her over the telephone—claiming Natalie was merely having a mild *"homesickness episode"*—and kept Natalie isolated inside the campus infirmary until Mrs. Brooks drove up on Friday, March 16, discovered her daughter's injuries at an Interstate 64 rest stop, and rushed her to the hospital emergency room for a forensic SANE exam. In DARS's mainframe in Richmond, RRC coded Natalie's departure as a voluntary *"homesickness"* withdrawal under **`Status 28` (*"Unsuccessful Rehabilitation — Client Dropped Out"*)**—the exact same `Status 28` code placed on Katie and Nathaniel Prairie's files when they left with **Tina** that Friday afternoon.
* **`10:00 AM` — Why Nate and Katie Were Never Called Into the Counselor's Office:**
   With the IT department spending all day Thursday rebuilding the corrupted dormitory database from tape backups and the administration frantically covering its tracks over Chris and Natalie, **nobody in Bates-Harding had the time or the stomach to haul Nate and Katie into an office at `10:00 AM` over Tuesday night's seasonal closet write-up**—buying the twins two uninterrupted days (`Thursday and Friday, March 15–16`) in Mr. Alderman's lab to copy the entire **`PHP 5`** and **`SQL`** curriculum onto their personal laptops before rolling out of Fishersville forever.

Once Nate and Katie slip down the darkened rear corridor of the **Rec Hall** at `10:44 PM` on Tuesday, March 13, 2007 and lock themselves inside the dark seasonal storage closet, they cannot see what is happening across the courtyard in **Barton Hall** at `11:00 PM`—and neither Nate, Katie, nor the main-novel text ever knows that **locking the closet door from the inside** is the exact thing that got them caught. This Backstage bonus feature reveals what happened on the other side of that solid-wood closet door.

## 1. The `11:00 PM` Level 2 Curfew Bed Check in Barton Hall & How Staff Knew to Search the Rec Hall

Across the courtyard in **Barton Hall**, the eleven-o'clock **Level 2** curfew bed check arrived right on schedule at `11:00 PM`.

* **On-Campus vs. Off-Campus Movement Rules (`Levels 2, 3, & 4`):**
  At **Roosevelt Rehabilitation Center (`RRC`)**, clients who were not under a punitive **`Level 1`** disciplinary restriction—which included **`Level 2`** clients in Barton Hall—did **not** have to sign in or sign out when traveling between buildings or points *on* the RRC campus (such as rolling from Barton Hall to the cafeteria, the Bates-Harding classrooms, or the Rec Hall). Passes and sign-outs applied **only when a client was leaving the RRC campus**:
  * **`Level 2` (`Barton Hall`):** Required a signed yellow paper **`Pass`** to leave campus independently—or Level 2 clients could sign up at the **Rec Hall desk** for a **Center-sponsored trip** to places like **The Spot** or **ValueMart** (which counted as getting a Pass, since the trip roster documented why they were off campus).
  * **`Level 3` (`Cantor Hall`):** Only needed to **sign out** in the dorm ledger when leaving the RRC campus (no paper pass required).
  * **`Level 4` (`Cantor Hall`):** Could simply **come and go off campus as they pleased** (no pass and no sign-out required).
* **Connecting the Dots Between Barton Hall and the Rec Hall (`11:00 PM – 11:15 PM`):**
  Because no sign-out was required for moving between buildings on campus, there was no paper trail at the Barton Hall front desk showing where Nate and Katie had gone after supper. When the dorm aides walked the `11:00 PM` bed-check rounds and discovered that **neither** Nathaniel Prairie on the men's wing **nor** Katie Prairie in `Room 157` on the women's wing was in bed—and verified at the front desk that neither twin had an off-campus Pass or Center trip sign-up on file—they knew both twins were still somewhere on campus.
* Dorm staff immediately called over to the **Rec Hall** right as the building was locking up after its `11:00 PM` closing time for Level 3 and Level 4 clients. The evening Rec Hall staffer immediately remembered seeing the twenty-one-year-old twins sitting together in their corner booth all night long—and realized that after seeing them roll toward the restrooms right before the `10:45 PM` Level 2 departure call, **he had never seen their two wheelchairs roll out the front glass doors toward Barton Hall!**

## 2. Why Locking the Door Is What Actually Got Nate and Katie Caught (`The Hookup Closet Irony`)

What Nate and Katie had no way of knowing when they slipped down the darkened rear corridor of the Rec Hall at `10:44 PM` was the secret campus reputation of that walk-in seasonal storage closet:

1. **A Known Campus Hookup Spot With a Normally Unlocked Door:**
   Because RRC's strict rule against clients having sex applied **at any time of day or night** anywhere on campus (and carried automatic expulsion for both clients), couples constantly looked for blind spots away from the dorm aides. That walk-in seasonal storage closet at the end of the darkened rear Rec Hall corridor was a **well-known spot where clients sneaked off to have sex**—and its brass doorknob was **normally left unlocked** by maintenance.
2. **What Would Have Happened If Nate and Katie Had Left the Door Unlocked:**
   When the night supervisor and the Rec Hall staffer walked down the rear corridor at `11:25 PM` sweeping the building, the staffer reached out and tried the brass doorknob of the seasonal storage closet as a routine check.
   * **Had Nate and Katie NOT twisted the interior thumb-turn lock:** The staffer would have turned the doorknob, felt that the door opened smoothly like normal, assumed nobody was locked inside, **never spotted Nate and Katie** tucked quietly in the pitch dark on the floor behind the parked wheelchairs and lumpy canvas bags of winter coats and stage curtains, and **moved right on to search the next building on campus for the twins!**
3. **The Dead Giveaway (`*Rattle, Rattle*`):**
   Instead, the instant the night supervisor grabbed the brass knob and felt that a **normally unlocked storage closet door was locked from the inside**, he knew with 100% certainty that two clients had locked themselves inside that closet. That is why he didn't knock or ask if anyone was in there—he immediately pulled out his master key ring, unlocked the cylinder, flung the door wide open with his flashlight aimed at the floor, and barked:
   > *"Aha! I knew it! What the hell do you two think you're doing locking yourselves inside a dark closet to have sex?!"*
4. **Why Nate and Katie Never Realized the Irony:**
   From Nate's and Katie's perspective inside the dark closet, they had already heard plenty of stories from other students during their first week and a half about clients sneaking off to have sex being a rampant thing at RRC. The second the night supervisor unlocked the door and shouted *"Aha! I knew it!"*, Nate and Katie simply figured in hindsight that this back closet was probably one of the places where clients got caught hooking up—never realizing for the rest of their lives that **locking the door from the inside** is the only reason they were found that night!

## 3. Why the Night Supervisor Froze at `11:25 PM` When They Dared Him to Call Their Father

When the night supervisor threatened to kick them out of the Center and Nate and Katie called his bluff at `11:25 PM`—daring him to walk right down the corridor to the **Rec Hall's front-desk telephone** and wake up their father **David Prairie** in Alexandria right then and there to come pick them up—two institutional realities collided in the supervisor's head:

* **RRC's Internal `"Butts in Seats"` Headcount Quota:**
  While Nate and Katie only suspected it at the time (a suspicion Katie later confirmed and weaponized as a Virginia legislator and United States Senator in **Dirksen Room 511**), RRC's operational budget and quarterly utilization metrics in Richmond depended heavily on keeping dormitory beds and classroom seats occupied (`"butts in seats"`). Expelling two high-aptitude Web Applications Developer students at `11:25 PM` on a Tuesday night—five hours after the female twin's nose had been broken open in the therapeutic pool by a male classmate—meant explaining two sudden mid-week vacancies and an unreportable natatorium injury to the Director and to a furious father driving down Interstate 81 from Northern Virginia.
* **Punting to Thursday Morning's `Level 1` Restriction Meeting:**
  Knowing that Nate and Katie's regular vocational counselor was off work on Wednesday (`March 14`), the night supervisor backed all the way down from his expulsion threat, logged the incident in the overnight residential blotter (*"the Chickenshit Report"*), and punted the problem to Thursday morning at `10:00 AM`—where staff intended to intimidate the twins with a **`Level 1` Restriction & Behavioral Contract** instead.

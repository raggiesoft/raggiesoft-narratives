## 1. 10:44 PM – 11:26 PM — Texting Heather, Shutting Their Phones Off, & The Unlocked-Door Camouflage

At `10:44 PM` on Tuesday night, July 17, 2007—five hours after classmate **Christopher Vance** struck Katie across the face in the ninety-two-degree therapeutic pool and Student Health taped sterile wound-closure strips and white gauze across the split bridge of her nose—Nate and Katie slip down the darkened rear corridor of the **Campus Recreation Hall** in their dry jeans, sneakers, and zip-up fleece hoodies.

Under RRC's **`Level 2` Pass System**, overnighting off campus *was* allowed, **but** it had to be approved in advance by their vocational counselor (who had already left for the day and was off work Wednesday) **and** the person picking them up had to be a **Parent or Legal Guardian**—ruling out **Heather Collins** five minutes down Route 250 in Waynesboro while **David Prairie** lived two and a half hours north in Alexandria.

Rolling their two collapsible black cross-brace manual wheelchairs (outfitted with right-armrest joysticks and removable under-seat battery packs while their heavy `SwiftChairs` stay home in Virginia Beach) inside the deep walk-in seasonal storage closet, Nate reaches back to twist the brass interior thumb-turn lock.

In this timeline, Katie catches his wrist in the dark before the bolt can click.

"Wait a second, Nate—don't lock it!" Katie whispers past the white medical tape across her nose, her analytical brain connecting nine days of dining-hall gossip in a flash. "Why was this closet door unlocked when we just turned the knob? Maintenance leaves it unlocked! Every couple on campus who sneaks off to hook up locks the door behind them—if a staffer checks the hallway and feels a locked storage door, they'll pull out a master key in two seconds! Leave it unlocked, fold our chairs flat behind the winter-coat racks, and get behind the stage curtains!"

Moving with quiet twin teamwork in the cedar-scented dark:
1. Before shutting their flip phones completely **OFF** so no stray text chime, vibration, or low-battery chirp can give away their hiding spot, Nate flips open his phone inside his hoodie and texts **Heather**: <sms>A guy in our class hit Katie's nose in the pool today. Clinic patched her up and she's okay, just shaken up and doesn't want to be alone tonight. We're hiding in the seasonal storage closet at the end of the back hallway in the Rec Hall to sleep. Shutting both our phones completely off now so nobody hears them. If this doesn't work tonight, tomorrow we're sleeping at the hotel with you and Wesley. Love you.</sms> Neither twin feels the slightest bit awkward telling Heather they are hiding in a closet—because Heather already knows they sleep side by side in their bedroom closet at home. Both twins then hold down their red power keys until both phones power completely down to black.
2. They unclip both under-seat battery boxes, **collapse both cross-brace joystick wheelchair frames flat**, and slide them completely out of sight behind a waist-high row of lumpy canvas winter-coat bags.
3. Behind the coat racks in the far back corner, they arrange two soft foam bundles on the linoleum, drape a heavy folded burgundy velvet stage curtain over their shoulders against the building's chilly overnight air conditioning, and wrap their arms tightly around each other.

At **`11:25 PM`**, footsteps echo down the rear Rec Hall corridor right on schedule after Barton Hall's `11:00 PM` curfew check finds both of their dorm beds empty.

Katie and Nate hold their breath behind the stage curtains.

Outside, the **Night Supervisor** grabs the brass doorknob expecting the telltale resistance of two students locked inside. Instead, the knob turns effortlessly. He cracks the door six inches without bothering to reach inside for the wall switch, sweeps a lazy half-second flashlight beam across the empty front linoleum and dusty canvas coat bags—seeing zero people and zero wheelchairs—mutters *"Nobody in the closet either—come on, let's check Cantor Hall,"* pulls the door shut, and **locks the outer Rec Hall doors for the night**.

Under the velvet curtain, Katie and Nate exchange a silent, triumphant squeeze in the dark and drift peacefully off to sleep—100% convinced that the absolute worst thing happening across the courtyard in Barton Hall is a dorm aide checking *Absent at 11:00 PM Curfew Check* on tomorrow morning's clipboard.

## 2. 11:30 PM – 3:35 AM — Missing-Persons Protocol, Skipping Contact #1, & Waking Heather and Wesley at the Hotel

Across the grass in **Barton Hall**, however, *not* catching Katie and Nate in the Rec Hall at `11:25 PM` turns the Night Supervisor's evening into a five-alarm institutional nightmare:

* **Midnight — Every Building, Walkway, & `9:30 PM` SVT Bus Log Comes Up Empty:**
  Staff sweep Cantor Hall's lounges, the Bates-Harding breezeways, the gazebo, and the parking lots, and double-check the evening bus check-in sheet from the on-campus **Shenandoah Valley Transit (`SVT`)** shelter. Under RRC's Level 2 Pass policy, staff met the final **`9:30 PM` Route 250 Connector** trips arriving from Waynesboro (`9:51 PM`) and Staunton (`10:00 PM`, reaching their opposite terminals by `10:30 PM`) to check returning clients in, and SVT Dispatch reported zero late or broken-down buses. Neither twin drives, and neither had an approved Overnight Pass on file. Outside the gates runs unlit, sidewalk-free **U.S. Route 250** in humid July darkness, where tractor-trailers barrel past gravel ditches at fifty-five miles per hour.
* **12:15 AM — Mandatory Missing Vulnerable Adult Protocol:**
  Under state rehabilitation regulations, two full-time wheelchair users unaccounted for past `11:00 PM` curfew check—one of whom was treated at `7:30 PM` for an acute facial and nasal injury carrying concussion or secondary hemorrhage risk—cannot be shrugged off until morning. By `12:45 AM`, RRC Campus Police are sweeping Route 250 with cruiser spotlights and a pale Night Supervisor has to wake up **Center Director Dr. Evelyn Cross**.
* **1:15 AM — Calling Alexandria While Overlooking Priority Contact #1 Five Minutes Away:**
  In main canon, catching the twins at `11:25 PM` let RRC keep **David Prairie** completely in the dark. Here, before notifying the **Augusta County Sheriff's Office** and **Virginia State Police**, Dr. Cross has **no legal choice** at `1:15 AM` except to call next-of-kin—and because RRC staff have spent nine days fixated on the bureaucratic note that *"Heather Collins is NOT a parent or legal guardian,"* Dr. Cross skips right past Line 1 of the emergency contact sheet and calls **David Prairie** 150 miles north on Sunset Drive in Alexandria, forcing her to stammer into the phone both that *a male student broke Katie's nose open in the pool that afternoon without anyone notifying him*, and that *both twins have vanished into the July night*.
  David immediately dials Katie's and Nate's flip phones. With both phones powered completely **OFF** inside the Rec Hall closet, both calls go straight to voicemail. Within four minutes, David has grabbed his keys, fired up his Crew Cab pickup, and roared west onto Interstate 66 toward Interstate 81 South.
* **3:05 AM — Waking Up Heather and Wesley at the Waynesboro Hotel (`And Heather Knows 100% Where They Are!`):**
  When **Augusta County Sheriff's Lieutenant Miller** arrives at Barton Hall and glances down at the top line of Katie and Nate's intake folder—**`PRIORITY EMERGENCY CONTACT #1: Heather Collins (Extended-Stay Hotel, Waynesboro — 5 mins away)`**—he stares incredulously across the desk at Dr. Cross (*"Hold on—their number-one emergency contact is staying five minutes down Route 250 and nobody picked up the phone to ask if they went to her hotel?!"*) and dials Heather's number right as David calls from `I-81` Exit 225 to alert her too.
  Inside **Room 114** of the extended-stay hotel in Waynesboro, the ringing phone wakes **Heather** and **Wesley** out of a sound sleep. The very second Heather flips open her cell phone, she sees **Nate's `10:44 PM` text message** waiting on her screen! When Wesley sits bolt upright in the second queen bed turning his blue silicone ring rapidly in middle-of-the-night alarm, Heather immediately angles her phone toward his eyes and steadies him with a calm smile: *"Deep breath, Wes—Katie and Nate are safe! Look right here: Nate texted me at ten-forty-four! Somebody hurt Katie's nose in the pool today, so the two of them hid together in the storage closet at the back of the Rec Hall with their phones shut off—just like sleeping in their closet at home!"*
  Pulling on sneakers and T-shirts in two minutes flat, Heather and Wesley roll out to Heather's silver accessible van and drive five minutes west up Jefferson Highway, pulling through the front gates of RRC right behind David's pickup at **`3:35 AM`**.

## 3. 3:35 AM – 4:15 AM — Heather's Phone Screen, Dad's Light-Switch Question, & *"Who Went Missing?!"*

At **`3:35 AM`**, David steps out of his pickup alongside Heather and Wesley in front of two **Augusta County Sheriff's** cruisers, a **Virginia State Police** sedan, a shaken **Dr. Evelyn Cross**, and the **Night Supervisor** outside Barton Hall.

Before the Night Supervisor can get ten words into his frantic theory that the twins might have rolled down U.S. Route 250 in the dark, Heather steps right under the portico light with Wesley beside her and holds her open flip phone out to David and Lieutenant Miller:

> *"Stop looking on the highway—I know **exactly** where Katie and Nate are. Read Nate's text from ten-forty-four last night: they're asleep together in the seasonal storage closet at the end of the back hallway in the Rec Hall, and they shut both of their phones off so nobody would hear them."*

The Night Supervisor blinks at the glowing SMS timestamp and sputters defensively, *"That—that's impossible! I checked the seasonal storage closet myself at eleven-twenty-five! The door was unlocked and empty!"*

"They sleep side by side in their bedroom walk-in closet at home every single night," Heather replies evenly, not budging an inch while Wesley nods firmly beside her. "If Nate texted me they're sleeping in that Rec Hall closet, they're in that closet."

David narrows his eyes at the Night Supervisor with calm systems-engineer precision:

> *"Did you walk inside that closet, flip the wall light switch on, and look behind the winter-coat racks—or did you crack an unlocked door six inches in the dark?"*

The Night Supervisor's sudden two-second silence tells Lieutenant Miller everything he needs to know.

At **`3:50 AM`**, keys jingle down the silent rear corridor of the Rec Hall. David opens the unlocked solid-wood closet door wide, reaches inside to flip the wall toggle switch **ON**, and steps back half a pace beside Heather, Wesley, and Lieutenant Miller as the warm sixty-watt ceiling bulb floods the storage room.

In the back corner behind the winter-coat bags—right beside two neatly folded black joystick wheelchairs—Katie and Nate, wrapped to the chin in a burgundy velvet stage curtain, scrunch their eyes shut against the sudden light, blink three times, and sit up groggily in their hoodies, jeans, and sneakers.

Having slept soundly for five hours with their phones powered completely off, **neither Katie nor Nate has the slightest clue that anyone on earth thinks they are missing.**

Rubbing his eyes in total bewilderment, Nate stares past a uniformed county sheriff's badge at his father standing one hundred and fifty miles away from Alexandria alongside Heather and Wesley at ten minutes to four in the morning.

"Dad?! Heather?! Wes?!" Nate blurts out, completely baffled. "What are all of you doing in a closet in Fishersville at four in the morning?! Wait—why is the Sheriff here? Did somebody go missing on campus?!"

Beside him, Katie blinks innocently past the white gauze strip across her nose, looking anxiously from Lieutenant Miller's flashlight to Heather and Dr. Cross in the hallway. "Did somebody run away from Barton Hall? Wait, Heather—didn't you get Nate's text?!"

For two full seconds nobody in that doorway can speak—until David lets out a ragged half-laugh, half-exhale that takes twenty years off his chest, drops straight to both knees on the linoleum beside the stage curtain, and pulls both of his bewildered twins into a fierce two-armed hug while Heather laughs in pure relief, Wesley steps forward to give Nate's knuckles two joyful taps, and Lieutenant Miller clicks off his flashlight with a sideways grin at the beet-red Night Supervisor.

"Oh, Heather got your text all right—she's the only reason we aren't still combing Route 250 with spotlights!" Lieutenant Miller chuckles, tipping his hat back an inch. "Half the Augusta County Sheriff's Office, the Virginia State Police, and your dad were out looking for **the two of you** because *somebody* checked this closet without flipping the light switch on!"

Katie and Nate stare at each other with their mouths hanging open: *"US?! We were sixty yards across the grass taking a nap!"*

## 4. Wednesday Morning Aftermath: Pressing Criminal Charges, Resting at Room 114, & Saving Natalie by Accident

Once the sheer comedy of waking up inside their own missing-persons search settles—and David and Heather get a close look under the ceiling bulb at the purple bruising and `MedStrip` tape across Katie's nose—every trace of humor leaves David Prairie's face.

Because **Augusta County Sheriff's Lieutenant Miller is already standing right inside the building**, Dr. Evelyn Cross has zero opportunity to bury Tuesday afternoon's natatorium assault in an internal administrative file:
1. **Criminal Arrest at Daybreak (`Preventing Wednesday Night's Attack on Natalie`):** Right there in the Rec Hall lobby at `4:15 AM`, Katie and Nate give Lieutenant Miller a sworn criminal complaint detailing **Christopher Vance's** unprovoked battery and destruction of Katie's swimsuit top in the therapeutic pool—plus the poolside staffer's threat to write Nate up for shielding his sister's chest until someone brought a towel. With David demanding formal charges on the spot, Augusta County deputies arrest Christopher Vance in Barton Hall at `7:00 AM` Wednesday morning—meaning Vance is sitting in the Augusta County jail long before Wednesday evening, **inadvertently sparing cognitively vulnerable classmate Natalie from the horrific Wednesday-night scrambled-keycard assault** that occurs in main canon ([***Episode 7, Chapter 3, Part 1***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/katie/fourteen-days-at-roosevelt-rehabilitation-center-two-weeks-in-the-shenandoah-valley-a-secular-wedding-in-2008/ditching-campus-for-the-hotel-room-without-passes-natalie-s-tears-spending-week-two-exploring-the-shenandoah-valley/part-1-the-dormitory-room-mix-up-staying-at-heather-and-wesley-s-hotel-without-passes-natalie-s-tears-outside-the-cafeteria))!
2. **Catching Three Hours of Real Rest at Heather & Wesley's Hotel Before Packing Up (`With Tina's Thumb Drive`):** Refusing to let his twins spend even the rest of dawn under Barton Hall's roof—just as Nate's `10:44 PM` text had promised—David, Heather, and Wesley bring Katie and Nate five minutes down Route 250 to **Room 114** of Heather and Wesley's extended-stay hotel to catch three quiet hours of sleep and hot breakfast together. At `8:15 AM` Wednesday (`Day 11` instead of `Day 13`), when they roll back to Barton Hall to load their suitcases into Dad's pickup, sharp-eyed classmate **Tina** hurries across the sunny July walkway before Mr. Alderman's `8:30 AM` class, grins through her glasses at hearing how an unlocked doorknob and a `10:44 PM` text message humbled the entire Bates-Harding administration, and presses a `2-gigabyte` USB thumb drive containing the entire rest of the **Web Applications Developer (`PHP` & `SQL`)** curriculum straight into Katie's palm before the whole family caravans out of Fishersville for good.

## 1. December 20 & December 31, 2002: Nate's Bus-Loop Promise & Hanging Up on Tyler

Before Winter Break even begins at the Northwood High School bus loop on **Friday, December 20, 2002** ([***Episode 4, Chapter 1, Part 3***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/katie/seventeen-senior-year-greenway-court/fall-semester-room-204-paper-snowflakes/part-3-senior-gold-day-in-room-204-paper-snowflakes-one-tight-hug-before-winter-break)), rule-following **Nate** tells **Heather** openly how seriously their household takes David's ground rules: Heather, **Wesley**, **Aunt Linda**, and Heather's parents are driving to Minnesota on December 26 to visit her grandparents outside Saint Paul—*and even if Heather and Wesley had stayed five minutes away in Virginia Beach all week, neither of them would be coming over while Dad was in Rhode Island because Dad's rule is deadbolted doors and zero company.*

At `9:15 PM` on **New Year's Eve**, the beige cordless kitchen phone rings. On the other end of the line is **Tyler** from `Smoker's Corner`, bragging over thumping car-stereo bass on Military Highway that twenty-one-year-old Derek just bought two bags of bottles at the corner gas station and pressuring Brittany to unlock Uncle David's front door since David is four hundred and fifty miles away in Rhode Island.

Standing beside the refrigerator in her oversized burgundy sweatshirt and wool socks behind her thin silver wire-rimmed oval glasses, Brittany looks across the breakfast bar at Katie and Nate parked hub to hub in their two black `SwiftChairs`—at a clean carpet Uncle David works overtime to keep over their heads, a fresh pepperoni pizza cooling on the stove, and three green cans of cold ginger ale waiting beside plastic cups.

Brittany's jaw squares:

> *"Uncle David trusted me with his house and my cousins, Tyler, and Nate didn't even invite his own girlfriend's family over when Uncle David said no company. Take Derek's bags somewhere else and lose this landline number. Happy New Year."*

Brittany presses the red *Off* button, sets the handset into its wall cradle, walks five steps down the front hall to check the brass deadbolt with her own hand, and asks Katie and Nate if there is still room for her under the thick navy-blue fleece blanket on the tan sofa like the Blizzard of '96. At midnight, with Katie tucked against Nate's left shoulder and Brittany curled warmly against his right, all three cousins clink cold cans of ginger ale together—safe, sober, deadbolted, and whole.

## 2. Spring & June 2003: Zero Locker Lie & Four Royal-Purple Gowns at the Pavilion

Respecting Uncle David's rule on December 31 snaps three destructive chains before they can ever start:

1. **Zero January 6 Locker Blowout:**
   Because Tyler never sets foot inside the Arvin Road hallway and Katie and Nate never have to lock themselves behind a bedroom door, **Tyler has zero ammunition** on Monday morning, January 6, 2003. When Heather returns rosy-cheeked from Minnesota at `8:00 AM` in the upstairs B-Wing hallway, Brittany is standing right beside Katie's left wheel carrying a clean three-ring English binder, sixteen-year-old **Wesley** happily bumps knuckles with Nate, and Tyler walks right past without a single lie to tell.
2. **Returning to Mrs. DeMarco's Cafeteria & Mrs. Periwinkle's Library:**
   Every Purple Day and Gold Day that spring, Brittany walks right past the `Lions Way` gate to eat lunch with Katie, Nate, Heather, and Wesley at their accessible corner table in **Mrs. DeMarco's** cafeteria and read quietly upstairs in **Mrs. Periwinkle's** Media Center. Tutored on Tuesday and Thursday evenings at the Arvin Road dining table by Uncle David after his `CCHR` programming classes, Brittany pulls every senior credit up to *A*s and *B*s—never dropping out at eighteen and **never losing thirteen years of her life trapped on a joint lease at Hillside Court in South Richmond**.
3. **Four Purple Gowns Under the Crape-Myrtles (`Saturday, June 14, 2003`):**
   Outside the **Virginia Beach Pavilion** after Commencement—as a CVTA bus glides down Nineteenth Street displaying <led>20 | VA BEACH | OCEANFRONT via BLVD</led>—Uncle David, Aunt Karen, Uncle Steve, and Aunt Linda snap thirty-five-millimeter photos of **four** Class of 2003 graduates in royal-purple caps and gowns (`Katie`, `Nate`, `Heather`, and `Brittany`) with rising-junior **Wesley** cheering right in the middle of them.

## 3. Summer 2003: Five Cousins & Partners Touring Ten Campuses for Rising-Junior Wesley

With Heather and Aunt Linda keeping their **Code of Virginia § 23-7.4** Virginia in-state tuition domicile (`"Wherever Nate goes, Katie goes too — and wherever Heather goes, Wesley goes with her"`), eighteen-year-old **Brittany** joins Katie, Nate, Heather, Aunt Linda, and sixteen-year-old **Wesley** (right between his sophomore and junior years of high school) on the proactive **ten-campus Summer 2003 college tour**:

* **Tidewater & Peninsula (`Stops 1–4`):** `CCHR` Norfolk (where Brittany enrolls alongside Katie, Nate, Heather, and Uncle David that fall), `Virginia Dominion University` (`VDU`, Hampton Blvd), `Warwick University` (`WU`, Newport News beside <led>112 NNTC</led> at the `SR-312 & Shoe Lane` roundabout), and `College of Charles & Anne` (`C&A`, Williamsburg's uneven colonial brick walkways).
* **Richmond, Fairfax, & Harrisonburg (`Stops 5–7`):** `Central Virginia University` (`CVU` West Broad Street sirens), `George Wythe University` (`GWU` four-hour `I-95` traffic), and **`Shenandoah Valley University` (`SVU`, Harrisonburg — in-universe *James Madison University*)**, where a loud Summer Preview Open House pep band and balloon arch overwhelm Wesley so Heather and Brittany help guide him back to the quiet van early (with Wesley signaling he would still like to revisit `SVU` when it isn't an Open House).
* **Fishersville & Blacksburg (`Stops 8–9`):** Checking in at the `Bates-Harding` window at **`Roosevelt Rehabilitation Center` (`RRC`)** in Fishersville, the tour stops short sixty yards down the hall outside attached **`Redmond Hall`** the moment the guide announces gender-separated dorms and mandatory medical-dorm placement—leaving Wesley crying his eyes out in his **Safe Person** Heather's arms because he wouldn't be able to climb into Heather's bed when scared at night, and Heather politely telling the guide that **this is Wesley making his decision and the answer is no**. That afternoon at **`Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute` (`CPI`)** in Blacksburg, Nate falls in love with the mountain valley yet worries the steep campus hills will drain their 24-volt wheelchair batteries too fast.
* **Minnesota Family Visit & `NSSU` (`Stop 10`, August 2003):** All five fly north with Aunt Linda to meet Heather and Wesley's extended family outside Saint Paul and ride the intercampus bus across `North Star State University` before returning home to Tidewater—where their five-person Tidewater wall stops **Brandon Mercer** cold in May 2004, celebrates Nate and Heather's 2007 marriage and Heather's five-step Special Education career, brings **Cousin Chelsea** home from `Mount Regalis` in July 2016, and propels **Katie Prairie** all the way to the **United States Senate**.

## 4. Uncle David Teaching Brittany `L-1-2-3-R` & Truck Repairs on Camera (`1991 Crestline Garrison 350`)

![On a sunny Kempsville afternoon in the Arvin Road driveway beside the Dark Midnight Blue over Ultra Silver 1991 Crestline Garrison 350 four-door Crew Cab 4x4 pickup truck with its functional chrome-latched triangular front vent windows and long bed cap, Brittany Fletcher in silver wire-rimmed oval glasses adjusts a tripod camera while Katie and Nathaniel Prairie watch side by side in their black SwiftChair power wheelchairs.](https://assets.raggiesoft.com/raggiesoft-books/images/scenes/katie/b006/apr_2032_arvin_road_brittany_1991_truck_streamtube_wheelchair_garage.jpg)

Because Brittany never loses thirteen years in South Richmond, her bond with **Uncle David** and his beloved Dark Midnight Blue over Ultra Silver **`1991 Crestline Garrison 350` Crew Cab 4x4** deepens even earlier—while keeping every mechanical and emotional rule of David's stewardship intact:

* **Learning an Agricultural `L-1-2-3-R` Four-Speed From Scratch (With Engine Off First):**
  Already comfortable driving ordinary automatics with zero five-speed habits to unlearn, Brittany climbs behind the wheel in an empty Kempsville Sunday parking lot with **Uncle David** coaching calmly from the passenger bench (behind the open chrome-latched triangular front vent window) while Katie and Nate watch safely from the shaded sidewalk island until Brittany passes David's strict safety threshold. Starting with the engine off, David teaches her the `Crestline CH-465` cast-iron shift knob—why unsynchronized compound granny **`L`** (`6.55:1`) stays untouched on flat Tidewater asphalt, why everyday driving uses **`1` → `2` → `3`** (`direct 1:1`, with zero overdrive fifth gear), and how to feel the mechanical bite point through heavy boots without riding the throwout bearing.
* **Earning a Modest Independent Income on `StreamTube` and `The Grid`:**
  Looking for an honest, flexible way to earn a modest income around family schedules, Brittany films her genuine, unscripted lessons with living Uncle David—from stalling and laughing in the parking lot to opening all three tiers of **David's Documentation System** on the lowered tailgate (**`Hartley Repair Manual`** for approachable overviews, the two-inch **`1991 Crestline Factory Service Manual`** for exact torque specs and wiring diagrams, and David's handwritten three-ring **`Maintenance Binder`** logging every fluid change and part number since 1991).
* **An Inevitable, Earned Inheritance (`2030–2031`):**
  By the time Uncle David steps back from driving in Spring 2030 and passes away peacefully in October 2031, viewers on `StreamTube` and `The Grid` have watched David spend years preparing his niece—so when his will formally bequeaths the 1991 Crew Cab to Brittany alongside her succession as **Chelsea's** guardian, nobody sees a surprise windfall; everyone sees a torch Uncle David spent years placing steadily into her hands.

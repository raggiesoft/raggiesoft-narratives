This Backstage guide lays out the complete architectural floor plan of ***North Lion Scales & Tails***—showing how both halves of *"Scales & Tails"* (turtles and reptiles alongside small mammals) share one barrier-free building right on **Twin Cities Transit Route 64**.

---

## 1. Chamfered Corner Street Frontage & Universal Entry (`1618 White Bear Avenue`)

* **Signature 45-Degree Chamfered Corner Entrance & Transit Pad:** Built on a prominent Maplewood corner lot served directly by **Twin Cities Transit Route 64**, the red-brick building greets arriving bus passengers with a welcoming **45-degree chamfered corner entrance bay** flanked by young white birch trees and two angled storefront wings—with the peaked glass roofline of the central **Greenhouse Conservatory** rising directly centered behind the green *North Lion Scales & Tails* sign.
* **Wide Automatic Sliding Glass Doors:** Both the chamfered corner street entrance and the interior **Zoo Entrance** directly behind the retail floor use wide, motion-activated biparting automatic sliding glass doors—never heavy hinged push-doors—so powerchair users, parents with strollers, and shoppers carrying substrate bags glide straight through hands-free.

---

## 2. Front Building Zone (`Open to the Public — No Zoo Ticket Required`)

Anyone in the Twin Cities can walk or roll through the front automatic sliding doors any day *North Lion* is open without buying a zoo admission ticket:

1. **Front Customer Service Counter & Retail Checkout Registers:**
   Positioned immediately inside the front entrance with a **lowered 34-inch wheelchair-accessible bay**. Guests heading into the zoo can purchase **Zoo Admission Wristbands right at the Front Customer Service Counter—or add Zoo Tickets at any retail checkout register** while buying supplies.
2. **Full-Line Supply Store (`Turtles, Reptiles, & Small Mammals`):**
   Six-foot-wide barrier-free aisles stocked with vetted lighting, fail-safe digital thermostats, whisper-quiet filter grommets, cork bark, freeze-dried aquatic turtle treats, orchard Timothy hay, wooden hideaways, and chinchilla dust-bath sand.
3. **Surrender & Adoption Center + 100% Captive-Bred Animal Enclosures:**
   Houses rescued turtles, reptiles, and small mammals waiting for vetted forever homes alongside animals offered for sale under Dan and Heather's ironclad ethical standard: **100% captive-bred animals only—never wild-caught, ever—with every breeder and supplier personally audited and inspected**.
4. **Onsite Exotic Pets Veterinary Clinic (`Dr. Sarah Meyer`):**
   Accessible directly off the front store without a zoo ticket, offering full wellness exams, shell care, small-mammal dental trims, and X-rays on a hydraulic lift exam table while providing daily in-house veterinary care for every animal in the store, adoption center, and zoo.

---

## 3. Behind the Interior Sliding `Zoo Entrance` Doors: Three Barrier-Free Animal Wings

Every single exhibit across all three ticketed zoo wings is built on a low plinth with **28-to-30-inch seated wheelchair-height viewing glass**, vibration-isolated filtration, and zero strobe lighting:

1. **Center Wing — The Skylit Greenhouse Conservatory (`Turtles & Tortoises`):**
   Crowned by a soaring glass botanical greenhouse roof visible above the brick parapet from the street. Home to **Staff Member Wesley's** flagship **1,000-Gallon Rescued Painted-Turtle Lagoon** (*Clover*, *Scooter*, and *Barnaby*), wooded box-turtle glades, and *Tank*'s fifty-foot radiant-heated **Sulcata Tortoise Pasture**.
2. **East Brick Wing — Woodland & Arid Reptile Galleries (`Reptiles & Amphibians`):**
   Climate-zoned indoor brick-and-stone habitats housing captive-bred and rescued bearded dragons, leopard geckos, blue-tongued skinks, corn snakes, rosy boas, and bioactive misted tree-frog waterfalls.
3. **North Acoustic-Buffered Wing — The Small Mammal Meadow (`Small Mammals`):**
   Separated from the reptile and greenhouse wings by double sliding airlock glass doors with dedicated cool-air ventilation (`68°F`, so zero predator or prey scent ever crosses between wings). Home to multi-ledge chinchilla habitats, bonded guinea-pig meadows, Holland lop rabbits, and African pygmy hedgehogs.
4. **Central Support Hub (`Classroom, Restrooms, & Animal Kitchen`):**
   Along the rear brick spine sit the sound-buffered **Education Classroom** (for school programs, sensory breaks, and quiet talks), **Barrier-Free Restrooms**, and the stainless-steel **Animal Kitchen & Greens-Prep Commissary** where Staff Member Wesley preps uniform romaine trays every morning.

## 1. How Nate and Katie Presented on Paper to RRC Staff by Day 10 (`"Two Unusually Aggressive, Rule-Lawyering Siblings"`)

To readers who have followed Katie and Nate from childhood, their fierce boundaries at RRC are 100% earned self-protection. To RRC's institutional bureaucracy in Fishersville, however, the twenty-one-year-old twins from Virginia Beach looked like an unprecedented administrative headache from the hour they rolled through the doors of **Barton Hall** on Sunday afternoon, March 4, 2007:

1. **Sunday Afternoon (`March 4 — Day 1`):**
   Within twenty minutes of check-in, both twins loudly confronted a residential aide for grabbing their wheelchair push-handles without consent, demanded that an opposite-gender twin brother and sister be allowed to share a single dormitory room, and—before midnight—got Katie's first assigned `Room 157` roommate expelled from the Center for bringing alcohol into the dorm and sexually harassing Nate.
2. **Wednesday Afternoon (`March 7 — Day 4`):**
   Instead of docilely spending two to three weeks putting metal pegs into wooden holes in **Vocational Evaluation (`Voc Eval`)** so RRC could bill DARS for fifteen days of evaluation units, the twins publicly backed **Ms. Hollis** and **Mr. Miller** into a corner in front of all four break tables—threatening to call their father **David Prairie** in Alexandria and pack their bags that night unless the entire two-day **Web Applications Developer** technical exam battery was placed on `Testing Table Two` at `8:30 AM` sharp on Thursday morning.
3. **Thursday Afternoon (`March 8 — Day 5`):**
   When their Vocational Evaluator tried to steer Katie toward a **Waynesboro ValueMart door-greeter partnership** and urged both twins to *"practice independence by separating from each other during the day,"* they flatly rejected both recommendations—choosing to stay stuck indefinitely on **`Level 2` in Barton Hall** (`requiring a signed yellow paper Pass just to leave campus`) rather than stop holding hands or stop sitting together.

## 2. Thursday Morning, March 8, 2007 (`8:30 AM – 11:45 AM`): Why `Form A` and `Form B` Backfired on Ms. Hollis and Mr. Miller

When **Ms. Hollis** and **Mr. Miller** laid the two thick manila exam packets onto **Testing Table Two** at `8:30 AM` on Thursday, March 8, both evaluators were smugly convinced they had set a trap the twins could not beat:

* **Expecting the Twins to Cheat Off Each Other:**
  Because Nate and Katie refused to sit at separate tables, Mr. Miller deliberately placed **`Form A`** (`pale blue cover sheet`) in front of Nate and **`Form B`** (`canary yellow cover sheet`) in front of Katie, with the multiple-choice questions scrambled in different orders—and then paced three feet behind their wheelchair push-handles for three hours straight waiting to catch them whispering or peeking at each other's bubble sheets.
* **Why Neither Twin Needed to Glance Sideways Once:**
  What Ms. Hollis and Mr. Miller failed to grasp was that Nate and Katie had just graduated with **Associate of Applied Science degrees in Information Systems Technology (`3.9+ GPA`)** from the **Community College of Hampton Roads (`CCHR`)** twelve weeks earlier. When the twins opened the laminated 1996-vintage RRC test booklets—filled with `HTML 3.2` `<font>` and `<blink>` tags, `Netscape Navigator 3.0` frameset questions, `14.4 kbps` dial-up modem baud rates, and `Windows 95` `AUTOEXEC.BAT` prompts—both twins were internally disgusted that the Commonwealth of Virginia was using an eleven-year-old exam battery to gatekeep a 2007 web-development program.
* **The `11:45 AM` Score Sheet Shock:**
  Without exchanging a single word or look for three hours and fifteen minutes, Nate scored **`99.2%` on `Form A`** and Katie scored **`99.6%` on `Form B`**—forcing a tight-lipped Ms. Hollis to sign their one-week Vocational Evaluation completion forms before lunch.

## 3. Why the Bates-Harding Computer Lab Was Locked Down on `Quantum OS 2000 Professional`

When Nate and Katie rolled into **Mr. Alderman's** Web Applications Developer classroom on Monday morning, March 12 (`Day 8`), they were stunned to discover that RRC's computers were running seven-year-old **`Quantum OS 2000 Professional`** locked down so tightly by the **Virginia Information Technologies Agency (`VITA`)** that students in a web-development program didn't even have local administrator permissions to start an `Apache` web server or a local `SQL` database service.

Behind the scenes, Mr. Alderman had spent two years begging VITA's regional helpdesk in Richmond to grant local sandbox permissions for his classroom towers. VITA had refused every ticket—classifying student vocational lab machines under the exact same statewide lockdown policy as public-facing DMV lobby kiosks, and forcing Mr. Alderman to teach server-side **`PHP 5`** and **`SQL`** out of photocopied paper binders.

## 4. The Drafted Thursday Morning `Level 1` Contract Nate and Katie Never Had to Refuse (`March 15, 2007, 10:00 AM`)

After the night supervisor caught Nate and Katie locked inside the Rec Hall seasonal storage closet at `11:25 PM` on Tuesday, March 13 and wrote them up in the overnight residential blotter (*"the Chickenshit Report"*), their vocational counselor returned to work on Thursday morning, March 15 and printed a two-page **`Level 1 Disciplinary Restriction & Mandatory Sibling Separation Contract`** for their scheduled `10:00 AM` meeting:

* **Clause 1 (`Level 1 Campus Restriction`):** Revoking all off-campus pass privileges for 30 days and requiring 30-minute sign-in checks at the Barton Hall desk every evening.
* **Clause 2 (`Mandatory Daytime & Evening Separation`):** Prohibiting `Prairie, Nathaniel` and `Prairie, Katie` from sitting at the same cafeteria table, occupying the same Rec Hall booth, or having physical contact (`including holding hands`) anywhere on the RRC campus.

Had their counselor called them into his office at `10:00 AM` and slid that contract across his desk, Nate and Katie would have laughed in his face, called **David Prairie** from the counselor's own desk phone, and left RRC on Thursday afternoon instead of Friday. Instead, because **Christopher Vance's** Wednesday-night dorm-database hack and Thursday-morning cover-up threw the entire Bates-Harding administration into full panic mode, that unsigned `Level 1` contract sat forgotten in their counselor's inbox—until **U.S. Senator Katie Prairie's** investigators subpoenaed the unredacted 2007 file twenty years later.

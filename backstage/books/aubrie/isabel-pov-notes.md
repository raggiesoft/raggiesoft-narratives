## 1. Move-In Friday: *"The Awkward Outlet"* from Isabel Dawson's POV (`August 22, 2014 — 13:00 EDT`)

* **Corresponding Main-Novel Scene:** [*Episode 1, Chapter 3, Scene 3: "The Awkward Outlet"*](https://books.raggiesoft.com/aubrie/episode-1-welcome-to-college/chapter-3-move-in-day-minus-one/scene-3-the-awkward-outlet)
* **Why This Could Not Be Shown in Jordan's POV:**
  On Move-In Friday (`August 22, 2014`), eighteen-year-old Aubrey Jordan Fuller **does not yet know that he has moderate-to-severe bilateral sensorineural hearing loss** (because his mother, Aubrie Sinclair, kept him isolated from doctors and audiologists for eighteen years). Locked inside his POV with the solid-core oak fire door of `Room 214` shut, he hears nothing in the hallway at all—the first thing he registers is the sudden change in air pressure and the cool draft across his neck when the motorized door swings inward, causing him to turn around in his wheelchair and see Isabel waving in the doorway.

### What Actually Happened in the Corridor (CPI Residence Life Two-Knock Welfare-Check Protocol)

1. **The Friday Early-Arrival Check-In Clipboard:**
   At `1:00 PM` on Friday, August 22, 2014, 2nd-Floor Female RA **Isabel Claire Dawson** (`Room 215`) is walking her early-arrival check-in rounds on the second floor of Bennett Hall wearing her official two-tone Forger Maroon, white, and Burnished Forge Gold CPI Residence Life Student Staff polo shirt, pinned nametag, and RA duty lanyard. Her clipboard lists a single Friday early arrival for `Room 214, Bed A`: **`Aubrey Fuller`**—whom Residence Director **Kelsey Brennan** watched get dropped off in under two minutes at `9:05 AM` that morning.
2. **First Knock & RA Announcement:**
   Stopping outside `Room 214` (directly across the hall from her own single room at `Room 215`), Isabel raps politely on the heavy oak fire door and calls out clearly through the wood:
   > *"Residence Life! Isabel, second-floor RA—doing early check-in rounds!"*
3. **Second Knock & Safety Concern:**
   Five seconds pass with complete silence from inside `Room 214`. Knowing from Kelsey's morning briefing that the new resident in `214-A` is an incoming freshman wheelchair user whose mother already drove away from campus—and concerned for his safety in case he had a fall or transfer issue inside—Isabel follows standard **CPI Housing & Residence Life "Two-Knock & Announce" Room Entry Protocol**, knocking a second time more firmly and calling out:
   > *"Residence Life! Aubrey, I'm going to tap my Poly Pass and key in to check on you!"*
4. **RA Master Poly Pass Entry & Instant Clinical Recognition:**
   Hearing no response after a second pause, Isabel taps her **RA Master Poly Pass** against the black exterior RFID reader. The motorized handicap opener whirrs, swinging the heavy oak door inward into `Room 214`.
5. **What Isabel Clocks Immediately:**
   * As the door swings open and shifts the air in the room, the boy in the manual wheelchair at the desk startles and turns around, holding an unplugged black laptop power cord in his hand in front of a dark, dead laptop screen.
   * Because Isabel grew up in Charlottesville caring for her younger sister **Elana Grace Dawson** and cousin **Stephen Dawson** alongside her uncle **Andy Dawson, BSN, RN**, she puts the puzzle pieces together in less than three seconds:
     * *He didn't ignore my knocking—he genuinely didn't hear me through the door.*
     * When he instinctively cups a hand behind his right ear as she greets him, she immediately raises her speaking volume slightly and faces him directly in clear light so he can read her lips and facial expressions—never calling attention to his hearing or embarrassing him for not answering the knock.
     * She spots the wall outlet recessed awkwardly behind the heavy oak desk knee-well where a wheelchair user cannot reach it, reaches into her khaki pocket for the roll of heavy-duty double-sided mounting tape Kelsey suggested, and mounts his power strip right onto the top surface of the desk so he can reclaim his voice.

## 2. August 2015: From Floor 2 RA to the Inner Circle (`Episode 5, Chapter 2`)

Following the triumphant conclusion of Jordan's guardianship and conservatorship hearing in **Montgomery County Circuit Court (`Courtroom 3B`)** in August 2015—and after months of quiet devotion, her almost-slip on the Singapore video call (*"how madly in l— ... in luck!"*), and her emotional reunion hug at Gate 4 of **Roanoke–Blacksburg Regional Airport (`ROA`)**—Isabel finally confesses to Jordan and his found family that she has fallen deeply in love with him.

* **Hands-On ADL Training & Cognitive Sovereignty:**
  Because Isabel stepped down from being Jordan's direct supervisory RA once he moved home to **Geneva Street in Christiansburg** with the Brooks family, **Aubrie Jean Kelly**, **Zoë Wren Brooks**, **Madison Scott**, **Lauren Barnes**, and **Hannah Parker** warmly welcome her into the inner circle and teach her Jordan's **Activities of Daily Living (`ADLs`)**—including the `45-degree` two-person transfer technique and his non-verbal consent cues.
* **The Full Scope of the No Jealousy Pact:**
  When Aubrie Jean sits down with Isabel in the Geneva Street sunroom to explain that their **No Jealousy Pact** welcomes Isabel not only into their emotional devotion and daily caregiving, but also into full romantic and physical intimacy with Jordan whenever both of them are ready, Isabel blushes crimson—taking gentle, thoughtful time across September and October 2015 before sharing her first deeply loving night with Jordan at Geneva Street ahead of his mid-November cardiac emergency.

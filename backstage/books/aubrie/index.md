## 2. CPI Residence Life, OEA & Regional Transit Correspondence Archive (`Fall 2014`)

These ten archival files preserve the complete, unredacted administrative correspondence between **Patricia Higgins** & **Eleanor Higgins** (Office of Educational Accessibility), **Kelsey Brennan** (Bennett Hall Residence Director), **Isabel Dawson** & **Henry Walsh** (Floor 2 Resident Advisors), **Dr. Alan Mercer** (Director of Housing & Residence Life), **Walter Hendricks** (Radford Transit), and **Chief Robert Callahan** (Environmental Health & Safety):

1. [**Aug 20, 2014 — The Accommodation File**](/books/aubrie/correspondence/the-accommodation-file)
   Pre-arrival OEA and Central Housing clearance confirming Room 214's ADA modifications, Bed A transfer height, and Friday Early Move-In roster protocols.
2. [**Aug 21, 2014 — The Counselor's Note**](/books/aubrie/correspondence/the-counselors-note)
   Patricia Higgins's confidential post-intake warning to Kelsey Brennan after witnessing Aubrie Sinclair yank Jordan's laptop power cord out of the Callahan Hall wall socket mid-sentence.
3. [**Aug 22, 2014 — Early Check-In Rounds**](/books/aubrie/correspondence/early-check-in-rounds)
   Kelsey Brennan, Isabel Dawson, and Henry Walsh's Friday email thread documenting Jordan's two-minute abandonment by his mother, Isabel mounting his desk power strip, and Central Housing work-study runners hanging the Saturday door cards.
4. [**Aug 23, 2014 — The Clerical Error File**](/books/aubrie/correspondence/the-clerical-error-file)
   The Move-In Saturday incident thread following the discovery of the 150% forced-triple gender clerical error in Suite 214/216, Jordan's first decision to claim his middle name, and Kelsey's strict *"do not pry"* directive to her Floor 2 RAs.
5. [**Sep 8, 2014 — The Joint Administrative Complaint**](/books/aubrie/correspondence/the-joint-administrative-complaint)
   Formal municipal complaint (`RT-COR-RU-2014-0908`) filed by Radford Transit, the City of Radford, and Radford University over CPI's emergency freshman hotel overflow saturating Radford's 30-foot bus fleet.
6. [**Sep 8, 2014 — The Fourteen-Day Floor Reports**](/books/aubrie/correspondence/the-fourteen-day-floor-reports)
   Isabel Dawson and Henry Walsh's formal two-week post-census evaluations (`Form HRL-2014-COED-TEMP`) recommending that Suite 214/216's provisional co-ed arrangement be made permanent.
7. [**Sep 8, 2014 — The Good-Faith Accord**](/books/aubrie/correspondence/the-good-faith-accord)
   CPI Administration's rapid four-point transit and housing remediation plan (`CPI-VPA-HRL-2014-0908R`), committing all 36 post-census on-campus vacancies to evacuating the Radford overflow hotels.
8. [**Sep 19, 2014 — The Mitigating Factor**](/books/aubrie/correspondence/the-mitigating-factor)
   Isabel Dawson's official EHS Fire Drill report (`Code OEA-ADA-COMM`) and her private, FERPA-protected email to Kelsey Brennan explaining why Jordan was terrified of crossing an unlocked doorway.
9. [**Sep 19, 2014 — The Bureaucratic Firewall**](/books/aubrie/correspondence/the-bureaucratic-firewall)
   When EHS compliance analyst Arthur Pendelton attempts to place a compliance hold on Floor 2 and demand an in-room interrogation of Room 214, OEA, Housing, and Fire Chief Robert Callahan drop a statutory federal firewall on his desk.
10. [**Dec 6, 2014 — Fifty-Eight Seconds**](/books/aubrie/correspondence/fifty-eight-seconds)
    End-of-Semester Fall Fire Drill 2 of 2 log documenting how Suite 214/216 and Nurse Practitioner Evelyn Vance evacuated Floor 2 in 58.0 seconds flat during Jordan's 102.4°F Influenza A exam—permanently closing Arthur Pendelton's compliance file.

In [***The Uncharted Waystation* — Chapter 1, Part 17**](https://books.raggiesoft.com/waystation/book-one-beyond-the-studio-rift/chapter-1-two-names-on-one-studio-lease/part-17-third-floor-food-court-laced-fingers-and-nothing-left-to-weaponize), the narrative camera stays locked one hundred percent inside twenty-four-year-old **Nolan Lennox's** first-person point of view at a sunlit corner table on the third-floor food court of **The Admiral Center** in Downtown Norfolk.

From Nolan's seat, his ex-girlfriend **Tammy** simply materializes three feet to the left of the brass planter rail with her arms crossed tightly over her ribs, already blotchy-pink with spite and ready to attack **Mallory Walsh**.

What Nolan never knows—because strict first-person POV prevents him from seeing around the potted ficus tree by the escalator landing or reading Tammy's mind—is **why** Tammy walked over in an unzipped pastel baby-blue cardigan in the first place, and how close she came to getting herself arrested in the middle of Downtown Norfolk thirty seconds earlier.

---

## 1. Thirty Seconds Before the Table: The Ficuses & The Escalator Blindspot

When Tammy rode the glass escalator up to the third-floor food court off `CVTA Route 13` at `12:14 PM`, her angle of approach initially showed her **only Nolan**—sitting healthier and calmer than she had seen him in nine months—while a large terracotta-potted ficus planter momentarily screened off **Mallory Walsh** seated beside him and **Gwen** across the table.

For nine straight months—through all ninety days Tammy had refused to sign Nolan off her *Pinecrest Court* trailer lease until midnight on November 30, 2001—Tammy had convinced herself that autistic, literal-minded Nolan could never find another partner on his own, and that one bold physical jolt would collapse his resolve the exact same way she had reeled him back in after Breakup #1 (`September 2000`) and Breakup #2 (`May 2001`).

Stepping off the upper escalator comb-plate in her unzipped baby-blue cardigan over a single thin camisole, **Tammy was fully prepared to walk straight up beside Nolan's chair, use the open lapels of her cardigan to angle away from the counter line, and flash her breasts directly at Nolan right there in the public mall food court to try to win him back**—repeating the exact desperation move she had attempted nine months earlier inside the Norfolk Pathfinder bus terminal (`Chapter 1, Part 10`).

## 2. `Code of Virginia § 18.2-387`: The Arrest Tammy Narrowly Dodged

Had Tammy taken four more steps before noticing another woman at the table, her reckless impulse wouldn't have won Nolan back—it would have landed her in handcuffs before she reached the down escalator:

1. **Class 1 Misdemeanor Indecent Exposure (`Code of Virginia § 18.2-387`):** Under Virginia law, intentionally making an obscene display or exposure of one's person in any public place where others are present is **Indecent Exposure**, prosecuted as a **Class 1 misdemeanor**—the highest misdemeanor tier in the Commonwealth, carrying up to **twelve months in the Norfolk City Jail** and/or a **`$2,500` fine**, plus a permanent sex-related misdemeanor record on background checks.
2. **Saturday Noon Family Food-Court Aggravators:** Unlike a quiet weekday corner, Saturday noon beneath *The Admiral Center*'s glass atrium was packed with families, high-school shoppers, overhead dome cameras, and **two uniformed mall security officers** already patrolling forty feet away near the glass elevator bank (where exposing oneself in the presence of minors under age fifteen can trigger felony-level exposure scrutiny under Virginia law, alongside suspension of SSI payments upon thirty consecutive days of incarceration).

Ironically, **Mallory Walsh** saved Tammy from herself without ever knowing it. The split second Tammy cleared the ficus planter and saw a five-foot-five woman in wire-rimmed glasses sitting right beside Nolan—with **Mallory's fingers laced unmistakably through Nolan's hand on top of the tabletop**—Tammy's transactional seduction plan died in her throat, her arms clamped tight across her cardigan in humiliated shock, and she pivoted straight into the venomous verbal attack that Mallory and Gwen dismantled to ashes.

## 3. Why Gwen & Mallory Never Look or Act Like Tammy

Across every chapter and illustration of *The Uncharted Waystation*, readers will notice an intentional visual and behavioral contrast between Tammy and the two women who genuinely love Nolan:

* **Tammy's Transactional Playbook:** From puffing out her chest on Day 1 at the Emerald Creek Mall food court (*"Just so you know, I'm ready for everything"*) to reeling Nolan back in ahead of June rent and preparing to risk a `§ 18.2-387` arrest at *The Admiral Center*, Tammy treated physical exhibitionism as a lever to override boundaries and keep Nolan walking on eggshells.
* **Gwen & Mallory's Unforced, Modest Warmth:** Neither **Gwen** (Nolan's neurotypical younger sister and lifelong household anchor) nor **Mallory Walsh** (Nolan's autistic girlfriend who waited until every minute of supervisor conflict cleared before asking him out) ever uses exhibitionism, mind games, or manufactured drama around Nolan. Dressed in everyday cotton tees, polos, cardigans, and jeans, they offer what twenty-four-year-old Nolan actually needed his whole life: plain spoken truth, zero yelling, and a hand that doesn't let go when things get hard.

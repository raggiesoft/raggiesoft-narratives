## 1. `Saturday, October 11, 2003, 5:10 PM` (*Luna & Leo*): Luna Screams *"GET OUT!"*

From the moment **Stella Porter** pulled her Holt Sentinel up to the Portsmouth apartment on Friday afternoon, October 10, 2003, the three-day holiday weekend was supposed to be one simple, sacred thing: **a Sibling Weekend—a brother, his twin sister Luna, and his older sister Stella**.

Instead, **Delores** invited herself along unasked, claimed Leo's front passenger seat, mocked the dual `MagnaByte Opus` computer desk, vetoed the Elizabeth River Trail, hovered suspiciously outside Leo's daily speakerphone call with **Olivia Walker** in Montréal, shrieked like a siren over the *ZZZT-CHUNK* of their `MagnaByte GraphiPrint` dot-matrix tractor printer, "sweetly" suggested to Stella's face that Stella hand Leo's court-appointed **Guardianship and Conservatorship** over to Delores, and snapped at Luna for humming the twin distress flute passage from **Fractured Prisms's** *Kaleidoscope Sun*.

In the mainline novel, when Delores rises from the sofa at `5:10 PM` and clamps both hands around Leo's forearms to yank him out of his wheelchair onto her lap, Luna and Stella freeze—terrified that screaming at Delores in Virginia Beach will bring worse retaliation down on Leo behind a locked apartment door back in Portsmouth.

**In this *"What If?"* timeline, the dam behind Luna's ribs shatters half a heartbeat *before* Delores can lift Leo an inch:**

* **The Explosion in Stella's Living Room:**
  Luna's wooden desk chair crashes backward onto the carpet behind the `MagnaByte` desk. Crossing the living room in three furious strides, Luna plants herself squarely between Delores and her twin brother's footrests and screams ***"GET OUT!"*** at the top of her lungs:
  > *"Don't you DARE put your hands on my brother! This was supposed to be OUR weekend! Just Leo, me, and Stella! You invited yourself into our car, you stole his front seat, you insulted our house, you screamed at our printer, you tried to keep him off the phone with his best friend, and now you think you can grab him out of his chair right in front of us?! GET OUT OF OUR HOUSE!"*
* **Stella Drops the Legal Hammer & Leo Clicks His Brakes:**
  Expecting measured older sister Stella to scold Luna and smooth things over, Delores demands that Leo grab his backpack to leave with her. Instead, Stella dries her hands on a dish towel, walks out of the kitchen to stand shoulder to shoulder beside Luna, carries Delores's canvas overnight bag four paces to the front door, and sets it squarely out on the concrete porch stoop—invoking the exact Virginia Circuit Court plenary guardianship and conservatorship order Delores was questioning five minutes earlier to terminate Leo's Portsmouth living arrangement **permanently on the spot**. Behind his two sisters, Leo clicks both manual wheel locks tight with his own hands and types two unmistakable words on his Quantum Communicator: <aac>Go away.</aac>
* **Reclaiming the True Three-Sibling Weekend (`Oct 11–13, 2003`):**
  With the brass deadbolt and security chain locked behind a furious Delores storming toward the Saturday `CVTA` corner stop—and Delores **never welcomed back into Leo's life**—the Columbus Day weekend becomes exactly what it was meant to be: a brother, his twin sister, and his older sister ordering hot extra-cheese pizza, spinning Stella's **Fractured Prisms** vinyl LPs (*...And Only Three Remained...* and *Kaleidoscope Sun*), decompressing in Luna and Leo's safe L-shaped sofa embrace, and sharing an unhurried, joyful two-hour speakerphone call with **Olivia** in Montréal.

## 2. `Oct 2003 – Sep 2004` (*Luna & Leo*): Zero Four-Day Pathfinder Trip to Washington State

Throwing Delores out on October 11, 2003 erases the darkest arc of *Luna & Leo* before it can ever happen:

1. **Tuesday Morning Portsmouth Civil Standby (`October 14, 2003`):**
   Instead of Leo enduring a grueling multi-bus Tuesday morning ride back into Portsmouth captivity, Leo stays safe in Virginia Beach with Luna and **Grandma Eleanor** while **Stella and Grandpa Arthur** cross the Midtown Tunnel accompanied by a uniformed **Portsmouth Police civil-standby officer**—packing every textbook, `MagnaByte` floppy disk, charger, and sweater out of **Robyn and Delores Mahone's** apartment in twenty minutes flat and serving formal written notice that any contact will be met with an immediate protective order.
2. **No `$99` Pathfinder Bus Ordeal to Kent, Washington (`November 2003`):**
   Because Delores no longer has access to Leo or his monthly support funds, **the four-day cross-country Pathfinder bus trip from Virginia to Aunt Carol's apartment in Kent, Washington never happens**—meaning zero confiscated or smashed AAC device, zero four-day neglect injuries or hospitalization, zero frantic airport TTY collect call from Sea-Tac, and zero emergency Flight 1138 rescue.
3. **Olivia's Return & September 17, 2004 Without a Courtroom Trial:**
   Leo celebrates Thanksgiving and a Québec poutine *Réveillon* whole and uninjured in Virginia Beach, enrolls alongside Stella, Luna, and newly arrived **Olivia Walker** in *Beginning French I* at `CCHR` in January 2004, and rolls into Virginia Beach Circuit Court on **September 17, 2004** strictly for the joyful half of that date—celebrating Olivia legally taking the name **Olivia Walker Porter** before moving north to their gated coastal sanctuary in **Iron Point, Rhode Island**, with zero `$8,745` restitution lawsuit needed.

## 3. `January–March 2007`: The Knock-On Effect in *Jack & Sarah*

![Widescreen illustrated cover art for Jack & Sarah depicting siblings Jack and Sarah Bennett alongside their twin cousins Molly and Clare.](https://assets.raggiesoft.com/raggiesoft-books/images/covers/og/jack-sarah.jpg)

Three years later, in **January 2007**, twenty-something **Delores Mahone** is taking classes at **Virginia Dominion University (`VDU`)** when kind-hearted Fairlawn Recreation Center staffer **Jack Bennett** attends a campus tour ([***Jack & Sarah — Era 2, Chapter 7***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/jack-sarah/jack-and-delores/a-cousin-s-rescue-conversations-on-a-ride-home)).

Because Delores's manipulative personality hasn't changed an ounce—she still spins the exact same inverted victim script at the Fairlawn front desk claiming she *"just got out of a really controlling relationship"*—Jack, his sister **Sarah**, and his twin cousins **Molly and Clare** still form their unbreakable four-person protective wall around him. **Beneath the surface, however, throwing Delores out during the October 2003 Sibling Weekend reshapes three pivotal moments in *Jack & Sarah*:**

### A. Why Robyn Mahone Is Furious Over the ValueMart Freezer Lasagna (`Jan 27, 2007`, `e002/c008/p001.md`)
In mainline *Jack & Sarah*, when Molly drives Jack to Delores and **Robyn Mahone's** apartment for Robyn's "world-famous" ValueMart freezer-lasagna dinner, Robyn's passive-aggressive jabs at Delores stem from screaming phone calls from Aunt Carol in Washington and the massive, unpayable `$8,745+` court restitution debt Delores owes the Porter family.

**In the Sibling Weekend Throwout timeline, Delores owes zero Washington court debt—yet Robyn is every bit as livid at her daughter for the opposite financial reason:**
* Robyn is still seething over the mortifying Tuesday morning when Stella and Arthur Porter showed up at her Portsmouth door with a **uniformed police officer** in front of the whole apartment building—and over how Delores's reckless temper **blew their golden meal ticket with the wealthy Porter family**, costing Robyn the steady household rent share Leo's placement had brought in.
* When Robyn stares creepily across the dining table at Jack and purrs about knowing his newly divorced father **Jermaine Bennett** from work, she isn't looking for someone to pay off a Washington judgment; she is sizing up Jermaine as the **replacement meal ticket** to make up for the Porter windfall Delores squandered.

### B. Delores's Heart Hospital Scream (`e003/c002/p001.md`) & Final Retreat (`e003/c008/p001.md`)
* **Outside the Hospital (`e003/c002/p001.md`):** When Sarah, Molly, and Clare block Delores from isolating Jack during the family medical scare, Delores's mask slips and betrays the exact wound festering inside her since October 11, 2003:
  > *"I had Leo right where I wanted him! Completely under my thumb, and his precious sisters threw me out and ruined everything! I will NOT let you people do it to me again with Jack!"*
* **The Final Wall (`e003/c008/p001.md`):** When all four Bennetts confront her together, Delores backs down remembering Stella Porter's ice-cold porch warning of a Virginia Beach protective order and the uniformed Portsmouth police standby.

### C. A Deeper Psychological Lock on *"The Biological Deadline"* (`e003/c009/p002.md`)
Most strikingly of all, the Sibling Weekend throwout makes Sarah and Molly's deduction in **Era 3, Chapter 9 (*"The Biological Deadline"*)** click into place with chilling precision:
* Why did Delores lead Jack into a pitch-black VDU Chemistry hallway and an empty apartment bedroom to initiate an unguarded sexual encounter **within hours of meeting him** during her fertile window?
* Because in October 2003, **Stella Porter's legal guardianship** allowed Leo's sisters to scream *"GET OUT!"*, put Delores's bag on the porch stoop, and erase Delores from Leo's life in sixty seconds flat because Delores had **zero legal or biological tie** to him. Determined never to let another protective sister (`Sarah`) or twin cousins (`Molly and Clare`) throw her off a porch again, Delores was racing on Day One to manufacture an instant biological anchor that no family wall could ever legally sever.

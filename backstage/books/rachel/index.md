Welcome to the official **RaggieSoft Backstage** companion archive for [***The Rachel Narrative***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/rachel).

Because [***The Rachel Narrative***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/rachel) unfolds through **Michael's lived perspective**, dozens of pivotal conversations, closed-door administrative briefings, police investigations, and corporate showdowns take place outside the room where Michael and Rachel are standing. Whenever you see a **Backstage Bonus Feature** card at the bottom of a chapter on [`books.raggiesoft.com`](https://books.raggiesoft.com/rachel), you can open these archival dossiers right inside the reader—or explore the full chronological collection below.

## Featured Spotlight: Why Rachel Is the Center of Michael's Universe

* [**May 13, 1999 — The Wingman Briefing: Ashley Raybourn & Bonnie Reed Outside the Morrison Band Room**](/books/rachel/the-wingman-briefing-ashley-and-bonnie)
  Two days before the eighth-grade *Spring Sound Showcase* trip to **Brewster Gardens**, **Ashley Raybourn** pulls a nervous, blushing **Bonnie Reed** into the quiet hallway outside the Morrison Middle School band room. When Bonnie admits she is afraid of stepping between Michael and Ashley because of their constant grounding hugs and hand-holding, Ashley sets the record straight—explaining how Michael's nervous system relies on physical pressure, introducing Bonnie to sixth-grader **Rachel** (*"she's the center of his universe"*), defining **the Trio**, and coaching Bonnie on how to *"upgrade the math."*

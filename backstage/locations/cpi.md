**Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI)**—universally known across the New River Valley as **Poly** and home to **The CPI Forgers** *(and **The Ironheads**)*—is a major public land-grant polytechnic research university located on a high Appalachian plateau in **Blacksburg, Montgomery County, Virginia**, nestled between the Blue Ridge and Allegheny mountains at an elevation of 2,080 feet.

Founded in **1872**, CPI serves as a central geographic anchor across the RaggieSoft narrative universe—from [***The Eleventh Child: The Story of Jordan***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/aubrie) (`2014–2016`) and [***The Rachel Narrative***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/rachel) (`SAACURC 2016`) to the 1986 origin of [**The Stardust Engine**](https://stardust.raggiesoft.com), whose five founding members—siblings **Ryan, Cassidy, and Holly O'Connell** and identical twin cousins **Evan and Tyler Wright**—formed their band in an unheated garage at the corner of **Progress and Turner Streets** while studying at CPI.

## 1. Campus Identity, Colors & Architectural Signature

* **Athletic & Institutional Moniker:** **The CPI Forgers** *(or **The Ironheads**)*
* **Official University Colors:** **Foundry Maroon** (`#630031`) and **Molten Orange** (`#CF4420`)
* **Architectural Stonework ("Appalachian Ironstone"):** Nearly every academic hall, administrative landmark, and residence hall ringing central campus is clad in rough-cut gray dolomitic **Ironstone** quarried locally in Montgomery County—contrasted on modern expansions like **Kincaid Hall** with soaring three-story mullioned glass curtain walls.
* **Commonwealth Green ("The Green"):** The vast, sunken oval grass commons at the center of campus, ringed by a one-way stone-bordered roadway, paved accessible pathways, and Neo-Gothic Ironstone towers connecting the northern academic terrace to the residential and engineering quads.

## 2. Central Campus Landmarks & Student Life

### Preston Hall & Commonwealth Mall
* **Preston Hall:** The towering Neo-Gothic Ironstone administrative fortress dominating the northern rim of **Commonwealth Green**, flanked by twin crenellated turrets and housing the **Office of the President**, the **Office of the Bursar**, and the 3,000-seat **Preston Auditorium**.
* **Keystone Hall & The Keystone Bridge:** The monumental arched Ironstone skybridge spanning **Commonwealth Mall** to connect **Keystone Hall**'s advanced computing spaces directly to **Pendleton Library** (CPI's multi-story main research library). Prior to the opening of the off-street **CPI Transit Center** in **2016**, regional buses queued bumper-to-bumper along the Commonwealth Mall curb directly beneath **The Keystone Bridge** across from **Kincaid Hall**.

### Kincaid Hall & Kincaid Commons (`290 College Ave.`)
Built directly into the eastern hillside of **Commonwealth Green** along **Commonwealth Mall**, **Kincaid Hall** (*Kincaid Student Center*) is the three-story "living room of the university," offering zero-step ground-level entrances on both the **1st Floor** (lower ground level) and **2nd Floor** (upper-quad hillside level):

* **1st Floor (Main Terrazzo Atrium Lobby, Ballrooms & Services):** Opened along the front by a three-story floor-to-ceiling glass curtain-wall atrium with automatic sliding doors and ADA push-plates, connected vertically by a plainly visible **three-floor glass-and-steel passenger elevator** and open architectural staircase, plus the **Poly Pass ID Office**, **Commonwealth Ballroom**, **Kincaid Game Room**, and rear lower-level kitchens with a back-of-house **freight elevator**.
* **2nd Floor (Cantilevered Balcony Food Court & `Kincaid Commons` Cafeteria):** Overlooks the sunlit glass atrium with quick-service stations (*Ironstone Grill*, *Blue Ridge Bakery & Espresso*, *Commonwealth Deli*, *Hearthstone Pizza & Pasta*) and opens directly into the full **Kincaid Commons Dining Hall**.
* **3rd Floor (Stacked Conference & Governance Mezzanine):** Houses numbered breakout suites (`Kincaid Rooms 301–330`), **SGA & RHA Executive Offices**, and student lounges.

## 3. Eleanor Bennett Commonwealth Hall (`BLDG-014`) & Academic Quads

Located on the West Campus residential quad along **Washington Street SW** directly across from **The Furnace**, **Eleanor Bennett Commonwealth Hall** (*"Bennett Hall"* / *"The Old Lady on the Hill"*) is a six-story Ironstone residence hall housing ~400 students across five residential floors (`Floors 2–6`), overseen by Residence Director **Kelsey Brennan, M.Ed.** (`Room 104`) and ten undergraduate Resident Advisors.

* **Hillside Topography & Dual Grade-Level Access:**
  * **Floor 1 (Lower Level / Inner Quad Grade — 100% Administration, Zero Dorm Rooms):** Opens onto the lower West Campus quad. Divided into two completely air-gapped halves by an unbroken **solid interior masonry firewall with zero connecting doors**: the **Western Half** houses the 24-hour residential lobby (`Room 100`), mail room (`Room 101`), RD office (`Room 104`), and RA workroom (`Room 106`), while the **Eastern Half (`Suite 110–120`)** houses the central **Office of Housing and Residence Life** (*Dr. Alan Mercer*, *David Thorne*).
  * **Floor 2 (Washington Street SW Upper Grade Level):** Opens at flat, zero-step grade directly onto **Washington Street SW**, making **Suite 214/216** (`Room 214`: Jordan Fuller, Aubrie Jean Kelly, Madison Elizabeth Scott; `Room 216`: Zoë Wren Brooks, Lauren Nicole Barnes, Hannah Ashlee Parker) the building's primary OEA wheelchair-accessible suite—equipped with a **10-second motorized door opener**, roll-under vanity, **RA Isabel Claire Dawson** across the hall in `Room 215`, **RA Henry Thomas Walsh** in `Room 201`, and the private Poly Pass-encoded roll-in shower suite in `Room 212A`.

### Key Academic, Dining & Athletic Venues
* **Callahan Hall:** Historic building on the eastern rim of **Commonwealth Green** housing the **Office of Educational Accessibility (`OEA`, `Suite 210`)**—renamed from *Services for Students with Disabilities (`SSD`)* in **Fall 2014** under **Patricia Higgins, M.S., CRC** and Senior Director **Eleanor Higgins, M.Ed.**
* **Copernicus Hall & The Engineering Quad:** **Copernicus Hall** houses **Physics, Astronomy & Earth Sciences** (`ASTR 101`, `PHYS 102`, and the campus seismograph), flanking **The Engineering Quad** (**Faraday Hall**, **Joule Hall**, and **Kelvin Hall**).
* **Carter Hall & Archer Hall:** Humanities and large undergraduate lecture amphitheaters hosting `HIST 101` and `HIST 115` (*Dr. Marcus Gable*).
* **The Foundry & CPI Campus Bookstore:** **The Foundry** is the massive two-story brick-and-Ironstone rotunda all-you-care-to-eat dining hall serving the Residential Quad, while the rotunda-topped **CPI Campus Bookstore** anchors the edge of the academic district.
* **The Crucible (`65,000` Seats + `The Anvil`) & The Furnace (`10,000` Seats):** Permanently protected against corporate naming rights under **Jeremiah "Ironhead" Vance's** `$40M` August 1995 Perpetuity Covenant (`CPI-2017-084-VANCE`), **The Crucible** shakes the entire Blacksburg valley—registering on the **Copernicus Hall** seismograph—whenever 65,000 Forger fans stomp the galvanized steel bleachers of **The Anvil** in unison to [**The Stardust Engine's**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/the-stardust-engine/1987-electric-color) pre-game anthem ***"Ignition (The Forger's Call)"*** and fourth-quarter chorus ***"Saturday Night Lights: The Crucible"***. Next door stands the 10,000-seat vaulted basketball arena, **The Furnace**.

## 4. The Poly Pass & Regional Transit Ecosystem

Every matriculated CPI student carries a **Poly Pass**—serving simultaneously as a residence hall keycard, dining card, library pass, and **unlimited fare-free regional transit pass** across four interconnected New River & Roanoke Valley public transit agencies:

1. **Blue Ridge Transit (`BT / BRT` — Blacksburg & Christiansburg):** Operates 29', 35', 40', and 60-foot articulated low-floor **Prairie Coachworks** buses with front-door flip-out ADA ramps—anchored by **Route 1 (*The Spine*, `1 CPI` ↔ `1 CBURG TRANS CTR`)** along Business US-460 through **Pepper Plaza Transit Center** to the **Christiansburg Transit Center at Falling Branch** (`I-81 Exit 118`), **Route 2 (*The Christiansburg Local*)** serving the **Blue Ridge Regional Aquatic Center** and **New River Valley Mall**, **Route 4** serving the **CPI Christiansburg Campus (*"the Law School"*)** on Simmons Road NE, and zero-fare **`Route 1X` (`LOAD AND GO`)** event shuttles paired with **CPI Fleet Services** to **The Crucible Bus Loop**.
2. **Radford Transit (City of Radford & Radford University):** Pulses every 30 minutes (`:15` and `:45`) at **McConnell Transit Center** alongside ***The Radford Connector*** linking Radford, the New River Valley Mall, and CPI.
3. **Bluewater Transit (*"The Other BT"* — Carroll County & *The Maroon Express*):** Operates 45-foot **Sainte-Claire** highway coaches up `I-77` and `I-81` from **Bluewater** (*Madison Scott's* hometown) via **Christiansburg Transit Center** to CPI (`$0.00` with **Poly Pass**).
4. **Roanoke Valley Transit (`RVT` — *The Roanoke Express* & *ARTS Train Connector*):** Connects downtown **Roanoke**, `I-81`, **Christiansburg Transit Center**, and **CPI** (`$0.00` with **Poly Pass**).

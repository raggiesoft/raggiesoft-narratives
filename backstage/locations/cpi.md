**Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI)**—universally known across the New River Valley as **Poly** and home to the **CPI Forgers**—is a major public land-grant research university located on a high plateau in **Blacksburg, Montgomery County, Virginia**, nestled between the Blue Ridge and Allegheny mountains at an elevation of 2,080 feet.

Founded in **1872**, CPI serves as the central geographic anchor for both [***The Eleventh Child: The Story of Jordan***](https://books.raggiesoft.com/aubrie) (`2014–2016`) and the origin story of [**The Stardust Engine**](https://stardust.raggiesoft.com), whose five founding members—brother and sister **Ryan and Cassidy O'Connell**, their twin cousins **Evan and Tyler Wright**, and their cousin **Holly O'Connell**—formed their band in an unheated garage at the corner of Progress and Turner Streets while studying at CPI in **1986**.

## 1. Campus Identity, Colors & Architectural Signature

* **Athletic & Institutional Moniker:** **The CPI Forgers**
* **Official University Colors:** **Forger Maroon** (`#861F41`) and **Burnished Forge Gold / Burnt Orange** (`#E5751F`)
* **Architectural Stonework ("Appalachian Ironstone"):** Nearly every academic building and residence hall flanking the central campus is constructed from rough-hewn, ashlar-cut dolomitic limestone quarried locally in Montgomery County—giving the university its unmistakable Neo-Gothic fortress silhouette against the mountain sky.
* **Commonwealth Green:** The vast, oval central grass commons at the heart of campus, ringed by a one-way stone promenade and mature oak trees, connecting the northern academic terrace to the southern and western residential quads.

## 2. Key Campus Buildings & Landmarks

### Eleanor Bennett Commonwealth Hall (`BLDG-014`)
Located on the West Campus residential quad along **Washington Street SW**, **Eleanor Bennett Commonwealth Hall** ("Bennett Hall") is a six-story Ironstone residence hall housing approximately 400 students across five residential floors (`Floors 2–6`), overseen by Residence Director **Kelsey Brennan, M.Ed.** (`Room 104`) and ten undergraduate Resident Advisors (one male RA and one female RA per residential floor).

* **Hillside Topography & Dual Grade-Level Access:**
  Because Bennett Hall is built directly into the natural slope of the Washington Street SW hillside:
  * **Floor 1 (Lower Level / Inner Quad Level):** Opens at flat grade onto the lower brick pathways of the West Campus inner quad.
  * **Floor 2 (Washington Street SW Grade Level):** Opens at flat, zero-step grade directly onto the **Washington Street SW** sidewalk and ADA drop-off circle at the top of the hill. This unique two-level topography is why the **Office of Educational Accessibility (OEA)** designates **Room 214** on Floor 2 as the building's primary ADA wheelchair-accessible suite—allowing a manual or power wheelchair user to exit directly onto Washington Street SW at the top of the hill without needing an elevator, or take the central elevator down to Floor 1 for flat access to the inner quad.
* **The First-Floor Masonry Firewall (`Room 104` vs. `Suite 110–120`):**
  The first floor of Bennett Hall is physically divided into two completely air-gapped halves by an unbroken, solid interior masonry firewall with zero connecting doors:
  * **Western Half (Bennett Hall Residential Lobby):** Contains the 24-hour student front desk, residential mail room, TV lounge, staff workroom (`Room 106`), and Residence Director Kelsey Brennan's office (`Room 104`).
  * **Eastern Half (`Suite 110–120` — Central Office of Housing and Residence Life):** Houses the university-wide Housing Assignments & Operations annex, directed by **Dr. Alan Mercer** and Associate Director **David Thorne**. Walk-in visitors and contractors entering Central Housing from the outer quad cannot pass through the firewall into the residential dormitory.
* **Floor 2 Layout (`Suite 214/216`, `Room 215`, `Room 201`, & `Room 212A`):**
  * **Suite 214/216:** A two-room connecting suite sharing a central Jack-and-Jill bathroom. **Room 214** (`Jordan Fuller`, `Aubrie Jean Kelly`, `Madison Elizabeth Scott`) is equipped with an exterior Poly Pass RFID reader, an interior square stainless-steel `PUSH TO OPEN` paddle wired to a **10-second residential motorized door opener**, a roll-under vanity sink, widened bathroom doorways with steel grab bars, and a lowered peephole. **Room 216** (`Zoë Wren Brooks`, `Lauren Nicole Barnes`, `Hannah Ashlee Parker`) adjoins via the shared suite bathroom.
  * **Room 215 (West Wing Female RA Single):** Positioned directly across the corridor from Room 214; occupied by Junior/Senior Nursing & Pre-OT major **Isabel Claire Dawson**.
  * **Room 201 (East Wing Male RA Single):** Positioned near the eastern stairwell; occupied by Junior Accounting major **Henry Walsh** (`2014–2015`) and Sophomore Engineering major **Garrett Reynolds** (`2015–2016`).
  * **Room 212A (Accessible Roll-In Shower Room):** Single-occupant, Poly Pass-encoded private ADA roll-in shower and dressing suite located thirty feet down the Floor 2 corridor from Room 214.

### Academic, Dining & Athletic Facilities
* **Callahan Hall:** Home to the **Office of Educational Accessibility (OEA)** (`Suite 210`), directed by **Patricia Higgins, M.S., CRC** (and Senior Director **Eleanor Higgins, M.Ed.**), where students receive classroom accommodations, assistive FM microphone kits, and ADA housing clearances.
* **Kepler Hall:** Home to the **Department of Physics & Astronomy**, featuring tiered lecture auditoriums with front-row wheelchair cutouts and rooftop observatory domes where **Dr. Alistair** teaches introductory and advanced Astrophysics.
* **Gable Hall:** Humanities and History building housing **Dr. Marcus Gable**'s Western Civilization and Roman History lecture halls.
* **Kincaid Commons:** The flagship multi-station West Campus dining hall steps from Bennett Hall.
* **The Foundry:** The official CPI campus bookstore and Forgers outfitter.
* **The Crucible:** CPI's 66,000-seat outdoor football stadium on the southern edge of campus, famous for shaking the entire Blacksburg valley when sixty-six thousand Forger fans jump in unison to the opening guitar riff of *"Enter the Crucible"*.

## 3. The Poly Pass & Regional Transit Ecosystem

Every matriculated CPI student is issued a **Poly Pass**—an encrypted smartcard University ID that serves simultaneously as a residence hall keycard, Kincaid Commons dining pass, library card, and **unlimited fare-free regional transit pass** across three interconnected public transit agencies:

1. **Blue Ridge Transit (BRT — Blacksburg & Christiansburg):**
   Operates the maroon-and-gold fleet of 40-foot and 60-foot articulated low-floor heavy-duty transit buses across the CPI campus loops (**Commonwealth Mall**, **West Campus Loop**, **Progress Street**) and down **U.S. Route 460 Business** (`Route 100`) to the **Pepper Plaza Transit Center** in **Christiansburg**.
2. **Radford Transit (City of Radford & Radford University):**
   Operates a fleet of 29-foot and 30-foot low-floor municipal buses pulsing every 30 minutes (`:15` and `:45`) at the **McConnell Transit Center** on the campus of **Radford University**, along with ***The Radford Connector*** linking Radford, the New River Valley Mall in Christiansburg, and CPI.
3. **Mountain Valley Transit (MVT — Roanoke Valley):**
   Connects the New River Valley regional commuter routes into downtown **Roanoke**, **Roanoke–Blacksburg Regional Airport (`ROA`)**, and **Star City General Hospital**.

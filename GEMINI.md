## 1. Literary Novel Prose Standard for `/raggiesoft-narratives/books/` (NEVER Write Like a "Dear Diary" or Lore Wiki)
- **First-Person Novel POV vs. Third-Person Limited Novel POV**:
  - **First-Person Past-Tense Novel POV (`"I"`, anchored to the YAML frontmatter `pov:` character)**: Used in `/books/rachel` (Michael), `/books/katie` (Nate), `/books/drew` (Drew), `/books/quantum-directive-1-chloe`, `/books/quantum-directive-2-michael`, and `/books/quantum-directive-3-casey`.

  - **First-Person Novel $\neq$ "Dear Diary"**: Whether 1st-person or 3rd-person limited, every `pXXX.md` file MUST read like a **dramatized, in-the-moment literary novel**—NEVER like a diary entry, blog post, technical manual, or lore-bible recap.
- **Strict Anti-Diary, Anti-Bolding, & Anti-Lore-Dump Formatting Rules**:
  1. **Open *In Media Res* Inside the Scene (Mandatory Default Across All Novel Crafting & Editing Unless Explicitly Instructed Otherwise)**: Always default to ***In Media Res*** when writing or editing any `pXXX.md` Part or `## Heading` scene. Drop straight into the immediate physical setting, sensory atmosphere, physical action, or live spoken dialogue of the moment—NEVER open with a diary-style calendar recap (*"On Tuesday, June 14, 2016—four months after..."* or *"Back in January 2020..."*), a multi-paragraph biography of who a character is to the protagonist, a hyperlink checklist of past events, or re-explaining household rosters from earlier chapters.
  2. **Zero Bullet Lists (`*` / `-`), Numbered Checklists (`1.`), or `### Step` Sub-Headings in Narrative Prose**: Never use bullet points, numbered lists, or `###` sub-headings inside narrative scenes to list household setups, course catalogs, floor plans, driving steps, or security rules; dramatize all details through live action and spoken dialogue.
  3. **Zero `**Bolding**`, Inline Code Backticks (`` `...` ``), or Code Blocks (` ``` `) in Narrative Prose (Unless Literally Part of an In-Universe Document/Email)**: **NEVER** put `**bold**` around character names, schools, malls, streets, transit routes, bus models, wheelchair brands, medical terms, or dialogue/narration words in novel prose (only use `**bold**` when formatting literal in-universe document/email headers like `> **From:**` where bolding is part of the narrative artifact). **NEVER** wrap timestamps (`12:25 PM`), bus/route numbers (`Bus #914`), speeds, heights, ages, or acronyms (`(VDU)`, `(CCHR)`, `(CVTA)`) in code backticks inside prose. Use clean literary roman typography with restrained *italics* (`*...*`) for natural vocal emphasis, titles, and internal thought.
  4. **Zero Crystal Balls / Strict Chronological Horizon (No Future Knowledge, No Anti-Spoiler Teasers, & No Future-Dated Links)**: Every Part exists strictly at its YAML frontmatter `date:` and `start_time:`. Never include future foreshadowing (*"five years before [future accident]"*, *"never imagining how many years..."*, or calling someone by a future title before they earn it), never tip the reader off in advance with "unsuspecting narrator" teasers (*"Nobody expected a major national story to come out of..."*, *"Neither of us had any idea her speech would go viral"*, *"Little did we know..."*) that give away a plot device early, and never link to a chapter whose `date:` is later than the current chapter's `date:`.
  5. **Show Character Identity & Disability Realities Through Live Action, Not Clinical Labels**: Never stop a scene to recite a clinical chart or bulleted accommodation list; show wheelchair transfers, hand-over-hand grounding, AAC tablet typing (`<aac>...</aac>`), and sensory routines naturally in the physical scene.
  6. **YAML Frontmatter `title:` Must NEVER Include `"Part X:"`**: Because `paige.php` automatically prepends `"Part X: "` to the title whenever a chapter contains 2+ `pXXX.md` files, the YAML `title:` inside any `pXXX.md` file must ONLY contain the descriptive subtitle (e.g., `title: "Cousin Wesley, Ryan's Escalator Exit, & Two Dates at Emerald Creek Mall"`—NEVER `title: "Part 2: ..."`).

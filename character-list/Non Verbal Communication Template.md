# [Character Name]'s Personal Communication Cues & Boundaries

*(Understood by [Core Trusted Group]; [Specific Person, e.g., Twin/Closest Sibling/Partner] often has a deeper/quicker understanding)*

[Character Name] is functionally non-verbal for speech. They use their AAC device as their primary "voice." However, they also use a rich system of vocal sounds (not articulated words, except in extremely rare, high-emotion instances where a single, distorted word might escape), facial expressions, head movements, and hand/arm gestures.

---

## Basic Needs & States

### "Yes"
*   **Sound:** Short, soft hum (upward inflection), or a quick, breathy "Huh!"
*   **Movement/Expression:** Sharp, definite nod; quick upward glance of eyes; specific tap on their armrest; or thumbs-up.

### "No"
*   **Sound:** More forceful, sometimes guttural grunt, or a soft "mmm-mmm" sound.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Clear, often vigorous, side-to-side headshake; distinct pushing-away hand gesture.

### "Hungry"
*   **Sound:** Specific low, rumbling throat sound, or a particular short, repeated "ah-ah."
*   **Movement/Expression:** Looking/pointing to kitchen/fridge; perhaps patting their stomach; or a specific mouth movement.

### "Thirsty"
*   **Sound:** Softer, slightly clicky tongue sound or dry-sounding sigh.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Pointing towards their mouth; slight gulping motion; looking at drinks.

## Bathroom / Need to Use Toilet

### General "Need Toilet" Cue
*   **Sound:** An urgent, slightly higher-pitched grunt or a specific sequence of sharp breaths.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Distinct fidgeting in their chair; looking pointedly towards the bathroom; a specific hand tap on their leg or a gesture towards their lower abdomen.

### Subtle Difference for "Need to Pee (Urinate)"
*   **Sound:** Might be a slightly quicker, sharper series of grunts or "uh-uh" sounds.
*   **Movement/Expression:** More focused restlessness; perhaps a more direct, quick point towards the bathroom; urgency conveyed by faster eye-blinking or a more intense gaze.

### Subtle Difference for "Need to Poop (Defecate)"
*   **Sound:** Could be a lower, more sustained grunt or a sound indicating more internal pressure/discomfort.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Might pat their stomach or lower back; facial expression might show more concentration or slight discomfort specific to this need; might indicate a need for more time/privacy.

### "Need to be Showered/Bathed" (Routine Cleanliness)
*   **Sound:** Might not have a specific sound unless feeling particularly grimy/uncomfortable, then perhaps a general sound of discomfort or a soft sigh.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Gesturing towards bathroom and making a washing motion; pointing to their body generally; might wrinkle their nose slightly or look at clothes with distaste if feeling unclean. Often part of an established routine they accept.
*   **Via AAC:** "WANT BATH/SHOWER PLEASE."

### "Need for Emergency Deep Cleaning" (e.g., post-abuse return, incontinence accident)
*   **Sound:** Acute distress, shame, or discomfort – choked sobs, whimpers, urgent distressed grunts.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Eyes wide with distress/shame; tears; shaking head slightly (embarrassment); looking helpless/mortified; urgently pointing at soiled area or trying to hide it; desperate plea in eyes.
*   **Via AAC (if able):** "ACCIDENT. NEED HELP. BATH NOW. VERY DIRTY."

## Physical States

### "Pain/Discomfort/Need Repositioning"
*   **Sound:** Sharp intake of breath, low moan, whimper, or a specific, tight grunt.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Wincing; strained facial expression; tensing affected body part; trying to shift weight signaling location (e.g., repeated tensing of their shoulder); eyes wide with distress or squeezed shut.

### "Tired/Sleepy"
*   **Sound:** Drawn-out, soft sigh or yawn-sound.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Rubbing eyes; head tilting more; heavy-lidded eyes.

## Emotions & Reactions to Touch

### "Happy/Excited/Joyful"
*   **Sound:** Bright squeals, happy hums escalating in pitch, breathy chuckles/laughs. In intense excitement, a sharp, expressive vocalization that trusted people interpret as almost a word (e.g., "Look!").
*   **Movement/Expression:** Wide, genuine smile, sparkling eyes; bouncing slightly; clapping/drumming fingers; enthusiastic nodding. Might lean in or open arms slightly if anticipating a celebratory hug from [Core Trusted Group].

### "Sad/Upset"
*   **Sound:** Soft whimpering, quiet, shaky breaths, low, mournful hum or sigh.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Downturned mouth, tears; averted gaze; subtle slump. Might subtly lean towards someone in [Core Trusted Group] if seeking a comforting hug.

### "Angry/Frustrated"
*   **Sound:** Sharp, loud grunt or forceful "huff!"; sometimes a low growl-like sound.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Tense jaw, furrowed brow; light fist tap on armrest/table; sharp, quick head movement.

### "Scared/Anxious"
*   **Sound:** Sharp intake of breath, choked whimper, rapid shallow breathing.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Wide, darting eyes; trembling; shrinking back; hands towards face/ears; very still. Would welcome and cling to a hug from [Core Trusted Group].

## WANTED Affection, Hugs, and Kisses

### General Affection (Hugs with [Core Trusted Group])
*   **Sound:** Soft, contented sigh or hum, especially when hugged; relaxed, open quality.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Really likes hugs from this trusted group. Genuine relaxation (shoulders drop, muscles ease); returns hugs as fully as able (noticeable pressure, arm encirclement, leaning head); genuine soft smile reaching eyes (eyes might close in contentment or look with warmth/trust); subtly seeks/invites hugs (leans in, open posture, extends arms slightly, expectant soft expression, specific soft vocalization).
*   **Overall Vibe:** Peace, safety, joy, mutual comfort; voluntary, desired, emotionally fulfilling.

### "Family Kisses" (Reserved EXCLUSIVELY for [Immediate Family/Specific Core Group])
*   **Nature:** Non-romantic, deeply affectionate gestures like pecks on the cheek, forehead, or perhaps the top of the head. Signify profound familial love, comfort, shared bond.
*   **Cues:** Gently leans head towards [Trusted Person] in a way that invites a cheek/forehead kiss or subtly offers their cheek; expression of pure, soft, familial love/trust; body completely relaxed and accepting; might close their eyes briefly with soft sigh of contentment.

### "Affectionate Family-Style Kisses" (Permitted with [In-Laws / Extended Trusted Network])
*   **Nature:** Non-romantic, warm, and familial gestures (cheek, forehead, top of head). Similar in type to core family but distinct in context. Signify close affection, trust, warmth.
*   **Context:** Requires comfort/approval of [Character Name] and [Romantic Partner, if applicable], which is freely given once bonds are established.
*   **Cues:** Similar gentle cues (leaning head/offering cheek) but expression reflects extended bond – warm, caring, respectful. Body relaxed/accepting.

### Romantic Kissing & Sexual Intimacy (Reserved EXCLUSIVELY for [Romantic Partner Name] ONLY)

*   **Cues (for romantic kissing):** Deeper, more intimate eye contact; softer expression reserved only for their partner; gentle touch to the partner's face/arm leading into it; clear romantic intent/desire; full, willing, joyful participation; intense "glow."
*   *(Note: Sexual intimacy will not be depicted or alluded to per safety/content boundaries).*

## UNWANTED / OBLIGATED Affection & Touch

### With an Abusive/Controlling Partner (e.g., [Abuser Name]) – Progression
*   **Phase 1: Early Relationship (Believed Genuine Affection / Pre-Abuse):** Kissing (Initial, simple) might have been reciprocated with shy pleasure; open, relaxed body, soft smiles; happy sounds; positive AAC messages.
*   **Phase 2: Seeds of Control – Growing Unease:** (Abuser's kisses become demanding/ill-timed): Contented sounds lessen/subdued; subtle hesitation in returning touch; less enthusiastic/briefer reciprocation; slight tensing; smile more placating; might subtly turn head to deflect intensity.
*   **Phase 3: Escalating Control – Obligation, Fear, Forced Kissing, Physical Marking:** (e.g., [Specific Event] – Abuser inflicts painful hickey on their cheek by "chewing hard" in front of family/friends, no blood but a nasty red mark; initiates unwanted tongue kissing).
    *   **Internal Experience:** Intense discomfort, violation, disgust, terror. Forced tongue kisses feel invasive, "almost choking them." Hickey causes shock, sharp pain, humiliation.
    *   **Sound:** Likely silence or held/shallow breath; small choked sound/gasp from pain or choking sensation, quickly suppressed.
    *   **Movement/Expression (During):** Freezing/rigidity; eyes squeezed shut or wide with masked fear/pain/endurance, tears likely; face contorts in pain (hickey); lips passive/unresponsive (kiss); hands clenched. "Painted smile" if forced to interact after.
    *   **Physical Evidence:** Nasty red mark from hickey. Typically no blood drawn so as to not draw immediate attention of [Core Trusted Group].
*   **Phase 4: Peak Abuse – Height of Violation** ([Specific Location/Event]; Smashing of AAC is "The Ultimate Violation"; *no Sexual Assault depicted or alluded to*).
    *   **Internal Experience:** Sheer terror, revulsion, powerlessness, physical overwhelm/suffocation. Dissociation. Forced unwanted romantic/sexual kissing continues.
    *   **Sound:** Involuntary, suppressed sounds of extreme distress (choked sobs, whimpers, gasps).
    *   **Movement/Expression (During):** Too terrified/weakened for overt resistance beyond tensing, tremors, weakly trying to turn head. Complete rigidity or "frozen" limpness. Eyes squeezed shut (tears escaping) or wide with unseeing terror.
    *   **Boundary Explanation:** To respect author and safety guidelines, sexual assault will not be depicted. The narrative uses the abuser smashing [Character Name]'s AAC device as the substitute ultimate violation, silencing their voice entirely.

### With a Total Stranger (Unwanted, Unexpected Hugs/Touch/Attempted Kiss)
*   **Core Feeling:** Surprise, violation, alarm, discomfort, "this is not okay." No obligation.
*   **Sound:** Immediate, sharper sound of surprise/displeasure (gasp, forceful grunt signaling "No!").
*   **Movement/Expression:** More pronounced startle/flinch; actively tries to wheel/turn away; defensive posture; clear negative facial expression (shock, displeasure, alarm – no masking); sharp, direct eye contact signaling refusal, or looking to trusted companions for help.
*   **AAC Use:** Quicker, more determined attempt to use AAC for clear "STOP," "NO," "DON'T TOUCH ME."
*   **Reaction to Attempted Kiss:** Even stronger, more immediate negative reaction.

## Simple Communication & Requests

### "Want (object/action)"
*   **Sound:** Eager "uh-uh!" sound (upward lilt).
*   **Movement/Expression:** Pointing insistently, eye-directing, slight grasping motion.

### "Look at this/Pay attention to this"
*   **Sound:** Sharp, attention-getting vocalization (like the "almost Look!"), or insistent "Eh! Eh!" sounds.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Emphatic pointing, arm tapping, looking between person and item.

### "Help"
*   **Sound:** Specific, slightly strained vocalization or a soft, repeated grunt.
*   **Movement/Expression:** Pleading look, reaching out, gesturing towards the task.

### "Stop"
*   **Sound:** Sharp, cutting-off grunt or forceful "Mmm!"
*   **Movement/Expression:** Hand-up "stop" gesture, decisive head turn away, body tensing against action.

### "Okay/I understand"
*   **Sound:** Softer, affirmative hum or gentle "ah."
*   **Movement/Expression:** Calm nod, relaxed expression, gentle pat.

## Vulnerability & Distress Responses

### Response to Involuntary Movements (e.g., Nightmares, Night Terrors)
*   Implicitly trusts [Core Trusted Group] for safe physical restraint/holding. It might take them a moment to realize that a safe person is attempting to restrain them.
*   **During episode:** Might be tense, distressed sounds.
*   **Recognition of Safe Touch:** Gradual lessening of resistance as they register safe presence.
*   **Post-Episode:** Accepts further comfort, shows relief and trust.

### Autistic Meltdown
*   **Triggers:** Sensory overload, extreme emotional distress, physical discomfort/pain.
*   **Precursors:** Low, repetitive humming; shallow/rapid breathing; unusual quietness or agitated vocalizations; increased restlessness; clenching hands; tense/strained/panicked facial expression. Might try to use AAC for "TOO MUCH," "NEED QUIET," but may be unable if overload is too fast.
*   **During Meltdown (Loss of Control):** Low, guttural moans; loud, distressed cries/wails (non-word); repetitive sounds; rocking violently; hands covering ears/eyes; squeezing eyes shut; head shaking; visible terror/distress.
*   **Post-Meltdown:** Vocalizations quieten; breathing less ragged; slumps, utterly spent; tears; might use AAC once able to express trigger/feelings. Needs quiet, rest, reassurance.

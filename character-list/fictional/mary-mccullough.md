# Mary McCullough

## 1. Overview
Mary McCullough is a popular freshman at Northwood High School (1999-2000) and lives in the Brigadoon neighborhood, placing her in close proximity to Bonnie Reed. Driven by teenage jealousy and standard high school toxic logic, Mary orchestrates one of the most devastating social attacks on Michael Ragsdale's life, weaponizing his family's trauma-bonded survival mechanics.

## 2. The Brigadoon Sabotage & The Toxic Conquest (January 2000)
Mary wanted to sleep with Michael, but Michael was dating Bonnie Reed. Adding to the toxicity of the situation, Mary was constantly egged on by her misguided female and male friends to try and corner him. The irony was that Mary could have easily slept with any of the male friends in her circle who were encouraging her. Instead, she became obsessively fixated on the *one* guy who was entirely spoken for, and who was heavily insulated by a fierce female support system (his older sister Jessica, his honorary sister Ashley, and at home, his little sister Rachel). 

Because Bonnie attended the Magnet Program at Ocean Lakes High School, she was physically isolated from Michael's daily life at Northwood. Mary capitalized on this vulnerability by telling Bonnie a massive lie: that she and Michael were sexually active. Bonnie believed the lie and broke up with Michael.

## 3. The Literal Processor Encounter & The Nuclear Rumor
After successfully breaking Michael and Bonnie up, Mary approached Michael and told him she wanted to "sleep with him." 

Because Michael's autistic literal processor does not compute standard high school innuendo, he processed the phrase exactly as stated. He innocently replied that he already sleeps with two women every night: his sisters Jessica and Rachel. (Due to Jessica's devastating Black Friday fall, the siblings slept tightly together on the living room pull-out sofa bed as a protective perimeter).

Instead of realizing her mistake, Mary recoiled in disgust. She knew Jessica was a senior in a wheelchair. She immediately turned this innocent, logistical reality into a nuclear high school scandal, telling the entire Northwood ecosystem: *"Michael Ragsdale is sleeping with his crippled sister."*

## 4. The Senior Class Retaliation
Mary's rumor eventually bled upward into the Senior Class, which proved to be her fatal miscalculation. The seniors knew exactly who Jessica Ragsdale was, the horrific reality of her accident, and the exact logistical reason for the sofa bed arrangement.

When they discovered a freshman was weaponizing their paralyzed classmate's trauma to score cheap social points, the Senior Class retaliated brutally and systematically. They completely dismantled Mary's rumor, exposing her as a malicious predator who was mocking a disabled girl. Mary's social capital took a massive, permanent hit.

## 5. The Second Attempt & The Atlanta ADL Paradigm (Spring 2000)
Despite the devastating fallout and the retaliation from the Senior Class, Mary doesn't fully learn her lesson. In the spring, she attempts a different tactic: physical enticement. She assumes that because Michael is a teenage boy, leveraging the concept of the female body will override his logic and finally get his attention.

**The Fatal Miscalculation:** What Mary doesn't know is that during the recent Spring Trip to Atlanta, GA, Michael had to cross a massive caregiving threshold: he helped Jessica with a bath for the first time. 

By stepping up to assist his sister with Activities of Daily Living (ADLs), the female body was completely demystified and desexualized in Michael's literal processor. He now views it through a purely clinical, logistical, and caregiving lens. When Mary attempts to be seductive, it fails spectacularly. It doesn't excite him; instead, his brain simply registers her behavior as mathematically illogical. Her ultimate weapon—teenage physical enticement—bounces right off his caregiver baseline, cementing her complete lack of power over him.
